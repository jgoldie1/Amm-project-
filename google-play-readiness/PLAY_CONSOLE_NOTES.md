# Play Console Readiness Notes

Before submission, verify the final shipped Android app matches every Play Console declaration.

- Target audience and content declarations must match real age access.
- Families requirements apply when children are included in the target audience.
- Data Safety answers must match every SDK and data flow in production.
- Content rating must reflect livestreaming, UGC, chat, marketplace, gaming, and other relevant features.
- Reviewer credentials must allow Google to inspect gated functionality.
- Account deletion disclosures must point to working in-app and web flows.
- Digital purchase implementation must be reviewed against the current Google Play billing rules that apply to the final product and markets.
- Privacy policy and safety policies must be publicly accessible and consistent with actual behavior.

Do not mark the app ready for submission until the production integration checklist and test plan are complete.
