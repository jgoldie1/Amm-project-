# TryAMM Omniverse Integration

This branch adds the shared control-plane definition for the TryAMM ecosystem.

- Social short video, livestreaming and PK
- Enter Globe, teleportation and time-travel simulations
- 13 Living Worlds and separate StarVerse exploration network
- Quantum Tag, Battle Deck, original paranormal hunt, original creature battle and original mischief-creature worlds
- Volcano Console and universal Bluetooth controller layer
- Isaiah AI TV, OmniBox and AMM Billboard Showcase
- Store Builder, Holo Menu, food delivery, rideshare and marketplace boundaries
- 64-track AI studio, advanced stems, microphone control, record label and music streaming
- All American University, UIC/Greenville/HBCU concept network and Middleverse Workforce
- Jacobie Vision Cybersecurity, Aniyah CrossBorder, real estate and OmniCare 360
- Internal permissioned ledger and 13569 digital collectible war

## Shared services

All modules must use the same identity, age lanes, permissions, feature flags, asset registry, wallet ledger, analytics, moderation, accessibility, partner approvals and audit logs.

## Release truth

This is a verified pre-alpha integration layer, not a claim of a shipped product. Native engine builds, live media/backend deployments, final licensed art, performed audio, device testing, accessibility participant testing, security/legal review and platform certification require genuine external evidence.
