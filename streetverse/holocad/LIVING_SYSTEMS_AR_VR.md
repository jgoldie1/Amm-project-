# HoloCAD Living Systems — AR/VR extension (development pilot)

This extends the existing HoloCAD v24 building schema. It is isolated in draft PR #695 and **does not replace** BJ, StreetVerse driving, NPCs, or other playable-world behavior.

## Subjects and layers
- Human (24 schematic parts): body envelope, skeleton, muscle, heart, lungs, liver, stomach, intestines, kidneys, simplified airway and vascular layers
- Canine (18 parts): illustrative quadruped limbs, skull, spine, muscle and internal organ shapes
- Automobile (23 parts): body shell, chassis, powertrain, drivetrain, wheels, brakes, electrical controls and cabin
- Apartment (16 parts): roof, walls, floors, stairway, plumbing, electrical conduits and HVAC

The total 81 low-poly parts are **original schematics**. They are neither licensed medical anatomy datasets nor OEM engineering drawings and contain no physical manufacturing tolerances.

## 3D, augmented reality, virtual reality
- All subjects have WebGL 3D inspection in ordinary desktop or phone browsers.
- **ENTER AR** checks secure HTTPS context and genuine `navigator.xr.isSessionSupported('immersive-ar')`. With a supported AR device and explicit approval, the same scene renders as a miniature floating schematic near the viewer. This first pass does **not** anchor the model to a real-world surface, estimate scene depth, or scan organs.
- **ENTER VR** checks `immersive-vr` and starts a headset session when permission is granted. WebXR controllers can select a part with **trigger/select**; **squeeze** cycles teardown separation. One controller is sufficient for these basic actions.
- **EXIT XR**, headset session-end, mode change, and component disposal restore the normal 3D viewer.
- Unsupported hardware, insecure pages, and declined XR permissions leave the 3D parts list and controls available rather than faking immersive XR.
- On-screen CAD controls remain available outside XR, with 44+px touch targets. Browser DOM overlay inside immersive XR is device-dependent; the current headset controller functions cover selection and separation. Full in-headset labels and a spatial CAD toolbox are future work.

## Interactive training behaviors
- Layer toggles show or hide systems.
- Raycast, touchscreen, mouse, or headset trigger can choose a component.
- Explosion slider and headset squeeze separate it; reassembly returns it to its starting position.
- `MARK FAULT` and `SIMULATED REPAIR` update **visual training status only**; they do not diagnose or repair a real animal/person/car/building.
- Exports a versioned JSON parts **manifest**, not an approved 3D printable solid or authenticated game item.
- No player XP, inventory rewards, weapons, or real maintenance advice are generated by the lab.

## Release and performance verification
- Source regression contract: `node amm-omniverse/tests/streetverse-holocad-living-xr-contract.mjs`.
- Existing building validator: `node streetverse/holocad/tests.mjs`.
- TypeScript: `cd amm-omniverse && npx tsc --noEmit`.
- HoloCAD CI workflow includes a Vite build.
- Additional release requirements: hands-on Quest/Android AR browser session tests, framerate/memory checks, controller selection on both hands, resize/orientation, graceful XR permission refusal, one-hand workflows, and collision isolation from StreetVerse gameplay.

**Status: development pilot; source/build tests are not equivalent to verified headset playback.**
