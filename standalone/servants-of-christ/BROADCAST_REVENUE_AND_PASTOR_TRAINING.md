# Servants of Christ — Broadcast Business & Pastor Training
Status: proposed programs, not active sales or promised income.

## Advertising and sponsorship
- Sponsor spots: 15, 30, or 60 seconds, inserted only into rights-cleared programming, with ad labeling and a review queue.
- Show sponsorship: branded introduction, end-card, and sponsor directory.
- Community business directory: paid business listings clearly distinguished from ministry recommendations.
- Event sponsorship: underwriting for conferences, educational events, and community service.
- Commercial production: optional fee for scriptwriting, captions, translation, voiceover with consent, and video editing.
- Do not sell personal prayer data, imply religious endorsement of products, or interrupt sensitive counseling with ads.
- Keep worship access free; separate donations from ad sales and commercial receipts.
- Ad contracts should define inventory, impressions or placements, dates, makegoods, refunds, restrictions, and approval rights.

## Suggested pilot rate card (illustrative; not market-validated)
- 15-second pre-roll on a verified replay: $25 per scheduled placement
- 30-second pre-roll: $45 per scheduled placement
- Local sponsor directory listing: $20 per month
- Event sponsor package: $150 per event
- Commercial production: quote individually based on work and rights
Do not charge until actual audience reach, fulfillment, contracts and payment rails are verified. Never guarantee views or results.

## Other revenue pathways
- Approved physical merchandise and books, including print-on-demand where appropriate.
- Paid optional workshops and courses; disclose credentials and avoid unverified accreditation.
- Event registration and sponsorship.
- Media production services for other ministries, billed separately.
- Optional memberships for extra resources, not access to prayer or essential worship.
- Verified voluntary donations, with tax claims only if substantiated.
- Approved affiliate partnerships with clear disclosures.

## Pastor Ofori training assistant — guided curriculum
1. Sign in securely, two-factor authentication, role permissions, privacy and passwords.
2. Review the dashboard: ministry content, schedules, commerce, audience analytics.
3. Upload one rights-cleared sermon; title, description, scripture, caption, thumbnail and review.
4. Schedule a prerecorded broadcast and test playback on mobile.
5. Run a private live rehearsal with an authorized streaming provider and moderator.
6. Create a sponsor slot, preview the ad, approve it, and publish only under a signed agreement.
7. Add one product and test a non-live checkout with provider test credentials.
8. Review earnings reports: ads, services, store and donations in separate ledgers.
9. Use Ofori AI to answer how-to questions grounded in approved manuals, with screenshots and human escalation.
10. Publish and review weekly; captions, content permissions, community feedback and backup checks.

## AI coach design
- Voice or large one-tap text prompts: “Show me how to schedule a service”, “How do I approve a commercial?”, “Where is the sales report?”
- Context-aware step-by-step help, with one task per screen and a repeat button.
- Guided simulations and rehearsal mode; no real publishing or charges without explicit confirmation.
- Role-specific permissions for pastor, producer, moderator, finance, and volunteer.
- AI never claims to have uploaded a sermon, launched a broadcast, signed a contract or transferred money unless the backend confirms it.
- Require human review of theological interpretations, ad suitability and AI-generated translations.

## Production dependencies
Broadcast provider, content licenses, video storage and delivery, admin authentication, editorial CMS, sponsor order system, ad insertion or placement scheduling, invoices/receipts, payment processor, moderation, captions, analytics, privacy terms, and verified integrations with TRYAMM.
