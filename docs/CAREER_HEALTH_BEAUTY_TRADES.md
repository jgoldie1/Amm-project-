# TRYAMM Career Pathways — Beauty, Wellness & Healthcare Support

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Expand All American Marketplace University + Student JARVIS + Launchpad into job-ready career pathways for esthetician, massage therapy, medical assistant and CNA learners while clearly separating TRYAMM education from state licensing, approved training, clinical-hour and certification requirements.

## Shared pathway
EXPLORE → REQUIREMENTS BY LOCATION → LEARNING PLAN → FUNDING/SCHOLARSHIP MATCH → APPROVED TRAINING PARTNER WHERE REQUIRED → SKILLS/PRACTICE → EXAM/CREDENTIAL READINESS → EMPLOYER MATCH → BUSINESS/CAREER JARVIS → CONTINUING EDUCATION.

Student JARVIS tracks deadlines, prerequisites and missing documents. Learning Passport tracks TRYAMM coursework and verified external credentials separately.

## 1. Esthetician
TRYAMM can provide foundational learning in skin science, sanitation, client communication, business operations, retail/product education, scheduling, accessibility, ethics, marketing and financial literacy.

External gate: state/local licensing and required approved school hours/testing where applicable. TRYAMM must not represent a learner as licensed unless authoritative verification exists.

Career/business path:
ESTHETICIAN LEARNER → LICENSE/VERIFICATION → SALON/SPA JOB OR START BUSINESS → BUSINESS PASSPORT → JARVIS → BOOKING → HOLO MARKETPLACE → PRODUCT SALES → HOLO COUPONS → JIN PAY → CLIENT FOLLOW-UP → SUPPLIER EXCHANGE → HR/GROWTH.

## 2. Massage Therapy
TRYAMM can provide anatomy/physiology foundations, client communication, business operations, scheduling, accessibility, hygiene/safety, ethics, scope-of-practice education and exam-prep support.

External gate: jurisdiction-specific education hours, licensing/certification, background checks and exams where applicable. No medical diagnosis or treatment claims unless permitted by licensed scope.

Business path supports studio/mobile-service operations, appointments, merchant profile, customer reminders, intake-form workflow, invoicing, Holo Marketplace service listings and Holo Delivery only for related retail products—not for representing physical care as remote delivery.

## 3. Medical Assistant
TRYAMM can provide academic foundations, terminology, communication, administrative workflows, patient-service concepts, scheduling, basic healthcare-office technology and exam/career preparation.

Clinical skills, externships, injections, specimen handling and other hands-on procedures require approved supervision/training and applicable legal scope. The platform must not certify clinical competence from online completion alone.

Career pathway:
FOUNDATION → APPROVED PROGRAM/EXTERNSHIP WHERE REQUIRED → EXAM/CERTIFICATION READINESS → EMPLOYER MATCH → ONBOARDING → CONTINUING EDUCATION.

## 4. Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA)
TRYAMM can provide prerequisite review, study support, communication, resident/patient dignity, infection-control theory, workplace readiness, financial literacy and exam preparation.

External gate: state-approved nurse-aide training, required clinical hours, competency evaluation/background requirements and registry status. TRYAMM Passport must distinguish `TRYAMM COURSE COMPLETED` from `STATE/EXTERNAL CREDENTIAL VERIFIED`.

## Career Opportunity Match
Opportunity Engine can match learners to:
- approved schools/programs
- scholarships/grants

- employers
- accessible jobs
- childcare/transportation resource navigation
- continuing education

Match states remain LIKELY | POSSIBLE | VERIFY | NOT A MATCH; no guaranteed admission, license, certification or job.

## Accessibility
Every pathway inherits Accessibility Passport: captions/transcripts, screen-reader structure, keyboard/switch access, voice controls, one-handed mode, reduced motion, enlarged targets/text and communication preferences. Clinical/physical job requirements must be represented neutrally; accessibility settings must not be used to hide opportunities.
