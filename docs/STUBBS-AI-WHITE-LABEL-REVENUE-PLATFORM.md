# Stubbs AI White-Label Revenue Platform

## Goal
Turn Stubbs AI into a configurable AI product that can be branded, trained on approved business knowledge, connected to TryAMM/Jinn tools, and sold repeatedly to businesses, creators, restaurants, agencies, ministries, schools, professionals, and partners.

## Product model
One core AI runtime, many branded deployments.

Each customer deployment can configure:
- assistant name, logo, avatar/hologram, voice and tone
- business/creator knowledge base
- FAQs, policies, catalog/menu/services
- storefront and HoloMenu links
- Jinn invoices, payment links and receipt lookup
- HoloCoupons and HoloPass loyalty
- appointment/reservation workflows
- lead capture and CRM notes
- multilingual/HoloLingo behavior
- accessibility defaults
- approved actions and approval thresholds
- call-center routing and escalation
- analytics and conversion attribution

## Deployments
- website chat widget
- TryAMM business profile
- HoloMenu assistant
- mobile/PWA assistant
- SMS/chat channels where legally/provider-supported
- voice/call-center assistant
- QR/NFC assistant launch
- livestream/shop-live copilot
- internal employee copilot

## Revenue streams
1. Setup/customization fee.
2. Monthly SaaS subscription.
3. Usage/AI-credit margin.
4. Voice/call-minute margin where permitted.
5. Premium integrations.
6. Managed knowledge-base service.
7. Lead-generation/conversion tools.
8. Holo avatar/voice customization packages.
9. Business automation add-ons.
10. White-label/reseller licensing for agencies.
11. Marketplace revenue share on third-party agents/templates.
12. Enterprise/private deployment and support contracts.

## Example configurable plans (illustrative only)
- Starter: $29-$49/month + usage; basic branded assistant.
- Growth: $99-$199/month + usage; business knowledge, leads, coupons, reservations and analytics.
- Pro: $299-$499/month + usage; voice/call-center, automation, multiple staff roles, advanced integrations.
- Enterprise/Agency: custom setup + monthly platform fee + usage + support SLA.

Prices are not hard-coded; Admin Revenue Policy controls future offers and grandfathering.

## Stubbs AI Builder
A self-service wizard should let a customer:
1. Choose business type.
2. Name/brand assistant.
3. Upload/link approved knowledge.
4. Connect storefront/menu/services.
5. Choose allowed actions.
6. Configure language/accessibility.
7. Test in sandbox.
8. Choose plan/credits.
9. Publish to selected channels.

## Vertical templates
- Restaurant AI: menu Q&A, reservations, order links, HoloCoupons, catering leads.
- Retail AI: product discovery, inventory questions, coupons, order status.
- Creator AI: fan Q&A, schedule, merch, tickets, affiliate campaigns.
- Black Business Growth AI: directory profile, procurement alerts, funding-readiness, supplier discovery.
- Professional Services AI: intake, qualification, appointment booking, document checklist.
- Real Estate AI: property Q&A, lead capture, tours, scheduling.
- Ministry/Faith AI: approved teachings/resources, events, donations links, pastoral escalation boundaries.
- School/Academy AI: course navigation, enrollment FAQ, tutoring support with policy guardrails.
- Call Center AI: inbound triage, FAQ, lead qualification, call summaries, human handoff.

## Usage economics
Stubbs AI should meter provider cost by request/model/tool/voice usage. Cost Manager calculates true unit cost and applies a configurable markup/credit price. Never offer unlimited expensive model usage without caps or fair-use controls.

Example flow:
Customer buys $50 AI credits -> provider/infra cost is measured -> reserve covers usage -> remaining contribution margin is TryAMM revenue after applicable fees/tax accounting.

## Reseller/agency channel
Agencies can create branded assistants for clients under an agency account. TryAMM can charge:
- agency platform subscription
- per-assistant fee
- usage margin
- optional revenue share
- premium support/integration fees

## Marketplace
Publish approved AI templates/agents in a Stubbs AI Marketplace. Third-party builders can sell agents/templates under configurable developer/platform revenue-waterfall policies.

## Data/security rules
- tenant isolation
- role-based access
- explicit data-source permissions
- audit logs for tool actions
- no invented business facts
- no autonomous money movement without configured approval
- sensitive actions require step-up authentication
- customer data is not casually shared across tenants
- deletion/export controls

## Why it scales
Build the AI engine once, sell customization repeatedly. Revenue becomes recurring through subscriptions, usage, add-ons, integrations, reseller seats and marketplace fees rather than only one-time app development.
