# All American Network × FaithVerse × Servants of Christ × Holo Ads
Status: Integration contract and pilot handoff. Not a production activation.

## Existing code reused
- MinistryBroadcastNetwork defines ministry ownership, distribution lanes, rights, live moderation, captions and no-pay-to-pray.
- TryammBroadcastNetwork defines channel registry and programming guide.
- HoloAdsServer defines approved campaigns, verified events and authoritative settlement.
- Holo Ads Studio is a draft-only advertiser interface at /holo-ads-studio.html.
- Servants of Christ has an independent /watch site and BROADCAST_REVENUE_AND_PASTOR_TRAINING.md with a pilot rate card.

## Separation of assets and ledgers
1. FaithVerse scripture, worship, prayers and pastoral counseling remain free of mandatory ads and commercial gating.
2. Servants of Christ retains editorial control; approved sponsorships can be included in rights-cleared shows, replays, conferences and directory pages after written approval.
3. All American Network distributes independently approved programming; never presume redistribution rights or established FAST/OTT carriage.
4. HoloLab creates scripts, reviewed captions, ads, audio and permitted creatives; video rendering/export and delivery require proof.
5. Holo Ads tracks approved sponsors and inventory. A draft or simulated impression is never billable. Real-money billing and revenue share require server-verified delivery, valid signed contracts and reconciled provider payments.
6. Donations, ad purchases, memberships and merchandise payments have separate purposes and ledgers. No charitable tax deduction representation without verified status.

## Pilot release sequence
- Program owner approves a 30-second local-business sponsorship for one replay (or workshop).
- Confirm the show is rights-cleared, with music permissions, captions and a ministry-approved ad position.
- HoloLab delivers an approved media asset (not only a plan).
- All American Network or Servants site presents a private test replay with labeled sponsor message.
- Validate playback, click destination, disclosure and accessible controls on real mobile devices.
- Test payment with approved payment provider test mode, immutable owner notification, refund path and reconciliation.
- Only activate paid sponsorship after delivery logs, pricing, audience visibility, ad review, owner signoff and a production payment path are verified.

## My Tribe opportunity
Disability-owned businesses may supply captioning, audio description, approved narration, editing, accessible design and ad production services to ministry and network customers. Respect founder choice; never target people using inferred disability or religion.

## Outstanding blockers
External streaming provider credentials and authorization; tested public site deployment; consistent channel program guide; actual licensed content; verified billing and settlement; payout/tax onboarding; accessibility QA and moderation.
