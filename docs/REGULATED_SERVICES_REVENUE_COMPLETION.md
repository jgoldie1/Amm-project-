# TRYAMM Regulated Services + Revenue Completion

Status: LOCKED BUSINESS/COMPLIANCE SPECIFICATION

## Product principle
TRYAMM is the technology/marketplace/orchestration layer unless and until a specific TRYAMM entity is separately licensed or enrolled for a regulated activity. Qualified professionals deliver regulated services. The platform verifies, matches, schedules, communicates, collects permitted platform/software fees, and records evidence.

## Emergency shortcut
`I NEED HELP NOW` must sit above normal booking. If a user indicates imminent physical danger, medical emergency, or another emergency category, route to appropriate emergency/public resources and do not hold the user inside a normal marketplace funnel.

### Lawyer-on-demand traffic-stop mode
A user can open `LEGAL HELP NOW` and choose a situation such as traffic stop, arrest/detention concern, housing, family, business, immigration, employment or general legal question. The app may provide general educational rights information and connect the user to an available qualified lawyer. It must not impersonate counsel or obstruct lawful instructions from authorities.

## Provider verification
Regulated listings need:
LICENSE/CREDENTIAL → JURISDICTION → STATUS → EXPIRATION → INSURANCE/OTHER REQUIREMENTS WHERE APPLICABLE → PLATFORM APPROVAL → PERIODIC RECHECK.

The provider profile shows the verification state and what is/was verified without publishing unnecessary credential identifiers.

## Revenue models
Different professions have different fee-sharing/referral restrictions. Therefore TRYAMM supports multiple revenue models instead of applying one percentage everywhere.

Preferred models:
1. `Platform subscription`: professional/business pays monthly for Business JARVIS, storefront, scheduling, CRM, Quantum Zapier, analytics and lead-management tools.
2. `Booking/software fee`: fixed disclosed technology/booking charge when lawful.
3. `Referral fee`: only where the professional rules and jurisdiction permit it.
4. `Sponsored access`: employer, foundation, school, ministry/nonprofit or public program funds access.
5. `Provider-paid marketing`: clearly labeled advertising/promotional placement compliant with applicable professional advertising rules.

Never silently take a percentage of regulated professional fees when fee-sharing rules prohibit it.

## Tax lane
Paid federal tax return preparers must hold their own valid PTIN. IRS lists the 2026 PTIN obtain/renew fee as $18.75. TRYAMM can provide readiness, workflow, education, reminders and provider verification, but cannot issue a PTIN.

## Telehealth / Medicaid lane
Telehealth is a delivery method, and Medicaid coverage/payment/provider rules vary by state and payer. Provider and service must satisfy applicable state/provider requirements. Qualified/enrolled provider status, patient eligibility, covered service, documentation, telehealth permissions and payer billing rules must all be verified before claim preparation/submission.

Architecture:
PATIENT REQUEST → VERIFIED PROVIDER → ELIGIBILITY/COVERAGE CHECK → APPOINTMENT → SECURE TELEHEALTH → DOCUMENTATION → CODING/BILLING REVIEW → CLAIM PREPARATION/SUBMISSION THROUGH AUTHORIZED BILLING PATH → REMITTANCE/RECONCILIATION.

TRYAMM must not bill Medicaid under an unqualified provider or manufacture clinical documentation/codes.

Health information is segregated from social/advertising systems. When HIPAA applies, vendors handling PHI/ePHI need appropriate controls and business-associate arrangements where required.

## Accessibility
All service lanes inherit Accessibility Passport: captions, keyboard/switch access, one-handed operation, screen-reader semantics, large text/targets, reduced motion, communication preference and interpreter/accessibility matching. A disability setting is never disclosed to a provider without permission except where required to fulfill an explicitly requested accommodation.

## Profitability gates
Before a service is promoted aggressively, track:
CAC → activation → paid conversion → revenue/user → variable service cost → contribution margin → support/compliance cost → retention → payback period.

A service may be strategically valuable but must not be mislabeled profitable until production data demonstrates positive unit economics.
