# TryAMM Creator Universe — Victor Handoff

## Mission
Help creators turn ideas, talent and owned/licensed media into lawful monetizable work. AI should act as a helpmate: it can draft, organize, edit, generate assets, translate, prepare distribution, test workflows and surface monetization opportunities, while the creator controls identity, rights, approvals and publication.

## Product surfaces
- **StarVerse — Anyone Can Be a Star**: creator discovery, auditions, talent pages, virtual stages, fan experiences and production pathways.
- **Isaiah AI TV**: AI-assisted creator television, channels, shows, live/recorded programming and interactive experiences.
- **HoloVerse**: AR/VR/MR/holographic performances, worlds, characters and interactive media.
- **OmniBox**: original/licensed short-form and episodic vertical drama, series and creator storytelling hub.

### Branding rule
Do not advertise, reference or imply affiliation with DramaBox. The connected repository currently has no DramaBox string. Going forward use **OmniBox** internally as the working product name, but perform formal trademark clearance before public launch.

## Creator Workstation
Each eligible creator gets a page/workspace with:
1. AI idea studio and research assistant.
2. Script room, episode planner and season planner.
3. Storyboard, shot list and virtual production planning.
4. Casting, authorized avatar and My Digital Twin tools.
5. Quantum Speed Engine / Asset Forge for original 3D/4D assets.
6. HoloVerse virtual sets and holographic production.
7. Music/audio/voice workflow with rights tracking.
8. Video editing, captions, dubbing and translation.
9. Thumbnail, poster, trailer and promo generation.
10. Rights/provenance ledger.
11. Universal Social Fabric publishing/distribution connectors.
12. Monetization controls, storefront, licensing and analytics.
13. AI CreatorOps/GameOps issue reporting, bounded repair and audit trail.

## Creator page
Each creator may have a public page with portfolio, shows, games, music, products, live events, memberships, tips, sponsor offers, digital goods and licensed assets. Private controls stay in the Creator Workstation.

## My Digital Twin / AI Twin Clone
A creator may create an authorized digital twin using their own scan, voice, movement and performance data, or materials for which they have explicit rights.

Store granular permissions for:
- likeness;
- voice;
- games;
- shows;
- advertising;
- territories;
- duration;
- allowed roles/tasks;
- revocation and deletion.

Modes:
- avatar only;
- AI assistant twin;
- authorized virtual performance double;
- creator clone workflow for approved repetitive work.

Possible uses:
- acting in original/licensed shows;
- multilingual dubbed performances;
- approved AI assistant for bounded creator/business tasks;
- virtual stunt/performance double;
- Living Game World / HoloVerse appearances;
- reusable creator-owned character versions.

Do not silently impersonate real people. Require consent and identity controls, and add synthetic-media disclosure where audiences could reasonably mistake generated content for an authentic recording or statement.

## Clone / copy / remix boundary
The platform may technically clone a creator's own authorized avatar, duplicate project templates, copy creator-owned scenes/assets, remix creator-owned work, and generate derivative versions where rights allow.

That is **not permission to rip/copy protected shows, films, characters, music, code, branding or catalogs owned by others**. Use original work, public-domain material, properly licensed content, or creator-owned assets.

## Monetization
Support modular monetization:
- subscriptions;
- PPV;
- gifts/tips;
- ads and revenue share;
- sponsorships;
- tickets;
- marketplace/digital goods;
- licensing;
- creator services;
- brand deals.

Every payout path requires ledgering, fraud controls, tax/compliance handling, clear creator terms and rights verification before production launch.

## Market expansion
The same creator identity and Digital Twin should be reusable—with permission—across StarVerse, Isaiah AI TV, OmniBox, HoloVerse, Living Game World, livestreaming, music/podcasts, marketplace, education and brand work. This turns one creator profile into a multi-market business identity rather than isolated accounts.
