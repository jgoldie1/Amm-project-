# TryAMM Self-Funding Creator Economy

## Goal
TryAMM should not subsidize unlimited expensive AI. Every expensive generation or agent task must be metered so subscription revenue, credits, marketplace fees and other platform revenue cover direct operating cost first, then fund growth and profit.

## Pricing architecture
Use a hybrid model:
1. Free acquisition tier for discovery and light creation.
2. Paid monthly plans with included OmniCredits.
3. Paid credit top-ups for heavy AI/media/3D usage.
4. Marketplace/transaction revenue.
5. Business/agency/API contracts.

Never promise unlimited frontier AI, unlimited long-form video, unlimited 3D generation or unlimited agent work without strong fair-use and spend guardrails.

## OmniCredits
OmniCredits are an internal usage abstraction. The public credit cost of each action can remain stable while the internal router selects the most cost-effective acceptable provider/model.

Before running a metered job, estimate:
- AI/provider cost;
- GPU/compute cost;
- storage and egress;
- payment fees allocated to the transaction;
- retry/failure allowance;
- support/fraud reserve where relevant.

Then assign a credit charge that preserves the target margin. Heavy jobs should show an estimate before the creator confirms.

## HoloGPT routing
HoloGPT should be the orchestrator, not always the most expensive model. Route work by task:
- low-cost model for classification, rewriting, metadata and routine support;
- mid-tier model for scripts, planning and common creator assistance;
- frontier model only for hard reasoning, premium production or user-selected quality;
- cache reusable system/context results;
- use batch/background queues for non-urgent workloads;
- enforce per-user and platform-wide daily spend limits.

## Recommended initial plans
- Free: $0, 50 OmniCredits.
- Creator: $19/month, 500 credits.
- Creator Pro: $49/month, 1,800 credits.
- Studio: $99/month, 4,500 credits.
- Business/Agency: $249/month, 14,000 credits.

These are launch hypotheses, not guaranteed final prices. Validate actual unit economics with real provider invoices before public launch and adjust credits rather than silently absorbing cost increases.

## Gross-margin target
Target roughly 60-75% blended gross margin over time. Do not force every individual feature to hit the same margin; acquisition features may be low-margin or free, while premium generation, marketplace, business tools and licensing can carry higher contribution.

## Creator-first monetization
The platform should help creators make money through:
- subscriptions/memberships;
- tips and gifts;
- pay-per-view and tickets;
- advertising/revenue share;
- sponsorships and brand deals;
- marketplace and digital goods;
- licensing of creator-owned IP;
- creator services;
- StarVerse, Isaiah AI TV, OmniBox and HoloVerse monetization.

Fees must be transparent. Creator earnings and platform fees should be ledgered separately.

## Required billing controls before launch
- Stripe or equivalent subscription billing;
- credit ledger with immutable transaction history;
- idempotent usage charging;
- cost-estimate service;
- provider-cost table/versioning;
- prepaid balance and auto-refill opt-in;
- monthly hard caps and user-configurable spend limits;
- fraud/chargeback controls;
- refunds/failed-job credit restoration policy;
- tax/VAT/sales-tax handling as applicable;
- creator payout ledger and reconciliation;
- finance dashboard showing revenue, COGS, gross margin and provider spend.

## Founder dashboard
James should be able to see at minimum:
- MRR/ARR;
- paid users and plan mix;
- credits sold/used/outstanding;
- HoloGPT/model spend by provider;
- video/image/3D compute spend;
- gross profit and gross margin;
- creator GMV and payouts;
- marketplace/platform fees;
- top loss-making features/users;
- abnormal usage/fraud alerts;
- forecast runway.

## Faith/mission principle
The commercial model can reflect the founder's stated mission of stewardship and helping creators become productive and financially independent. The financial system should remain transparent, lawful and measurable: serve creators well, cover real costs, maintain reserves, reinvest in the platform and earn sustainable profit.
