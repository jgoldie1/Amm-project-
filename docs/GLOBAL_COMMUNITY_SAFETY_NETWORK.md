# TRYAMM Global Community Safety Network

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## Mission
Create a paid, global, non-vigilante community-safety system focused on prevention, visibility, de-escalation, safe accompaniment, youth opportunity, verified reporting, and connection to appropriate local services. The network does not replace police, emergency medical services, licensed security, attorneys, or other regulated professionals.

## Core principles
- No vigilantism, weapons patrols, intimidation, retaliation, suspect hunting, or punishment.
- No impersonation of police/security officers.
- No detention, searches, interrogation, or pursuit beyond what ordinary law permits.
- De-escalate, disengage, document safely, and route to appropriate authorities/resources.
- Privacy, anti-bias, accessibility, youth safeguards, and auditability are mandatory.
- Local law controls what roles/services can operate in each jurisdiction.

## Paid roles
1. Community Safety Ambassador — visible presence, directions, accompaniment, de-escalation, resource navigation.
2. Safe Walk / Safe Ride Companion — accompanies students, seniors, workers, creators, disabled users, nightlife workers, and others who request it.
3. Youth Peace Coach — sports/music/gaming/creator/trade activities and conflict prevention; screening/training required.
4. Event Safety Steward — non-security crowd information, accessibility help, lost-person support, emergency routing.
5. Community Resource Navigator — connects people to food, housing, legal aid, mental-health/crisis resources, reentry, education, transportation.
6. Safety Dispatcher — verifies requests, routes companions/providers, maintains check-ins.
7. Safety Trainer — de-escalation, first aid/CPR where appropriately certified, accessibility, bystander intervention, conflict resolution.
8. Licensed Partner Lane — regulated security, medical, legal, transport or other services only through appropriately licensed external providers.

## Stubbs AI / JARVIS safety flow
REQUEST → RISK TRIAGE → LOCAL RULE CHECK → ELIGIBLE ROLE/PROVIDER → MATCH → LIVE CHECK-IN → ESCALATE IF NEEDED → COMPLETE → FEEDBACK → AUDIT.

JARVIS may help users prepare a report, identify local resources, share trip/check-in status with consent, or call attention to emergencies. It must not direct confrontation, pursuit, retaliation, or unlawful surveillance.

## Safe Walk / Companion workflow
User requests destination/time → selects accessibility/communication preferences → verified ambassador accepts → optional trusted-contact share → check-in start → location minimized to trip need → arrival confirmation → route/session closes → retention policy applied.

## Business model
Potential revenue sources:
- individual Safe Walk/Safe Ride service fees
- monthly family/student/senior memberships
- school/college contracts
- employer/nightlife-worker safety contracts
- apartment/property/community subscriptions
- event steward contracts
- business district / merchant association contracts
- municipal/nonprofit grants or service contracts where eligible
- sponsor-funded free rides/walks for eligible populations
- training/certification-course fees where TRYAMM is legally permitted to provide training
- platform referral/booking fees for appropriately licensed external providers

Never charge a percentage of emergency aid, public benefits, or victim compensation.

## Suggested pricing architecture (illustrative; jurisdiction/cost dependent)
- Free safety account: $0
- Individual Plus: $7.99–$14.99/month for priority check-ins, trusted contacts, discounts
- Family Safety: $19.99–$39.99/month
- Safe Walk booking: base + time/distance, e.g. $8–$25 local session where economically viable
- Campus/Employer/Property: contract pricing per covered person/location
- Event steward: hourly/per-event contract pricing
- Licensed partner referrals: disclosed platform/lead fee only when lawful and contractually permitted

Actual prices must be set from local labor, insurance, taxes, licensing, transport, support and demand economics.

## Worker economics
Paid ambassadors/stewards must see compensation terms before accepting work where feasible. Worker classification, minimum wage, overtime, insurance, background checks, taxes, and licensing vary by location and must be handled locally.

## Global deployment
Country/region packs define:
- permitted role names and activities
- security-guard licensing boundaries
- background-check rules
- youth-worker requirements
- data/privacy requirements
- emergency numbers/resources
- labor/worker classification
- insurance requirements
- transportation rules
- tax/VAT/GST obligations
- languages/accessibility

A feature is not offered in a jurisdiction until the local pack is verified.

## Community Gap Finder integration
Aggregate, privacy-preserving signals may identify gaps in safe transportation, youth programming, lighting/reporting, event support, accessibility or community services. Never publish individual victim/help-request locations as a heat map.

## Youth Peace / crime-prevention pathway
CONFLICT RISK → SAFE SPACE → MENTOR/COACH → SPORTS/MUSIC/GAMES/TRADES/CREATOR PROJECT → LEARNING PASSPORT → PAID INTERNSHIP/JOB/ENTREPRENEURSHIP → SERVICE/MENTORSHIP.

The goal is to create alternatives, relationships and economic pathways rather than label youth as criminals.

## Business Safety
Participating merchants can request closing-time accompaniment, employee safe-walks, incident documentation support, accessibility assistance, fraud/scam education, and connection to licensed security providers where needed.

## Safety evidence and accountability
- visible role/identity verification
- training status
- session audit trail
- user consent for location/contact sharing
- complaint/appeal process
- worker/user block/report tools
- abuse monitoring
- incident escalation protocol
- periodic red-team tests for stalking, impersonation, harassment, bias, misuse of location and vigilante behavior

## Sustainability contribution
This network can add recurring revenue and paid work while serving the mission. It contributes to the Platform Sustainability Engine through eligible subscription/service/contract revenue, while worker compensation, insurance, taxes, partner payouts and restricted grant/sponsor funds remain separate obligations.
