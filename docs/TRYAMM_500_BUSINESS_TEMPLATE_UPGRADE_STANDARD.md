## Shared non-AI upgrade standard for every template
1. Mobile-first semantic HTML, responsive page, keyboard/one-handed access, contrast and reduced-motion support.
2. Clear brand-specific hero: what the business does, service area, one primary call to action and truthful customer benefits.
3. Industry-specific lead path: phone or email, booking request, purchase request, quote form, class enrollment, or business-specific next step.
4. Never route contact information to TRYAMM when the tenant should own it; use a properly configured per-business destination with consent, validation, anti-spam and privacy disclosure.
5. Service/product cards, pricing presentation where approved, FAQs and customer proof only when accurate and authorized.
6. SEO basics: distinct title, meta description, canonical, social sharing, accessible images, structured data only when accurately supported.
7. Performance: lightweight initial load, optimized approved images, no required 3D/media autoplay, no unnecessary third-party scripts.
8. Customer ownership: independent dashboard, content, data, domains and legally authorized media assets.
9. Security: server-validated input, scoped authorization, no raw credentials/client secret exposures, verified payments, backup and restore rehearsal.
10. Clear package scope, deliverables, support terms, maintenance and optional add-on consent.

## Vertical modules (same foundation; relevant features only)
- Salon/beauty: appointment request, service gallery, product sales when checkout ready, consent for before/after photos.
- Logistics: shipper/carrier intake, quote form, dispatch lead handoff; no claims of live carrier brokerage.
- Music studio: service booking, sample reels, rights intake and delivery checklist.
- Training/education: course catalog, inquiry, enrollment only when fulfillment exists.
- Faith/charity: event schedule, teaching media, safe moderated inquiry; compliant donation route if enabled.
- Accessibility/mobility: fully usable no-AI services, larger controls, plain language, accessible forms.
- Restaurant/retail: menu/catalog, ordering or reservations only with real operational destinations.
- Security: service inquiries; do not advertise unverified licensing or high-risk services.
- Home services: service areas, quote capture and trust markers only when verified.
