# TRYAMM Regulated Services + Community Safety

Status: LOCKED ARCHITECTURE / PROVIDER-GATED

## 1. Tele-services marketplace
TRYAMM can operate a marketplace/orchestration layer for qualified providers without pretending TRYAMM itself is the licensed professional.

Lanes:
- Tele-law / lawyer on demand
- Tele-tax / bookkeeping
- Tele-health
- Tele-insurance
- Tele-realty
- Remote notarization where permitted
- Tutoring / interpretation / sign-language services
- HR / recruiting
- Technical / cybersecurity support
- Non-medical beauty consultations

Each provider profile stores jurisdiction, credential/license where applicable, verification status, permitted service scope, professional liability/insurance status where required, pricing model, availability and complaint/quality records.

### Lawyer on demand
Example user intent: `I was pulled over; I need legal help.`
TRYAMM may route to an available licensed attorney in the relevant jurisdiction, present transparent pricing, initiate a secure consultation and record consent. Stubbs AI can organize facts and questions but does not impersonate counsel or give jurisdiction-specific legal representation unless the response comes from an authorized attorney/provider workflow.

### Tele-tax
Paid federal return preparation must be performed by a preparer with a valid PTIN where required. The platform can charge marketplace/software/referral fees where lawful, while the preparer remains responsible for tax-preparation compliance.

### Tele-health / Medicaid
Medicaid billing is enabled only for qualified enrolled providers, covered services, eligible beneficiaries, correct jurisdiction/payer enrollment and proper documentation/coding. TRYAMM can provide scheduling, eligibility checks, claims workflow, documentation support, denials management and revenue-cycle software; it must never submit unsupported claims or bill Medicaid for non-covered/unrendered services.

## 2. Platform revenue models
Possible monetization, subject to applicable law and professional rules:
- monthly provider SaaS subscription
- booking/platform fee
- software/AI Actions usage
- telephony/video/SMS pass-through plus margin where lawful
- verified lead/referral fee only where professional rules permit it
- billing/revenue-cycle percentage for eligible providers where permitted and contractually documented
- enterprise contracts with clinics, firms, agencies, schools, employers and nonprofits

Do not use prohibited fee-sharing arrangements with lawyers, healthcare professionals, insurance producers or other regulated professions. Jurisdiction and profession rules override generic pricing.

## 3. Community Guardian Network
A lawful, non-vigilante civilian safety and assistance network inspired by the idea of visible community guardians but redesigned around prevention, de-escalation and service.

Roles:
- Community Ambassador
- Safe Passage Escort
- School/Transit Ambassador
- Event Safety Steward
- De-escalation / conflict-interruption partner
- Resource Navigator
- Missing-person / welfare-check support in coordination with authorized agencies
- Business corridor ambassador

Not roles:
- police impersonation
- armed patrols by default
- detention/arrest beyond ordinary lawful rights
- pursuing suspects
- punishment or vigilante enforcement

Core workflow:
OBSERVE → DE-ESCALATE → CREATE DISTANCE → HELP VULNERABLE PERSON → CALL AUTHORIZED EMERGENCY/COMMUNITY RESOURCE WHEN NEEDED → DOCUMENT FACTS → FOLLOW-UP/RESOURCE NAVIGATION.

## 4. Safety JARVIS
Safety JARVIS can:
- route non-emergency assistance
- surface verified nearby resources
- coordinate voluntary escorts/check-ins
- provide de-escalation scripts/training prompts
- create incident notes with privacy controls
- alert supervisors for higher-risk situations
- identify community safety gaps from aggregate data

It must not tell users to confront, chase or physically engage suspected offenders.

## 5. Community safety revenue
This can be a profitable social-impact business through:
- city/business-district contracts
- school/campus/event contracts
- property-management/community contracts
- training/certification programs
- employer sponsorships
- grants for violence prevention/reentry/youth services
- subscription dashboards for participating institutions
- paid safe-passage/event steward staffing

Measure: incidents de-escalated, escorts completed, referrals, response time, youth participation, businesses served and user-reported safety—not arrests or confrontation counts.

## 6. Sustainability connection
Regulated services and Community Guardian revenue feed the Platform Sustainability Engine only after provider obligations, refunds, restricted funds, taxes and other liabilities are separated.

Goal remains 3.00x eligible platform revenue / measured infrastructure cost, while maintaining legal compliance, fair provider compensation and service quality.
