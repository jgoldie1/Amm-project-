# TRYAMM / StreetVerse — University Proof & Sustainability Brief
Status: proposal materials, not an independently verified production demonstration.

## Purpose
Seek an exploratory research/industry collaboration for accessible immersive Living Worlds development, Chicago-centered interactive storytelling, and sustainable creator/business services.

## Evidence sequence
1. Marcus Explores StreetVerse — cinematic concept: environment and vision.
2. Marcus Meets the Stranger — cinematic concept: intended dialogue and choices.
3. Underground Discovery — cinematic concept: investigation storytelling.
4. The City Remembers — cinematic concept: continuity and progression.
5. Unedited mobile gameplay capture — separate technical evidence, showing actual route, input, mission, NPC and any failures.
6. Source walkthrough — safe development branch; existing mobile control, world staging, creator/commerce and recovery features, separated by verified/unverified status.

The four Marcus reels were previously cataloged; **original playable MP4 asset availability is not verified**. Never claim their scenes are playable game mechanics. Do not assert a device test passed unless logs and footage support it.

## Proposed 90-day collaboration
- Days 1–21: independent code/build and accessibility audit; rights, performance and cost assessment; agree success criteria.
- Days 22–56: one Chicago playable slice: Circle Park, guarded entry, BJ character movement, one NPC, mission, accessibility input, optional business portal preview.
- Days 57–90: supervised mobile testing, performance profiling, regression and rollback verification; deliver technical report and customer-discovery recommendations.
University student labor, fees, intellectual-property ownership, publication, safety and accessibility review require written agreements.

## Business sustainability
Offer **optional StreetVerse Maintenance & Support** as a commercial service, not a tax or a mandatory surcharge. Proposed examples pending full costing:
- Basic gameplay remains free.
- Business storefront support: illustrative $19/month.
- Enhanced virtual presence: illustrative $49/month.
Do not double-charge customers whose existing service package includes maintenance. No checkout or fee is activated by this document.

## Minimum proof to accept money
- Written scope and included support response times.
- Verifiable customer and storefront access.
- Server-side checkout confirmation, invoicing, refunds and cancellation.
- Cost caps for game hosting, streaming, AI, storage and media.
- Test result for actual storefront or mission launch.
- Consent and licensed use of logos, videos, music and voice recordings.

## Initial institutional ask
20–30-minute exploratory meeting; eligibility, costs, suitable faculty/team, pilot feasibility, and whether student project, research, incubation or sponsored-development terms fit. No institutional partnership, funding or endorsement is assumed.
