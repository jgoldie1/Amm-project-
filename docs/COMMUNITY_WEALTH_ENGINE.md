# TRYAMM Community Wealth Engine

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Turn Learning Passport + Aniyah Financial Literacy + 12-Circulation Challenge + Supplier Exchange + Launchpad + Emergency Path + University + Soul Ascension + Kingdom Missions into one measurable education, opportunity, restoration and community-wealth system.

This is not a closed economy and does not promise that a specific dollar literally circulates twelve times. The goal is to strengthen local/community supplier relationships, ownership, skills, jobs, procurement capacity and economic resilience while remaining open to national and global trade.

## Community Wealth Graph
Represent participating economic relationships as a privacy-preserving graph:
- households/learners
- creators/workers
- businesses
- suppliers
- mentors
- schools/colleges/training partners
- faith/community organizations
- sponsors/funders
- anchor institutions
- missions/projects

Track aggregate flows and outcomes, not private public-facing bank balances.

## 12-Circulation Challenge
Learning mode starts with simulated money and shows how purchasing decisions affect a network. Learners earn Passport achievements for completing simulations and demonstrating understanding.

Real-world mode measures voluntary participating-network activity such as:
- repeat purchasing
- supplier-to-supplier purchasing
- local/community procurement
- jobs and gigs created
- vendor contracts
- business-to-business referrals
- procurement gaps filled by new businesses

## Community Gap Finder
Stubbs AI analyzes consenting/aggregate Supplier Exchange demand and identifies missing capacity. Example: participating organizations repeatedly purchase a service outside the network because no qualified participating supplier exists. The system can recommend training, entrepreneurship, recruitment or supplier-development missions to fill the gap.

## Purchasing Circles
Participating organizations may voluntarily aggregate legitimate demand for common goods/services to seek better pricing and supplier access, subject to applicable competition/procurement law. No price fixing, market allocation or exclusionary conduct.

## Anchor Partnership pathway
Build partnership packages for hospitals, universities, colleges, schools, municipalities, employers, cultural institutions and large businesses that want to increase local procurement, hiring, supplier development or place-based impact.

Flow:
ANCHOR NEED → SUPPLIER GAP → TRAIN/VERIFY VENDORS → MATCH → PROCUREMENT → PERFORMANCE → IMPACT REPORT.

## Sponsor/Funder Engine
Each mission becomes a fundable unit:
MISSION → TARGET POPULATION → BUDGET → MILESTONES → EVIDENCE → OUTCOMES → REPORT.

Example packages:
- 100 Learning Passports
- 100 Aniyah Financial Literacy learners
- foster-youth Launchpad cohort
- Second Chance/reentry cohort
- Peace Mission / violence-prevention programming
- Soul Ascension community episode
- school/college readiness cohort
- devices/connectivity
- trade/certification support
- Supplier Exchange vendor-readiness cohort

Funding sources may include earned TRYAMM revenue, sponsorship, grants/contracts, philanthropy, educational/workforce partnerships, events/broadcasting, in-kind support and an appropriately structured charitable/endowment partner. Tax-deductibility is never promised without an eligible receiving entity.

## Money Engine controls
Restricted funds remain separate from platform operating revenue and creator/rightsholder liabilities. Every funded mission records funding source, restrictions, approved budget, disbursement category, evidence, unused balance and closeout status.

## Community Spotlight
Neighborhood → City → State/Region → National → Continental → Global.

Spotlight verified outcomes, businesses, students, mentors, creators, missions and community stories. Do not rank human worth or turn assistance history into a public reputation score.

## When Heaven Meets Earth
Faith/service framing: translate scripture-inspired values into voluntary service and measurable stewardship—feeding, teaching, peacemaking, mentoring, restoration, opportunity, responsible ownership and care for community. Software records missions and outcomes; it does not claim to manufacture spiritual status.
