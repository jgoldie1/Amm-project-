# StreetVerse Chicago Game Expansion

This document locks the next visible StreetVerse game slice to original TRYAMM systems and assets.

## Included systems
- Living Chicago block with traffic, pedestrians, businesses, trees, CTA-style transit, weather and day/night state.
- Player walking and vehicle enter/exit/drive loop.
- Benny mission guidance and minimap/HUD.
- Living ecosystem: bounded bird flocks, pigeons, fish schools, urban wildlife, and future horses/cattle herd zones.
- Founder Alpha checkpoint mission tied conceptually to the existing server-authoritative reward contract; no client-created cash rewards.
- Mobile-first controls, reduced-motion support and low-cost distance simulation.

## Original-content rule
StreetVerse may learn from generic open-world gameplay patterns, but it must not copy Rockstar/Take-Two assets, maps, characters, trademarks, proprietary code, or game data. Chicago world art, missions, businesses, characters and mechanics should be TRYAMM-original or properly licensed.
