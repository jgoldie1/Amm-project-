# TRYAMM Media Universe — All American Network + Isaiah AI TV + StarVerse

Status: CANONICAL MEDIA VISION

## Purpose
Turn TRYAMM Living Omniverse into a media creation, live participation, talent discovery and distribution system. One Passport should let a person watch, broadcast, debate, podcast, report, perform, audition, collaborate, clip, publish and move between 2D, AR, VR and mixed-reality experiences.

## Network stack
- All American Network (AAN): umbrella network/distribution brand for live and on-demand programming.
- Isaiah AI TV: youth/future-facing AI-assisted channel and studio lane with age-appropriate permissions and safety controls.
- StarVerse: talent/performance world where users can train, audition, perform, collaborate, build a fanbase and develop a creator career.
- Anyone Can Be a Star: inclusive talent-discovery pathway; participation is open subject to age, rights, moderation and program eligibility, but fame/income is never guaranteed.
- TRYAMM Live: real-time creator/live-event surface.
- TRYAMM Reels: short-form discovery, clips, highlights and promotion.
- HoloGPT Media Copilot: accessibility, captions, translation, rundown help, clip suggestions, production assistance and source-aware research support. AI must not invent news facts.

## Program formats
1. Live streams: solo, co-host, guest panels, events, concerts, sports/competition where rights permit, game/world broadcasts and community programming.
2. Debate: timed turns, moderator controls, topic/source cards, fact/source lane, audience questions, polls, post-show clips and civility/safety controls. Political/current-events programming must distinguish reporting, opinion and sponsored content.
3. Podcasts: audio/video rooms, remote guests, chapters, transcripts, clips, captions, translation, RSS/export integrations later, and spatial podcast rooms in XR.
4. News/media: newsroom rundown, assignment desk, verified source/provenance records, live field reports, StreetVerse News for fictional world events, and a hard separation between real reporting and fictional/simulated stories.
5. StarVerse shows: auditions, showcases, music, dance, comedy, acting, fashion, spoken word, instrumental performance, DJs, creators, filmmakers and other lawful talent categories.
6. Business/creator TV: storefront demos, product shows, interviews, entrepreneurship competitions, Launch Fund stories and sponsored programming with clear disclosures.
7. Education/community: classes, career labs, accessibility programming, public-service content and community town halls.

## Anyone Can Be a Star pathway
DISCOVER -> CREATE PROFILE -> CHOOSE TALENT -> TRAIN/PRACTICE -> AUDITION/SHOWCASE -> COLLABORATE -> GO LIVE -> CREATE REELS -> BUILD AUDIENCE -> QUALIFY FOR PROGRAMS -> BOOK/SELL/CREATE -> BUILD CAREER -> MENTOR OTHERS.

The system may rank/recommend based on transparent engagement/quality/safety signals, but must not promise stardom. Include discovery lanes for new creators so existing popularity does not permanently dominate exposure.

## Streaming architecture contract
Use two delivery classes:
- Interactive real-time: WebRTC-class transport for hosts, guests, debates, panels, backstage and low-latency interaction.
- Scaled audience broadcast/VOD: adaptive HTTP streaming such as HLS/CMAF through CDN-capable distribution.

Canonical flow:
CAMERA/MIC/SCREEN/WORLD FEED -> INGEST -> REAL-TIME ROOM -> MIX/COMPOSE -> RECORD -> TRANSCODE -> ADAPTIVE STREAM -> CDN/DISTRIBUTION -> PLAYER -> CLIP/REEL -> ARCHIVE/VOD.

Required services: identity/entitlements, room roles, moderation, captions, translation, recording consent, stream health, transcoding, thumbnails, scheduling, chat, reactions, polls, analytics, rights/provenance, ad markers, archive policy and emergency kill controls.

## Debate system
Roles: host, moderator, debater, fact/source producer, guest, audience.
Features: opening statements, timed responses, rebuttals, cross-examination, audience questions, source cards, transcript, chapter markers, accessibility, post-show fact/source notes and Reel extraction.
Do not have AI declare contested political claims true/false without reliable sourcing; surface evidence and uncertainty.

## News integrity contract
Every real-world news item should carry source/provenance, timestamp, reporter/producer attribution where applicable, rights status, REAL/DOCUMENTED/OPINION/SPONSORED label and correction history.
StreetVerse/SpaceVerse fictional news must be labeled FICTIONAL/SIMULATED and sourced from authoritative world-state events.
Never convert rumors into factual news automatically.

## XR media
### AR
Place virtual studio screens, teleprompter, comments, guests, products, HoloGPT, scoreboards and portals around the creator while preserving phone fallback.

### VR
Enter virtual studios, concert stages, debate halls, podcast rooms, newsrooms and StarVerse venues. Audience members can occupy controlled social spaces and watch performances.

### Mixed Reality
Turn a real room into a production studio: spatial monitors, switcher, rundown, guest windows, chat, captions, analytics, teleprompter and HoloGPT production assistant.

### Multidimensional broadcast
A broadcast may combine live camera, StreetVerse/SpaceVerse world feed, timeline reconstruction, AR overlays, virtual set, audience data and interactive Pocket Dimension. These are synchronized media/data dimensions, not a claim of literal extra physical dimensions.

## Omniverse broadcasting
A creator can broadcast from a StreetVerse venue, interview a guest in MiddleVerse, perform in StarVerse, cut to a SpaceVerse expedition, use Quantum Time for clearly labeled simulation/reconstruction, publish clips to Reels and archive the full program to AAN/Isaiah AI TV.

Potential revenue lanes, subject to rights/payment/compliance implementation:
- subscriptions/memberships
- virtual tickets

- creator subscriptions
- Holo Ads and sponsorships
- brand placements with disclosure
- merchandise/marketplace commerce
- premium events
- music/video distribution services where rights are verified
- business showcases
- creator tools/storage

Do not activate real-money flows until provider checkout, entitlement, ledger, refunds, fraud controls, tax/reporting and payout verification are production-certified.

## Creator revenue contract
REVENUE EVENT -> PROVIDER VERIFICATION -> RIGHTS/REFUND/FRAUD CHECK -> CONTRACTUAL CREATOR SHARE -> PLATFORM SHARE -> APPLICABLE APPROVED ALLOCATION BASE -> IMPACT/LAUNCH ALLOCATIONS -> PAYABLE LEDGER -> SETTLEMENT.

Never calculate charitable/Launch Fund allocations ahead of contractual creator obligations unless the final legal/accounting policy explicitly defines otherwise.

## Talent rights
Before monetized distribution, record ownership/license for video, music, beats, samples, images, likenesses, performances, locations, trademarks where necessary, AI-generated assets and sponsor materials. Unknown rights status blocks monetized/syndicated distribution.

## Accessibility
Captions, transcripts, text chat, screen-reader labels, keyboard/switch access, voice control, translation, audio description support, readable contrast, adjustable text, reduced-motion mode and left/right-hand adaptable controls should be first-class requirements.

## Youth lane
Isaiah AI TV youth participation must be server-separated from adult-gated spaces, use age-appropriate discovery/contact controls, prohibit sexualized youth content, restrict risky monetization/contact patterns and provide guardian/program controls where legally/product appropriate.
