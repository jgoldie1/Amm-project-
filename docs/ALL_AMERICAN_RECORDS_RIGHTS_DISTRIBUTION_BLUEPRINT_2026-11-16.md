## 3. Artist rights and money
- Artist keeps sound recording copyrights and composition copyrights they own. No transfer just to participate.
- Enumerate individual masters, permitted territories/platforms, license start/end, takedown obligations, exclusions, sublicensing limits, marketing rights and access to metadata.
- Suggested 24-month limited release license, mutual renewals, no unrestricted perpetual exclusive master license. Any security interest is a distinct attorney-reviewed instrument, never implied.
- Define revenue base, DSP/aggregator fees, taxes/refunds, approved recoupables, no cross-collateralization, quarterly or better statement frequency, audit rights, no hidden fees.
- No automatic share of performance, touring, publishing, merch, sponsorships, films, acting, PRO songwriter shares or SoundExchange featured-performer share.
- Refunds and settlement are server-authoritative and require payment provider validation; no simulated royalties paid as real cash.

## 4. Original Music Registry (internal source of truth, NOT an official copyright office, PRO or ISRC agency)
Data collections:
- Creator identity + privacy/consents; stage vs legal name; credited roles, collaborators, contact and signed split sheets.
- Composition ID + authorship, shares summing 100%, publisher/administrator, PRO affiliation and IPI/CAE if verified, ISWC only when officially assigned.
- Recording ID + owners and splits, producer/performer credits, final WAV checksums, sample provenance, ISRC only once officially assigned by authorized agency/distributor, version history.
- Release ID + artwork/metadata/UPC when valid, explicit track order, territories, dates, delivery receipts and takedown states.
- Licenses: recording, composition, sync, sample, beat, featured artist, image/video, performances, expiry/territory/medium and approval evidence.
- Royalties: source statements, period, currencies, deductions, ledger entries, holds, review/dispute, payee share, corrections, export.

External routes should distinguish:
- U.S. Copyright Office: statutory copyright registration (composition and recording can be separate).
- ASCAP/BMI/SESAC/etc: performing-rights organization affiliations and composition repertory, not a substitute for copyright registration.
- The MLC: eligible U.S. streaming mechanical royalties, registration/claim by entitled rightsholder.
- SoundExchange: eligible U.S. noninteractive digital sound-recording performance royalties; featured performers receive direct payments according to its policies.
- ISRC: identifies an individual recording; UPC identifies a release/product. Never invent official IDs.
Every integration remains “manual checklist/verification” until an authorized, tested connector is installed and approved.

## 5. Sample Clearance Desk — fail closed
Intake includes work/recording sampled, start/end timecode, duration, sample audio hash, underlying original composition, recording owner and publishers, interpolation vs sampling, intended use (DSP, sync, ads, game, radio), territory, term, fee, royalty split and signed permissions. 
- Original recording sample: master-use authorization + composition authorization generally needed.
- Interpolation of a composition without source audio: composition authorization generally still required.
- Cover licensing is a separate workflow, not an automatic cure for samples, adapted lyrics, or audiovisual synchronization.
- State machine: DRAFT -> OWNER_RESEARCH -> PERMISSION_REQUESTED -> TERMS_NEGOTIATED -> SIGNATURES_VERIFIED -> CLEARED; or DENIED/EXPIRED/DISPUTED. Only CLEARED items eligible to release.
- Keep evidence (rights holder identity, signed terms, approved media, signed files, dates, royalty obligations) privately encrypted; no sample file leak or silent autogenerated approval.
- Generate performer/producer collaboration and split sheet checklist, conflict/duplicate ISRC checks, explicit veto/hold.

## 8. Minimum safe acceptance (one-button test additions)
1. Artist signs up and uploads ONE owned WAV with consent, owner verified, collaboration splits total 100.
2. Two-sample negative tests (uncleared source master and unauthorized interpolation) prevent release; approved original passes.
3. Conflicting ownership, wrong payee, duplicate ISRC, expired license or unapproved artwork blocks distribution.
4. Fake aggregator sandbox receives metadata once (idempotent retries); rejected tracks retain failure receipts.
5. Simulated statement reconciliation produces deterministic artist 75%/label 25% on the agreed base with no touring/publishing sweep and no real payouts.
6. Rights-cleared play in Verse Radio, authorized music-video scene, DJ promo excerpt, and StreetVerse in-game radio; unauthorized sync and public replay rejected.
7. Accessibility: one-hand/touch/keyboard labels, readable status, subtitles, no forced autoplay; mobile/Chromebook tests.
8. Audit log, backup and isolated restore; rollback for rights revocation and platform takedown.
9. No production/real-money activation until exact proofs pass, lawyer reviews contracts, artist consents are recorded, and owner explicitly authorizes service budgets.
