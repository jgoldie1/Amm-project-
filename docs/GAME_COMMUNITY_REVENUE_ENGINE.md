# TRYAMM Game Community Revenue Engine

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## Core loop
GAMES → CREWS/COMMUNITIES → TOURNAMENTS/MISSIONS → LIVE → CLIPS/REPLAYS → SPONSORS/ADS/TICKETS → CREATOR/PLAYER EARNINGS → COMMUNITY IMPACT → NEXT EVENT.

Quantum Discord coordinates rooms, crews, squads, leagues, schools, families, ministries, creator communities and fan groups. Quantum Zapier automates approved reminders, invites, brackets, highlights, results, sponsor messages, clip workflows and event announcements.

## GameVerse portfolio
Support a common platform contract across sports, racing, fighting, adventure, story/living-world, space, creature/collection, rhythm/music, educational, accessible/casual, AR/VR/MR and community-mission games. Individual games can use different engines/clients, but they should share identity, accessibility, moderation, community, events, rewards and Money Engine integrations.

## Community model
- World/community
- Crew/team/family/guild
- public/private/invite-only rooms
- voice/text/video where enabled
- LIVE stage/watch party
- event calendar
- tournament/mission board
- creator/fan clips
- Marketplace/merchandise links
- opportunity/education/community mission links

Minors receive age-appropriate defaults and safeguarding.

## Tournament/event lifecycle
DRAFT → RULES → ELIGIBILITY → REGISTRATION → CHECK-IN → BRACKET/MATCHMAKING → LIVE → RESULTS → REVIEW/DISPUTE → FINAL → PAYOUT/REWARD → ARCHIVE/CLIPS.

Real-money prizes remain behind geographic/legal/payment feature gates. Free-to-play/simulated competitions can launch separately.

## Revenue lanes
Potential eligible platform revenue can include:
- game/experience subscriptions
- cosmetics/digital content where lawful and platform-policy compliant
- event/tournament service fees
- tickets/PPV
- sponsorships
- Holo Ads
- Marketplace/merchandise fees
- creator production tools/HoloGPT Credits
- premium replay/storage/editing
- league/team business tools
- education/esports institutional contracts

Do not count player/creator liabilities, restricted charity funds, taxes, prize liabilities or provider settlements as platform revenue.

## Sponsor engine
Sponsor package stores: campaign, game/event, audience/geography, placements, deliverables, budget, restricted allocations, approval state, evidence and post-event metrics. Sponsorship does not control competitive outcomes, moderation or charity voting.

## Money Engine separation
Every event/transaction separates:
PLATFORM REVENUE | CREATOR EARNINGS | PLAYER/TEAM PRIZES | OFFICIAL/STAFF PAY | SPONSOR-RESTRICTED FUNDS | CHARITY ALLOCATION | REFUNDS/RESERVES | TAX/PROVIDER OBLIGATIONS.

No single `balance` field may represent all obligations.

## Charity/community voting
Eligible events may allocate a disclosed portion to approved charities/community missions. If users vote, voting rules, eligibility, anti-bot controls, tie handling, geographic restrictions and official rules must be published. Charity selection cannot convert an otherwise prohibited lottery/gambling mechanic into a lawful one.

## Quantum Discord integration
Game event automatically creates or connects:
- crew room
- opponent/event room
- spectator room
- moderator/official room
- sponsor/community room where appropriate
- post-event archive/highlight room

## Quantum Zapier recipes
Examples:
1. Event scheduled → create rooms → notify registered players → calendar reminder.
2. Match ends → verify result → update bracket → announce next match.
3. Approved replay created → detect candidate highlights → creator approves clips → publish to Reels/Community Spotlight.
4. Sponsor campaign activated → insert approved placement → collect delivery evidence → update sponsor report.
5. Tournament finalized → Money Engine computes obligations → human/finance approval where required → payout queue.
6. Safety report → pause automation/publication → moderation queue.

## Viral flywheel
PLAY → MOMENT → CLIP → SHARE → COMMUNITY JOINS → REMIX/CHALLENGE → LIVE EVENT → SPONSOR → REWARD → PLAY AGAIN.

Avoid spam invitations, fake engagement and forced sharing. Viral growth should come from useful/fun participatory moments.

## Accessibility
All common game/community surfaces inherit Accessibility Passport. Games publish accessibility metadata and support remappable/one-handed/reduced-motion/caption/visual-audio alternative options where technically applicable.

## Fair play/security
- authoritative match/results for competitive modes
- anti-cheat/tamper signals
- bot-vote/engagement protection
- dispute/appeal flow
- moderation evidence
- server-side prize eligibility
- idempotent payout/event finalization
- age/geography gates for regulated features
- no client-authoritative money/prizes

## Platform Sustainability integration
Track per-game/event:
ELIGIBLE PLATFORM REVENUE ÷ MEASURED INFRASTRUCTURE COST = SUSTAINABILITY RATIO.

Target platform-wide ratio: 3.00×. A popular game that loses money on every active user is not automatically sustainable; AI, LIVE video, storage, bandwidth, moderation and prize operations must be measured.

## Success metrics
Players retained, communities formed, matches completed, LIVE viewers, creator clips approved, sponsor revenue, creator/player payouts, infrastructure cost per active player, safety incidents/resolution, accessible-play satisfaction, community mission participation and sustainability ratio.
