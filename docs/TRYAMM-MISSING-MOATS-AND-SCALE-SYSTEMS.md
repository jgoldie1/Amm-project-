# TryAMM Missing Moats and Scale Systems

These are high-leverage systems that make the platform harder to copy, safer to operate, and more valuable per customer.

## 1. Universal Identity + Business Passport
One reusable verified identity/business profile across TryAMM, Jinn, HoloPass, HoloMenu, procurement, funding, creator tools, rides, delivery, StarVerse, AM64, Middleverse and partner apps.

Controls: consent, role separation, verification level, revocation, device/session history, country eligibility and tenant boundaries.

## 2. Developer Platform + Agent Marketplace
Expose approved APIs, webhooks, SDKs and scoped tools so third-party developers can build agents/apps on TryAMM.

Required controls: OAuth/scoped tokens, tenant isolation, rate limits, spend limits, approval gates, sandbox, versioning, marketplace review, billing, revocation and audit logs.

Revenue: developer plans, API usage, marketplace commissions, enterprise integrations and white-label licensing.

## 3. Consent + Data Vault
Give users/businesses one place to see what data they shared, why it is used, which apps/agents can access it, and to revoke access.

Add export/delete workflows, retention rules, child/teen protections, data residency configuration and sensitive-data classifications.

## 4. Global Compliance Control Plane
Country/territory capability registry for payments, payouts, tax/VAT/GST, marketplace rules, consumer protection, privacy, age rules, telecom/voice, rides, delivery, stored value, music/TV rights and regulated services.

Unsupported features stay disabled until verified.

## 5. Jacobie Risk Graph
Link accounts, devices, IP/risk signals, payment instruments, merchants, referrals, coupons, creators, payout destinations and agent actions into a fraud/risk graph.

Use it to detect account farms, fake referrals, coupon rings, stolen accounts, payout hijacking, bot voting, fake streams and coordinated abuse.

## 6. TryAMM Ad Exchange
One ad operating system spanning front scroll, HoloTV, HoloMusic, Business Live, Shop Live, games, HoloLocal, HoloMenu and Middleverse.

Capabilities: targeting based on consented signals, creator attribution, coupon attribution, local ads, sponsored listings, brand safety, frequency caps, campaign budget controls and transparent reporting.

Never mix paid ranking with verification claims.

## 7. Unified CRM + Customer Graph
Businesses should have one customer view for purchases, bookings, loyalty, coupons, support, subscriptions, consent and campaign attribution.

Do not expose data across unrelated merchants without valid permission.

## 8. Enterprise White-Label Platform
Package TryAMM modules for cities, universities, chambers of commerce, media networks, events, franchises and corporations.

Possible bundles: storefront, Jinn, Stubbs AI, call center, HoloMenu, HoloCoupons, Live, HoloTV, CRM, analytics, Jacobie Protect and Middleverse spaces.

## 9. Command Center + Disaster Recovery
A real operator control center for uptime, incidents, payments, streaming, fraud, AI costs, queues, database health, CDN/storage, backups and country status.

Required: backups, restore drills, RPO/RTO targets, multi-region strategy, graceful degradation, provider failover, incident runbooks and status communications.

## 10. AI Evaluation + Model Router
Before Stubbs AI scales, create evaluation suites for accuracy, hallucination, tool safety, latency, cost and tenant leakage.

AI Cost Manager routes by task/quality/budget and can fail over between approved providers. High-risk actions still require human approval.

## 11. Trust + Reputation Passport
Portable but context-aware trust signals: verified purchase, completed booking, fulfilled order, merchant verification, dispute history, creator reliability and agent/tool trust.

Avoid one universal social-credit score. Different contexts use different transparent signals.

## 12. Knowledge + Learning Network
Business Academy, creator education, AI tutorials, AM64 lessons, StarVerse coaching and workforce training should share progress, credentials and recommended learning paths.

Monetization: subscriptions, certifications where legitimately issued, sponsored education and enterprise training.

## 13. Accessibility Personalization Profile
Persist user-selected accessibility preferences across the platform: large targets, high contrast, captions, simplified mode, voice navigation, reduced motion, screen-reader optimization, one-hand mode and translation settings.

## 14. Offline/Low-Bandwidth Mode
Critical for global scale. Add adaptive media quality, download/sync where rights allow, queue-and-retry actions, lightweight storefront/menu pages and SMS/USSD/provider-assisted options where appropriate.

## 15. Partner Marketplace
Let vetted partners offer logistics, accounting, legal/tax referrals, insurance, marketing, production, cybersecurity and business services.

TryAMM earns configurable referral/marketplace revenue where legally permitted and clearly disclosed.

## 16. Experimentation Platform
Feature flags, A/B tests, cohort analytics and rollout controls across countries, age lanes, businesses and creator groups.

Every experiment needs guardrails for safety, fairness, privacy and revenue accounting.

## 17. Revenue Intelligence
A unified Revenue Opportunity Map and unit-economics engine showing revenue, gross margin, AI/provider cost, payment fees, support cost, fraud losses and retention by vertical.

Do not scale a feature just because gross revenue is high; scale when contribution margin and retention justify it.

## Recommended priority
1. Identity/Business Passport + Consent Vault.
2. Jacobie Risk Graph + Command Center/DR.
3. Developer/Agent Platform.
4. Unified CRM + Ad Exchange.
5. Global Compliance Control Plane.
6. AI Evaluation/Router.
7. Enterprise white-label.
8. Offline/low-bandwidth global mode.

These systems should share the existing TryAMM identity, Supabase/Postgres, Jinn ledger/payment orchestration, Stubbs AI, GrowthOS, HoloPass and Jacobie Cybersecurity rather than becoming isolated products.
