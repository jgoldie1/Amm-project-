# TRYAMM Legal Launch & IP Protection Pack

Status: DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW / LAUNCH GATE

This is an operational legal checklist and template pack, not a substitute for advice from a licensed attorney in the relevant jurisdiction.

## 1. IP ownership map
Before public launch, identify the owner/licensor for every material asset:
- TRYAMM / All American Marketplace names and logos
- Stubbs AI / HoloGPT / JARVIS brand elements
- Judah lion/lamb artwork and splash assets
- source code, smart contracts, prompts, agents, schemas and documentation
- music, jingles, sound recordings and compositions
- videos, films, animation, game assets and 3D models
- user-interface designs, icons and original artwork
- creator content, templates and marketplace listings
- third-party libraries, fonts, models, datasets, stock assets and open-source dependencies

Each item must be labeled: OWNED | LICENSED | OPEN SOURCE | USER-SUPPLIED | PARTNER-SUPPLIED | UNKNOWN. UNKNOWN blocks commercial launch of that asset.

## 2. NNN / NDA / non-use / non-circumvention
Use a narrowly drafted agreement for contractors, vendors, agencies and strategic partners when sensitive unpublished IP or business plans are disclosed.

Minimum terms:
- definition of Confidential Information
- purpose-limited use
- no unauthorized disclosure
- reasonable security obligations
- return/destruction on request
- exclusions for public/priorly known/independently developed information
- compelled-disclosure process
- no reverse engineering where lawful/applicable
- limited non-circumvention only for specifically introduced counterparties/opportunities
- no claim to general industry relationships or knowledge
- no transfer of ownership except by written assignment
- injunctive-relief clause subject to applicable law
- governing law/venue
- survival period

Do not rely on an NNN alone to transfer code, music, design or invention ownership. Pair it with an IP Assignment / Work-Made-for-Hire agreement where appropriate.

## 3. Contractor / employee IP assignment
Every developer, designer, producer, editor, musician, animator, marketer and consultant who creates material for TRYAMM should have a signed agreement covering:
- services/scope
- confidentiality
- work-made-for-hire language where legally effective
- present assignment of transferable IP rights to the agreed owner
- treatment of pre-existing/background IP
- third-party/open-source disclosure obligation
- moral-rights waiver/consent where legally permitted
- warranties of authority/originality
- license for necessary background tools
- source files/passwords/credentials handoff
- no unapproved subcontracting
- payment and milestone terms
- termination and continuing confidentiality

## 4. Copyright registration plan
Copyright can protect original software code, websites, music, videos, movies, animation, games, artwork and other original works fixed in a tangible medium. Registration is handled by the U.S. Copyright Office, not the USPTO.

Create a registration queue with: WORK TITLE | TYPE | AUTHOR(S) | OWNER/CLAIMANT | PUBLICATION STATUS | FIRST PUBLICATION DATE | SOURCE FILE | THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL EXCLUDED | APPLICATION STATUS | REGISTRATION NUMBER.

Priority queue:
1. core TRYAMM source-code releases;
2. Judah/brand visual artwork if original and owned;
3. original jingle/compositions + sound recordings;
4. original commercials/videos;
5. major game/animation works;
6. original books/white papers/documentation;
7. substantial original website/UI artwork/content.

The Copyright Office permits registration of software and other digital content and has specific group-registration options; do not put multiple unpublished works into a Standard Application when a group form is required.

## 5. Trademark strategy
Run clearance searches before investing heavily in names/logos. Candidate marks include TRYAMM, ALL AMERICAN MARKETPLACE, STUBBS AI, HOLOGPT, JIN PAY, SOUL ASCENSION and other product names, subject to clearance and actual use.

Use TM/SM for claimed marks when appropriate. Use ® only after federal registration for the goods/services covered by that registration.

Trademark docket fields: MARK | OWNER | GOODS/SERVICES | FIRST USE | SPECIMEN | SEARCH RESULT | APPLICATION SERIAL | STATUS | RENEWAL/MAINTENANCE DEADLINES.

## 6. DMCA / user-generated-content launch gate
If TRYAMM hosts user-uploaded content, implement:
- Copyright/DMCA policy
- notice intake form/email
- counter-notice flow
- repeat-infringer policy
- takedown audit log
- preservation of required evidence
- designated DMCA agent registration where the company seeks the applicable U.S. safe-harbor benefits

The Copyright Office currently charges a small fee for designated-agent registration; maintain the public contact information and renewal/update obligations.

## 7. Terms of Service
Terms should cover at minimum:
- account eligibility and age requirements
- teen/minor protections
- acceptable use
- creator/user content license granted to the platform
- user ownership representations
- intellectual-property complaints
- moderation/suspension/appeal
- Marketplace transactions
- Holo Delivery/package services
- contests/awards/voting
- simulated vs real-money features
- AI/JARVIS limitations and approvals
- HoloGPT Credits/AI Actions are platform-use credits, not cash unless separately stated by a regulated product
- subscriptions/fees/refunds
- seller/merchant obligations
- third-party services
- warranty disclaimers/limitations subject to law
- indemnity where appropriate
- dispute-resolution/venue language reviewed for enforceability
- termination/data export
- changes/notices

## 8. Privacy Policy + consent architecture
Privacy documentation must accurately describe actual data practices. Collect only what is needed, apply least-privilege access, and provide meaningful controls.

High-sensitivity data classes include:
- identity verification
- financial data
- precise geolocation
- disability/accessibility preferences
- emergency/help requests
- child/teen data
- health/telehealth data
- legal/tax/insurance-service intake

Do not use crisis/help or accessibility data for targeted advertising. Obtain affirmative consent before collecting/sharing sensitive data when required.

## 12. Regulated professional-services marketplace
Telehealth, lawyer-on-demand, tax/bookkeeping, insurance, realty, remote notarization, Medicaid-billable services, massage/esthetic services and other licensed services require provider verification and jurisdiction-specific rules.

TRYAMM should generally act as a technology/marketplace/administrative platform unless it has the licenses and contracts needed to be the regulated provider. Do not collect a percentage of legal fees, medical fees, insurance commissions, real-estate commissions, Medicaid reimbursements or other regulated professional compensation unless counsel confirms that fee structure is lawful for the specific jurisdiction/service.

Use separate provider agreements, credential verification, malpractice/professional-liability requirements where appropriate, service disclaimers, emergency limitations and clear billing ownership.

## 13. Medicaid / healthcare launch gate
Do not mark Medicaid billing LIVE until:
- qualified provider/entity status is verified;
- payer enrollment and provider identifiers are verified;
- covered services and coding rules are confirmed;
- claims/billing partner agreement is executed where applicable;
- HIPAA/BAA and health-data controls are implemented where required;
- fraud/waste/abuse controls and audit logging exist;
- clinical documentation responsibilities are defined;
- telehealth/service-location rules are verified by jurisdiction and payer.

## 14. Contests, awards, giveaways and voting
Maintain Official Rules for every contest/prize program. Separate skill contests, sweepstakes, paid competitions and charitable votes. Store eligibility, geography, age, dates, judging/voting method, prize description, tie-breaks, fraud/bot controls, sponsor identity and tax disclosures.

Paid chance-based games/lotteries remain GATED unless specifically lawful and licensed in the applicable jurisdiction.

## 15. AI / JARVIS legal controls
Public documentation should state:
- AI can make mistakes;
- consequential actions require user confirmation where appropriate;
- professional-service outputs are not substitutes for licensed professionals;
- personal/business data use is permissioned and disclosed;
- users can correct relevant profile facts;
- automated matching explains important criteria where practical;
- agent execution is auditable and revocable.

Add additional sector-specific notices only where those services are actually enabled.

## 18. Launch status rule
Each legal item is tagged: MISSING | DRAFT | COUNSEL REVIEW | SIGNED/FILED | VERIFIED | LIVE.
No item becomes VERIFIED merely because a template exists.

## 19. Pre-launch legal gate
PUBLIC launch can proceed only when the product features being made public have matching legal/policy coverage. High-risk features stay disabled through feature flags until their licenses/provider contracts/approvals exist.

Core minimum before broad public launch:
- company/entity authority confirmed;
- contributor/contractor IP chain-of-title reviewed;
- Terms + Privacy + Community Guidelines published;
- DMCA/copyright process operational for UGC;
- seller/refund rules for Marketplace;
- contest rules for any live prizes;
- security/privacy controls implemented;
- trademark clearance started for primary brand;
- copyright-registration queue created;
- all regulated services clearly GATED unless provider/legal requirements are complete.
