# OAS 1.0 — Omniverse Album Standard

OAS is TRYAMM's human-led, AI-assisted production and release standard for music that can exist as audio, video, spatial, AR, VR, MR and StreetVerse experiences from one approved musical master timeline.

## Core rule

AI may assist with composition scaffolds, MIDI, arrangement options, orchestration, mix analysis, accessibility, spatial proposals, video/world planning and administration. Human creators make the meaningful creative selections, edits, performances and approvals that define the released work.

## Required production gate

CREATE → LISTEN → HUMAN_EDIT → AUTHORSHIP_LOG → RIGHTS_SAMPLE_CHECK → APPROVE → RECORD → MIX → SPATIALIZE → BUILD_WORLD → VERIFY → MASTER → RELEASE

A project should not advance to approval while its human-authorship log is still draft. It should not advance to mastering while its rights/sample review is uncleared.

## Digital Master Vault

Each OAS project can preserve:

1. Composition Master — MIDI, notation, chords, lyrics and arrangement.
2. Multitrack Master — original source recordings.
3. Dry Performance Master — important unprocessed performances.
4. Stem Master — drums, bass, music, orchestra, choir, vocals and FX.
5. Stereo Mix Master.
6. Instrumental Master.
7. Performance Master.
8. Clean Master where needed.
9. Spatial Master.
10. AR/VR/MR Master package.
11. Archive Master.
12. Distribution Master.

- Stereo
- Instrumental
- Performance
- Clean

- Vertical short/reel
- AR

The creator can release only a subset. Optional formats must never be required to hear the music.

## OAS AI Movie Director

The movie/world timeline binds music to visual and interactive events:

BAR → BEAT → LYRIC → INSTRUMENT → CAMERA → LIGHT → ENVIRONMENT → INTERACTION

The AI Movie Director proposes shots, scenes and world reactions; a human creator/director approves the final result.

## Artist Inspiration Engine

Artist inspiration remains editable and may be added later. The system should transform inspiration into broad musical characteristics such as rhythm, harmony, instrumentation, arrangement energy, performance character and cinematic scale. It must not be used as a deliberate copying mechanism for another work's lyrics, melody, recording or signature material.

## Aniyah Virtual 64-Track Studio

Creators can begin from a phone, tablet or computer and later upgrade into human studio help. The virtual studio can organize a full 64-track project even when the creator records only a few source tracks.

Suggested groups:

- 1–12 Drums
- 13–18 Bass
- 19–28 Music
- 29–40 Cinematic/Orchestra
- 41–48 Choir
- 49–60 Vocals
- 61–64 Production/Prints

## Accessibility

OAS projects should support timed lyrics/captions, translation-ready text, audio-description planning, high-contrast lyric presentation and a non-headset experience so AR/VR hardware is optional.

## OAS-001

The first reference album is `JUDAH: WAR IN THE SPIRIT`, a 12-song album plus the bonus testimony track `Still Standing (Yahavah Kept Me)`. Its production method is instrumental-first: compose and approve the music before final lyrics are fitted to the approved pockets.
