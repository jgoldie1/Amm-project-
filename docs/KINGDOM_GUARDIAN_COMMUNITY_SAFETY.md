# Kingdom Guardian Community Safety

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT / BUSINESS SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Create a lawful, non-vigilante, non-law-enforcement community safety model focused on visibility, hospitality, de-escalation, escorts, youth outreach, resource navigation, incident reporting, environmental care and fast connection to emergency/public services.

This is not a private militia, street-enforcement group, arrest team, or substitute police force.

## Core model
VISIBLE PRESENCE → GREET/HELP → OBSERVE → DE-ESCALATE WHEN SAFE → CONNECT TO SERVICES → DOCUMENT → ESCALATE TO 911/PUBLIC SAFETY WHEN NECESSARY → FOLLOW UP → MEASURE OUTCOMES.

## Core modes
- SAFE WALK: request a trained companion/escort for walking to transit, school, work, vehicle or nearby destination.
- SAFE ARRIVAL: trusted-contact style check-in/check-out and arrival confirmation.
- COMMUNITY PRESENCE: visible trained ambassadors at approved events, corridors and business districts.
- DE-ESCALATION: trained verbal conflict-reduction and bystander-safety practices.
- YOUTH PATHWAY: route young people into sports, music, coding, jobs, mentoring, trades, entrepreneurship and paid/eligible opportunities.
- LOST SHEEP / EMERGENCY PATH: private connection to food, shelter, transport, legal aid, reentry, counseling/crisis resources and other services.
- BUSINESS SAFETY NETWORK: businesses can share non-sensitive safety notices, lighting/access concerns and verified incident trends without creating rumor feeds or public suspect lists.
- SAFE PLACE NETWORK: vetted participating businesses/community organizations can opt in as visible places where someone can request help or wait safely.

## No-vigilante rules
Participants do not chase, detain, interrogate, search, threaten, profile, punish or impersonate police/security officers. No crowdsourcing suspect identities. No public doxxing. No weapons requirement. No secret gang lists built from rumor or appearance. No autonomous AI accusation or criminal-risk scoring.

If a jurisdiction requires a security license for a paid activity, TRYAMM must either obtain/partner for the required license or exclude that activity.

## Incident flow
SEE/REQUEST → ASSESS IMMEDIATE DANGER → CALL/ROUTE TO APPROPRIATE EMERGENCY SERVICE WHEN NEEDED → CREATE PRIVATE INCIDENT/HELP RECORD → SAFE ACTION → HANDOFF/RESOURCE → FOLLOW-UP → AGGREGATED GAP ANALYSIS.

## Stubbs AI / Guardian JARVIS
Guardian JARVIS is a dispatch, training and resource-navigation assistant. It may:
- assign approved routes/zones
- surface check-ins and welfare tasks
- show verified resources
- produce incident forms
- assist translation/accessibility
- remind ambassadors of escalation rules
- summarize non-sensitive shift activity
- identify aggregate environmental/service gaps
- prepare sponsor/grant reports from aggregate outcomes

It may not decide that a person is a criminal, order detention, identify someone as gang-affiliated from appearance, encourage confrontation or replace emergency dispatch.

## Unit economics / 3.00x target
Every contract must be modeled in the Platform Sustainability Engine.

DIRECT SERVICE CONTRIBUTION RATIO = CONTRACT REVENUE / (ambassador wages + payroll burden + insurance + training + uniforms/equipment + supervision + communications/software + direct operating cost).

The service business should target positive contribution after labor and insurance. TRYAMM platform economics separately target the 3.00x eligible platform revenue / infrastructure-cost goal.

Do not confuse gross contract revenue with platform margin.

## Example pricing method
Final pricing depends on wages, insurance and jurisdiction. Build price from:
FULLY LOADED LABOR + SUPERVISION/DISPATCH + INSURANCE/COMPLIANCE + TRAINING/EQUIPMENT + SOFTWARE/ADMIN + TARGET OPERATING MARGIN.

Then quote one transparent hourly/monthly/event package to the customer.
