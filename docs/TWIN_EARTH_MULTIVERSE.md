# Twin Earth, Middleverse and Multiverse Roadmap

## Twin Earth

Twin Earth is a digital-world layer that stores geographic coordinates, realm state, teleport events, NPC state, missions and player ownership. It can use licensed map, route, place, satellite and photorealistic 3D-tile providers after keys, billing and terms review are complete.

The current code provides coordinate validation, world-session records, teleport events, realm selection and privacy/safety restrictions. It does not download or clone Google Earth. Google imagery must be requested through approved Google Maps Platform services and displayed under their terms, attribution and billing requirements.

## Teleportation

Teleportation means fast travel between approved digital coordinates. It supports portals between metaverse, middleverse and multiverse realms. Restricted private residences, schools, military sites and critical infrastructure must be blocked or abstracted.

## Original game slate

TryAMM must use original worlds and names rather than copying Call of Duty, Ghostbusters or Pokemon Go:

- Battlefront Echo: original squad-based science-fiction action.
- Spectral Response: cooperative paranormal investigation with original spirits and tools.
- Yogihoo World: location-based creature exploration and arena battles.
- Photon Tag: geofenced AR laser-tag-style play with phone, Bluetooth controller, VR and MR modes.

## Intelligent NPCs

NPCs have archetypes, goals, short memory, moods, location, knowledge tags and safety boundaries. Production NPC intelligence requires server persistence, deterministic game rules, moderation, prompt-injection defense, cost limits and human-authored lore.

## Feeling damage

The system may simulate impact using phone vibration, controller rumble, spatial sound, visual effects and optional wearable haptics. It must not intentionally cause pain or injury. Accessibility settings must allow haptics, flashes and intense audio to be reduced or disabled.

## Quantum Speed Engine

Quantum Speed Engine is an interoperability layer with shared contracts for player profiles, coordinates, combat, NPC state, inventories, arena matches and audio cues. It does not magically merge Unity, Unreal and Godot into one runtime.

Recommended use:

- Web/Three.js for browser demos, storefronts and lightweight WebXR.
- Unity for mobile, broad XR and cross-platform gameplay.
- Unreal for high-fidelity open worlds, cinematics and dedicated-server builds.
- Godot for open tooling, lightweight clients and community-mod workflows.

## 3D/4D holographic generator

3D means rendered spatial assets. 4D means time-based state changes, simulation, animation and persistence. On ordinary displays, holograms are visual simulations. AR/VR/MR hardware provides spatial presentation but does not create unsupported free-space physical holograms.

## AI training

The training pipeline uses approved knowledge, tool routing, human-reviewed examples, evaluations and only later fine-tuning. Private conversations, secrets and personal information cannot be used without consent and cleaning.
