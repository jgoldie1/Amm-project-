# TRYAMM Complete Economic, Learning, Creator & Faith Loop

Status: launch architecture / implementation contract. Individual modules are not considered production-GREEN until their runtime, security, payment, rights, accessibility and device gates pass.

## Mission

TRYAMM connects education, skills, work, creation, entertainment, commerce, ownership and reinvestment into one ecosystem.

LEARN -> PRACTICE -> VERIFY SKILLS -> FIND WORK -> CREATE -> PLAY -> STREAM -> PUBLISH -> SELL -> ADVERTISE -> VERIFIED TRANSACTION -> EARN -> BUILD A BUSINESS -> HIRE -> TEACH/MENTOR -> REINVEST -> LEARN.

## Launch systems

### All American University
Pre-K through K-12 learning pathways, trades, workforce training, professional certifications, college pathways and advanced learning. Any representation of accredited degrees or regulated credentials requires the appropriate authorized/accredited provider or partnership.

### Faith & Paleo-Hebrew Learning Center
Opt-in faith and heritage education with Paleo-Hebrew alphabet and inscription study, transliteration, vocabulary, historical context, Biblical/modern Hebrew comparisons, scripture study, audio, reading plans, Sabbath/New Moon study, accessibility, teacher-led learning and certificates of completion. Historically supported material must be distinguished from reconstructed pronunciation or faith-tradition interpretation.

### Faith Bible
Searchable scripture and study experience with audio, notes, cross-references, timelines/maps, Paleo-Hebrew overlays, study plans, accessible formats and interactive learning. Publishing and editions connect to Kingdoms Press and Omni Rights.

### Judah Works / Omni Services
Original services and work marketplace: profiles, skills, portfolios, service listings, search/matching, orders, milestones, delivery, reviews, disputes and verified payments. Community economic-development programs may focus outreach and training on African-American and Tribe-of-Judah creators and businesses while employment and marketplace eligibility remain fair and lawful and do not exclude people based on protected identity.

### Judah Works Academy
No experience -> training -> practice project -> portfolio -> skill verification -> entry opportunity -> reviews -> advanced training -> higher-value work -> team lead -> business owner.

### Local Business Digitization Corps
Workers help businesses create storefronts, websites, catalogs, commercials, reels, livestream commerce, 3D product assets, StreetVerse presence and advertising campaigns.

### Holo AI Studio
Original AI-assisted production tools for scripts, short video, advertising, animation, voice, backgrounds, trailers, product media and StreetVerse content. Do not copy proprietary competitor code, branding or content.

### Omni Reel
Short-form creation, editing, publishing and discovery. Connect gameplay and creator capture to captions, music, stickers, green-screen/background tools, phone save/export, Omni Box distribution, storefronts and monetization.

### Omni Drama
Original vertical episodic entertainment system with short episodes, seasons, cliffhangers, creator channels, ad-supported viewing, subscriptions/premium unlocks and interactive stories. Original implementation and catalog; no copying proprietary DramaBox software/content/branding.

### TRYAMM Live + Omni Box
Live creator/community distribution plus multi-surface publishing for finished creator media.

### StreetVerse and game worlds
Gameplay, missions, businesses and performances generate practice, entertainment and creator moments that can flow into Omni Reel and Omni Drama.

### Holo Ads + product placement
Businesses purchase promotion and eligible creators/publishers can earn contractual shares. Interactive placements can lead to marketplace listings.

### All American Marketplace + creator storefronts
Commerce for lawful products, services, subscriptions and eligible digital/game goods.

### Omni Rights
Authorship, provenance, permissions, licenses, editions and contractual revenue-split records for original media, music, characters, game assets, advertisements and publications. Records support rights administration but do not themselves create legal copyright ownership.

### Holo Credit
Initially a closed-loop promotional/platform-credit layer. Keep it separate from cash creator earnings. Lending, deposits, securities or other regulated financial products require compliant/licensed partners and appropriate legal review.

### Get Paid to Create / Play
Only funded and server-verified programs: sponsored missions, skill challenges, creator campaigns, tournaments and promotional pools. Never allow the client to mint payable balances.

## Work and job engine

Potential work lanes include employees, appropriately classified contractors/creators, marketplace businesses/sellers and participants in eligible funded programs. Roles can include instructors, mentors, curriculum creators, researchers, language teachers, editors, narrators, accessibility specialists, translators, AI-media producers, writers, actors/voice talent, animators, game/world builders, 3D artists, musicians, sound engineers, producers, livestream hosts, moderators, ad sales, product-placement coordinators, customer support, rights/licensing staff, software engineers, QA/device testers, cybersecurity and local-business onboarding.

Do not advertise projected opportunities as jobs already created. Report funded positions, active sellers, paid creators, completed orders and verified payouts separately.

## Team commissioning

CUSTOMER BUDGET -> WRITER -> PRODUCER/DIRECTOR -> AI/3D ARTIST -> VOICE/ACTOR -> EDITOR -> MUSIC/SOUND -> CAPTIONS/TRANSLATION -> QA -> OMNI RIGHTS -> DELIVERY -> SERVER-VERIFIED SPLITS -> PAYOUT.

## Money contract

CUSTOMER MONEY -> SERVER VERIFICATION -> AUTHORITATIVE LEDGER -> PLATFORM FEE + CONTRACTUAL SPLITS -> CREATOR/SELLER/WORKER PAYABLE BALANCE -> PAYOUT.

Holo Credit is not interchangeable with cash earnings unless a future compliant program explicitly supports that conversion.

## Complete demand flywheel

EDUCATION -> SKILLS -> OPPORTUNITY -> PRODUCTION -> DISTRIBUTION -> ENTERTAINMENT/GAMEPLAY -> COMMERCE -> ADVERTISING -> OWNERSHIP/RIGHTS -> VERIFIED INCOME -> EMPLOYMENT/BUSINESS CREATION -> MENTORSHIP -> EDUCATION.

Business demand feeds Judah Works. Skill gaps feed All American University. Graduates and creators supply Judah Works. Holo AI Studio helps production. Omni Reel, Omni Drama, TRYAMM Live and StreetVerse create distribution and audience. Marketplace and Holo Ads create commercial demand. Omni Rights administers permissions/splits. Verified payments turn real customer revenue into payable earnings.

## Release truth

A module is not GREEN because it appears in this document or exists as a route. GREEN requires applicable code/build tests, runtime test, asset/license validation, security test, payment/provider test, accessibility test, production deployment verification and real-device smoke test.

Primary success metric: how many people learn a useful skill, obtain a real opportunity, generate verified legitimate income, grow a sustainable creator/business practice, and eventually hire or train someone else.
