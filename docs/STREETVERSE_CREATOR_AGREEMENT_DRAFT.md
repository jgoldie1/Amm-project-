## 1. Program and definitions
The Platform operates a digital game and creator marketplace. "Creator Content" means original or properly licensed clothing, hair, buildings, missions, animations, and other approved digital assets. "Game Credits" are fictional, nonredeemable gameplay units with no cash value, no transfer to external wallets, and no right to exchange for money. "Real-Money Sales" are actual customer purchases processed by an authorized payment provider.

## 2. Eligibility, onboarding and identity
Creator must be legally eligible to contract, meet applicable age requirements, provide accurate identity/business details, and complete any required payment-provider onboarding, sanctions screening, tax documentation and fraud checks. No cash payment is due until onboarding and compliance requirements are satisfied. The Platform may suspend listings or payouts for verified fraud, legal orders, safety risks or unresolved disputes, with notice and appeal where appropriate.

## 3. Intellectual property and license
Creator retains ownership of their original Creator Content. Creator grants Platform a worldwide, nonexclusive, sublicensable solely as necessary, royalty-bearing license during the listing term to host, reproduce, distribute, adapt for technical compatibility, display, promote and deliver Creator Content within StreetVerse and related authorized services. Creator warrants ownership or adequate third-party permissions for all assets, music, likenesses, brands, textures and animations. No unauthorized copyrighted, trademarked or privacy-invasive content is permitted. Platform retains ownership of its engine, trademarks, tools and preexisting materials. Termination ends new sales after reasonable wind-down, subject to customers' existing licenses and legally required records.

## 4. Review and publication
All submissions are pending until approved by an independent moderator. Automated scans and human review may check licensing, malware/scripts, prohibited content, accessibility, performance budgets and technical compatibility. Approval is not a guarantee of sales or a legal determination of ownership. Creators may appeal rejected submissions through [support contact]. Changes to published assets require renewed review.

## 5. Prices, creator revenue and fees
**Proposed default split, subject to written approval:** 70% Creator / 30% Platform of **Net Receipts**, defined as customer payments actually settled, minus sales taxes collected/remitted, refunds, chargebacks and payment-provider transaction fees attributable to the sale. The Platform must show itemized statements and explain any additional deductions before sale. Prices and promotional discounts require Creator's agreement under a published pricing policy. No minimum sales or earnings are promised. This real-money split is a proposal and is NOT enabled by current code.

## 7. Refunds, disputes and fraud
Customers receive the refund rights required by law and the storefront's disclosed policy. Platform investigates customer disputes, payment fraud, stolen assets, self-purchases, artificial transactions and reward manipulation. Creator is notified of chargebacks attributable to their sales and can submit evidence. No arbitrary forfeiture of undisputed earned amounts. Platform and Creator will cooperate on valid takedown notices and intellectual-property disputes, with appeal and counter-notice processes as required by law.

## 8. Tax, privacy and compliance
Each party handles its own applicable income taxes; Platform and payment provider handle reporting, withholding, and sales-tax responsibilities as legally allocated. Creator agrees to the privacy notice describing necessary processing of identity, tax, payout and transaction information. Sensitive verification documents should be handled by the authorized provider, not stored in public game assets. Platform must publish clear consumer terms, digital-content licensing and refund policies before taking real-money orders.

## 9. Community Development Fund
A separate written fund policy must identify the legal entity, eligible net-revenue basis, percentage, accounting controls, recipient eligibility, selection conflicts, reporting, charitable-solicitation implications, grant agreements and independent approval. No creator or player is guaranteed a fund distribution. No fund collection, promise, or disbursement begins until reviewed and authorized.

## 10. Game economy separation
Fictional Game Credits are not wages, investments, deposits, securities, cryptoassets or cash equivalents. Credits cannot be redeemed for real money, transferred outside the game or used to buy real-money payout eligibility. Gameplay progression and mod purchases using credits are governed by separate game rules. No payment for recruiting participants or promises of wealth through token appreciation.
