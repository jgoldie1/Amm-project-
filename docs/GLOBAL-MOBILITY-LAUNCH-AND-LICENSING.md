# TryAMM Global Mobility & Delivery Launch Pathway

## Core operating model
Launch country-by-country and city-by-city. Prefer licensed/approved providers first. TryAMM may provide the customer interface, merchant tools, routing logic, tracking, support and analytics while regulated or insured partners perform functions that require licenses, permits, insurance or specialized operational authority. Do not assume one country's approvals transfer to another.

## Country launch dossier
For every country create a dated dossier containing: legal entity/tax status, transport/delivery licensing analysis, payments/payouts model, worker classification analysis, insurance requirements, privacy/location rules, background/identity screening rules, vehicle rules, consumer-protection/refund rules, tax reporting, local support contacts, emergency/escalation process, regulator/authority links, outside counsel/reviewer, and renewal dates.

## Insurance gate
Before an owned/contracted network goes live, document applicable commercial auto/non-owned/hired auto, general liability, cargo/goods-in-transit, workers compensation/employers liability where required, cyber/privacy, umbrella/excess, and any locally required passenger/transport cover. Coverage must be confirmed by a licensed broker/carrier for the specific country, city, vehicle class and service. AI may prepare comparison checklists but cannot certify coverage.

## Licensing gate
Identify permits/licenses for ride-hail/TNC/private hire/taxi, courier/delivery, food handling where relevant, business licensing, vehicle inspection, driver permits, payments/money transmission, and marketplace-specific obligations. Use licensed partners until TryAMM has the required authority. Maintain a renewal calendar and evidence repository.

## Driver/courier classification gate
Do not globally label workers as independent contractors or employees. Classification must be reviewed per country/state/province/city and operating model. Document control factors, scheduling, pricing control, equipment, exclusivity, benefits, tax handling, minimum pay, expense reimbursement and deactivation/appeal rights. Human legal review is required before launch.

## Safety gate
Minimum program: identity verification, driving/license verification where applicable, background screening where lawful, vehicle/bike eligibility, safety training, zero-tolerance impairment policy, emergency/SOS workflows, incident reporting, insurance validation, fraud controls, location/privacy consent, account takeover protection, ratings/complaints, suspension/escalation, and periodic re-screening where lawful.

## Support gate
Provide in-app support, driver/courier support, merchant support, incident escalation, refund/dispute workflows, lost-item process, safety escalation, accessibility support, multilingual support plan, and 24/7 emergency coverage where the service/risk requires it. AI may triage and summarize, but urgent safety decisions must escalate to trained humans.

## Demand-density gate
Measure orders/trips per zone per hour, pickup distance, idle time, acceptance rate, cancellation rate, repeat rate, on-time rate, merchant density, driver supply and provider quote frequency. Start partner-only. Consider owned/contracted fleet economics only when density is sufficient to reduce cost without harming service quality.

## Profit gate
Calculate contribution after provider/driver pay, incentives, payment fees, insurance allocation, support, fraud/refunds, promos, taxes/fees, maps/communications, background checks, and realistic idle time. Default code gate is 12% contribution margin; target 20%+ where sustainable. This is an operating assumption to tune with real data, not a guarantee.

## AI help TryAMM should use
HoloGPT/AI can maintain jurisdiction checklists, summarize regulatory updates for human review, flag missing documents, forecast demand, predict zone profitability, compare partner quotes, detect fraud/anomalies, score onboarding completeness, draft support responses, generate incident summaries, monitor renewal dates, and produce launch-readiness reports. All legal/compliance conclusions need qualified human approval.

## Partner-to-owned-network progression
1. Research market and legal model.
2. Launch pickup and licensed third-party fulfillment where permitted.
3. Prove repeat demand and positive unit economics.
4. Add approved local fleet/courier partners.
5. Obtain required TryAMM licenses/permits and insurance only where economics justify it.
6. Pilot one constrained zone.
7. Compare owned/contracted economics against external providers.
8. Expand only after safety, support, density and contribution-margin KPIs pass.

## Global expansion rule
Never copy a U.S. compliance assumption into Africa, Europe, Asia, the Caribbean, Canada, Latin America or the Middle East. Every country needs its own launch dossier and partner/legal review. Cross-border payments remain partner-orchestrated until TryAMM has all required registrations/licenses for the exact activity and jurisdictions involved.

## Required technical integrations before production
Durable database; TryAMM Passport/auth; secure document storage; KYC/identity provider where needed; background screening adapters; maps/geocoding/navigation; insurance verification workflow; payout provider; tax reporting; provider delivery/ride adapters; signed idempotent webhooks; fraud engine; support case system; SOS/incident system; audit ledger; analytics; consent/privacy management; deletion/retention controls; observability; disaster recovery; and end-to-end QA.
