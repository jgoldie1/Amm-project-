# TRYAMM Community Peace & Opportunity System

Status: LOCKED OPERATING MODEL

## Core loop
PROBLEM → PERSON → SUPPORT → OPPORTUNITY → JOB/TRAINING → RELATIONSHIP → STABILITY → COMMUNITY OWNERSHIP → LOWER RISK → HELP THE NEXT PERSON.

The system is prevention, de-escalation, resource navigation, mentorship and opportunity infrastructure. It is not vigilantism, private policing or unlicensed security work.

## Roles
- Community Ambassador
- Youth Mentor
- Conflict Mediator
- Resource Navigator
- Reentry Coach
- Program Facilitator
- Outreach Coordinator
- Community Data Coordinator
- Logistics Support
- Digital Community Moderator

Role permissions are least-privilege and location/program specific. People do not receive police powers, weapons authority or detention authority merely by joining a TRYAMM program.

## Revenue / contract pathways
- property-management ambassador contracts
- retail/business-district programs
- event safe-presence staffing where lawful
- school/college support programs
- hospital/community-navigation partnerships
- workforce/reentry contracts
- youth sponsorships
- municipal grants/contracts
- philanthropy
- employer-funded Second Chance programs
- licensing the TRYAMM software/training system

Where a role becomes regulated security, healthcare, counseling, legal practice or another licensed profession, TRYAMM routes to qualified/licensed partners rather than relabeling the service.

## Integrated platform systems
Emergency Path + Lost Sheep + Launchpad + Student JARVIS + Community Gap Finder + Opportunity Match + Supplier Exchange + Money Engine + Community Spotlight.

### Workflow
1. Identify an unmet need or recurring hotspot using lawful, privacy-preserving aggregate data.
2. Route a person to a trained ambassador/mentor/resource navigator or qualified professional.
3. Stabilize immediate needs through Emergency Path / Lost Sheep.
4. Build a concrete next-step plan in Launchpad.
5. Match to education, work, apprenticeship, business, creator, supplier or reentry opportunities.
6. Track milestones and barriers, not a social-credit score.
7. Measure outcomes in aggregate.
8. Community Spotlight tells consented success stories and reports sponsor/contract impact.

## Safety boundaries
- no vigilantism
- no impersonating law enforcement
- no detention/search/seizure powers
- no weapons program by default
- no gang/criminal prediction scoring
- no public exposure of emergency/help requests
- no autonomous AI decision to confront or pursue people
- call qualified emergency/public services when immediate danger requires them
- preserve evidence and incident records only under lawful policy and minimum necessary data

## Training stack
De-escalation, conflict mediation, trauma-aware communication, youth engagement, resource navigation, reentry support, disability/accessibility awareness, digital safety, reporting, incident boundaries, CPR/first aid where properly certified, customer service, data privacy and local referral knowledge.

## Contract measurement
Possible KPIs: referrals completed, youth engaged, training enrollments, job/apprenticeship placements, reentry placements, conflicts de-escalated where reliably documented, resource connections, employer partners, repeat incident reductions where causally defensible, business participation and participant retention.

Never promise crime reduction from platform activity alone; report observed outcomes and confounding limits.

## Profitability model
Revenue contracts and licensing must exceed wages, supervision, training, insurance, background/credential checks where appropriate, software/support, travel, equipment, administration and reserves. Feed each program's real revenue/cost into Platform Sustainability Engine.

Target hierarchy:
- program contribution margin positive
- platform self-support ratio >= 1.00x
- reserve-building >= 1.50x
- strategic TRYAMM target = 3.00x eligible platform revenue / measured infrastructure cost

## Technology layer
