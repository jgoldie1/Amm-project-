# TRYAMM Next-Level Competitive Integration

Status: LOCKED ARCHITECTURE / RECOVERY-AWARE

## Purpose
Turn TRYAMM from a collection of features into one permissioned operating system spanning person, school, creator, business, commerce, delivery, vehicle, home and community. Preserve completed work; do not rebuild existing routes/assets/schemas without evidence.

## 1. One JARVIS continuity
PERSONAL JARVIS → STUDENT JARVIS → CREATOR JARVIS → BUSINESS JARVIS → VEHICLE JARVIS → HOME JARVIS.

Stubbs AI is the intelligence foundation. HoloGPT is the conversational/spatial interface. JARVIS is the user/business-specific operating assistant. Context handoff is explicit, scoped and revocable.

## 2. JARVIS Commerce Protocol
Agent-to-agent commerce contract:
REQUEST → REQUIREMENTS → VERIFIED CATALOG/INVENTORY → OPTIONS → COUPON → DELIVERY QUOTE → CHECKOUT PREVIEW → HUMAN APPROVAL → JIN PAY → MONEY ENGINE → FULFILLMENT → TRACKING → PROOF/RETURN.

Example: Personal JARVIS asks participating Restaurant/Business JARVIS instances for a meal matching budget and user-selected dietary requirements. Business JARVIS may answer only from verified merchant data. It cannot invent allergy safety, availability or pricing.

## 3. Company Digital Twin
Each business can maintain an authorized structured twin:
- business goals and policies
- products/services/catalog
- customers and consent state
- orders/returns
- employees/roles
- suppliers/procurement
- inventory
- creator/marketing projects
- Jin Pay/Money Engine summaries
- Holo Delivery/Package status
- HR workflow state
- grants/opportunities
- security/compliance state

Business JARVIS reasons against this model rather than relying on chat memory as business truth.

## 4. Business Pulse
Daily/real-time signal layer: revenue trends, fulfillment risk, inventory shortages, customer-support queue, supplier changes, applications/onboarding, marketing performance, delivery exceptions, grant deadlines, security alerts and actions awaiting approval.

Output prioritizes: NOW | TODAY | THIS WEEK | WATCH.

## 5. Agent Council
Specialist agents: Strategy, Engineering, Marketing, Sales, Support, Operations, Finance, HR, Procurement, Grants, Creator, Delivery, Security and Accessibility.

Chief-of-Staff JARVIS synthesizes disagreement. Finance may challenge Marketing; Security may block unsafe execution; HR may flag workforce impact. Consequential actions remain subject to Permission Firewall and human approval.

## 6. Business Simulator
SIMULATE → COMPARE → APPROVE → SMALL TEST → MEASURE → SCALE OR ROLLBACK.

Use for promotions, free-delivery experiments, pricing scenarios, staffing plans, inventory decisions and campaign allocation. Simulation is advisory, not a guaranteed forecast.

## 7. JARVIS Skill Marketplace
Future approved skills/agent packages for restaurants, logistics, music studios, salons, construction, real estate, creators, education, churches/ministry, nonprofits and other verticals. Skills declare permissions, data requirements, actions, cost/AI Actions and safety classification.

## 8. HoloGPT Credits / AI Actions
User-facing metering shows understandable AI Actions and HoloGPT Credits. Credits fund eligible compute/render/agent workloads; they are not creator earnings, cash, deposits or Jin Pay balances. Money Engine keeps these ledgers separate.

## 9. Holographic front door
Website/app proposition: `Your World. Your Business. Your JARVIS. — Powered by Stubbs AI + HoloGPT.`
Primary intents: FOR ME | SCHOOL | CREATE | START A BUSINESS | SHOP/ORDER | DRIVE/DELIVER | COMMUNITY.
JARVIS routes intent into existing platform systems instead of forcing users to learn a large menu tree.

## 10. Competitive defense
The moat is authorized continuity and completed journeys, not feature count:
PERSON ↔ EDUCATION ↔ SKILLS ↔ ACCESSIBILITY ↔ CREATOR WORK ↔ BUSINESS ↔ EMPLOYEES ↔ PRODUCTS ↔ SUPPLIERS ↔ CUSTOMERS ↔ PAYMENTS ↔ DELIVERY ↔ VEHICLE ↔ HOME ↔ COMMUNITY.

Defend with exportability, explainable recommendations, permission boundaries, audit evidence, accessible UX, seller/creator ownership controls and interoperable provider adapters.
