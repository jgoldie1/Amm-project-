# TryAMM IP, Risk, Generational Wealth & Education Plan

## Purpose
Protect the company, founders, creators, users, assets and long-term family value while converting reusable knowledge and IP into durable, auditable revenue-producing systems. This is an engineering/business control plan, not legal, tax, securities, estate-planning or insurance advice. Qualified counsel and licensed professionals must approve jurisdiction-specific implementation.

## 1. IP Protection Stack
Use layered protection rather than relying on one mechanism.

### Patents
Maintain an invention register with inventor names, conception dates, technical diagrams, prototypes, prior-art searches, public-disclosure dates, filing deadlines and assignment status. Evaluate patentability before public disclosure for genuinely novel technical inventions. Never label a feature patented unless it is actually patented; use patent-pending only when a qualifying application is pending.

### Trademarks
Maintain a brand register for TryAMM, HoloGPT, HoloVerse, product names, logos and source identifiers. Search conflicts before launch, document first use, map each mark to actual goods/services, register priority marks where justified, and monitor confusing uses.

### Copyright
Track human authorship and contributions for source code, music, books, videos, graphics, animation, UI, documentation and other protectable works. Preserve source files and creation history. Record AI assistance separately. Do not assume prompt-only AI output is copyrightable.

### Trade Secrets
Classify confidential algorithms, pricing logic, prompts/workflows, supplier data, security methods, model routing, unreleased inventions and business intelligence. Apply need-to-know access, NDAs/confidentiality terms, encryption, secret managers, logging, offboarding, repository controls and incident response. Information cannot be treated as a trade secret while being freely published.

### Digital DNA + Provenance
Every eligible asset should have cryptographic hashes, perceptual fingerprints, owner/creator identity, version lineage, rights evidence, AI-generation disclosure and usage history. Add C2PA/Content Credentials interoperability where technically appropriate. Digital DNA supports evidence and traceability; it does not replace legal ownership or registration.

## 2. Lawsuit-Risk Reduction Gates
No system eliminates lawsuit risk. Reduce exposure with mandatory gates:
- Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, creator/seller agreements and acceptable-use rules reviewed by counsel.
- DMCA/copyright notice-and-takedown process and repeat-infringer policy where applicable.
- Trademark complaint, counter-notice/dispute and asset recall workflows.
- Explicit likeness, voice and Digital Twin consent with scope, territory, term, revocation and compensation terms.
- Music master, publishing, sample, sync, performance and game-use rights as applicable.
- AI disclosure and human-authorship records.
- Seller warranties plus proportionate indemnity/insurance requirements for higher-risk commercial sellers.
- No unlicensed clones, deceptive celebrity endorsements, copied proprietary UI/code/assets or misleading competitor affiliation.
- Product safety, accessibility, child/teen safety, privacy and security reviews before launch.
- Regulated services routed to licensed providers until TryAMM itself holds required licenses.
- Cyber, technology E&O, general liability, media/IP liability, D&O and other coverage evaluated with a licensed broker based on actual operations.
- Litigation hold and evidence-preservation procedure.

## 3. IP Ownership Chain
Every employee, contractor, collaborator, artist, developer and vendor relationship must answer:
1. Who owned pre-existing IP?
2. What new IP is being created?
3. Is it assigned or licensed?
4. What rights remain with the creator?
5. What territories, media and term apply?
6. Are royalties/splits documented?
7. Can AI models train on it? Default: no unless expressly opted in under clear terms.
8. Can likeness/voice be synthesized? Only with explicit authorization.

Store executed agreements and evidence references in a private rights vault linked to Digital DNA records.

## 4. Generational Wealth Pathway
Do not promise wealth. Build a repeatable pathway:

Create -> Protect -> Own -> License -> Distribute -> Measure -> Reinvest -> Diversify -> Transfer knowledge/ownership responsibly.

### Revenue-producing IP portfolio
Track each asset as a business asset with owner, cost, revenue, license count, renewal dates, territories, dependencies and risk status. Reusable assets can earn through marketplace licenses, subscriptions, enterprise licenses, games, music, HoloVerse experiences, commercials, education, books, merchandise and approved syndication.

### Reinvestment waterfall
Board/owner-approved policy should prioritize taxes/obligations, operating reserves, security/legal/compliance, creator payouts, product reinvestment, debt obligations, diversified reserves/investments and owner distributions. Percentages must be set with accounting/tax advice and actual cash-flow data.

### Estate/succession layer
Use qualified estate/business counsel to evaluate wills, trusts, operating agreements, buy-sell provisions, beneficiary designations, IP assignments, successor managers, key-person coverage and continuity plans. Never place IP into a trust/entity solely from AI-generated instructions without professional review.

### Family knowledge continuity
Create a private Legacy Knowledge Vault containing invention histories, source-code maps, brand/IP registers, operating manuals, financial literacy courses, security procedures, passwords via proper password-management/estate-access mechanisms, key professional contacts and succession instructions.

## 5. TryAMM Knowledge & Education System
Create TryAMM Academy / Creator & Ownership University as a practical learning layer.

Learning tracks:
- Financial literacy: budgeting, banking, credit, taxes basics, fraud awareness and investing concepts without personalized investment promises.
- Entrepreneurship: entity basics, pricing, bookkeeping, customer discovery, contracts, insurance and operations.
- Creator ownership: copyright, trademarks, licensing, royalties, publishing, music rights and Digital DNA.
- AI literacy: prompting, verification, AI disclosure, human authorship, model risks and safe automation.
- Coding/game/3D: web development, game design, asset pipelines, rigging, animation, XR and reusable assets.
- Cybersecurity: account security, phishing, secure development, privacy and incident response.
- Commerce: sourcing, inventory, shipping, tariffs basics, marketplace selling and customer service.
- Career/workforce: AI-assisted work, call-center skills, portfolios, certifications, apprenticeships and second-chance pathways.
- Family legacy: age-appropriate money, ownership, entrepreneurship, stewardship and succession education.

Every course should have objectives, lessons, exercises, assessment, accessibility, captions/translations, version date, sources, instructor/AI disclosure and completion evidence. Certificates must not imply government/professional licensure unless formally authorized.

## 6. Creator Wealth Dashboard
Provide creators with understandable metrics:
- owned assets and verified DNA records;
- revenue by asset/product/channel;
- licenses and expiration/renewal dates;
- royalty splits and pending payouts;
- costs and net contribution;
- top reusable assets;
- rights gaps blocking monetization;
- learning recommendations;
- tax-document availability;
- fraud/dispute/takedown status.

HoloGPT may explain metrics and suggest actions but must not guarantee returns or provide unlicensed personalized legal, tax or investment advice.

## 8. Competitive Positioning
Do not claim nobody else does this. Many companies separately provide generative media, 3D assets, stock/marketplaces, provenance, creator monetization, virtual worlds, music streaming or AI production. The differentiation to validate is the integration: one identity/rights/asset lineage connecting generation, reusable assets, holographic/XR experiences, games, music, video, commerce, education and monetization. Competitive advantage depends on execution, rights-cleared supply, creator economics, reliability, distribution and defensible IP—not feature count alone.

## Definition of Completion
Architecture is not completion. Production completion requires deployed infrastructure, migrations applied, secrets configured, external providers contracted, legal/insurance review, rights and consent operations staffed, security testing passed, payment reconciliation verified, load tests passed, backups restored in a drill, and end-to-end user journeys tested in production-like environments.
