# Original AMM Omniverse Systems

These systems may use broad genre ideas such as connected worlds, teams, advanced suits, portals and alternate-history simulations, but must not copy protected characters, names, logos, costumes, dialogue, stories or distinctive visual identities from Marvel, DC or other franchises.

## Original product systems

### AMM Quantum Leap Engine
A world-transition and asset-streaming system that saves player state, releases the current world's resources, streams the destination and restores the player. It creates a cinematic time-and-space jump illusion without claiming physical time travel.

### HoloPresence Transference
A remote-presence system that captures an approved avatar or volumetric representation, transmits voice and movement, and reconstructs the representation at a remote digital or display endpoint. The person is not physically teleported.

### Echo Memory System
A versioned record of world events, player decisions, concerts, community votes and mission outcomes. Echoes may be replayed as simulations or used to compare possible outcomes; they do not alter real history.

### Quantum Mantles
Configurable player capability profiles combining original equipment, skills, accessibility support, reputation and mission roles. Mantles must use original names, silhouettes, symbols, color arrangements and origin stories.

### Aegis Weave
A fictional and research-oriented adaptive equipment platform for the game universe. In software it controls shields, holographic decoys, translation, drones, hazard sensing and movement assistance. Physical claims require separate engineering proof.

### Holo Sentinels
Original remote-presence and rescue specialists who use projected twins, drones and sensors.

### Quantum Architects
Original builders who stabilize portals, repair worlds and construct temporary environments.

### Living World Keepers
Original guardians responsible for ecological, civic and technical stability across connected worlds.

### Restoration Guild
Original non-combat specialists: healers, educators, engineers, farmers, logistics operators and community rebuilders.

### Echo Travelers
Original explorers who enter historical reconstructions and possible-future simulations.

## Originality guardrails

1. No use of Marvel, DC, character, team, location, artifact or storyline names in production content.
2. No near-copy of a famous costume silhouette, chest emblem, mask, shield, hammer, ring, vehicle or color arrangement.
3. No recreated catchphrases, origin stories or famous scenes.
4. Creator uploads must pass name, image, logo and story similarity review.
5. Marketing must describe portal travel and holographic teleportation as simulation, remote presence or cinematic effects unless physical capabilities are independently demonstrated.
6. Every user-generated character must include an originality declaration and content-rights warranty.
7. Disputed content must be unpublished while reviewed.

## Product value

Together these systems make TryAMM more than a set of separate games. They create a persistent network where identity, reputation, accessibility settings, inventory, quests, creator events, commerce and community decisions can travel between worlds.
