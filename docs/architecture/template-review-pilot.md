# Ten-template honest-feedback pilot

One $49 DIY template license for each of ten selected businesses; selection is manual and no live availability counter is presented. Hosting, domains, paid AI and assisted setup are separate. No positive rating is required. Critical feedback or declining advertising permission does not revoke the gift.

Each catalog card explains suitable businesses, likely benefits and what the demo includes. Initial reviews follow 7–14 days of actual use. Six-month reviews follow the actual launch date, clamped to the last valid day of the target month. Written/audio alternatives are available.

The form downloads a local JSON record. It can prepare a concise support draft but never claims submission or acceptance without server receipt. Operator configuration and real authenticated support testing still block live intake. There is no automated reminder, application approval, upload or publication. Feedback stays in memory until explicit download or support-draft handoff.

Advertising consent is optional and unchecked, distinct from feedback. Confirm consent against the final submitted Reel and display the free-template disclosure. Do not fabricate endorsements, promise revenue or present self-reported sales as verified results.

Before opening live intake, configure an accountable operator and tested private storage, publish retention/deletion terms and consent withdrawal contact, and test ownership controls. Record a baseline, early check-in and six-month usage, satisfaction, ease, issues and optional self-reported outcomes. Ten businesses provide qualitative learning, not a representative success rate.
