# TRYAMM Community Guardian / Peace Ambassador

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT + BUSINESS SPECIFICATION

## Mission
Create a lawful, non-vigilante community safety and opportunity network that reduces harm through visible presence, de-escalation, safe-walk escorts, youth engagement, service navigation, conflict interruption partnerships, event support, and rapid connection to qualified emergency/public-safety resources when needed.

This is not a private police force, militia, vigilante group, or armed patrol system.

## Core principles
- unarmed by default
- observe, deter, de-escalate, document, assist, refer
- never chase suspects or conduct unauthorized searches/detention
- no profiling by race, religion, disability, age, clothing, neighborhood, or social group
- minors require age-appropriate safeguarding
- emergency situations route to appropriate emergency services
- licensed security work is separated and only provided through properly licensed entities/personnel where required
- community guardians are service ambassadors first

## Services
### Safe Walk / Safe Ride Coordination
Request an escort from school, transit, work, event, store, parking area, worship service, or community center. Escorts may be volunteer, sponsored, or paid depending on program rules and local law.

### Peace Presence
Visible trained ambassadors in identified public/community spaces focused on assistance, directions, de-escalation, welfare checks, lost-person help, accessibility assistance, and connecting people to resources.

### Youth Peace Missions
Sports, music, gaming, creator projects, coding, trades, entrepreneurship, mentoring, internships, community service, financial literacy, and restorative conflict-resolution programs.

### Conflict Interruption Partnership
TRYAMM may route or contract with qualified community-violence-intervention organizations and trained credible messengers. Staff need dedicated training, supervision, mental-health support, and clear escalation rules.

### Event Ambassador Teams
Customer-service and safety-support roles for festivals, churches, schools, community meetings, markets, creator events, Soul Ascension, sports events, and TRYAMM gatherings. Any role that crosses into regulated private-security work requires the appropriate license/provider.

### Senior / Disability Assistance
Safe-walk support, transportation coordination, accessibility navigation, fraud/scam education, lost-item assistance, and resource routing. Accessibility Passport settings remain private and consent-controlled.

### Neighborhood Help Desk
Report non-emergency community issues such as broken lighting, abandoned property concerns, unsafe walkways, illegal dumping, or recurring service gaps. Community Gap Finder aggregates trends without exposing individuals.

## Guardian JARVIS
Guardian JARVIS assists ambassadors with:
- shift/mission checklist
- route and rendezvous information
- de-escalation scripts and training refreshers
- accessibility requirements the user chose to share
- resource directory
- incident documentation
- escalation rules
- check-in/check-out
- supervisor contact

Guardian JARVIS does NOT instruct users to pursue, threaten, restrain, interrogate, or punish people.

## Incident workflow
REQUEST/OBSERVATION → ASSESS URGENCY → ASSIST/DE-ESCALATE → CALL QUALIFIED HELP IF NEEDED → DOCUMENT MINIMUM NECESSARY FACTS → SUPERVISOR REVIEW → REFERRAL/FOLLOW-UP → PRIVACY-PRESERVING IMPACT METRICS.

## Revenue model
This can be a profitable social-enterprise line while preserving free/sponsored services.

Revenue lanes:
1. Property/business ambassador subscriptions for non-security customer-assistance/community-presence services where lawful.
2. Event ambassador staffing contracts.
3. School/church/community-center safe-arrival/departure programs.
4. Sponsored Safe Walk hours funded by businesses, foundations, campuses, hospitals, or municipalities.
5. Training contracts for de-escalation, accessibility, customer-service, and community-resource navigation where TRYAMM is qualified to provide the training.
6. Software SaaS: Guardian Dispatch, incident workflow, scheduling, training Passport, analytics, sponsor reporting.
7. Institutional Community Gap Finder dashboards.
8. Grants/contracts for eligible youth-development, reentry, community-violence-intervention, and capacity-building programs.
9. Employer programs and neighborhood/business-district service contracts.
10. HoloGPT/AI Actions for institutional Guardian JARVIS use.

Do not charge victims for emergency help. Do not sell crisis/incident personal data.

## Why this improves the world
