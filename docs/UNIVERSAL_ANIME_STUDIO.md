# TryAMM Universal Anime Studio

## Scope

The studio supports original anime creation across historical eras, broad visual treatments, all major genres and multiple output formats. It must not copy protected franchises, characters or living artists.

## Product modules

1. Idea and world builder
2. Series bible generator
3. Character creator and reference sheets
4. Character consistency lock
5. Script, scene and shot-list generator
6. Storyboard and animatic generator
7. Image and video generation adapters
8. Voice, lip-sync, music and sound workflow
9. Timeline editing and compositing
10. Captions, dubbing and translation
11. Quality-control review
12. Publishing to TryAMM feeds, livestreams and premieres
13. Creator rights, licenses and provenance
14. Credits, subscriptions and marketplace monetization

## Required production infrastructure

- PostgreSQL or Supabase database
- Object storage for images, audio and video
- Authentication and role-based access
- Job queue and generation workers
- Image-generation provider
- Video-generation provider
- Voice and lip-sync provider
- Content moderation
- Usage metering and creator credits
- Stripe and app-store billing
- Monitoring, retries and audit logs

## Delivery phases

### Phase 1
Catalog, project creation, character sheets, storyboards and still-image generation.

### Phase 2
Short shots, trailers, voices, music, captions and social export.

### Phase 3
Character continuity, longer episodes, team collaboration and timeline editor.

### Phase 4
Game, AR, VR and holographic exports plus licensing and marketplace tools.
