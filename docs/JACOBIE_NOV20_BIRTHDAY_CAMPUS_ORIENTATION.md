# Jacobie November 20 Birthday + Campus Orientation Mission

This mission is the first playable onboarding contract for the CampusVerse/CollegeBook/CrossVerse PK foundation.

## Purpose
Teach TRYAMM by doing: arrive, create/load a character, configure accessibility/privacy, meet Jacobie and student guides, discover CollegeBook, choose a major/interest, attend the birthday event, perform a first skill mission, join a friendly PK, learn Holo LIVE production, save evidence to Skills Passport, and unlock the next campus pathway.

The girlfriend character is independent. Relationship changes alter the relationship edge only; her missions, skills, friends, creator portfolio, businesses and school history remain intact.

## Party as onboarding
The birthday event provides optional stations for music/DJ, reels/photo creation, student creator booths, club discovery, friendly PK and Holo LIVE production. Students can participate as competitors or as hosts, commentators, producers, editors, graphics/statistics operators, audio/DJs, ambassadors, entrepreneurs, mission creators or accessibility/caption operators.

## Campus inspiration
Use public campus-life categories only as inspiration unless the institution authorizes an official representation. Greenville publicly describes student clubs, esports, music, athletics, theatre, student government, Black Student Union and other student activities, and emphasizes experiential learning. The implementation must not imply Greenville sponsorship or endorsement.

## Progression
Birthday -> App orientation -> Character/Passport -> CollegeBook -> Major/Interest Mission -> Cross-major team -> CrossVerse PK -> Holo LIVE -> Skills Passport -> Campus chapter -> Middleverse career preview.

## Family business growth engine
This is reusable. Future sibling/company launches receive distinct stories and businesses while sharing the onboarding, character, mission, creator, ledger and career primitives. Do not clone Jacobie's business identity onto another child.

## Acceptance gates
- mission can be configured without hard-coded Greenville branding
- independent relationship history persists
- major/interest mission assignment is data-driven
- PK supports competitor and production roles
- accessibility setup appears before action-heavy gameplay
- no client-authoritative real-money award
- community-college and transfer identities can enter without reset
- school marks/official partnership features remain gated
