# LivingWorld Production Block 001

Status: IN PRODUCTION
Start date: 2026-08-28

## Goal
Deliver one end-to-end LivingWorld vertical slice connecting Seven Lights, Living Scroll, ScrollVerse, Kingdoms Press, Hebrew/Paleo-Hebrew learning, source verification, and TRYAMM persistence.

## Canon and product hierarchy
- Kingdoms Press — parent publishing house
- Living Scroll Press — Scripture and education imprint
- Seven Lights Books — manga, comics, graphic novels, Ari’el parables
- Kingdoms Kids — coloring, alphabet, sticker, puzzle and activity books
- Kingdoms Education — workbooks, teacher guides, homeschool and family study
- Kingdoms Audio — audiobooks, language/pronunciation and accessible audio
- Kingdoms Interactive — ebooks, AR/VR/MR and TRYAMM-connected books
- Kingdoms Creator Press — approved creator publishing pipeline

## Pilot flow
TRYAMM -> Seven Lights -> Episode 1 / Ari’el -> Explore the Scripture -> Living Scroll -> Genesis 1 -> Genesis 1:1 -> Hebrew/Paleo-Hebrew -> word study -> Strong's/lexicon/context -> Show Me Your Source -> lesson/quiz -> save progress -> Kingdoms Press companion -> ScrollVerse entry.

## Source integrity
The UI and data model must keep these categories visibly separate:
1. Scriptural text
2. Manuscript/source material
3. Language/lexical evidence
4. Historical/archaeological evidence
5. Scholarly interpretation
6. Religious tradition
7. Educational reconstruction
8. Seven Lights fiction

AI rule: AI may explain approved material but may not rewrite canon, invent Scripture, hide uncertainty, or present fiction/reconstruction as historical fact. If reliable evidence is insufficient, say so.

## Living Scroll navigation
READ | LISTEN | SPEAK | WRITE | PALEO-HEBREW | STRONG'S | 88-BOOK LIBRARY | 1611 STUDY | ETHIOPIAN SOURCES | ATLAS | TIMELINE | ENTER SCROLLVERSE | SHOW ME YOUR SOURCE

## Hebrew learning loop
SEE -> HEAR -> TRACE -> WRITE -> CHECK -> USE IN WORD -> FIND IN SCRIPTURE -> READ -> INVESTIGATE

## Publishing bridge
BOOK -> SCAN/TAP -> AR -> WATCH -> LIVING SCROLL -> HEBREW -> SOURCE -> MISSION -> SCROLLVERSE -> RETURN TO BOOK

Products include manga, comics, graphic novels, Hebrew and Paleo-Hebrew workbooks, coloring books, activity/sticker/puzzle books, teacher guides, homeschool editions, Family Sabbath guides, audio editions and interactive books.

## First acceptance test
A user can open the Living Scroll pilot, select Genesis 1:1, open language study, see source/provenance labels, complete one learning interaction, and persist completion state. AR/VR/MR entry points may initially be adapters/placeholders but must not falsely claim a reconstruction is literal history.

## Expansion after pilot
Genesis -> Torah (Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy) -> approved 88-book curriculum registry. The exact 88-book registry must be explicitly defined and source-reviewed rather than assumed to be a universally agreed Ethiopian canon count.

## Seven Lights connection
Seven Lights remains the entertainment canon. Ari’el can direct viewers to Explore the Scripture, but Living Scroll must visibly transition out of fiction before presenting Scripture or historical study.
