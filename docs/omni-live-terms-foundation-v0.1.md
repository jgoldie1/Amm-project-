# TRYAMM Omni LIVE Terms Foundation v0.1

This document is an engineering/product policy baseline and must receive legal review before public real-money launch.

## Core operating rules
- External streaming services remain independent. TRYAMM does not control their uptime, APIs, fees, moderation, payouts, refunds, or policies.
- An external gift, tip, subscription, or monetization event does not become a TRYAMM balance merely because TRYAMM displays or reacts to a verified event.
- Omni PK points are engagement scores, not cash, deposits, stored value, or guaranteed prizes.
- Material rule changes must be versioned and users should be notified when legally or operationally required.
- Recording, replay, clipping, and Reel capture require the applicable creator/user consent.
- Youth and adult experiences must remain separated with age-appropriate defaults and controls.
- Users must have access to report, block, mute, moderation, and appeal/support pathways appropriate to the product surface.
- Copyright, trademark, impersonation, harassment, fraud, exploitation, illegal content, and evasion of platform/payment controls are prohibited.
- AI translation, moderation, captioning, and generation can make mistakes; original source content should remain available when practical.
- Platform availability and feature support can vary by country, account status, API approval, and third-party policy.

## Sections required before public launch
Eligibility and accounts; youth accounts; acceptable use; creator content/license; IP complaints; LIVE/multistreaming; third-party platforms; Omni PK; gifts and virtual items; creator earnings; purchases/refunds; sponsorship/ads; marketplace; recording/Reels; AI/translation; moderation; privacy/data handling; suspension/termination; service availability; disputes; liability; governing law; updates; contact/support.

## Launch configuration
Initial priority: TRYAMM, YouTube, Twitch, custom RTMP. Other adapters are added only when their specific broadcast/chat/event/monetization capabilities are technically and contractually verified.
