# YAHAVAH Way Grocery + Beauty Supply Network

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT / BUSINESS ARCHITECTURE

## 1. YAHAVAH Way Grocery
A distributed grocery and household essentials network integrated with TRYAMM Marketplace, Supplier Exchange, Holo Fridge, Holo Delivery, Jin Pay, Money Engine, Community Circulation and Platform Sustainability Engine.

### Revenue streams
- optional consumer membership
- grocery retail margin or marketplace commission
- supplier/vendor plans
- sponsored placements and campaigns
- Holo Coupons
- Holo Delivery/service fees
- Group Buy coordination fees where lawful and disclosed
- private-label margin
- business/community bulk purchasing plans
- premium Business JARVIS analytics for merchants

### Customer modes
- ordinary grocery shopping
- budget-first shopping
- family/group order
- household replenishment from Holo Fridge inventory
- local/community-business discovery
- sponsor-funded meal/grocery credits with restricted-use controls
- community fridge/pantry resource navigation

### Supplier loop
Supplier Exchange → distributor/farm/manufacturer → YAHAVAH Grocery merchant/network → customer/group buyer → Holo Delivery → Money Engine → Community Circulation metrics.

### Holo Fridge integration
Household inventory can suggest restock lists, expiration-aware meal planning and lower-waste purchases. AI suggestions are advisory and must not invent food-safety or allergy facts.

## 2. African American Beauty Supply + Wig Marketplace
A dedicated discovery and commerce lane for hair, wigs, extensions, barber/beauty supplies, skincare, accessories and related services from verified sellers and brands.

### Product categories
- wigs and lace systems
- braiding hair and extensions
- natural-hair products
- barber supplies
- salon/esthetician supplies
- skincare and beauty accessories
- bonnets, scarves and protective-care items
- professional tools and equipment

### Holographic commerce
- Holo Try-On where technically supportable
- AR wig/hair-style preview
- 3D product rotation
- LIVE shopping and tutorials
- creator affiliate/storefront integrations
- before/after demonstrations with consent and disclosure

AI previews are visual simulations, not guarantees of real-world fit, color, texture or clinical effect.

### Service marketplace
Optional listings for licensed/qualified hairstylists, barbers, estheticians and other providers where local rules require credentials. Booking, deposits, cancellations and reviews flow through the same commerce stack.

### Revenue streams
- product margin/commission
- seller subscriptions
- sponsored placement
- Holo Coupon campaigns
- affiliate/creator commissions
- Holo Delivery/package fees
- service-booking platform fees
- professional bulk/salon plans
- private-label products
- Supplier Exchange vendor plans

## 3. Private Label / House Brands
Future YAHAVAH Way and TRYAMM house brands may cover eligible grocery staples, household goods, personal-care and beauty products after supplier, labeling, safety, trademark and regulatory review.

Do not market ingestible, cosmetic, hair-growth, medical or therapeutic claims beyond verified/legal labeling.

## 4. Group Buy
Families, churches, schools, businesses, salons, community groups and organizations can combine demand for eligible goods.

CREATE GROUP → COMBINE QUANTITY → SUPPLIER QUOTES → COMPARE → PARTICIPANTS APPROVE → JIN PAY → FULFILLMENT → DELIVERY/PICKUP.

No hidden forced purchasing; pricing, minimums and fees are disclosed before commitment.

## 5. Accessibility
Both Grocery and Beauty inherit Accessibility Passport: one-handed mode, voice control, large targets/text, keyboard/switch navigation, captions/transcripts, high contrast and reduced motion.

## 6. Business creation pathway
A learner or seller can progress:
ACADEMY/LEARNING PASSPORT → BUSINESS PASSPORT → FORMATION/EIN PATH → DOMAIN/DNS → STUBBS HARMONY → MARKETPLACE → SUPPLIER EXCHANGE → JARVIS → JIN PAY → HOLO DELIVERY → HR → COMPANY DIGITAL TWIN → BUSINESS PULSE.

## 7. Profitability / Sustainability contribution
Every revenue event is classified by Money Engine. Platform Sustainability Engine tracks eligible TRYAMM revenue versus variable infrastructure/provider cost.

Target: 3.00× sustainability ratio over measured infrastructure cost, without counting creator earnings, taxes, restricted mission funds, supplier settlements, refunds/reserves or other liabilities as TRYAMM revenue.

### Example contribution engines
GROCERY: memberships + margin/commission + supplier plans + ads + coupons + delivery + private label + group buy.
BEAUTY: product margin/commission + seller plans + services + sponsored placements + delivery + creator commerce + private label.
