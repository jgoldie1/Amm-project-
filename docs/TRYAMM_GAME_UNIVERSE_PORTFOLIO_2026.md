# TRYAMM Game Universe Portfolio — Canonical Vision 2026

Status: CANONICAL PORTFOLIO VISION

## Rule
All game families must use original names, characters, art, worlds, audio, mechanics expression and story. Genre inspiration is allowed; copying protected franchises, characters, monsters, maps, logos, dialogue, music or distinctive presentation is not.

## Shared foundation
TRYAMM Passport -> HoloGPT -> Match/Party -> Game Mode -> World State -> Timeline -> Reels/Live -> Creator/Commerce -> Entitlement/Ledger.

The portfolio connects to StreetVerse, StarVerse, MiddleVerse, SpaceVerse, Multiverse and QuantumVerse while keeping each game understandable as its own experience.

## 1. Quantum Tag
A signature non-lethal spatial competition built for phone, AR, VR and mixed reality. Players tag, capture, defend and traverse energy zones/portals. Modes may include solo, teams, territory, relay, stealth-tag, time-shift tag and SpaceVerse arenas. Accessibility alternatives must support seated/limited-motion play and configurable controls.

Monetization candidates: cosmetics, arena themes, seasonal passes, creator arenas, sponsored tournaments, spectator events and funded competitions. Never sell competitive power that destroys fair play.

## 2. Holographic Battle Decks
Original collectible strategy/battle system. Players build decks of original creatures, heroes, constructs, locations, effects and tactical abilities; cards/units can appear as holographic/spatial pieces in AR/MR/VR.

Core loop: COLLECT/UNLOCK -> BUILD DECK -> BATTLE -> RANK -> EARN COSMETIC/PROGRESSION -> CREATE/TRADE ONLY UNDER APPROVED ECONOMY RULES -> TOURNAMENT.

Use original IP only. Do not copy Yu-Gi-Oh!, Pokemon, Magic or anime franchises. Randomized paid items/loot mechanics require age/region/legal review; default toward transparent direct-purchase and earnable content.

## 3. Anime-inspired original battle universe
Create a wholly original animation-inspired combat world with TRYAMM-owned characters, factions, powers, lore, transformation language and visual identity. No borrowed anime characters, signature attacks, names, costumes or story worlds.

Modes: story campaign, co-op, arena, tournament, boss encounters, StarVerse animated performances and creator-made approved challenges.

## 4. Paranormal Response Unit
Original AR/MR paranormal investigation and containment game inspired by the broad ghost-hunting genre, not any specific franchise. Phone camera/AR reveals fictional anomalies; players scan evidence, solve environmental puzzles, stabilize portals and contain entities using original equipment and lore.

Loop: ALERT -> TRAVEL/PORTAL -> SCAN -> INVESTIGATE -> IDENTIFY -> CONTAIN -> CASE FILE -> REEL -> WORLD CONSEQUENCE.

No Ghostbusters names, characters, logos, proton packs, ghosts or copied presentation.

## 5. Global Conflict / WWIII fictional strategy-action universe
Original fictional near/future conflict experience, not Call of Duty. Keep countries/factions/scenarios fictionalized where useful and separate entertainment from real-world operational instruction. Modes may include campaign, squad objectives, rescue, defense, logistics, drones as abstract game systems, cyber-puzzle simulation, diplomacy/strategy and large-team competitive play.

No copied Call of Duty maps, operators, UI, killstreaks, branding, storylines or assets. Avoid real-world instructional weapon construction, evasion or attack planning.

## 6. Gremlin-style creature chaos -> original Mischief Creatures universe
Replace any franchise-specific gremlin identity with original TRYAMM creatures, rules, names and visual design. Gameplay: creatures multiply/evolve under fictional conditions, invade homes/businesses/StreetVerse districts, sabotage systems, hide, cooperate or become companions. Comedy + chaos + collection + community defense.

## 7. Volcano Game System
Original dynamic disaster/world-event engine centered on volcanoes and expandable to earthquakes, storms, floods and other simulated hazards. Volcano states: dormant -> unrest -> warning -> eruption -> evacuation/rescue -> aftermath -> rebuilding -> ecological/economic recovery.

Connect Fire/EMS, StreetVerse News, businesses, transport, family, shelters, Reels, HoloGPT and Timeline/Butterfly consequences. Real hazard education must use reliable sources; fictional forecasts must be labeled simulation.

## Economy guardrails
Real-money checkout must be provider-hosted/verified. One transaction -> one authoritative receipt -> entitlement -> ledger entry. Refund/fraud/tax/payout rules must be certified before real-money creator payouts.

Holo Credits and virtual game currencies are utility/game systems, not promises of investment profit. Do not market virtual assets as securities or guaranteed appreciation.

Creator payouts require rights verification, eligibility and authoritative settlement records.

## Portfolio revenue principle
Do not measure only purchase price. Optimize lawful lifetime value through retention, cross-world discovery and creator/business participation without pay-to-win exploitation.

PLAYER -> GAME -> REEL/LIVE -> ANOTHER GAME/WORLD -> CREATOR ASSET -> EVENT -> MEMBERSHIP/COMMERCE -> RETURN.

## Impact connection
After contractual creator/player obligations and the legally/accountingly defined allocation base, approved TRYAMM allocations may support the Player Business Launch Fund, Storehouse, Saving the World, qualified charity and Servants of Christ according to the separately adopted policy. Never calculate these from money that contractually belongs to creators or from gross receipts without the final accounting rule.
