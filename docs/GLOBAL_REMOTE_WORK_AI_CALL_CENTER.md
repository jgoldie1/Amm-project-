# TRYAMM Global Remote Work + AI Call Center

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT / EMPLOYMENT / REVENUE SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Create a global work-from-home pathway where people can train, qualify, work, supervise, sell, support customers and build careers from home while Stubbs AI/JARVIS assists rather than replacing human accountability.

## Core lanes
- customer service
- inbound sales
- appointment setting
- order support
- marketplace seller support
- Holo Delivery support
- technical support
- billing support
- education/tutoring support
- accessibility support
- creator/artist support
- business onboarding
- grant/opportunity navigation support
- multilingual support
- community-resource navigation

Regulated work such as legal advice, medical advice, insurance sales, securities activity, tax preparation, debt collection or other licensed functions must route only to appropriately licensed/authorized people or providers.

## Worker journey
APPLY → SKILLS/ACCESSIBILITY PROFILE → TRAINING → SIMULATED CALLS → QUALITY CHECK → ELIGIBLE QUEUE → REMOTE SHIFT/GIG → SUPERVISION → PAY/EARNINGS RECORD → LEARNING PASSPORT → PROMOTION/SPECIALIZATION.

## Worker JARVIS
Each worker can receive a scoped JARVIS that can:
- prepare the next case
- summarize authorized customer history
- surface approved scripts/knowledge
- translate where permitted
- suggest responses
- detect missing information
- create follow-up tasks
- flag escalation conditions
- track training and performance goals

JARVIS may not fabricate policy, silently promise refunds/credits, impersonate a licensed professional or disclose data outside the worker's permission scope.

## AI + human routing
Use a tiered queue:
1. SELF-SERVICE JARVIS — FAQs and simple account navigation.
2. AI-ASSISTED HUMAN — worker handles customer with real-time JARVIS support.
3. SPECIALIST — trained/authorized worker.
4. LICENSED/REGULATED PROVIDER — only where credentials/jurisdiction permit.
5. SUPERVISOR / SAFETY / FRAUD — escalated cases.

Goal: automation handles repetitive work while humans handle judgment, empathy, exceptions and regulated decisions.

## Global workforce architecture
Workers may participate only where TRYAMM/employer structure, labor classification, payroll/payment, tax, privacy and data-transfer requirements permit. Maintain country/region eligibility, work authorization where applicable, language, timezone, pay basis, currency, accessibility needs and equipment requirements.

Do not label everyone an independent contractor merely to avoid employment obligations. Classification follows real working relationship and local law.

## Accessibility-first remote work
Integrate Accessibility Passport:
- screen-reader compatible console
- one-handed operation
- keyboard/switch navigation
- captions/transcripts
- speech-to-text/text-to-speech
- adjustable font/contrast
- reduced motion
- flexible communication modes
- alternative non-voice queues where available

Accessibility settings never reduce opportunity ranking by default.

- resolution quality
- policy accuracy
- customer feedback

- escalation correctness
- privacy/security compliance

- training completion

Allow coaching, correction and appeals for consequential employment decisions.

## Anti-spam / telecom safety
Outbound calling/messaging must respect consent, do-not-call requirements, calling-time rules, caller-ID rules, recording consent, AI/voice disclosure requirements and applicable telecom/consumer-protection law.

Default growth strategy: inbound support, opt-in leads, existing-customer service, requested callbacks and permissioned outreach—not mass unsolicited robocalling.

The Platform Sustainability Engine tracks CALL_CENTER revenue against:
- worker compensation
- telephony/minutes
- AI inference
- transcription/translation
- CRM/storage
- SMS/email
- supervision/QA
- security/compliance
- support/admin

Target remains 3.00× platform sustainability overall; the call-center line should also maintain positive contribution margin before rapid scaling.

## Example economics
Do not hard-code promises. Example only:
CLIENT PAYS $30/hour → direct worker/provider/telecom/AI cost $20/hour → $10/hour contribution before shared overhead.

At 10,000 productive hours/month, that example yields $100,000 contribution before shared overhead. Actual pricing varies by geography, skill, licensing, language, SLA and service complexity.

## Community / employment effect
Student JARVIS or Launchpad can move a person through:
TRAIN → REMOTE WORK → INCOME → FINANCIAL LITERACY → SPECIALIZE → SUPERVISE → START BUSINESS → HIRE/MENTOR OTHERS.

This creates a direct employment engine for disabled workers, caregivers, parents, rural workers, returning citizens where lawful, students/adults seeking flexible work and global multilingual talent.
