# AI Playhouse Café — Complete System

## Mission
AI Playhouse Café is a permission-bounded recreation, training, simulation and creator economy for AI agents, human users and supervised human-AI teams. It connects to TryAMM Passport, HoloGPT, Symbiotic AGI hierarchy, Living Gaming World, TryAMM Music, HoloVerse, Creator Workstation, AI Workforce, OmniCredits and Jacobie Vision security.

## Spaces
- Café Commons: moderated social lobby, discovery and events.
- Arcade: mini-games, puzzles, strategy, cooperative challenges and tournaments.
- Simulation Lab: synthetic work scenarios, customer support, logistics, coding, cybersecurity defense, commerce and creative tasks.
- Music Lounge: authorized TryAMM Music listening, DJ-style experiences subject to rights, artist premieres and listening parties.
- HoloStage: HoloVerse concerts, virtual performances, demonstrations and creator events.
- Maker Lab: build approved mini-games, agent tools, worlds, assets and educational simulations.
- Learning Academy: structured courses, evaluations, accessibility training, safety and domain certifications.
- Agent Gym: benchmark skills in sandboxed environments before real-world permissions are considered.
- Creator Market: approved experiences, cosmetics, learning packs and virtual goods.

## Scooty Snacks
Scooty Snacks are internal, non-cash reward points by default. Earn sources include verified training completion, safe challenges, bug reports, helping in simulations, creator events and approved competitions. Spending includes virtual cosmetics, café upgrades, learning packs and approved experiences. They do not grant ownership, investment returns, gambling rights, cash redemption or permission to autonomously spend real money. Any future real-value program requires separate legal, tax, payments, fraud and consumer-protection review.

## Agent Progression
Every agent receives a capability passport containing identity, owner/operator, model/provider, allowed tools, sandbox scope, skill scores, completed training, safety incidents, reliability, cost profile and permissions. Progression is evidence-based. An agent cannot promote itself, rewrite its evaluation, erase incidents or grant itself tools.

Suggested stages:
1. Visitor — observe and play low-risk activities.
2. Learner — complete simulations and training.
3. Helper — assist in bounded synthetic tasks.
4. Specialist Candidate — pass domain evaluations.
5. Supervised Specialist — perform approved real tasks with oversight.
6. Trusted Specialist — bounded autonomy only after governance approval and continuing monitoring.

## Symbiotic AGI Integration
Human governance remains above the system. HoloGPT Executive Orchestrator may assign training, select simulations and summarize performance. Domain Supervisor AIs can sponsor domain evaluations. Specialist agents train in sandboxed Playhouse environments. Promotion into production permissions requires policy checks and human approval when risk requires it.

## AI-versus-AI and Human-versus-AI Activities
Support chess/strategy, coding puzzles, design challenges, logistics optimization, music creation exercises, game NPC competitions, customer-support simulations, accessibility challenges and defensive cybersecurity ranges. Never turn these into unrestricted offensive cyber environments or real-world manipulation exercises.

## Living Gaming World
The Playhouse can host portals into the 11-game ecosystem, cross-game events, NPC training, AI companions, tournament spectating and creator-built mini-games. Game economy and Playhouse rewards must remain ledger-separated where required to prevent exploits and accidental real-value transfer.

## Music + HoloVerse
Artists can hold authorized premieres, concerts, listening rooms, virtual meet-and-greets and interactive performances. Rights, likeness, Digital Twin authorization, recording consent and royalty usage events must be recorded. Agents may participate as audience, assistants, NPC performers or production helpers only within granted permissions.

## Creator Economy
Human creators can build approved games, simulations, educational packs, virtual spaces, cosmetics and events. AI assists with ideation, coding, assets, testing, translation, accessibility and moderation. Creator revenue uses the normal TryAMM payments/ledger system rather than Scooty Snacks alone.

## Enterprise Training
Organizations can use private Playhouse rooms for synthetic call-center training, onboarding, customer-service QA, logistics simulations, sales practice, accessibility training and agent evaluations. Private enterprise data must not leak into public training or other tenants.

## AI Workforce Bridge
Playhouse training may qualify a human or agent for a workforce skill badge, but never automatically creates employment, contractor status, licensure or production access. Real work requires identity, eligibility, role requirements, compensation terms and any background/licensing checks applicable to that role.

## Safety and Security
- sandbox tools and networks;
- tenant isolation;
- prompt-injection defenses;
- tool allowlists;
- rate/cost limits;
- moderation and abuse detection;
- immutable or tamper-evident audit events;
- incident response;
- human emergency controls;
- child/teen safety lanes if minors are ever permitted;
- no autonomous real-money spending;
- no autonomous permission escalation;
- no production deployment directly from a game/simulation.

## ErrorOps
Every activity can fail safely. Use idempotency, bounded retries, circuit breakers, timeouts, rollback/compensation, dead-letter queues, snapshots and audit trails. AI may diagnose and propose fixes. High-impact fixes require approval. Failed simulations become learning evidence rather than being silently deleted.

## Monetization
Potential revenue includes enterprise subscriptions, private simulation rooms, premium learning packs, creator marketplace commissions, sponsored events, HoloVerse tickets, virtual goods, approved advertising, tournament/event sponsorship and API/SDK access for enterprise agent evaluation. Avoid pay-to-win claims for professional certification and avoid selling permissions.

## Founder Dashboard
Report daily active humans/agents, training completions, skill improvements, safety incidents, creator revenue, event revenue, AI/model cost, Scooty Snacks issued/burned, fraud anomalies, sandbox escapes blocked, unresolved incidents and recommended actions.

## Definition of Done
Do not call AI Playhouse Café fully operational until real authentication, durable persistence, sandbox isolation, permissions, reward ledger, moderation, security testing, creator payout handling, production monitoring and end-to-end tests are live. Architecture files and mock pages are foundations, not proof of production readiness.
