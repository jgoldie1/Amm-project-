# TRYAMM Campus Media Role + Revenue Lock

## Campus role
Create a flexible paid role for Jacobie Stubbs while attending Greenville University: **Campus Media & Cybersecurity Ambassador**.

The role is structured around school first. Work is opt-in, asynchronous where possible, and should be capped by an agreed weekly schedule. Compensation must be documented through payroll or contractor processes as legally appropriate; do not disguise compensation as prizes or Holo Credits.

### Paid work lanes
- Campus creator outreach and creator onboarding.
- Film/reel product-placement inventory tagging and campaign QA.
- Holographic/3D advertising campaign QA across StreetVerse, SpaceVerse/Time Machine, LIVE, Reels/Omni Box, GameVerse, marketplace, music, TV/Drama Box and creator channels.
- Cybersecurity hygiene checks, authorized QA and incident/report triage; no unauthorized access or offensive security activity.
- Sponsor campaign reporting and placement verification.
- Accessibility and mobile-device QA.
- Event/athletics content coordination only where permissions and rights are secured.

### Compensation architecture
- Base hourly/project compensation from TRYAMM operating payroll/contractor budget.
- Optional documented performance bonuses from TRYAMM's platform share of successfully settled campaigns.
- Never pay wages from customer custodial balances, creator liabilities, restricted Get Paid to Play reserves, taxes, refunds, chargeback reserves or provider settlements.

## New revenue lanes
### Product placement
Paid placements in licensed/authorized TRYAMM movies, reels, creator productions, TV/Drama Box programming and game/world experiences. Every placement needs campaign ID, advertiser, creative approval, placement location, disclosure requirements, contracted price, delivery proof and settlement record.

### Holographic and 3D advertising
Premium inventory includes holographic banners, spatial pop-ups, interactive 3D product experiences, virtual billboards, branded locations, sponsored missions, world takeovers, creator integrations and event sponsorships.

### Drone-enabled media
Drone inventory is limited to lawful, authorized physical aerial capture/inspection/delivery-support workflows and simulated/in-world drone experiences. No claim that a physical drone is literally invisible. Any low-visibility/stealth concept remains a simulation/design concept unless independently verified and legally authorized. Physical operations require applicable aviation, privacy, property, safety and insurance controls.

## Authoritative settlement waterfall
ONE TRANSACTION -> ONE PAYMENT/SETTLEMENT ID -> ONE CAMPAIGN/ORDER -> ONE SETTLEMENT RECORD -> ONE AUTHORITATIVE WATERFALL.

1. Gross settled revenue.
2. Taxes and payment/provider fees.
3. Refund, dispute and chargeback reserves.
4. Contractual creator/rightsholder/merchant/provider obligations.
5. Restricted campaign obligations and funded Get Paid to Play allocation.
6. Infrastructure and operating protection.
7. Growth/marketing and contingency reserves.
8. Eligible distributable platform surplus.
9. Pending verified surplus policies, including Servants of Christ and Kenosha Shelton legacy allocations, only when legally/accounting approved and active.
10. Remaining TRYAMM retained surplus/reserves.

## Hard invariants
- No dollar is allocated twice.
- Holo Credits are closed-loop platform credits, not wages, deposits, investments or guaranteed cash redemption.
- Get Paid to Play cannot commit or pay more real money than its restricted funded reserve.
- Creator/player/customer money cannot fund legacy or ministry distributions.
- Legacy/ministry allocations apply only to the defined eligible distributable platform surplus and remain inactive while verification is pending.
- Product-placement and advertising revenue is not recognized as distributable merely because an invoice was issued; use verified settled revenue.
- Campaign delivery proof and refunds/disputes must reconcile before final surplus distribution.

## Recommended financial controls
- Minimum operating runway gate before discretionary surplus distributions.
- Campaign-level contribution margin and CAC/ROAS reporting.
- Reward-reserve coverage ratio >= 1.0 before cash reward activation.
- Creator/rightsholder payable coverage ratio >= 1.0 at all times.
- Separate restricted-reserve and operating-cash reporting.
- Monthly settlement reconciliation and immutable audit reference per posting.
- Sponsor concentration limit so one advertiser cannot become a critical single point of failure.
- Dynamic Holo Ad pricing based on reach, placement, duration, world, creator and verified engagement, with a contractual floor price.
- Frequency caps and brand-safety controls so monetization does not overwhelm the user experience.

## Platform effect
This architecture adds revenue without promising payouts before funding exists. Product placement, Holo/3D ads, sponsorships and creator campaigns can finance infrastructure, paid campus work, growth and restricted skill/creator reward pools. The authoritative waterfall prevents those uses from raiding creator/customer liabilities or double-spending the same settlement.
