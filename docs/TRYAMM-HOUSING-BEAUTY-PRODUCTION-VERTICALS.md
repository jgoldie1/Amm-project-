# TryAMM Housing, Beauty, and Production Verticals

## Purpose
Expand TryAMM into a connected marketplace for housing, beauty services, property, photography, and video production while keeping regulated activities behind verified partners and lawful workflows.

### Voucher workflow
- User creates a Wallet Passport profile
- User may upload voucher documents into encrypted storage
- User grants explicit consent for approved landlords or housing navigators to view required fields
- System extracts only necessary data such as issuing authority, bedroom size, expiration, and portability status
- Application checklist and document status are tracked
- TryAMM does not promise approval or represent a housing authority
- Property owners must follow fair-housing and local source-of-income rules where applicable

## 6. Required Safeguards
- Do not claim Housing Choice Voucher approval authority
- Do not expose voucher documents publicly
- Do not allow discriminatory listing filters or steering
- Verify professional licenses where required
- Require releases for commercial photography and video
- Keep professional fees, platform fees, taxes, refunds, and partner payouts separately recorded
- Use local counsel and compliance review before regulated launches

## Strategic Effect
These verticals transform TryAMM from a collection of listings into a connected local-commerce operating system. Housing creates recurring user need, beauty creates frequent transactions, and photography/video provides the content engine that markets every other business on the platform.
