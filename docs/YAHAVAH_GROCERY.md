# YAHAVAH Grocery

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## Vision
YAHAVAH Grocery is a digital-first, location-light grocery network designed to compete on convenience, membership value, community sourcing, bulk buying, Holo Fridge intelligence, and flexible fulfillment without requiring TRYAMM to own a traditional supermarket building in every market.

## Core model
APP/WEB/HOLO STORE → CATALOG → MEMBERSHIP/BULK PRICING → HOLO FRIDGE → CART → HOLO COUPON → JIN PAY → FULFILLMENT ROUTER → PICKUP/DELIVERY/PARTNER WAREHOUSE/DARK STORE/LOCAL SUPPLIER → LIVE TRACKING → RECEIPT → FRIDGE UPDATE.

## Location-light fulfillment
YAHAVAH Grocery may operate through a mix of:
- supplier-direct fulfillment
- partner warehouses/3PLs
- approved dark stores/micro-fulfillment centers
- participating local grocers
- farms/co-ops
- community pickup hubs
- ghost-inventory partnerships
- scheduled bulk drops
- Holo Delivery couriers
- approved third-party parcel/cold-chain providers

Do not advertise physical inventory or same-day availability unless an authoritative inventory source confirms it.

## Membership concept
Free tier: browse, standard pricing, basic Holo Fridge and standard offers.
Plus tier: enhanced bulk pricing, member-only Holo Coupons, delivery benefits subject to economics, family/shared carts and larger AI allowance.
Business/Community tier: bulk purchasing, purchasing circles, supplier pricing, scheduled delivery windows and community/group orders.

Membership economics are dynamically checked by Platform Sustainability Engine; benefits that lose money at scale must be capped, repriced or funded by suppliers/sponsors.

## Better-than-warehouse-club differentiation
- No required giant physical warehouse visit.
- Household-size and bulk-size purchasing in one catalog.
- Split-a-case / group-buy capability where supplier rules permit.
- AI-assisted meal planning based on user-authorized Holo Fridge inventory.
- Holo preview of products, package sizes and storage needs.
- automatic household inventory decrement/replenishment suggestions.
- supplier/local/community discovery.
- scheduled recurring essentials.
- sponsor/community meal credits.
- accessibility-first ordering.
- family/caregiver delegated ordering permissions.
- multilingual JARVIS ordering support.

## Holo Fridge integration
Personal Holo Fridge stores user-authorized inventory, quantity estimates, expiration/quality reminders, favorites, dietary preferences and reorder thresholds.

Commands:
- `JARVIS, what can I cook tonight?`
- `What am I running low on?`
- `Build a grocery cart under $75.`
- `Don't reorder anything I already have.`
- `Find lower-cost alternatives.`
- `Split this bulk order with my group.`

AI does not make medical nutrition claims unless backed by qualified/approved sources.

## Holo Pantry + household inventory
Extend Holo Fridge to pantry, freezer, household supplies, diapers/baby needs, paper goods and cleaning supplies. Users can scan receipts/barcodes or manually update stock. Camera/computer-vision features remain opt-in.

## Group Buy / Purchasing Circle
Users, families, churches, schools, neighborhoods and businesses may combine demand for eligible non-restricted items:
CREATE GROUP → TARGET PRODUCT/QTY → PARTICIPANT COMMITMENTS → SUPPLIER QUOTE → FINAL PRICE → PARTICIPANT APPROVAL → JIN PAY → BULK FULFILLMENT → PICKUP/DELIVERY.

No automatic binding purchase until participant approval/payment requirements are satisfied.

## Supplier network
Supplier Exchange feeds YAHAVAH Grocery with verified farms, manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors, co-ops and local businesses. Track certifications/claims only when documented. Never label a business as certified merely from self-description.

## Private label / YAHAVAH Select
Future private-label products may be sourced through vetted manufacturers. Product safety, labeling, allergen, nutrition, recall and regulatory requirements remain mandatory.

## Community food pathway
YAHAVAH Grocery integrates with Holo Fridge Share and Emergency Path without publicly labeling recipients.

SPONSOR/MISSION FUNDS → RESTRICTED MEAL/GROCERY CREDIT → ELIGIBLE ORDER → FULFILLMENT → PRIVACY-PRESERVING IMPACT REPORT.

## Delivery and cold chain
Fulfillment router can classify ambient, refrigerated, frozen and fragile goods. Provider eligibility depends on cold-chain capability, distance, weather, timing and food-safety controls.

## Revenue model
Potential eligible TRYAMM revenue sources:
- membership
- disclosed grocery margin/commission
- supplier marketplace/service fees
- sponsored placement clearly labeled
- Holo Coupon campaigns
- Holo Delivery/service fees
- group-buy service fee
- private-label margin
- business/community bulk plan
- AI Actions/HoloGPT Credits for premium planning/rendering

Supplier/customer funds owed elsewhere remain separate from TRYAMM revenue in Money Engine.

## Sustainability requirement
Every benefit must report contribution economics. Target platform self-support ratio remains 3.00× at the consolidated TRYAMM level, while YAHAVAH Grocery should independently track contribution margin, fulfillment cost/order, acquisition cost, repeat rate, spoilage/refund rate, supplier margin and delivery subsidy.

## Business JARVIS / Company Digital Twin
YAHAVAH Grocery Digital Twin tracks catalog, suppliers, inventory feeds, orders, member cohorts, fulfillment partners, coupons, delivery capacity, pricing, margin, risk, food-safety/recall alerts and Community Gap Finder signals.

Business Simulator can test:
- free delivery thresholds
- membership pricing
- bulk discount levels
- new supplier economics
- local micro-fulfillment launch
- private-label introduction
- group-buy programs
- sponsor-funded grocery missions
