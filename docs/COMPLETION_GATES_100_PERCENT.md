## Revenue completion gate
The app is not economically complete until production telemetry can calculate:
`Sustainability Ratio = eligible platform revenue / measured platform infrastructure cost`.

Milestones:
- <1.00x = subsidized
- 1.00–1.24x = break-even
- 1.25–1.99x = self-supporting
- 2.00–2.99x = reserve-building
- >=3.00x = strategic sustainability goal achieved

Also require positive contribution margin by major revenue product after direct variable costs.
