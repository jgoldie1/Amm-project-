# Nigeria Priority Launch Program

Nigeria is promoted into the active priority international build track. This document does not mark Nigeria production-ready; every service remains gated by evidence, provider approval, legal review, and successful pilot testing.

## Launch objective

Build a Nigeria-specific TryAMM environment that supports creators, music, media, stores, holographic advertising, education, workforce services, and approved cross-border commerce through shared platform-kernel services.

## Initial service order

### Stage 1 — sandbox foundation
- Nigeria country profile
- NGN display and accounting support
- English localization
- Nigeria timezone and address formats
- Seller and business-verification records
- Nigeria-specific age, privacy, consumer, content, advertising, and tax review placeholders
- Partner approval registry
- Audit logging and launch-gate enforcement

### Stage 2 — creator and media pilot
- Short video and livestreaming
- Creator profiles and subscriptions
- Music streaming and TryAMM Records Nigeria
- Isaiah AI TV Nigeria
- University and HBCU/African education programming
- AMM Billboard Showcase using TryAMM-owned inventory
- Creator campaign compensation up to the approved participation cap, without platform ownership

### Stage 3 — business and marketplace pilot
- Nigeria seller onboarding
- Store Builder
- Holo Menu
- Digital products and services
- Holographic showroom conversion
- Local business advertising
- Product eligibility and restricted-product screening
- Customer support and dispute workflows

### Stage 4 — workforce and education pilot
- Middleverse Workforce Nigeria
- AI-assisted call-center training
- Customer support, moderation, translation, catalog management, design, software, cybersecurity, and media-production work
- All American University entrepreneurship, export-readiness, cybersecurity, AI, music-production, creator-business, and marketplace training

### Stage 5 — approved cross-border trade
- United States ↔ Nigeria
- Nigeria ↔ Ghana
- Nigeria ↔ Canada
- Nigeria ↔ United Kingdom only after it receives a separate country profile and corridor approval

## Required launch gates

Nigeria cannot move beyond sandbox until evidence exists for:

1. Country-specific legal review
2. Privacy and data-processing review
3. Consumer-protection and refund rules
4. Age-lane, youth-safety, and content rules
5. Payment collection and seller settlement provider approval
6. Tax and invoicing configuration
7. Product restrictions and marketplace policy
8. Advertising and influencer-disclosure rules
9. Cross-border customs and logistics providers
10. Cybersecurity review
11. Accessibility and localization testing
12. Customer support and incident response
13. Successful pilot transactions and reconciliations
14. Executive launch sign-off

## Technical requirements

- Country profile service reads Nigeria rules dynamically.
- Production traffic is blocked for unapproved services.
- Payments and settlements use approved external providers.
- Wallet balances remain separated into purchased, promotional, and creator-earnings balances.
- No creator available balance may become negative.
- Every payout, refund, reversal, and settlement must reconcile to a ledger transaction and audit event.
- Every trade order records seller jurisdiction, product origin, destination, classification, taxes, duties estimate, provider, carrier, and evidence references.

## Acceptance journey

An approved Nigerian seller signs in, completes business verification, opens a store, publishes a permitted digital or physical product, receives a compliant local price display, launches an approved livestream or holographic showcase, completes a pilot order through an approved provider, receives settlement, and produces complete audit and reconciliation records.

## Definition of launch-ready

Nigeria is launch-ready only when the required service-level gates are complete. Social viewing, creator tools, marketplace sales, payouts, physical trade, workforce services, and other regulated capabilities may each have different approval states.
