# TRYAMM Community Guardian / Safe Passage

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT + BUSINESS SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Create a lawful, non-vigilante community-safety network focused on prevention, safe passage, visible stewardship, de-escalation, youth engagement, incident routing, and rapid connection to appropriate services. This is not private policing and does not grant arrest, detention, search, interrogation, or law-enforcement authority.

## Core service lanes
- Safe Passage escorts for students, elders, disabled people, workers, creators, event attendees, and vulnerable travelers.
- Community Ambassador presence around schools, transit, business corridors, events, nightlife exits, and community programs where contracts/permissions exist.
- De-escalation and conflict interruption by appropriately trained personnel.
- Youth outreach and Teen Takeover alternatives through sports, music, gaming, trades, creator work, mentorship, jobs, and paid/eligible community missions.
- Lost Sheep / Emergency Path resource navigation.
- Community check-in points and verified safe businesses.
- Incident reporting and evidence-preservation pathways without public vigilantism.
- Holo Ride / Holo Delivery safety handoff for verified pickups/drop-offs.
- Accessibility-aware escort/request settings through Accessibility Passport.

## What Guardians may NOT do
- no impersonating police/security officers
- no weapons-based patrol model as the default TRYAMM program
- no unlawful detention, pursuit, search, seizure, interrogation, punishment, retaliation, or doxxing
- no gang/criminal labeling by algorithm
- no facial-recognition watchlists for ordinary community participation
- no public shaming maps of accused people
- no bounty or reward system for confronting suspects
- no encouragement to chase, corner, surround, or physically engage people except ordinary self-defense/emergency principles allowed by law

## Guardian roles
1. `Community Ambassador` — visible presence, directions, check-ins, resource connection.
2. `Safe Passage Guide` — scheduled escort/walk-with-me service.
3. `Youth Mentor` — opportunity, mentorship, recreation, school/career navigation.
4. `De-escalation Specialist` — trained conflict interruption where lawful and appropriate.
5. `Event Steward` — guest safety/navigation and escalation to licensed security/venue staff.
6. `Resource Navigator` — connects people to Emergency Path, shelters, transport, food, legal/social resources.
7. `Dispatcher` — receives requests, assigns qualified personnel, monitors check-ins and escalates emergencies.

Each role has training, screening, permission, geography, and service limits.

## Request flow
`REQUEST HELP / WALK WITH ME → LOCATION/DESTINATION → ACCESSIBILITY/COMMUNICATION NEEDS → RISK TRIAGE → ELIGIBLE GUARDIAN/PROVIDER → ACCEPT → CHECK-IN → SAFE ARRIVAL → CLOSE → OPTIONAL FEEDBACK`.

Imminent danger bypasses ordinary dispatch and directs the user toward emergency services/appropriate qualified responders.

## Safe Zone Network
Businesses, churches/ministries, schools, campuses, community centers, partner properties, and event venues can apply to become verified Safe Zones. Display verified facts only: open hours, accessibility, staff presence, phone, restroom/waiting availability if offered, and emergency escalation policy.

JARVIS does not make criminal determinations or instruct confrontations.

## Community Gap Finder
Aggregate, privacy-preserving signals can identify gaps such as:
- insufficient safe passage coverage after school
- transit-area safety concerns
- late-night worker escort demand
- youth opportunity shortage
- inaccessible safe routes
- insufficient lighting/property-maintenance reports (when sourced appropriately)
- shortage of trained de-escalation/community workers

Output: `GAP → EVIDENCE → PARTNERS → STAFFING/TRAINING → FUNDING → MILESTONES → OUTCOMES`.

## Profitability / revenue model
This can be a profitable social-enterprise line without charging vulnerable users for every safety interaction.

Potential payers:
- business improvement districts / commercial corridors
- schools, colleges, campuses, youth programs
- event venues and promoters
- property owners/managers
- hospitals/health systems for non-clinical escort/navigation programs
- employers with late-shift workforce programs
- transit-adjacent partners where lawful
- municipal/nonprofit contracts and grants
- business Safe Zone memberships
- sponsorships for youth/safe-passage programs
- optional household/family memberships for enhanced scheduled services
- enterprise dashboards/dispatch software licensing

Do not promise government/insurance reimbursement unless an actual contract/program permits it.

## Suggested pricing architecture
Pricing is a business-model starting point, not a claim of current market acceptance:
- Community Guardian software / Safe Zone business membership: $19–$49/mo per location.
- Business corridor / property dashboard: $99–$299/mo depending on locations/users/features.
- Scheduled ambassador contracts: priced from labor + insurance + training + supervision + software + target contribution margin.
- Event Safe Passage / Stewarding: contract/project pricing.
- Family scheduled Safe Passage Plus: optional subscription only where service capacity exists.
- Sponsored youth/community cohorts: fixed project budgets with measurable outcomes.

Target every paid contract to contribute positively after direct labor, insurance, travel, background screening, training, supervision, messaging/maps, and incident support.

## Sustainability link
Revenue category: `community_guardian_services` and `safe_zone_membership` feed the Platform Sustainability Engine only after provider/labor obligations and restricted funds are separated.

Target contribution calculation:
`eligible_platform_contribution = contract_revenue - direct_service_cost - provider_obligations - refunds/reserves`.

This protects the 3.00x platform sustainability goal from counting pass-through wages or restricted safety grants as free TRYAMM revenue.

## Outcomes dashboard
Track aggregate outcomes such as:
- safe-passage requests completed
- safe arrivals
- youth mentor matches
- opportunity referrals
- de-escalation calls resolved without injury where verified
- Safe Zones onboarded

Never publish a neighborhood/person `crime score` from unverified user reports.

## Training / governance
Before field deployment establish:
- code of conduct
- background screening appropriate to role and law
- de-escalation / trauma-informed training
- child safeguarding where minors are involved
- accessibility / disability etiquette
- emergency escalation protocol
- incident-reporting standard
- privacy/confidentiality standard
- insurance and worker classification review
- local licensing/security-law review to ensure activities do not cross into regulated private-security work
- supervision and complaint/appeal process

## Strategic effect
The business creates a prevention-to-opportunity loop:
`VISIBLE CARE → SAFER PASSAGE → TRUST → YOUTH/COMMUNITY CONNECTION → EDUCATION/JOBS/MENTORS → BUSINESS ACTIVITY → COMMUNITY INVESTMENT → MORE PREVENTION`.

The goal is to make people safer while creating legitimate jobs and contracts, not to create a civilian enforcement force.
