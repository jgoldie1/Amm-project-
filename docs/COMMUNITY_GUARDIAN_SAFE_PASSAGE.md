# TRYAMM Community Guardian / Safe Passage

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT + BUSINESS SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Create a lawful, non-vigilante community-safety network focused on prevention, safe passage, visible stewardship, de-escalation, youth engagement, incident routing, and rapid connection to appropriate services. This is not private policing and does not grant arrest, detention, search, interrogation, or law-enforcement authority.

## Core operating principle
PREVENT → DE-ESCALATE → CONNECT → DOCUMENT → REFER → FOLLOW UP.

## Core service lanes
- Safe Passage escorts for students, elders, disabled people, workers, creators, event attendees, and vulnerable travelers.
- Community Ambassador presence around schools, transit, business corridors, events, nightlife exits, and community programs where contracts/permissions exist.
- De-escalation and conflict interruption by appropriately trained personnel.
- Youth outreach and Teen Takeover alternatives through sports, music, gaming, trades, creator work, mentorship, jobs, and paid/eligible community missions.
- Lost Sheep / Emergency Path resource navigation.
- Community check-in points and verified safe businesses.
- Incident reporting and evidence-preservation pathways without public vigilantism.
- Holo Ride / Holo Delivery safety handoff for verified pickups/drop-offs.
- Accessibility-aware escort/request settings through Accessibility Passport.
- Lighting, blight, route-hazard and accessibility issue reporting to the appropriate owner/agency.
- Business corridor check-in routes and safe closing/opening assistance where lawful.

## What Guardians may NOT do
- no impersonating police/security officers
- no weapons-based patrol model as the default TRYAMM program
- no unlawful detention, pursuit, search, seizure, interrogation, punishment, retaliation, or doxxing
- no gang/criminal labeling by algorithm
- no facial-recognition watchlists for ordinary community participation
- no public shaming maps of accused people
- no bounty or reward system for confronting suspects
- no encouragement to chase, corner, surround, or physically engage people except ordinary self-defense/emergency principles allowed by law

## Guardian roles
1. `Community Ambassador` — visible presence, directions, check-ins, resource connection.
2. `Safe Passage Guide` — scheduled escort/walk-with-me service.
3. `Youth Mentor / Outreach Worker` — opportunity, mentorship, recreation, school/career navigation.
4. `De-escalation Specialist` — trained conflict interruption where lawful and appropriate.
5. `Event Steward` — guest safety/navigation and escalation to licensed security/venue staff.
6. `Resource Navigator` — connects people to Emergency Path, shelters, transport, food, legal/social resources.
7. `Dispatcher` — receives requests, assigns qualified personnel, monitors check-ins and escalates emergencies.
8. `Program Coordinator / Team Lead` — supervision, scheduling, QA, partner relations and worker support.

Each role has training, screening, permission, geography, and service limits. These roles remain distinct from `SECURITY GUARD` unless a jurisdiction review explicitly authorizes a separately licensed private-security service.

## Request flow
`REQUEST HELP / WALK WITH ME → LOCATION/DESTINATION → ACCESSIBILITY/COMMUNICATION NEEDS → RISK TRIAGE → ELIGIBLE GUARDIAN/PROVIDER → ACCEPT → CHECK-IN → SAFE ARRIVAL → CLOSE → OPTIONAL FEEDBACK`.

Imminent danger bypasses ordinary dispatch and directs the user toward emergency services/appropriate qualified responders.

## Safe Zone Network
Businesses, churches/ministries, schools, campuses, community centers, partner properties, and event venues can apply to become verified Safe Zones. Display verified facts only: open hours, accessibility, staff presence, phone, restroom/waiting availability if offered, and emergency escalation policy.

## AI Jurisdiction Review Engine
Every operating area receives a `JurisdictionProfile`:
COUNTRY → STATE/PROVINCE/REGION → COUNTY/DISTRICT → CITY/LOCALITY.

The AI helps gather, organize and flag rules, but an uncertain requirement is always `VERIFY_WITH_AUTHORITY` rather than guessed.

Review categories:
- private-security licensing / guard-card requirements
- volunteer vs employee/contractor classification
- business licensing
- insurance requirements
- worker/background-check rules
- youth/minor safeguarding requirements
- mandated-reporting duties if applicable
- uniforms/badges/vehicle-marking restrictions
- radios/body cameras/CCTV/audio-recording law
- privacy, consent and data-retention requirements
- first-aid/CPR expectations
- event/school property-access rules
- transportation/escort restrictions
- prohibited equipment/weapons
- labor/pay/overtime requirements
- local permits or partner agreements

Status per activity:
`ALLOWED | LICENSE_REQUIRED | PARTNER_REQUIRED | RESTRICTED | VERIFY_WITH_AUTHORITY`.

No approval is portable by assumption: a service cleared in one city/state/country must be reviewed before activation elsewhere.

## Volunteer / paid split
Volunteer Community Service can exist only for lawful, properly insured volunteer activities. Paid contracts are preferred for recurring frontline work so people doing difficult outreach are compensated and supported.

Street/outreach work can involve chronic exposure to trauma. Program requirements include supervision, manageable shifts, critical-incident decompression, worker mental-health/support referrals and retention monitoring.

## 3.00x rule
Community Guardian does not need every route or public-benefit program to achieve 3.00x. Some missions may intentionally operate near break-even or be subsidized. The overall TRYAMM target remains 3.00x eligible platform revenue / measured infrastructure cost, while Guardian separately tracks service-level contribution and mission outcomes.

The higher-margin part of Guardian should be software/dispatch/reporting/training and recurring partner contracts, not reducing frontline worker pay or safety investment.

## Evidence + continuous improvement
Evidence-supported community violence intervention often includes street outreach, credible messengers, multidisciplinary teams and service connections, but outcomes vary by implementation.

Therefore:
`BASELINE → PILOT → MEASURE → EVALUATE → IMPROVE → SCALE`.

Collect process and outcome data while minimizing sensitive-person data. The goal is to learn what actually improves safety and opportunity rather than market unproven claims.
