# Alphonso Gregory National CNA & Nurse Staffing Portal

## Purpose
Build a national healthcare staffing marketplace and workforce operating system inside TryAMM for CNA, LPN/LVN, RN, advanced-practice, home-care, facility, travel, per-diem, private-duty and related support roles.

## Operating principle
TryAMM must not represent that it is a licensed healthcare staffing agency in every state by default. The platform may operate as software, marketplace, credentialing, scheduling, recruiting, call-center and administrative infrastructure while staffing-employer, registry and agency functions are enabled only where the required entity registrations, state licenses, insurance, payroll, workers' compensation and partner approvals are active.

## Alphonso Gregory portal role

## Staffing model options
### Marketplace-only
TryAMM connects facilities with independent staffing businesses. TryAMM charges software, marketplace and administrative fees. The staffing partner remains employer and supplier.

### Managed staffing partner
A licensed/registered staffing partner employs and pays workers while TryAMM supplies technology, recruiting, scheduling, call-center and operations services.

### Direct staffing agency
Only after the operating entity has all required state registrations, insurance, payroll, workers' compensation, employment processes and healthcare-staffing approvals.

Temporary staffing relationships may create joint-employer responsibilities between the staffing agency and host facility. Contracts must define safety, supervision, training, records, wage/hour and leave responsibilities.

## Core workflow
Facility request -> jurisdiction and contract check -> credential requirements -> worker matching -> offer -> worker acceptance -> facility confirmation -> shift -> clock-in/out -> supervisor approval -> payroll/invoice ledger -> payout -> quality follow-up -> audit.

## AI and call center
Stubbs AI may assist with matching, reminders, document organization, scheduling, translation, attendance alerts and case summaries. Human staff must handle credential approval, employment decisions, clinical competency decisions, disputes, incidents and escalations.

Facility funds must be allocated into wages or partner payable, payroll taxes, insurance/reserve, processor fees, refunds/credits and TryAMM software or administrative revenue. Worker wages are never treated as platform revenue.
