# TryAMM Legacy Verticals — Jacobie, Aniyah, AM64 and Isaiah StarVerse

## Shared platform rule
These are distinct branded verticals that reuse TryAMM identity, Jinn/PayRouter, Stubbs AI, GrowthOS, Jacobie Cybersecurity, HoloPass, HoloAds, HoloMenu, unified feed/discovery and the Unified Value Ledger. Each vertical gets its own permissions, revenue policies and compliance gates.

---

## 1. Jacobie Real Estate + House Flipping

### Product
A real-estate operating and deal-management layer for investors, agents, contractors and property businesses.

### Core capabilities
- property lead intake
- distressed/off-market lead tracking
- MLS/authorized listing integrations where licensed/contracted
- deal pipeline: lead -> analysis -> offer -> contract -> rehab -> listing/rental -> close
- ARV and rehab-estimate worksheets with editable assumptions
- contractor bids and scope-of-work management
- project budget and draw tracking
- before/after media and Holo property tours
- document checklist and secure vault pointers
- investor/partner CRM
- appointment/showing scheduling
- seller lead capture
- property livestreams / Business Live walkthroughs
- HoloAds for listings and investor lead generation
- Jinn invoices, deposits and contractor/supplier payments where lawful and supported
- GrowthOS deal-health and marketing recommendations
- Jacobie Cybersecurity for document, payment, identity and wire-fraud protection

### Revenue paths
- monthly SaaS subscription
- premium deal-analysis tools
- lead-generation/advertising fees
- contractor marketplace/referral fees where legally permitted
- document/workflow add-ons
- white-label Stubbs AI real-estate assistant
- Holo property-tour production services

### Guardrails
TryAMM does not act as broker, agent, lender, escrow, title company or investment adviser unless appropriately licensed/contracted in the jurisdiction. Wire/payment changes require step-up verification and fraud controls.

## 2. Aniyah Cross-Border

### Product
A cross-border commerce, family/business coordination and diaspora trade app layer connecting eligible users and merchants across countries.

### Core capabilities
- multilingual storefront discovery
- diaspora shopping and gifting
- cross-border merchant profiles
- country-aware pricing/currency display
- Jinn/PayRouter routing to supported local rails
- shipping/customs status and documentation workflow
- HoloLingo translation
- digital receipts/invoices
- trusted-contact and family gifting workflows
- business supplier discovery
- Africa/Diaspora trade matching
- country availability rules for products/services
- fraud/risk checks and identity verification where required
- cross-border customer support via Stubbs AI + human escalation

### Revenue paths
- merchant subscriptions
- commerce/transaction fees where permitted
- FX/payment-service margin only through authorized providers and disclosed terms
- shipping/logistics referral or partner fees
- featured listings/HoloAds
- premium business tools
- white-label cross-border Stubbs AI assistant

### Guardrails
No unsupported money transmission, remittance, FX, stored-value or customs claims. Every country must pass payment, payout, tax/VAT/GST, privacy, consumer-protection, sanctions/export and product-restriction checks before activation.

## 3. AM64 Studio — 64-Track Vocal Coach + Auto-Tune

### Product
A creator music studio inside TryAMM/HoloMusic for recording, arranging, coaching and polishing vocals.

### Core capabilities
- up to 64 track project model
- vocal recording/import
- non-destructive editing
- track grouping and buses
- punch-in/comping workflow
- vocal coach mode: pitch, timing, breath, diction and phrasing feedback
- key/scale detection with confidence display
- pitch-correction controls: subtle -> creative effect
- formant-preserving options where technically supported
- harmony stack suggestions
- ad-lib and background-vocal planning
- metronome/count-in
- lyric/section markers
- stem export
- mix-note assistant
- collaboration/version history
- HoloMusic publishing handoff after rights confirmation
- rights/consent metadata for uploaded audio and voices

### Monetization
- free limited tier
- Creator subscription
- Pro Studio subscription
- AI/vocal-analysis credits
- premium pitch-correction/harmony tools
- cloud storage upgrades
- collaboration seats
- mastering/distribution partner referrals
- marketplace presets/templates where rights permit

### Guardrails
Pitch correction must not imply a specific third-party trademarked product. User must have rights/consent for uploaded vocals, beats, samples and cloned/custom voices. Publishing is blocked until rights declarations are complete.

## 4. Isaiah StarVerse — Anyone Can Be a Star

### Product
A talent-discovery, training and showcase universe where users can develop skills, perform, compete and be discovered without requiring traditional gatekeepers.

### Core experiences
- creator/talent profile
- audition clips
- live showcases
- talent categories: music, dance, comedy, acting, sports skills, spoken word, fashion, gaming/esports and more
- skill challenges
- judged competitions with transparent rules
- fan voting with anti-bot controls
- mentor/coaching marketplace
- Star Academy learning paths
- HoloStage performances
- HoloTV/Live showcase channels
- city/state/country talent discovery
- age-appropriate teen and adult lanes
- accessibility-first participation
- HoloPass achievements and Star Rank
- creator storefront, merch, tickets and memberships
- verified talent opportunities and casting/booking posts

### 'Anyone Can Be a Star' principle
Participation is open to eligible users, but visibility/rewards should be earned through authentic engagement, skill development, verified competition results and safe community behavior—not pay-to-win ranking.

### Monetization
- optional premium creator tools
- coaching/mentorship marketplace fees
- ticketed showcases
- sponsorships
- HoloAds
- memberships/fan clubs
- creator commerce
- premium production tools through AM64 Studio
- white-label talent AI through Stubbs AI

### Safety
Minors get separate safety rules, guardian/consent workflows where required, restricted messaging/contact, moderation and age-appropriate discovery. Casting/job opportunities require verification and anti-scam controls.
