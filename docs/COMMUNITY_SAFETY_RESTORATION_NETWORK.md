# TRYAMM Community Safety & Restoration Network

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Create a non-vigilante, non-security-guard community safety pathway focused on prevention, safe accompaniment, de-escalation, youth opportunity, bystander support, rapid resource navigation, neighborhood stewardship and measurable restoration outcomes.

## Core principle
PREVENT → ACCOMPANY → DE-ESCALATE → CONNECT TO HELP → DOCUMENT SAFELY → RESTORE → MEASURE → IMPROVE.

Members do not impersonate police, detain people, conduct searches, pursue suspects, carry out punishment, or perform unlicensed security work. Immediate danger routes to appropriate emergency/public-safety services.

- Safe Walk / Safe Ride accompaniment
- School arrival/dismissal community presence

- Youth Peace Missions

- Merchant closing-time accompaniment

- Community resource escorts
- Neighborhood cleanup/lighting/problem reporting
- Mentor and Second Chance pathways

## Safe Companion request
REQUEST → RISK SCREEN → AVAILABLE QUALIFIED COMPANION/TEAM → ACCEPT → CHECK-IN → ROUTE/MEETING POINT → PERIODIC SAFETY CHECK → ARRIVAL CONFIRMATION → INCIDENT/FEEDBACK → CLOSE.

Location sharing is opt-in, purpose-limited and automatically expires after the mission unless preservation is required for a reported safety incident.

## Roles
- Community Steward
- Safe Companion
- Youth Mentor
- Event Steward
- Resource Navigator
- De-escalation Lead
- Dispatcher/Coordinator
- Qualified partner professional

Each role has its own training/verification requirements. The platform must not call a person a licensed security professional unless independently verified.

## Training Passport
Required/optional modules can include:
- de-escalation
- conflict avoidance
- bystander intervention
- CPR/first aid through qualified providers where applicable
- disability awareness/accessibility
- youth safeguarding
- trauma-informed communication
- reporting boundaries
- emergency escalation
- privacy/location safety
- anti-bias / respectful service
- local program policies

TRYAMM training completion is not represented as a government/private-security license.

## JARVIS Safety Navigator
Examples:
- `I am leaving work late. Find a Safe Companion.`
- `My teenager needs a safe route from school.`
- `I see a conflict starting. What should I do safely?`
- `Find youth programs tonight.`
- `I need help getting to a resource center.`

JARVIS prioritizes avoidance, distance, trusted help and emergency escalation over confrontation.

## Youth Peace Missions
Give young people alternatives to street conflict through sports, game tournaments, music, Creator Studio, coding, trades, entrepreneurship, paid/eligible projects, mentorship, community service and school/college pathways.

Progress can connect to Learning Passport but never produces a criminal-risk score.

## Merchant / organization subscriptions
Potential revenue model:
- small-business Safe Closing plan
- school/nonprofit community-steward coordination
- event steward coordination
- residential/community association safety coordination
- employer late-shift Safe Companion benefit
- sponsored youth/peace cohorts
- city/foundation/nonprofit service contracts where eligible
- training/event fees

TRYAMM charges for software, coordination, training, scheduling, reporting and qualified-provider marketplace/referral services. If a service crosses into regulated private security or another licensed activity, it routes to properly licensed partners rather than being falsely performed by TRYAMM volunteers/stewards.

BACKGROUND/VERIFICATION WHERE USED + INSURANCE + TRAINING + SUPPORT/DISPATCH + SOFTWARE/MAPS/SMS + SAFETY OPERATIONS + PARTNER PAYMENTS + INCIDENT RESPONSE/LEGAL/COMPLIANCE.

## Impact metrics
Privacy-preserving metrics can include:
- Safe Companion missions completed
- successful arrivals
- youth enrolled in positive programs
- mentor matches
- referrals completed
- community events supported
- de-escalation trainings completed
- repeat incidents avoided/reduced only where evidence supports the measure
- jobs/gigs created
- partner response times
- user-reported safety confidence

Do not claim crime reduction causation without appropriate evidence.

## Community Gap Finder
Aggregate gaps can identify:
- unsafe transit/time windows
- lack of youth activities
- missing late-shift transportation
- insufficient community-resource navigation
- poorly lit/problem areas from public/user reports
- mentor shortage
- event/community-steward capacity gaps

Individual reports remain private and are not exposed as public crime intelligence.

## Safety boundaries
- no vigilantism
- no chasing/surveillance of suspected people
- no weapons program
- no detention/search/interrogation
- no public doxxing or suspect maps
- no predictive-policing or criminality scoring
- no monetization of crisis-location data
- minors receive age-appropriate safeguarding
- emergency escalation rules are explicit
