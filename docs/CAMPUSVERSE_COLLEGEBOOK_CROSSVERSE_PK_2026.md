# CampusVerse / CollegeBook / CrossVerse University PK — Foundation Contract

Status: FOUNDATION / CONFIGURABLE
Launch anchor: Jacobie character — November 20
Rule: institutional names, marks, official curriculum, and official partnerships require authorization. Public academic information may inform configurable templates but must not imply endorsement.

## 1. Persistent student identity
A student owns one persistent TRYAMM identity across CampusVerse, CollegeBook, Holo LIVE, StreetVerse, transfers, graduation, and Middleverse Jobs.

Profile modules:
- character/avatar and accessibility preferences
- campus and major/program
- clubs, athletics, creator channels, teams
- Skills Passport and mission history
- creator portfolio, reels, broadcasts, projects
- businesses/storefronts where eligible
- relationship graph with independent histories
- transfer/alumni/employment state

Relationships never delete another person's progress. A girlfriend/boyfriend/partner is an independent character. If a relationship ends, its edge changes to ex/previous partner while both characters retain missions, followers, projects, businesses, friendships, school history, and achievements. Future relationships create new edges instead of replacing people.

## 2. Jacobie launch chapter
Jacobie is the founding CampusVerse story character.
- release milestone: November 20

- college-life, athletics, international-relations/career and Jacobie Vision/cybersecurity story hooks may be configured where appropriate
- girlfriend has her own independent mission tree, interests, friends, creator identity and progression

## 3. Campus Mission Engine
Do not hard-code one school. Each campus is a tenant/configuration:
Campus -> Programs/Majors -> Mission Families -> Clubs/Teams -> Events -> PK Arenas -> Creator Channels -> Career Pathways.

Mission templates can cover:
- business/entrepreneurship
- computing, AI and cybersecurity defense
- engineering/design
- science/lab/environment
- criminal justice/civic learning
- digital media/film/broadcast
- education/tutoring
- equine/agriculture where offered
- exercise science, athletics and sport management
- music production/performance
- organizational leadership
- psychology/research education (no diagnosis/treatment simulation)
- theology/ministry/religious studies
- humanities, languages, mathematics and physical sciences
- pre-professional pathways
- school-specific programs loaded from an approved/current catalog

Mission types: learn, practice, collaborate, create, compete, serve, pitch, publish, broadcast, mentor, intern, transfer, graduate.

## 4. Cross-major missions
Complex missions require multiple disciplines.
Example startup launch:
engineering -> prototype
computing/cybersecurity -> software + security
business -> model + operations
finance/accounting -> budget
media -> campaign + broadcast
music -> audio package
sport/event management -> launch event

Each participant receives role-specific Skills Passport evidence.

## 5. CollegeBook
CollegeBook is the campus social/academic graph, not just a feed.
- verified/declared campus communities
- student profiles and character pages
- major/program groups
- clubs and athletics
- project/team discovery
- tutoring/mentorship
- events
- alumni connections
- transfer communities
- creator channels
- portfolio/project pages
- campus marketplace subject to platform/school rules
- safety/moderation/reporting
- privacy controls; minimize retention of identifiable student activity

## 6. CrossVerse University PK
PK is a reusable competition format:
- student vs student
- team vs team
- major vs major
- club vs club
- campus vs campus
- community college vs university
- HBCU showcases
- regional/national/international invitationals
- Verse vs Verse creator events

Competition families:
cyber defense, coding, engineering build, startup pitch, film/reel, music production, debate/knowledge, esports, sports challenges, cooking/hospitality, design, community-impact challenges.

Skill divisions: beginner, open/club, advanced/varsity where appropriate. Do not require elite skill to participate.

## 7. Holo LIVE student broadcast system
Every eligible PK/event can create production roles:
host, commentator, producer, camera, editor, graphics, statistics, social/reels, DJ/audio, accessibility/caption operator.

Viewer modes:
- main broadcast
- competitor/team POV where permitted
- campus commentary feeds
- student creator co-streams where rights permit
- highlights/reels
- replay/archive subject to consent and retention policy

Accessibility baseline: captions, text alternatives, scalable UI, alternative controls, speech-to-text where available, screen-reader-friendly navigation and adaptable competition formats.

## 8. Skills Passport -> Middleverse Jobs
Mission evidence may include completed projects, verified team roles, portfolios, competition participation, broadcasts and credentials supplied by authorized issuers.

Flow:
Campus mission -> Skills Passport -> portfolio -> internship/project opportunity -> Middleverse Jobs -> graduation/alumni career identity.

The platform must not claim an academic credential, grade, certification or employer endorsement unless verified by the issuer.

## 9. Community-college / transfer pathway
Community colleges are first-class CampusVerse tenants.
A transfer changes campus membership; it does not reset identity.
Retain:
character, followers, Skills Passport, creator portfolio, mission history, businesses, friends, clubs/alumni history and eligible achievements.

Support:
community college -> four-year university -> graduate/professional pathways -> alumni/career network.

Use reference archetypes rather than copying any institution:

Institution-specific branding, official campus maps, logos and partnership claims remain disabled until licensed/authorized.

## 11. Student creator economy
Possible earning surfaces subject to eligibility, law, platform rules, school rules and athlete/NIL compliance:
creator gifts, production work, permitted digital goods, marketplace sales, sponsorships, event production, business missions and creator campaigns.

All real-money rewards must be server-authoritative:
eligible event -> verified completion/event -> transaction -> ledger -> payable balance.
No client may award itself money.

Student-athlete/NIL activities require a separate compliance gate and cannot be treated as ordinary unrestricted creator payouts.

## 12. Safety and integrity
- age-appropriate lanes
- consent for character likeness/voice
- anti-harassment/reporting/moderation
- no public exposure of private class schedules or sensitive student data
- school/identity verification must minimize data collection
- educational missions are simulations unless an authorized institution explicitly recognizes them
- relationship mechanics require mutual consent for player-controlled romantic relationships
- no deletion of another player's identity/history after relationship changes

## 13. Expansion contract
Greenville is the Jacobie launch configuration.
The engine must permit later addition of HBCUs, universities, community colleges and international campuses without changing core code.

Canonical progression:
Jacobie + independent girlfriend character -> Greenville campus -> CollegeBook -> Major Mission Engine -> CrossVerse PK -> Holo LIVE -> Skills Passport -> HBCU/university/community-college network -> transfer/alumni -> Middleverse Jobs.
