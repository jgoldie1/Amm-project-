# CrossVerse Creator Scout 20

## Purpose
Founder-led creator scouting for the StreetVerse/CrossVerse Alpha.

## Cohort
- 10 BIGO LIVE creators
- 10 TikTok LIVE creators
- Cash Bae / CashleyBankss is the first BIGO benchmark already registered in the playable cast.

## Intake
For each creator record:
- platform
- creator name / handle
- screenshot or reference supplied by founder
- LIVE engagement observations
- on-camera presence
- audience interaction
- consistency
- collaboration / PK potential
- StreetVerse/CrossVerse fit
- consent/status for likeness use
- proposed CrossVerse role, StreetVerse mission, LIVE/PK role, and creator-business opportunity

## Selection
Maintain separate BIGO and TikTok scouting pools plus a combined shortlist. Do not create a photo-matched likeness without an approved reference/permission. A selected creator is not considered live until their character record, asset, gameplay integration, deployment, and device verification are complete.

## Release safety
This scouting program is post-Release-1 work. It must not inflate the fixed 16-hour Release-1 accounting or bypass Alpha release certification.
