# StreetVerse Chicago Martial-Arts Styles

Status: EXPERIMENTAL / DRAFT PR #251

## Historical integrity

The game uses two Chicago dojo-war-inspired styles:

### Green Dragon • Flow Reconstruction
A fictionalized gameplay reconstruction. Available sources establish the Green Dragons as a rival Chicago dojo group in the 1970 dojo-war story, but do not give a complete verified technical syllabus suitable for exact reproduction.

Game identity:
- mobility;
- rhythm;
- redirection;
- defensive spacing;
- counter windows;
- stamina efficiency.

### Black Dragon • Pressure Reconstruction
A mixed-history + fictionalized gameplay reconstruction inspired by documented descriptions of John Keehan / Count Dante's Black Dragons, their emphasis on harder contact, and the broader Chicago dojo-war environment.

Game identity:
- pressure;
- momentum;
- commitment;
- dramatic movement;
- burst scoring;
- spectator energy.

Neither style is presented as an authentic surviving curriculum.

## One-hand combat grammar

Six contextual inputs:
- GUARD
- STEP
- FLOW
- COUNTER
- BURST
- RECOVER

These are game verbs, not real-world fighting instructions.

The engine scores:
- timing;
- spacing;
- defense;
- rhythm;
- stamina;
- sportsmanship;
- crowd energy.

No essential two-button chords are required.

## Style combinations

Green Dragon reconstruction:
- Emerald Step = STEP → FLOW
- River Counter = GUARD → COUNTER
- Dragon Circle = FLOW → STEP → FLOW

Black Dragon reconstruction:
- Midnight Rush = STEP → BURST
- Black Wing = GUARD → STEP → BURST
- Dragon Pressure = BURST → FLOW → BURST

The combinations trigger animation/state/scoring events only. They do not explain where or how to physically strike a person.

## Time Machine integration

During the 1970 Chicago Dojo Wars Chrono mission:
1. player enters the historical reconstruction;
2. evidence label is shown;
3. player selects Green Dragon, Black Dragon or neutral Chicago dojo;
4. player completes a one-hand timing tournament;
5. player interviews simulated witnesses;
6. documented facts are separated from legend;
7. the finale is a nonlethal tournament;
8. the player returns to present-day StreetVerse with a history/bond unlock.

## Present-day unlocks

Potential rewards:
- Green Dragon Flow cosmetic stance;
- Black Dragon Pressure cosmetic stance;
- Chicago Martial Arts Historian title;
- dojo mentor bond;
- community tournament invitations;
- creator martial-arts stage;
- dragon-themed vehicle livery;
- historical lore cards;
- alternate A/B/C dojo missions.

The emphasis is cinematic roleplay, accessibility and Chicago history—not real-world injury instruction.
