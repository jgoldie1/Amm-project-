# Stubbs Security & Training — Business-in-a-Box

## Starter package contents
1. Branded responsive website and customer email inquiry flow (implemented as preview).
2. Stubbs AI and Holo GPT service knowledge: approved services, geographic coverage, pricing, escalation rules, disclaimers (not yet connected).
3. Quantum Command operational design: officer shift planning, client-site instructions, training renewal tracking, asset checkout, incident intake, and human-approved AI responses (planned conventional software; no physical quantum chip).
4. Lead and quote tracker: customer, site, service requested, staffing estimate, quote status, consent, follow-up date (planned).
5. Training management: qualified instructor directory, course approval, attendance, certificate issuance subject to credentials (planned).
6. Incident management: timestamp, assigned officer, client site, factual narrative, evidence reference, supervisor review, retention policy (planned).
7. Equipment inventory: item identifier, issue/return, inspection date, maintenance due, assigned staff (planned).
8. Security supplies starter list: branded shirts and jackets, ID holders, high-visibility vests, radios, charging docks, flashlights, first aid kits, notebooks, weather gear, approved vehicle dash cameras, basic training presentation equipment.
9. Compliance binder: verify state licensing, armed/unarmed authorizations, business registration, insurance, worker screening, written policies, contract templates, privacy, retention, and instructor approvals.
10. Financial planning: startup budget, recurring software costs, payroll, insurance, uniforms, radio replacements, and cash reserve.

## Release gates
- Do not accept paid security bookings before appropriate licensing and insurance are confirmed.
- Do not promise 24/7 dispatch, monitoring, live AI, automated response, or quantum computing without a verified service.
- Never put sensitive customer details or live patrol locations into a public website.
- AI may assist with documentation and scheduling but may not autonomously decide use-of-force or emergency actions.
- Weapons, regulated defensive devices, and firearms-related equipment require separate licensed procurement and are not included here.
- Validate pricing and vendor availability before purchase; no supplier or pricing is endorsed in this file.
