# TRYAMM Commerce & Supply Network Contract

Status: DESIGN / COMMITTED FOUNDATION. This document does not claim production deployment, active payment rails, active fulfillment partners, or live regulated services.

## Product thesis

TRYAMM connects physical businesses, AI-assisted digital twins, creator marketing, commerce, inventory, fulfillment, rewards, workforce, entertainment, and eligible partner services through one Passport.

Core loop:

BUSINESS CAPTURE -> BUSINESS PASSPORT -> OWNER-APPROVED DIGITAL TWIN -> STREETVERSE STORE -> OMNIREEL / OMNIDRAMA -> CREATOR ARMY -> MARKETPLACE -> INVENTORY RESERVATION -> PAYMENT -> FULFILLMENT -> DELIVERY -> SEPARATE LEDGERS -> REPLENISHMENT

## Business Capture / Scan-to-Twin

A Scout may capture a business only with owner/authorized consent.

Guided capture:
- front, left side, right side, rear when appropriate
- entrance and customer-facing interior
- approved signage
- counter/register area without exposing payment/security information
- menu, catalog, displays, and owner-approved customer areas

Do not capture private areas, credentials, payment data, or people without appropriate permission.

AI may extract approved signage, menu/catalog data, layout estimates, and media to prepare a storefront draft. AI-generated or extracted information must be owner-reviewable. Hours, prices, products, accessibility claims, and physical details must not be silently invented.

Flow:
CAPTURE -> EXTRACT -> DRAFT -> OWNER REVIEW -> CORRECT/REMOVE/APPROVE -> PUBLISH

## Economic separation

XP = progression/status.
Holo Credits = internal TRYAMM utility/reward credits; no automatic cash value or payable balance.
Omni Cash = eligible real-money transaction/earnings interface, subject to payment-provider and compliance requirements.

Holo Credits may support eligible digital utilities such as virtual gifts, avatar/store customization, creator tools, effects, AI-generation utility, GameVerse content, premium experiences, educational simulations, and approved promotional tools.

Holo Credits must not automatically convert to cash or be represented as bank deposits. Regulated financial products, prescriptions, gambling, or other restricted value transfers are outside the Holo Credit utility economy.

Separate ledger classes:
- XP
- Holo Credits
- merchant sales/proceeds
- creator/scout commissions
- TRYAMM revenue
- refunds/reversals

## TRYAMM Virtual Supply Network

SUPPLIER -> VIRTUAL WAREHOUSE -> VIRTUAL STORAGE -> HOLO COLD VAULT -> STORE/RESTAURANT/HOME -> ORDER -> PICK/PACK -> HOLO DELIVERY -> CUSTOMER -> INVENTORY UPDATE -> REORDER RECOMMENDATION

Virtual Warehouse represents inventory states across participating merchants, suppliers, warehouses, and fulfillment partners.

Virtual Storage maps eligible stock to logical/physical storage locations such as shelves, stockrooms, dry storage, refrigerated storage, freezer storage, and participating third-party facilities.

Holo Cold Vault represents temperature-sensitive inventory, lots, expiration/hold states, and cold-chain metadata where real integrations provide it.

Holo Fridge represents household or small-business food inventory, low-stock/expiration awareness, and user-approved replenishment. It may recommend purchases but must not silently buy.

Inventory states should include at minimum AVAILABLE, RESERVED, IN_TRANSIT, HOLD, SOLD, RETURNED, LOW_STOCK, and REPLENISHMENT_RECOMMENDED.

## OmniCare 360

OmniCare 360 is a health/wellness/navigation layer: wellness education, care navigation, licensed-provider telehealth integrations where available, prescription-savings information, accessibility, caregiver/family tools, and benefits/resource navigation.

Holo Credits may reward eligible wellness education/engagement but medical treatment must never depend on credits. Medical decisions remain with users and qualified professionals. Omni Cash may surface eligible payments/discounts through compliant providers; TRYAMM must not present itself as a bank, insurer, pharmacy, or licensed clinical provider unless those statuses are actually established.

## Jobs and workforce

The network may create work for Scouts, creators, photographers/video teams, catalog/menu specialists, digital-twin reviewers, virtual-store managers, delivery/fulfillment workers, customer support, inventory workers, beauty specialists, and business-onboarding agents.

Middleverse / AI Cafe may train users for these roles, with clear distinction between training and guaranteed employment.
