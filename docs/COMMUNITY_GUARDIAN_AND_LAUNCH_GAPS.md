# Community Guardian + Final Launch Gaps

Status: LOCKED SPEC / PRE-LAUNCH

## Community Guardian mission
Create a voluntary, non-vigilante community safety and prevention pathway focused on presence, de-escalation, youth engagement, safe-walk support, incident observation/reporting, resource connection and community restoration.

Do not operate as law enforcement, private security or armed patrol unless separately licensed and insured for that jurisdiction. No chasing suspects, detaining people, impersonating police, carrying weapons under the program, doxxing, racial/religious profiling, gang labeling, predictive-crime scoring or public shaming.

## Core lanes
- Safe Walk / Safe Ride coordination
- school dismissal / event visibility with approved partners
- neighborhood ambassador presence
- youth peace missions and late-day structured activities
- conflict de-escalation training
- bystander/intervention education
- lost-person / vulnerable-person support routing
- business closing-time escort requests
- transit-stop/community-event support
- emergency-resource navigation
- evidence-preserving incident reporting to appropriate authorities when needed
- Community Gap Finder for lighting, abandoned property, unsafe crossings, missing youth programs, food/transportation barriers and other environmental risks

## Guardian JARVIS
Guardian JARVIS may:
- route requests
- show approved training/checklists
- coordinate volunteers/partners
- maintain check-in/check-out
- suggest safe routes based on approved public/provider data
- notify authorized coordinators of overdue check-ins
- connect to emergency services or qualified partners when appropriate
- generate aggregate impact reports

Guardian JARVIS must not direct confrontation, pursuit, detention, retaliation or weapons use.

## Profit / sustainability model
Community Guardian can be a legitimate business/service layer if structured around lawful services rather than vigilante activity.

Possible revenue:
- institutional subscriptions from participating businesses, campuses, events, housing organizations or community partners
- contracted ambassador / safe-walk coordination where permitted
- training and certification-preparation courses (not false government/private-security licensing)
- event safety coordination software
- Guardian JARVIS / dispatch software subscriptions
- enterprise dashboards and incident-management SaaS
- sponsorships for youth peace missions and community visibility
- grants/contracts for eligible violence-prevention, workforce or community programs
- optional background-screening/credential-provider integrations billed transparently

Separate restricted grants/sponsorships from platform revenue in Money Engine.

## Workforce pathway
LEARN → de-escalation / first aid / communication / community-resource training → mentor shadowing → approved ambassador role → paid community work where lawful → supervisor/coordinator → training/operations/business pathway.

External certifications, background checks, security licenses, medical credentials and insurance are verified rather than invented by TRYAMM.
