# Alton Stubbs Community Peace & Safety

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT / OPERATIONS SPECIFICATION

## Mission
Create a lawful, unarmed, non-vigilante community safety pathway focused on prevention, presence, de-escalation, escort, reporting, youth engagement, event safety and connection to legitimate services. It is not a private police force and does not authorize pursuit, detention, punishment, weapons use or impersonation of law enforcement.

## Leadership
Alton Stubbs is the designated pathway lead for the TRYAMM community safety/security lane. Exact employment/contract authority, licensing and operational scope must be documented before live deployment.

## Service lanes
- Community Peace Ambassadors
- Safe Walk / Safe Ride coordination
- School dismissal / event presence where authorized
- Business corridor ambassador support
- Youth peace missions and mentorship
- Event safety and crowd guidance
- Missing/vulnerable-person escalation to appropriate authorities/services
- Conflict interruption through trained de-escalation
- Emergency resource navigation
- Property/site observation and incident reporting
- Digital safety/cyber awareness education

## Training
Required before field activation:
- de-escalation and conflict communication
- bystander intervention
- first aid/CPR/AED where appropriate
- trauma-informed interaction
- disability/accessibility awareness
- youth safeguarding
- harassment/discrimination prevention
- incident documentation
- radio/comms discipline
- legal boundaries and when to disengage
- privacy/body-camera policy if cameras are ever used
- emergency escalation and 911/public-safety coordination

## JARVIS Peace Assistant
AI may help with shift checklists, resource lookup, de-escalation scripts, translation, accessibility prompts, incident form completion, route/check-in reminders and after-action summaries. It may not direct physical confrontation, profiling, pursuit, detention or force.

## Community Peace App workflow
REQUEST HELP / SAFE WALK / EVENT SUPPORT / REPORT HAZARD → triage → assign authorized ambassador/provider → live check-in → escalate if necessary → close incident → privacy-preserving outcome report.

## Revenue pathways
Potential lawful revenue sources:
- contracted unarmed ambassador services for businesses/events/properties where permitted
- event safety coordination
- training programs
- employer/community sponsorships
- grants for violence prevention/youth engagement when eligible
- school/community contracts where procured and legally permitted
- business corridor subscriptions for non-police ambassador presence
- cyber/digital-safety training

Never charge a distressed person merely to report danger or access emergency routing.

## Metrics
Measure calls/requests handled, safe-walk completions, conflicts resolved without escalation, youth participation, referrals completed, hazards reported/resolved, business satisfaction, training completions and incidents escalated appropriately. Do not use arrest counts or aggressive enforcement as success metrics.

## Safety gates
No LIVE field operation until insurance, contracts, worker classification, background/safeguarding checks where lawful, training, local licensing requirements, uniforms/branding review, incident policy, emergency escalation, data privacy and legal review are complete.

## Connection to TRYAMM
Learning Passport → Peace Ambassador training → HR/recruiting → Alton Stubbs Community Peace → Quantum Discord community rooms → Emergency Path → Community Gap Finder → sponsor/grant engine → Community Spotlight → impact dashboard.
