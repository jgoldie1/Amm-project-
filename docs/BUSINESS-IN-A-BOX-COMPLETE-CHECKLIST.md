# TryAMM Business-in-a-Box — Complete Merchant Readiness Checklist

A merchant should not be marked ACTIVE until required items for its country/vertical are complete.

## Identity / legal
- legal business name
- DBA/display name
- owner/controller identity verification
- tax ID / business registration where required
- business address
- support email
- support phone
- payout bank/payment account
- terms accepted

## Storefront
- logo/banner
- description
- hours
- locations
- products/services/menu
- pricing
- modifiers/options
- inventory/availability
- pickup/delivery/booking rules
- refund/cancellation policy

## Payments — Jinn / PayRouter
- approved payment rails by country
- merchant/seller-of-record configuration
- Tap to Pay where supported
- online checkout
- local/Africa rails where supported
- verified webhook/event processing
- payout destination
- refund/chargeback workflow

## Tax / accounting
- product/service tax classes
- marketplace-facilitator determination by jurisdiction
- tax collector/remitter recorded per transaction
- tax engine/provider configuration
- monthly sales/tax statements
- bookkeeping/accounting exports
- applicable tax-document/reporting workflow

## Paperless receipts
- SMS receipt option
- email receipt option
- in-app receipt wallet
- QR receipt/verification option
- optional printer integration
- receipt includes line items, discounts, taxes/fees, tip, total, masked payment descriptor, policy/support links

## Growth
- HoloMenu
- QR/NFC HoloMenu touchpoints
- HoloCoupons
- promo/referral code
- HoloPass loyalty
- Creator Affiliate Marketplace eligibility
- HoloAds campaigns
- GrowthOS recommendations
- HoloLocal discovery
- HoloBusiness Live/shoppable streaming

## Operations
- orders dashboard
- reservations/appointments if applicable
- delivery integration if applicable
- unified business inbox
- customer support workflow
- review/reputation tools
- staff roles/permissions
- fraud/risk monitoring

## Payout / revenue
- business payout schedule
- Revenue Waterfall policy ID
- TryAMM subscription/setup fees
- transaction/commission rules
- delivery fees/driver pay where applicable
- creator/affiliate commissions where applicable
- taxes/processor fees/reserves separated
- merchant payable clearly visible

## Accessibility / language
- keyboard/screen-reader compatible storefront
- large touch targets / one-hand usability
- HoloAccess preferences
- HoloLingo translation-ready content
- captions/transcripts for business livestreams where applicable

## Africa / international
Per country:
- currency
- supported payment rails
- payout rails
- VAT/tax/invoicing rules
- local business onboarding requirements
- language/localization
- prohibited/restricted goods
- delivery/shipping/customs requirements
- consumer protection/refund rules

## Business communications
Recommended role inboxes:
- support@
- billing@
- orders@
- partners@
- legal@
- privacy@
- accessibility@

Recommended main phone routing:
1. Customer support
2. Orders / reservations
3. Billing / payments
4. Business growth / ads
5. Technical support
6. Accessibility
7. Escalation / safety

## Activation states
DRAFT → ONBOARDING → VERIFICATION → PAYMENT_READY → TAX_READY → STOREFRONT_READY → PILOT → ACTIVE

No merchant should skip required verification/compliance gates merely because the UI is complete.
