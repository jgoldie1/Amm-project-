# TRYAMM Community Safety & Opportunity Corps

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT / OPERATING MODEL

## Mission
FIX CONDITIONS + CREATE OPPORTUNITIES + BUILD RELATIONSHIPS + INTERRUPT CONFLICT + SUPPORT PEOPLE + INVOLVE PROFESSIONAL RESPONDERS WHEN NECESSARY.

This is not a vigilante group, militia, private police force, or substitute for licensed security/law enforcement/emergency services. The Corps is a prevention, de-escalation, support, navigation, opportunity, and safe-presence network.

## Core lanes
1. Safe Presence
- trained, identifiable community ambassadors in approved public/community spaces
- observe, greet, assist, de-escalate, connect people to help
- no weapons mandate, no detention powers, no pursuit, no impersonating police

2. Violence Interruption
- trained conflict mediators and credible messengers
- youth/gang conflict interruption, retaliatory-violence prevention, mediation and follow-up
- high-risk/imminent threats routed to appropriate emergency/professional responders

3. Youth Opportunity
- sports, music, gaming, coding, trades, entrepreneurship, paid/eligible gigs, mentoring, school help and safe evening programming
- connect Student JARVIS, Launchpad, Learning Passport and Community Gap Finder

4. Reentry / Lost Sheep
- job readiness, housing/resource navigation, IDs, education, legal-aid referrals, mentors, Second Chance employers and business launch

5. Victim / Family Support
- private resource navigation, emergency pathways, trauma-informed referral, transportation, food/housing support and follow-up

6. Community Problem Solving
- aggregate issues like abandoned property, dark blocks, unsafe routes, food deserts, job shortages, transit gaps and youth-program gaps
- Community Gap Finder turns them into missions with owners, budgets, funding paths and measurable outcomes

7. Professional Escalation
- emergency medical, fire, law enforcement, licensed security, mental-health crisis teams, domestic-violence specialists, child-protection authorities, legal counsel and other qualified professionals where appropriate

## JARVIS Safety Navigator
User asks: `I don't feel safe`, `there is a fight`, `my child needs somewhere safe tonight`, or `how can I help my block?`
JARVIS classifies only enough to route:
- immediate danger -> emergency/professional responder guidance
- conflict/non-immediate -> approved mediator/ambassador pathway
- youth opportunity -> programs/mentors/events
- victim/family needs -> Emergency Path/resource navigation
- neighborhood condition -> Community Gap Finder mission

Do not turn safety reports into public accusation feeds or predictive-crime scores.

## Community Ambassador training
Required curriculum can include:
- de-escalation
- conflict mediation
- trauma-informed communication
- first aid/CPR where appropriately certified
- youth safeguarding
- disability communication/accessibility
- naloxone/overdose response where lawful/trained
- domestic violence and trafficking referral boundaries
- documentation/privacy
- bias/civil-rights awareness
- when to disengage and call professionals

## Safety operations rules
- no vigilantism
- no punishment, detention, searches, interrogation or forced identification
- no chasing suspects
- no public naming/shaming from unverified reports
- no racial/religious/disability/criminality profiling
- no bounty for confrontation
- incident documentation minimized and access-controlled
- body-worn video, if ever used, requires explicit jurisdictional policy, retention rules, privacy review and consent/law analysis

Potential revenue:
- property-management/community ambassador contracts
- retail/business-district ambassador programs
- event safe-presence staffing
- school/college non-security support programs
- hospital/community-navigation partnerships
- workforce/reentry contracts
- youth-program sponsorships
- municipal/county grants/contracts where eligible
- foundation/philanthropic grants
- training/licensing of the TRYAMM operating software and curriculum
- employer-funded Second Chance/recruiting programs
- sponsored Community Missions

Separate any regulated private-security service into a properly licensed partner/entity where required by law.

## Performance metrics
Measure outcomes rather than arrests:
- conflicts de-escalated/referrals completed
- youth enrolled in programs
- jobs/gigs created
- reentry placements
- mentor matches
- resource connections
- repeat victim/family follow-up
- safety-condition gaps closed
- business/community satisfaction
- training completion
- response/escalation appropriateness

Do not claim causation when evidence only shows correlation.

## Sustainability integration
Revenue from contracts/software/training enters eligible platform revenue only after provider/worker obligations and restricted funds are separated. Sustainability Engine tracks contribution margin by program. Goal remains 3.00x platform sustainability without cutting essential safety quality.

## What it does
It changes TRYAMM's crime response from `find bad people` to `reduce the conditions and moments that produce harm, create alternatives, interrupt escalation, support victims, and call qualified responders when needed.`
