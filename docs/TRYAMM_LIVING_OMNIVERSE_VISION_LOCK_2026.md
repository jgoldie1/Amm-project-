# TRYAMM Living Omniverse — Vision Lock 2026

Status: CANONICAL PRODUCT VISION

## North Star
TRYAMM Living Omniverse is a persistent AI-assisted digital civilization where one Passport can move through StreetVerse, Metaverse, MiddleVerse, SpaceVerse, Multiverse and QuantumVerse while preserving identity, permissions, relationships, businesses, creator inventory, reputation and world history.

Nothing important happens without becoming history.

## World stack
- StreetVerse: living cities, families, careers, creators, businesses, public safety, justice and legacy.
- Metaverse: social spaces, concerts, stores, entertainment, creator worlds and events.
- MiddleVerse: connective spatial layer for portals, discovery, travel, identity and commerce.
- MiddleVerse AI: orchestration layer that routes users, worlds, services, missions and contextual assistance.
- SpaceVerse: Earth orbit, Moon, Mars, planetary/moon systems, stations, exploration and simulated space economies.
- Genesis Engine: scientifically labeled resource/life-support simulation for water extraction/recycling, atmosphere/habitat management, algae/plants, photosynthesis, food and settlement growth. Speculative terraforming must be labeled simulation, not current capability.
- Evolution Engine: educational population/ecology simulation plus separately labeled speculative evolution.
- Multiverse: alternate fictional worlds, branches and scenarios.
- QuantumVerse: Quantum Zoom, Quantum Time, provenance-aware scientific visualization, Pocket Dimensions and alternate simulations.
- Omniverse: umbrella connecting worlds, identities, economies, histories and creator assets.

## Shared engines
Passport/Identity -> HoloGPT -> Life Engine -> Economy Engine -> Mission/Story Director -> Timeline Engine -> Butterfly/Causality Engine -> World Oracle -> Quantum Time -> Creator/Commerce -> Impact Ledger.

Authoritative money, ownership, evidence, guilt, payouts and world-state changes remain server-controlled. AI may explain and propose; it may not invent authoritative facts.

## XR and multidimensional experience
The same canonical world state must support:
1. Phone/2D: default accessible experience.
2. AR: StreetVerse characters, missions, clue boards, Holo Ads, portals and Quantum overlays anchored into physical space.
3. VR: embodied entry into StreetVerse, SpaceVerse, Case Files, creator worlds and Pocket Dimensions.
4. Mixed Reality: persistent spatial dashboards, HoloGPT, portals, maps, clue boards, business controls and world objects placed around the user's real environment.
5. Multidimensional mode: not a claim of literal extra physical dimensions. It is the product term for navigating multiple synchronized information/world dimensions: physical location, virtual world, timeline, alternate branch, social graph, economic state, scale/Quantum Zoom and Pocket Dimension.

XR must be an interface to the same simulation, not a separate game. Preserve Passport, mission state, inventory, permissions, timeline and business state across 2D/AR/VR/MR.

## Spatial continuity contract
Every portable object/event should be addressable by world_id, entity_id, timeline_id, branch_id, spatial_anchor, scale_level, owner/permissions, provenance and last_authoritative_state.

Example journey:
PHONE STREETVERSE -> scan portal in AR -> enter business in VR -> open MR Command Nexus -> Quantum Zoom object -> move backward in Quantum Time -> inspect alternate branch -> return to present -> publish Reel -> Timeline records the event.

## Cross-world economy
Creators build portable approved assets/services that may work across compatible worlds. Businesses can progress from StreetVerse storefronts to Metaverse venues and fictional SpaceVerse operations. Real-world businesses and virtual businesses must be clearly distinguished.

Monetization candidates: memberships, Holo Credits/utility entitlements, cosmetics, creator marketplace fees, virtual businesses, Holo Ads, sponsored missions, business SaaS, Reels advertising, premium story seasons, SpaceVerse expeditions, QuantumVerse experiences, events, commerce/booking services and education/enterprise licensing.

## Impact ledger
Track transaction/source period, allocation base, percentage, destination, approval status, payment status, documentation and audit timestamp. Public impact claims must be based on verified disbursements/results, never projected or fictional outcomes.

## Safety, science and rights gates
- Label REAL / DOCUMENTED / SIMULATED / SPECULATIVE / FICTIONAL states.
- No claim that present technology can globally terraform planets.
- No sale of virtual celestial property as real extraterrestrial title.
- Separate youth experiences from adult-gated spaces at server/permission level.
- Real cases/news require provenance and must not invent accusations.
- Asset provenance/license/likeness/music/AI-origin metadata is required for production use.
- Real-money funding, lending, equity, charity and token/investment programs require their applicable legal/compliance lanes.

## Product moat
The moat is not the number of worlds. It is continuity:
ONE PASSPORT + ONE LIVING HISTORY + MANY WORLDS + MANY INTERFACES.

A decision can begin in StreetVerse, change a family or business, finance a virtual SpaceVerse operation, alter a settlement over simulated generations, become StreetVerse News/Reel history, and later be revisited through Quantum Time with the Butterfly Engine explaining the causal chain.
