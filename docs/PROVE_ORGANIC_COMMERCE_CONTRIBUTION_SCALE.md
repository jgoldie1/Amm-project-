# TRYAMM Launch Operating Model

Status: LOCKED STRATEGY

## Core sequence
PROVE ORGANIC → PROVE COMMERCE → PROVE CONTRIBUTION → SCALE CAREFULLY.

This sequence is a release and business discipline. It prevents TRYAMM from confusing feature count, registered users, simulated transactions or projected revenue with proof that the platform works.

## 1. PROVE ORGANIC
Goal: prove people return because TRYAMM is useful before heavy paid acquisition.

Evidence:
- activated users who complete a real first journey
- 7/30-day return behavior
- Student/Personal/Business JARVIS task completion
- creator publishing completion
- Marketplace browsing-to-save/cart behavior
- Accessibility Passport adoption and successful task completion
- community participation without spam/referral manipulation
- referral rate and direct/organic traffic

Gate to next phase: repeated usage from real cohorts, no critical safety/security blockers, and at least one end-to-end journey with reliable completion.

## 2. PROVE COMMERCE
Goal: prove people will pay and merchants/providers can fulfill.

Start with lower-regulatory-risk commerce first:
- Business/Creator subscriptions
- HoloGPT Credits / AI Actions
- Stubbs Harmony website services
- Forever Website entitlement
- domain/DNS management through approved registrar/reseller pathways
- Marketplace service/platform fees
- Holo Coupons / sponsored promotions
- creator tools / production services
- event/ticket service fees where permitted

Measure:
- gross transaction value vs eligible TRYAMM revenue
- conversion
- refund/dispute rate
- fulfillment success
- customer support burden
- payment/provider costs
- AI/data/storage/bandwidth cost per paying account
- contribution margin by product

Do not count creator earnings, restricted funds, taxes, provider settlements, refundable reserves or pass-through government/state fees as TRYAMM revenue.

## 3. PROVE CONTRIBUTION
Goal: prove each major paid product contributes positive economics after its direct variable costs.

Contribution = eligible product revenue - direct variable costs - refunds/credits - attributable provider costs.

A feature that grows usage but loses money per additional customer is not yet scale-ready.

Targets:
- positive unit contribution by mature paid product
- Platform Sustainability Ratio ≥ 1.00x before claiming self-supporting infrastructure
- intermediate reserve-building target ≥ 1.50x
- strategic TRYAMM target = 3.00x eligible platform revenue / measured infrastructure cost

At 3.00x, $1 of measured infrastructure cost is supported by $3 of eligible platform revenue. This does not mean $2 is profit: payroll, legal/compliance, taxes, insurance, sales/marketing, reserves, creator/provider obligations and other operating costs still matter.

## 4. SCALE CAREFULLY
Only scale cohorts, geography or paid acquisition after:
- security and abuse controls pass
- support/refund process works
- contribution margin is acceptable
- capacity is monitored
- accessibility QA passes
- regulatory/provider dependencies are gated correctly
- rollback/feature flags exist

Scale one constrained dimension at a time: geography, cohort, service category, or acquisition channel.

## Community Safety / Guardian Path
TRYAMM may support a lawful non-vigilante community-safety business/program focused on:
- visible community presence
- safe-walk / accompaniment services
- youth engagement and positive activities
- conflict interruption/de-escalation by appropriately trained people
- business/community check-ins
- resource navigation
- event/community steward roles
- incident reporting and emergency-service referral
- training in situational awareness, first aid/CPR where qualified, de-escalation, bystander support and safeguarding

Not permitted as product positioning without applicable licensing/authority:
- impersonating police
- detention/arrest powers beyond ordinary law
- weapons-based patrol services
- pursuit or confrontation mandates
- vigilante enforcement
- protected-class profiling or predictive-crime labeling

Business models can include community contracts, event stewardship, employer/campus accompaniment programs, subscriptions for approved safe-walk services, sponsored youth programs, and government/nonprofit contracts where eligible.

## Operating dashboard
TRYAMM should report:
ORGANIC HEALTH | COMMERCE HEALTH | CONTRIBUTION HEALTH | SUSTAINABILITY | SECURITY | ACCESSIBILITY | SUPPORT | RESERVES | SCALE STATUS.

The system should display the current phase and why it is or is not allowed to advance.
