# TRYAMM Community Safety & Restoration Business

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT / BUSINESS SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Create a profitable, non-vigilante community safety and restoration line that helps neighborhoods reduce harm through presence, prevention, escorts, youth pathways, de-escalation, reporting, events, resource navigation and partnerships. This is not policing and does not grant law-enforcement powers.

## Operating model
EDUCATION → OPPORTUNITY → BUSINESS → COMMERCE → DELIVERY → SAFETY → COMMUNITY → REVENUE → INFRASTRUCTURE → MORE FREE/USEFUL PATHWAYS.

Community Safety sits inside that loop as a paid service line that can also produce measurable community outcomes.

## Core roles
- Community Ambassador
- Safe Walk / Safe Ride coordinator
- Event Safety Ambassador
- Youth Peace Mentor
- School/after-school presence partner
- Business corridor ambassador
- Transit/parking-lot accompaniment partner
- Outreach/resource navigator
- De-escalation facilitator where trained
- Digital Safety / Scam Education ambassador
- Reentry/Second Chance mentor

No role may claim police authority, detain people, conduct searches, carry restricted weapons as part of ordinary ambassador duties, or engage in vigilantism. Licensed security work is a separate regulated lane when provided through appropriately licensed providers.

## Services
### Safe Walk / Safe Ride
Scheduled or on-demand accompaniment for students, seniors, workers, creators, customers and other users. Includes check-in/out, route sharing, arrival confirmation and emergency escalation pathways.

### Business Corridor Safety
Participating merchants can subscribe for ambassador presence, opening/closing accompaniment, customer escorts, incident reporting, lighting/accessibility issue reporting and connection to appropriate city/property resources.

### Event Safety
Non-enforcement event ambassadors for Soul Ascension, community events, schools, creator events and Marketplace activations. Crowd information, lost-person support, accessibility assistance, conflict de-escalation and escalation to licensed security/emergency services when necessary.

### Youth Peace Missions
Sports, music, gaming, media, coding, entrepreneurship, trades, mentoring, restorative activities and paid/eligible opportunity pathways that compete with violence for attention, status and belonging.

### Community Check-In
Voluntary neighborhood/business reporting for broken lights, dangerous intersections, abandoned-property concerns, accessibility barriers, repeated delivery problems and other non-emergency environmental issues. Aggregate trends can feed Community Gap Finder.

### Digital Safety
Scam awareness, account security, marketplace fraud education, online harassment reporting and youth digital-safety education.

## Revenue model
Possible legitimate revenue lanes:
- business corridor subscriptions
- property-management/community-association contracts
- event ambassador contracts
- school/community-organization contracts where permitted
- employer safe-walk/late-shift programs
- hospitality/venue support contracts
- transportation-hub partnerships
- sponsor-funded youth Peace Missions
- municipal/nonprofit service contracts where eligible
- training program fees for organizations
- enterprise dashboard/reporting subscriptions
- insurance/risk-partner programs only where lawful and appropriately structured

Do not charge people in imminent danger for emergency routing.

## Example pricing framework
Pricing is configurable by geography and labor economics. Suggested starting test bands, not guarantees:
- Small business corridor plan: $99–$299/month per participating business or pooled district pricing
- Event ambassador staffing: $35–$75/hour billed per trained ambassador depending on market, insurance and partner costs
- Safe Walk employer/school program: $500–$5,000+/month depending on service window and usage
- Property/community contract: $2,500–$25,000+/month depending on staffing, hours and scope
- Training/enterprise dashboard: subscription or per-seat pricing

Actual worker compensation, insurance, licensing requirements and local labor law determine viable pricing.

## Economics / Sustainability Engine
Track:
REVENUE → direct ambassador labor → insurance/provider costs → dispatch/messaging/maps → supervision → training → support → contribution margin.

Only positive contribution margin counts toward TRYAMM's 3.00× sustainability objective.

## Holo/JARVIS integration
Personal JARVIS can request a Safe Walk, share a route with consent and confirm arrival.
Vehicle JARVIS can coordinate approved Safe Ride arrival/pickup.
Business JARVIS can request corridor/event services and view incident trends.
Community JARVIS can surface aggregate issues and prevention missions.
Security JARVIS monitors abuse/fraud, not individual guilt.

## Community Digital Twin
Optional aggregate layer:
PEOPLE/PROGRAMS ↔ BUSINESSES ↔ SCHOOLS ↔ TRANSIT ↔ LIGHTING/ENVIRONMENT ↔ INCIDENT TYPES ↔ YOUTH OPPORTUNITIES ↔ SAFE ROUTES ↔ RESOURCES.

Use aggregate/privacy-preserving data. Never publish a map identifying a particular person as dangerous or predict a child's criminality.

## Community Gap Finder integration
Examples:
- repeated unsafe-walk requests → lighting/transit/safe-route gap
- youth opportunity demand → program/job/mentor gap
- repeated business corridor incidents → environmental/service gap
- accessibility complaints → infrastructure gap

Gap Finder routes to funding, partners, training, business opportunities and measurable missions.

## Measurement
Track outcomes such as:
- Safe Walk/Safe Ride completions
- arrival confirmations
- response/escalation time
- businesses served
- event shifts completed
- youth enrolled/completing programs
- mentor matches
- jobs/internships/business pathways created
- environmental issues resolved
- customer/participant safety satisfaction

Do not claim crime reduction caused by TRYAMM unless supported by a valid evaluation design.

## Guardrails
- no vigilantism
- no bounty hunting
- no unauthorized detention/search
- no public accusation feeds
- no predictive-policing scores on individuals
- no monetization of crisis data
- no weapons marketplace within this program
- no representing unlicensed ambassadors as licensed guards/police
- high-risk incidents route to qualified emergency/security professionals

## Launch path
1. Build app-side Safe Walk/check-in/arrival workflows.
2. Pilot with trained non-enforcement ambassadors and one partner corridor/event.
3. Measure cost per completed service and contribution margin.
4. Add supervisor/dispatch dashboard and insurance/provider requirements.
5. Sell B2B/B2G/B2N contracts only where scope and local rules are satisfied.
6. Feed positive contribution into Platform Sustainability Engine.
