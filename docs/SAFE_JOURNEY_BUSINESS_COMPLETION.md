# TRYAMM Safe Journey / Community Companion Service

Status: CODED CORE + PILOT PATHWAY / NOT YET LICENSED OR OPERATING

## Purpose
Provide visible, non-enforcement accompaniment and safe-journey support through presence, check-ins, safe routing, de-escalation, accessibility support, emergency escalation, documentation, and connection to qualified services.

This is not vigilante activity, armed security, policing, or law-enforcement impersonation. Jurisdiction-specific licensing, insurance, employment classification, background-check and contract requirements must be reviewed before operating.

## Customer segments
Hospitals; senior living communities; employers with night shifts; universities; housing organizations; shopping districts; community organizations; ministries; disability programs; funded programs; and eventually municipalities or other public-sector buyers where procurement rules are satisfied.

## Product journeys
1. Member books a journey or requests an immediate check-in.
2. Dispatcher or automated matching assigns an eligible trained worker.
3. Member receives worker identity/ETA and privacy notice.
4. Worker checks in and begins accompaniment.
5. Live location is used only with explicit consent and retained only as necessary.
6. Worker provides presence, routing and de-escalation; no enforcement role.
7. Incident/escalation workflow handles threats, medical issues, harassment, route problems or unreachable participants.
8. Member checks out safely; satisfaction and service evidence are recorded.
9. Organization receives aggregate SLA reporting, not unnecessary personal journey details.

## Contractable service packages
Pricing is illustrative and must be validated against actual labor, insurance, travel, technology and local wage costs before sale.

### Pilot Package
$7,500–$15,000 for 60–90 days depending on hours/coverage.
Includes onboarding, limited coverage hours, dispatcher support, journey reporting, training setup and pilot review.

### Site Coverage
Starting target: $5,000–$12,000/month per location for limited recurring coverage, with higher pricing for overnight, multi-worker, transport-heavy or 24/7 models.

### Per-Journey Sponsored Program
Target planning range: $25–$60 per completed standard journey before specialized staffing/transport needs. Contract pricing should be based on true loaded cost, service window and SLA.

### Enterprise / Multi-Site
Custom annual contract with minimum commitment, service levels, dedicated implementation, analytics and account management.

## Margin target
`gross contribution per service = contract revenue - direct labor - direct insurance allocation - direct travel/provider cost - direct technology/communications cost - refunds/credits`.

Do not promise 50% margin at launch. Use 50% as an optimization target only where ethical wages, insurance, safety, response time and service quality remain intact.

## Renewal target
A 50%+ first-pilot renewal rate can be tracked as an internal target, not a guarantee. Renewal evidence should use measurable service data:
- journeys requested/completed
- completion rate
- on-time rate
- average response time
- incidents and escalation rate
- member satisfaction
- coverage hours
- no-shows/cancellations
- cost per completed journey
- accessibility accommodation success
- partner complaints/resolution

## 500-Journey Evidence Package
At 500 completed journeys, produce a partner-ready report containing service volume, on-time performance, completion, incidents, response times, satisfaction, coverage patterns, cost per journey and lessons learned. Do not claim crime reduction unless a valid evaluation design supports that causal claim.

## Why organizations renew
The service is sold as measurable operational support: safer-feeling access, reliable accompaniment, documented journey completion, accessibility support, overnight/shift-change coverage, member satisfaction and a defined escalation process. Partners renew based on agreed outcomes and service quality, not fear-based marketing.

## Profitability pathway
Yes, this can become profitable if pricing exceeds fully loaded service cost and utilization is high enough. Main economic levers:
- multi-site contracts
- scheduled demand that improves worker utilization
- sponsored journeys
- annual commitments
- dispatcher efficiency
- employer/university/hospital contracts
- training/licensing partnerships
- careful geographic density to reduce travel time
- software subscription/analytics layer for partners

Never cut safety staffing, screening, insurance or wages merely to hit margin targets.

## Community outcome pathway
The service can create jobs in companionship, dispatch, accessibility support, training, supervision, operations and community engagement while offering a non-vigilante alternative centered on presence and de-escalation.
