# Digital Asset Economy + Business Time Machine + YAHAVAH Way Grocery

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## 1. Digital Asset Marketplace
TRYAMM supports the sale/licensing of digital designs for furniture, vehicles, homes/mansions, rooms, clothing, props, game objects, holographic scenes, music/video assets, architecture concepts and manufacturing-ready candidates.

### Asset states
CONCEPT → DIGITAL DESIGN → RIGHTS VERIFIED → MARKETPLACE LISTING → LICENSE/PURCHASE → MANUFACTURING CANDIDATE → STRUCTURAL REVIEW → MATERIAL REVIEW → SAFETY CHECK → MANUFACTURING APPROVED → BUILD.

Digital sale does NOT imply physical manufacturability. CAD/STL/STEP/other manufacturing exports remain subject to applicable engineering, structural, product-safety and regulatory checks.

### Revenue split
Each asset listing may define platform share, creator/designer share, collaborator/rightsholder share, affiliate/referral share and optional community/mission allocation. Money Engine is the source of truth. Never hard-code universal royalty percentages; use listing/contract terms.

### Asset classes
- digital furniture/interior packages
- vehicle concepts/accessories
- home/mansion/room designs
- clothing/fashion patterns
- game/AR/VR/MR objects
- holographic scenes/stages
- music/video templates and production assets
- creator brand packs
- manufacturing candidates
- education/tutorial assets

## 2. Business JARVIS Time Machine
Purpose: help owners learn from historical decisions and simulate alternatives without pretending to predict the future.

PAST → DECISION → CONSEQUENCE → ALTERNATIVE → SIMULATION → NEXT ACTION.

### Time Machine record
- event/date/time window
- observed business state
- decision taken
- available alternatives at that time
- measured consequence
- assumptions/unknowns
- counterfactual simulation(s)
- lessons learned
- recommended next action
- evidence links/metrics

### Example
`Free delivery campaign → orders +28% → margin down 9% → courier delays +17% → simulate $40 minimum → project improved contribution margin → approve small test.`

Counterfactuals are advisory and labeled as simulations; historical facts stay separate from generated alternatives.

## 3. YAHAVAH Way Grocery
A faith-aligned but publicly usable grocery/community-commerce concept emphasizing stewardship, fair dealing, healthy access, local/community suppliers, affordability and service.

### Core store lanes
FRESH | PANTRY | FAMILY | SCHOOL | SENIOR | LOCAL | BLACK-OWNED SUPPLIERS | DISABILITY-OWNED SUPPLIERS | FAITH/SET-APART | BULK/GROUP BUY | COMMUNITY FRIDGE | DELIVERY.

Participation in faith-branded programming is voluntary. Basic shopping, jobs and community-resource access do not require a religious identity.

### Grocery workflow
DISCOVER → VERIFIED PRODUCT DATA → CART → HOLO COUPON → GROUP BUY/SPONSOR OPTION → JIN PAY → PICK/PACK → HOLO DELIVERY/PICKUP → LIVE TRACKING → PROOF/ISSUE → MONEY ENGINE.

### Household + Holo Fridge
Personal Holo Fridge may maintain user-authorized inventory/expiration reminders and generate shopping lists. JARVIS should prioritize reducing food waste rather than maximizing basket size.

### Community Fridge / food access
Verified participating organizations can publish pantry/fridge availability and sponsored-meal inventory. Emergency Path can privately route users to eligible resources. Individual help requests are not exposed to advertising or public impact dashboards.

### Supplier Exchange integration
Grocery procurement may surface qualified farms, manufacturers, distributors, bakeries, food producers, packaging vendors and logistics providers. Community Gap Finder can identify missing supply capacity from aggregate demand.

### Group economics
Voluntary 12-Circulation participation: grocery revenue can support participating suppliers/workers/businesses where practical. Do not claim money is technically trapped in the community or force exclusionary purchasing.

### Ghost/home kitchen connection
Prepared-food listings may connect to verified restaurant, commissary, ghost-kitchen or legally permitted home/cottage-food businesses. Regulatory/food-safety status must be explicit.

### Pricing and sustainability
Potential eligible TRYAMM revenue sources: transparent marketplace/service fees, Holo Delivery fees, sponsored Holo Coupons, supplier services, Business JARVIS/merchant subscriptions, HoloGPT/AI Actions, premium data/analytics and approved advertising.

Restricted food-assistance/sponsor funds remain segregated from platform operating revenue.

## 4. Platform Sustainability relationship
Digital assets and grocery commerce add diversified revenue without requiring TRYAMM to own all inventory.

DIGITAL CREATOR → ASSET SALE/LICENSE → MONEY ENGINE SPLIT → TRYAMM ELIGIBLE REVENUE.

GROCERY MERCHANT/SUPPLIER → SALE → DELIVERY/PLATFORM SERVICE → TRYAMM ELIGIBLE REVENUE.

These feed the Platform Sustainability Engine while creator earnings, restricted mission funds, taxes, provider settlements, refunds and reserves remain excluded from available platform revenue.

## 5. 3.00× objective
TRYAMM target remains eligible platform revenue / measured infrastructure cost >= 3.00.

Digital assets can be high-margin because a verified digital product can sell/license multiple times with relatively low marginal delivery cost, though storage, rendering, moderation, rights validation and creator obligations still count.

Grocery/delivery is lower-margin and operationally heavier, so Business Simulator must model contribution margin, refunds, courier costs, spoilage/fulfillment issues and customer support before promotions scale.
