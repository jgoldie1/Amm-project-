# Faith Treasury Governance

## Purpose

Faith Treasury is a protected ministry accounting and governance layer. It must remain distinct from TRYAMM commercial revenue, El Saturn Fin commercial services, creator balances, marketplace revenue, and other company operating funds.

## Money separation

Contributions are recorded by ministry, donor, amount, date, and designated ministry fund. Ministry funds are not company working capital. Commercial charges for genuine services are separate transactions supported by written agreements, invoices, approvals, and reasonable pricing evidence.

## Related-party and partnership review

Before a ministry approves a partnership, joint venture, management arrangement, or other material related-party transaction, the governance record should document:

1. The charitable or religious purpose furthered by the arrangement.
2. The counterparty and whether it is related to an officer, director, founder, manager, or affiliated business.
3. Conflict-of-interest disclosures and recusals.
4. Independent decision makers responsible for approval.
5. Comparable offers, market pricing, valuation, or other fair-market-value evidence appropriate to the transaction.
6. Written terms, deliverables, duration, termination rights, and payment obligations.
7. A determination that the arrangement does not create impermissible private benefit or private inurement.
8. Periodic review dates and the evidence considered during each review.

## Periodic review cadence

Material partnerships and management arrangements should be reviewed at least annually and sooner when pricing, ownership, scope, control, compensation, or charitable purpose materially changes. High-risk or related-party arrangements should be reviewed before renewal or expansion.

## Faith Treasury controls

- Separate faith contribution ledger from commercial ledgers.
- Separate ministry settlement destination from company operating accounts.
- No automatic diversion of a tithe or offering into an affiliated company account.
- Related-party payments require independent review and fair-market-value/comparability evidence before approval.
- Restricted funds retain their designation through allocation and expenditure records.
- Donor-sensitive information stays off-chain; blockchain anchoring, if used, should contain only non-sensitive proofs or hashes.
- Contribution statements and tax language remain subject to ministry CPA/legal approval before production use.
- Audit exports preserve contribution, approval, contract, invoice, allocation, and expenditure evidence.

## Architecture

FAITH GIVING -> PROCESSOR VERIFICATION -> MINISTRY LEDGER -> DESIGNATED FUND -> INDEPENDENT AUTHORIZATION -> QUALIFYING MINISTRY EXPENDITURE -> AUDIT RECORD

RELATED-PARTY NEED -> CONFLICT DISCLOSURE -> COMPARABILITY / FAIR-MARKET-VALUE EVIDENCE -> INDEPENDENT REVIEW -> CONTRACT -> INVOICE -> PAYMENT -> PERIODIC REVIEW

This module is designed as a governance and recordkeeping control. It does not itself determine tax deductibility, legal status, bank-charter status, or whether a particular expenditure qualifies under federal or state law.
