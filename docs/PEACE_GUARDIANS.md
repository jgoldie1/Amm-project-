# TRYAMM Peace Guardians

Status: LOCKED COMMUNITY-SAFETY SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Create a healthy civilian pathway to reduce community violence without turning volunteers into police, armed security guards, vigilantes or bounty hunters. Peace Guardians focus on prevention, safe presence, de-escalation, youth support, resource navigation, accompaniment, mentoring and evidence-backed community partnerships.

## Brand / legal distinction
`TRYAMM Peace Guardians` is an original TRYAMM program. Do not present it as affiliated with the Guardian Angels organization or copy their protected branding/uniforms. Historical inspiration may be acknowledged editorially, but TRYAMM's model is public-health/community-service focused.

## Core principle
PREVENT → DE-ESCALATE → ACCOMPANY → CONNECT → DOCUMENT SAFELY → REFER → FOLLOW UP → MEASURE.

Peace Guardians do NOT:
- carry weapons as part of the program;
- chase or detain people;
- conduct searches;
- impersonate police/security officers;
- interfere with active law-enforcement operations;
- publish suspected offenders or unverified accusations;
- use disability, race, neighborhood, school, criminal history or behavior as a predictive 'danger score'.

Imminent-danger situations are routed to appropriate emergency services and trained responders.

## Lanes
### Safe Walk / Safe Arrival
Voluntary accompaniment for students, seniors, disabled users, workers, creators and community members traveling between approved locations. Check-in, expected arrival, trusted-contact option and arrival confirmation.

### Peace Routes
Community-verified safer walking/transit routes based on lighting, open businesses, public facilities, transit access and user-reported conditions. Avoid presenting any route as guaranteed safe.

### Violence Interruption Partner Lane
Qualified violence-interruption/community organizations can receive referrals where available. TRYAMM volunteers do not substitute for trained credible messengers or clinicians.

### Youth Peace Corps
Paid/volunteer youth leadership opportunities: sports, games, music, media, coding, trades, tutoring, community cleanup, mentoring and Peace Missions. The goal is belonging, skills, income pathways and leadership—not surveillance.

### School Arrival / Dismissal Network
Schools/community partners can organize trained adult presence, safe routes, check-in points and resource referrals subject to school/district approval and child-safeguarding rules.

### Hospital / Recovery Bridge
Where formal partners exist, connect people affected by violence to qualified hospital-based violence intervention, counseling, case management, mentoring, reentry, employment and family resources.

### Community Safe Places
Verified participating businesses, ministries, libraries, community centers and other approved sites may display `Peace Place` status with current hours, accessibility facts, available assistance and emergency-routing instructions. Verification state is visible.

### Conflict De-escalation
Training in communication, emotional regulation, bystander safety, recognizing escalation, maintaining distance, calling for professional help and leaving unsafe scenes. No physical-intervention requirement.

### Lost Sheep / Emergency Path integration
Food, housing, violence escape, transportation, recovery, legal aid, reentry, employment, youth/family and faith/community pathways are accessible from Peace Guardians without publicly exposing help requests.

## Stubbs AI / JARVIS support
JARVIS can:
- help a user choose a safe-next-step option;
- start a Safe Walk check-in;
- show verified Peace Places/resources;
- notify a trusted contact with consent;
- prepare incident/resource notes for the user;
- route to Emergency Path;
- suggest non-violent conflict-resolution resources;
- surface youth/jobs/training opportunities.

JARVIS must not diagnose criminal intent, identify 'likely criminals', encourage confrontation, or autonomously contact third parties except within explicit approved workflows.

## Community Gap Finder integration
Possible gaps:
- no after-school programs;
- insufficient youth jobs;
- poor lighting/unsafe infrastructure reports;
- no accessible safe-walk option;
- insufficient trauma/recovery support;
- shortage of mentors;
- transportation gap;
- lack of violence-interruption partners;
- vacant-lot/community-environment issues;
- insufficient reentry employment.

Output: NEED → EVIDENCE → PARTNERS → FUNDING → PROGRAM → MILESTONES → OUTCOME.

## Workforce pathway
Peace Guardians creates lawful non-law-enforcement roles such as:
- community navigator;
- safe-walk coordinator;
- youth mentor;
- outreach assistant;
- dispatch/check-in coordinator;
- resource specialist;
- accessibility coordinator;
- program trainer;
- community data/evaluation assistant;
- event peace ambassador.

Any role requiring a professional license, security license, mandated reporter training, clinical qualification or government credential must be performed only by appropriately qualified people.

## Impact metrics
Privacy-preserving measures:
- Safe Walks completed;
- youth enrolled in constructive programs;
- mentor matches;
- jobs/internships connected;
- referrals to qualified violence-intervention/recovery partners;
- Safe Places verified;
- community gaps funded/closed;
- repeat-resource engagement;
- participant-reported sense of safety;
- school/community project completion;
- crisis escalations handled according to protocol.

Do not claim crime reduction causation without appropriate evaluation design.

## Why this model
Community violence prevention works best as a comprehensive approach combining immediate risk reduction with improvements in economic security, education, youth opportunity, environments, outreach and coordinated services. Peace Guardians therefore connects directly to Launchpad, Student JARVIS, Supplier Exchange, Disability Opportunity, Lost Sheep, Community Gap Finder, grants/opportunities, Holo Delivery, Academy and Community Spotlight.
