# Community Gap Finder + Opportunity Alert Engine

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Turn community needs into actionable pathways: identify missing suppliers, jobs, training, services and resources; match qualified people and organizations to opportunities; teach users what they qualify for; and keep them engaged through useful ongoing alerts and progress.

## Community Gap Finder

### Inputs
Use consented, privacy-preserving and appropriately aggregated data from:
- Supplier Exchange demand and unsuccessful searches
- business capability profiles and procurement categories
- Talent/Opportunity searches
- Academy skill demand/completion
- voluntary community needs surveys
- Launchpad and Find Your Way resource categories (aggregated; never expose individual crisis requests)
- public/open procurement and economic-development data where permitted
- employer job/skill demand
- regional price/capacity signals

### Gap types
- missing local/community supplier
- insufficient supplier capacity
- job/skill shortage
- training shortage
- childcare/transportation/accessibility barrier
- food/housing/resource-navigation gap
- creator/media production gap
- technology/digital-access gap
- youth opportunity gap
- reentry/Second Chance opportunity gap
- disability-accessibility/employment gap

### Output
For each gap produce:
`NEED → EVIDENCE → LOCATION/SCOPE → PEOPLE AFFECTED → EXISTING CAPACITY → MISSING CAPACITY → TRAINING PATH → BUSINESS/SUPPLIER PATH → FUNDING PATH → PARTNERS → MILESTONES → IMPACT METRICS`.

The system recommends opportunities; it must not claim causal certainty from incomplete platform data.

## Grant & Funding Qualification Alerts

### User profiles
Users/businesses/organizations may voluntarily maintain an Opportunity Profile containing relevant non-sensitive eligibility facts such as entity type, location, industry/NAICS where applicable, years operating, revenue band, employee band, project type, requested funding range, certifications, nonprofit status, school/student status and program interests.

Sensitive/protected characteristics are collected or used only when necessary, lawful and explicitly relevant to a program; never infer disability, race, religion, criminal history or other sensitive eligibility from behavior.

### Matching states
Every funding opportunity is classified:
- `LIKELY MATCH` — available profile facts satisfy known mandatory criteria
- `POSSIBLE MATCH` — some criteria match but facts are missing
- `NOT A MATCH` — a known mandatory criterion fails
- `VERIFY WITH FUNDER` — eligibility language is ambiguous
- `CLOSED/EXPIRED`

Never promise a grant award. Display source, deadline, award range, match requirement, geography, eligibility, required documents, application link/source and last-verified date.

### Alerts
Users choose alert channels/preferences for:
- new likely-match grant
- deadline approaching
- application opened
- missing document
- application milestone
- scholarship/college aid
- supplier/procurement opportunity
- apprenticeship/job/internship
- creator sponsorship
- emergency/resource opportunity

No spam: digest controls, quiet hours and unsubscribe controls are mandatory.

## Grant Readiness Passport
A voluntary Passport pathway can track preparation rather than falsely certifying funding eligibility:
- entity/business basics complete
- EIN/registration documented where applicable
- SAM/UEI status where applicable
- capability statement
- budget template
- project narrative
- impact metrics
- financial documents checklist
- insurance/certification checklist
- references/past performance
- application calendar

## Disability Inclusion — First-Class Platform Requirement

Disabled people are participants, creators, students, workers, entrepreneurs, gamers, mentors and leaders—not a separate afterthought.

### Accessibility Profile
Voluntary settings can include:
- screen reader / semantic navigation
- keyboard-only navigation
- switch access
- voice control
- one-handed mode
- larger targets/text
- reduced motion
- captions/live captions
- transcript preference
- audio description
- high contrast
- simplified/cognitive-support mode
- extra-time preference for non-competitive learning where appropriate
- communication preference

Do not require a medical diagnosis to use accessibility features.

### Opportunity matching
Employers/programs may publish verified accessibility information: remote option, physical accessibility, adaptive equipment, flexible scheduling, captioning/interpreting availability and accommodation contact/process. The platform does not infer a user's disability or disclose it without permission.

### Disability Opportunity Lane
Optional discovery lane for accessible jobs, training, grants, assistive-technology resources, entrepreneurship programs, creator opportunities, transportation/housing resource navigation and disability-owned-business opportunities where relevant and lawful.

## Reasons to Stay on TRYAMM
Retention should come from recurring utility rather than addictive design:
1. `TODAY` — personalized next actions across learning, work, projects and community missions.
2. `OPPORTUNITIES FOR YOU` — grants, jobs, suppliers, scholarships, auditions and programs with explainable matching.
3. `LEARNING PASSPORT` — visible progress and next credential/project.
4. `MY PROJECTS` — saved Creator Projects, applications and missions.
5. `MY COMMUNITY` — businesses, mentors, groups, Soul Ascension and Community Spotlight.
6. `MY MONEY LEARNING` — simulated financial progress and 12-Circulation Challenge.
7. `MY IMPACT` — voluntary service/project outcomes.
8. `STUBBS AI NAVIGATOR` — asks what the user wants to accomplish and turns it into next steps.
9. `WEEKLY OPPORTUNITY DIGEST` — user-controlled summary of new matches and deadlines.
10. `GIVE BACK` — after reaching a milestone, mentor, hire, teach, buy from or support the next participant.

## Core workflow
`PROFILE/GOAL → GAP FINDER → MATCH ENGINE → WHY YOU MATCH → READINESS CHECKLIST → LEARN/FIX MISSING ITEMS → APPLY/BUILD/CONNECT → TRACK → RESULT → PASSPORT/IMPACT → NEXT OPPORTUNITY`.

## Privacy and safety
- crisis/help data is segregated from advertising/recommendation monetization
- sensitive eligibility data is minimized and permissioned
- explain why a match was made
- users can correct profile facts
- no grant/benefit approval guarantees
- no predatory lending ranking in emergency pathways
- minors receive age-appropriate safeguards
- accessibility preferences are not used to reduce opportunity visibility
