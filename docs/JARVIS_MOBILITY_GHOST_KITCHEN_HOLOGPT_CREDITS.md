## 1. JARVIS Holographic Command System
JARVIS is the universal front door to TRYAMM, powered by Stubbs AI + HoloGPT. It is an original TRYAMM interaction system inspired by the idea of a conversational vehicle/robot command assistant, without copying protected KITT/Voltron names, voices, characters, visual assets or trade dress in the shipped product.

Modes: PERSONAL | STUDENT | CREATOR | BUSINESS | HOME | VEHICLE | DELIVERY | SECURITY | COMMUNITY.

JARVIS should replace menu hunting with intent routing: user asks for an outcome; JARVIS finds the correct authorized TRYAMM workflow.

## 2. Vehicle JARVIS / Mobility Copilot
Vehicle JARVIS is a safety-first mobility assistant. Initial software capabilities:
- destination/route preparation through approved mapping provider
- trip planning, calendar-aware departure suggestions
- EV/fuel/charging planning where provider data exists
- maintenance reminders and service history
- vehicle manual/verified-data Q&A
- hands-free media/communications controls through approved integrations
- accessibility/one-handed/voice-first interface
- Holo Delivery driver/courier mode
- package/food pickup queue
- family trip/status sharing with consent
- parking-location memory
- emergency-assistance shortcut
- vehicle expense/mileage records for business users

Safety boundary: JARVIS does not claim autonomous driving, override safety systems, defeat driver monitoring, remotely control unsupported vehicle functions, or issue unsafe driving instructions. Safety-critical vehicle control requires certified vehicle/OEM interfaces and applicable regulatory approval.

## 3. Modular / Robotic JARVIS concept
Future hardware abstraction can support authorized devices: vehicle displays, phones, tablets, TVs, home devices, robots, delivery robots and drones. Use a capability registry so JARVIS asks what a device is certified/authorized to do rather than assuming control.

DEVICE → CAPABILITIES → AUTHORIZATION → SAFETY POLICY → HUMAN APPROVAL WHERE REQUIRED → COMMAND → TELEMETRY → AUDIT.

This lets multiple approved devices coordinate like a team without pretending TRYAMM has built transforming robots today.

## 5. Ghost Kitchen / Home Food Business Network
Create a food-business pathway for legally permitted operators:
HOME FOOD / COTTAGE FOOD (where permitted) | LICENSED HOME KITCHEN (where permitted) | SHARED/COMMISSARY KITCHEN | GHOST KITCHEN | RESTAURANT.

TRYAMM must not label a home kitchen approved merely because a seller signs up. Each seller/location has a verification state:
DRAFT → REQUIREMENTS IDENTIFIED → DOCUMENTS SUBMITTED → VERIFIED BY PLATFORM EVIDENCE REVIEW → ACTIVE, with SUSPENDED/EXPIRED states.

Required facts depend on jurisdiction and food type and may include local/state licenses, health-department approval, food-handler/manager credentials, commissary agreement, inspections, zoning/home-business permission, insurance, tax registration and restrictions on delivery/shipping.

Stubbs AI can create a jurisdiction checklist from authoritative sources but must label uncertain requirements `VERIFY WITH AUTHORITY`.

## 7. Virtual restaurant / multi-brand kitchen
A compliant kitchen may operate multiple disclosed virtual brands while retaining the legal operator and preparation location internally for compliance/audit. Customer-facing disclosures must comply with applicable law and platform transparency rules. Do not use virtual brands to hide unsafe operators or manipulate ratings.

Business JARVIS can coordinate menu demand, prep capacity, inventory, promotions, supplier replenishment and delivery demand across brands.

## 8. Holo Food Safety Passport
Per location, track evidence metadata and expiration dates for required approvals. Never claim TRYAMM certification where government/third-party approval is actually required. Allergen/ingredient information must originate from merchant/verified sources; Stubbs AI must not invent food-safety assurances.

## 9. Holo Delivery integration
Food, groceries and packages share Holo Dispatch infrastructure but maintain category-specific rules. Live tracking states:
CONFIRMED → PREPARING/PROCESSING → READY → COURIER ASSIGNED → PICKED UP → IN TRANSIT → ARRIVING → DELIVERED → PROOF/ISSUE.

Support scheduled delivery, group orders, sponsor-funded eligible meals, Holo Coupons, privacy-preserving tracking, masked contact, delivery proof and disputes/refunds.
