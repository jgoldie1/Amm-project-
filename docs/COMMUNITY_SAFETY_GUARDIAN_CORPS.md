# TRYAMM Community Safety Guardian Corps

Status: LOCKED SAFETY / BUSINESS / LOCALITY-AWARE SPECIFICATION

## Mission
Create a non-vigilante, non-police-substitute community safety network that helps people get home safely, de-escalate conflict, connect to resources, document hazards, support youth, and coordinate with lawful emergency/public-safety channels when needed.

The model is inspired by the idea of visible community guardianship, but avoids copying third-party names/branding and avoids unlicensed private-security activity.

## Core rule
Community volunteers do NOT automatically become security guards, police, bounty hunters, investigators, or armed responders.

Every locality is classified before launch:
`COMMUNITY_VOLUNTEER_ONLY | LICENSED_SECURITY_REQUIRED | VIOLENCE_INTERVENTION_PARTNER | EMERGENCY_SERVICES_ONLY | LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

Where a service crosses into private-security contracting, investigation, guarding, alarm response, detention, weapons, or other regulated activity, that function stays OFF until the required licensed provider, insurance, training, screening and legal approvals are in place.

## Services that can be designed as lower-risk community support
- Safe Walk / Safe Ride coordination
- school dismissal escort coordination with authorized partners
- elder check-in / wellness-call routing
- lost-child / vulnerable-person resource coordination with authorities and guardians
- de-escalation-trained community presence
- youth mentor / evening activity routing
- neighborhood hazard reporting: broken lights, unsafe crossings, abandoned lots, damaged infrastructure
- community event ambassadors
- transit-stop companion groups
- business closing-time walkout coordination
- victim-resource navigation
- violence-interruption partner referrals
- emergency-path routing
- CPR/AED/first-aid trained volunteer discovery where legally appropriate
- disaster/community response support where authorized

## Prohibited by default
- weapons patrols
- pursuit/chasing suspected offenders
- detention/arrest beyond what local law independently permits to ordinary citizens
- searches of people/property
- impersonating police/security
- collecting dossiers on residents
- facial-recognition watchlists
- predictive-policing scores
- gang/crime labels assigned by AI
- public shaming maps
- covert surveillance
- entering homes/property without lawful authorization
- handling evidence except as instructed by authorities

## AI Guardian Assistant — Powered by Stubbs AI
AI helps with coordination, not coercion.

Allowed assistance:
- classify incoming reports by urgency
- suggest de-escalation scripts
- route to 911/emergency services when danger is imminent
- route non-emergency issues to appropriate city/community resources
- match users with Safe Walk volunteers/partners
- suggest well-lit/public routes from approved map data
- translate messages
- accessibility adaptation through Accessibility Passport
- summarize incident notes with consent
- check volunteer training/credential readiness
- identify neighborhood service gaps from privacy-preserving aggregate data
- recommend youth/community activities instead of punitive interventions
- produce locality compliance checklists

AI must explain uncertainty and never claim legal authority.

## Illinois launch note
Illinois regulates private-security contractor activity and prohibits acting/advertising as a private-security contractor or agency without required licensure. TRYAMM therefore launches the Community Guardian Corps in Illinois as a community-support / routing / de-escalation / safe-walk network unless a licensed security partner is separately integrated. Paid guarding/private-security activity follows IDFPR requirements, including applicable registration/training. See current Illinois sources before activation.

`NEED → SAFE PLACE → MENTOR → SPORTS/GAMES/MUSIC/CODING/TRADES → SCHOOL SUPPORT → JOB/GIG → BUSINESS/CREATOR PATH → COMMUNITY SERVICE → MENTOR NEXT PERSON`.

Community Gap Finder identifies missing evening programs, safe transit, mentors, jobs, lighting, recreation, food access, reentry support and trauma resources using aggregated evidence.
