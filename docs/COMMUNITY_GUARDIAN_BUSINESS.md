# TRYAMM Community Guardian Network

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT + BUSINESS SPECIFICATION

## Mission
Create a healthy civilian community-safety network that reduces harm through visible presence, escorts, de-escalation, youth engagement, safe-business partnerships, rapid resource connection, and evidence-backed reporting. It is not a vigilante organization, police substitute, bounty system, or private militia.

## Core principle
PREVENT → DE-ESCALATE → ESCORT → CONNECT → REPORT → FOLLOW UP → MEASURE.

## What Guardians can do
- walking companions / safe escorts
- school-route presence and parent/guardian coordination where approved
- transit-stop and business-district visibility
- late-night worker escorts
- event/community presence
- trained de-escalation and conflict interruption within legal/non-force boundaries
- youth engagement and referral to Launchpad, sports, music, jobs, mentors, and education
- connect people to Emergency Path, shelters, food, transportation, legal/social-service resources
- observe and document public incidents from a lawful distance
- report hazards, broken lights, abandoned areas, unsafe infrastructure, and recurring community gaps
- Safe Business check-ins and verified refuge locations where businesses opt in
- wellness check-in routing to qualified services, not forced entry or impersonating emergency responders

## Prohibited
- weapons as part of standard Guardian role
- pursuit, detention, interrogation, searches, punishment, bounty hunting, gang/vigilante activity
- pretending to be police/security officers without required licensing/authority
- racial/religious/disability or neighborhood profiling
- publishing private victim/crisis information
- confrontation when withdrawal/911/emergency escalation is safer

## Guardian roles
COMMUNITY GUIDE | SAFE ESCORT | YOUTH MENTOR | EVENT AMBASSADOR | BUSINESS SAFETY LIAISON | RESOURCE NAVIGATOR | DE-ESCALATION LEAD | DISPATCH COORDINATOR.

Where a jurisdiction treats a paid activity as private security, guard, patrol, or similar regulated work, TRYAMM must route the service through appropriately licensed entities/personnel or disable that activity.

## Training Passport
Required modules can include:
- de-escalation
- bystander intervention
- first aid/CPR where provided by qualified trainers
- trauma-informed communication
- disability/accessibility awareness
- youth safeguarding
- conflict boundaries
- anti-profiling/bias controls
- incident documentation
- privacy and evidence handling
- emergency escalation
- local legal boundaries

TRYAMM training does not substitute for state/local licenses required for regulated security or clinical/social-service work.

## Community Safety Map
Privacy-preserving, non-predictive map can show:
- Safe Businesses
- staffed Guardian routes/shifts
- community events
- verified public safety resources
- transit/lighting/infrastructure issues
- community requests aggregated by category

Do not publish individual victims, alleged suspects, minors, private residences, or predictions that label a person as criminal.

## Revenue model
Community Guardian can be a profitable social-impact business when sold as lawful prevention/escort/community-support services.

Potential revenue:
1. Safe Business memberships — businesses pay for scheduled check-ins, employee escort windows, Safe Business listing, incident-support routing, training/resources, and community reports.
2. Property/business district contracts — malls, commercial corridors, campuses, hotels, parking operators, residential communities, and business associations contract for ambassador/escort services where lawful.
3. Event contracts — organizers pay for trained community ambassadors, accessibility support, wayfinding, de-escalation and guest escort, with licensed security separately contracted when required.
4. School/community partnerships — approved youth-safety, mentoring, route-support and after-school programming funded by schools, nonprofits, sponsors or grants.
5. Employer plans — late-shift escort/check-in benefits for staff.
6. Sponsor-funded Guardian Hours — corporations/philanthropy fund free escort/youth/community hours in selected areas.
7. Training contracts — organizations purchase de-escalation, accessibility, bystander, youth-safeguarding and community-response training from qualified instructors.
8. Municipal/nonprofit contracts — only through lawful procurement and program requirements.
9. TRYAMM subscription bundle — premium businesses can include Guardian/Safe Business features in Business JARVIS plans.

## Example pricing framework
Pricing is proposed and must be tested against wages, insurance, licensing, admin, technology and local market costs.
- Safe Business Lite: $29–$49/month
- Safe Business Plus: $99–$199/month
- Employer Escort Benefit: $5–$15/eligible worker/month plus usage or contracted hours
- Community Ambassador/Guardian service: approximately $30–$60+/service hour charged to contracting organization, subject to local wages/licensing/insurance; worker compensation is separate and transparent
- Event/community contract: quote-based
- Training: $49–$199/person or organizational contract pricing depending on course/instructor

Never set worker pay merely as a residual after platform fees. Model worker compensation, payroll/tax classification and insurance first.

## Impact metrics
Measure without creating a social-credit or predictive-policing score:
- escorts completed
- safe-business check-ins
- youth referrals to jobs/education/mentors
- conflicts de-escalated/self-reported
- resources connected
- hazards reported/resolved
- repeat partner contracts
- participant satisfaction
- response/check-in reliability

## Profit + mission loop
BUSINESS/SPONSOR/CONTRACT PAYS → TRAINED PEOPLE ARE EMPLOYED → COMMUNITY RECEIVES SAFE PRESENCE/ESCORT/MENTORING → VERIFIED IMPACT → COMMUNITY SPOTLIGHT/IMPACT REPORT → RENEWAL/EXPANSION.

## What this does
Creates lawful paid work that does not require every participant to become a conventional security guard; provides a pathway for youth/returning citizens/mentors to serve communities in non-enforcement roles; gives businesses and families a practical safety layer; and creates a recurring B2B/B2G/B2N revenue stream that can support TRYAMM while preserving a non-vigilante mission.
