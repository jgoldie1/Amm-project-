# My World: Open City and His & Hers Sports

## Purpose
This document restores two previously discussed TryAMM game programs to the active build registry and defines a safe, original beta scope.

## My World: Open City

My World: Open City is TryAMM's original large-scale open-city life sandbox. It may be inspired by the freedom and scale of modern open-world games, but it must not copy Grand Theft Auto characters, maps, missions, branding, dialogue, music, artwork or protected story elements.

### Beta pillars
- Walk, run, drive and use public transportation.
- Enter Herrin, Chicago-inspired and player-created districts.
- Buy or rent housing and business spaces.
- Work lawful jobs and operate businesses.
- Complete delivery, transport, construction, rescue, sports, music, creator and community missions.
- Use stores, Holo Menu, livestreams, Isaiah AI TV and Middleverse Workforce.
- Join optional emergency-service, racing and competitive challenge paths.
- Create districts and activities through approved user-generated content tools.
- Support keyboard, touch, controller and accessibility presets.

### Safety and IP rules
- No copied GTA assets or names.
- No glorification of real-world criminal organizations.
- Violence is optional, age-gated and separated from the nonviolent life-sandbox path.
- No real-money rewards from unverified gameplay.
- User-generated missions and districts require moderation and rights declarations.

### First vertical slice
1. Enter Globe to Herrin.
2. Spawn at a safe arrival hub.
3. Walk or drive to a job marker.
4. Complete one delivery or transport mission.
5. Visit a player store.
6. Join a livestream event.
7. Return to the hub and inspect progress.

## His & Hers Sports

His & Hers Sports is a shared sports platform with equal gameplay depth, production quality and career systems for men's and women's leagues.

### Sports included in the initial franchise
- Basketball
- Football
- Baseball and softball
- Soccer
- Track and field
- Boxing
- Volleyball
- Tennis
- Racing

### Core systems
- Create male or female athletes with equivalent customization depth.
- School, college, amateur, professional and international career paths.
- Team, league, tournament, season, playoff and championship systems.
- Coaching, training, scouting, recruiting, injuries, recovery and accessibility settings.
- Shared broadcast system through Isaiah AI TV.
- Herrin High School, Greenville University, UIC and future approved university connections.
- Equal statistics, highlights, sponsorship tools and creator coverage.
- Cross-sport profile and progression without forcing one athlete model on every sport.

### First vertical slice
1. Create an athlete.
2. Select basketball or track.
3. Choose a men's or women's competition lane.
4. Complete one tutorial event.
5. Record score, statistics and replay metadata.
6. Publish an approved highlight to Isaiah AI TV.
7. Inspect progression in the creator/player dashboard.

## Shared launch gates
- Original or properly licensed assets only.
- Age-lane and content-policy enforcement.
- Controller, keyboard, touch and one-hand accessibility testing.
- No cash-convertible gameplay rewards before anti-cheat and finance approval.
- Native Unity, Unreal or Godot builds require engine-specific performance and device testing.
- University and team names, logos, uniforms and buildings require permission unless fictionalized.
