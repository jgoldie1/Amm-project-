# TRYAMM Community Safety — Profitable, Non-Vigilante Service

Status: INTEGRATED / GATED

## Positioning
A membership and contract service for accompaniment, dispatcher-backed journey watch, closing-shift coverage, event/community presence, senior/disability accompaniment and safe-arrival check-ins. It is not private policing, vigilante enforcement or incident hunting.

## Revenue lines
- Individual membership
- Family membership
- Employer closing-shift package
- Campus/community contract
- Church/event package
- Senior/disability accompaniment
- Sponsored memberships
- Nonprofit/municipal contract
- Per-journey or per-event add-ons where appropriate

Revenue is earned for availability, dispatch coverage, scheduled accompaniment, contract performance, accessibility support and journey operations. No incident-bounty incentives, arrest incentives, confrontation bonuses or conflict-based compensation.

## Required launch gates
1. Training program approved for each role.
2. Insurance coverage reviewed and active.
3. Legal review by jurisdiction.
4. Written emergency/escalation protocol.
5. Dispatcher staffing/coverage plan.
6. Background-check process defined lawfully and fairly.
7. Privacy review and data-retention policy.
8. Synchronized Journey API tested.
9. Precise location expiration verified.
10. Simulation/red-team exercises passed.

Program cannot move from GATED to LIVE until all applicable gates are approved.

## Core journey
BOOK/SCHEDULE → MEMBER CHECK-IN → ACCOMPANIMENT/DISPATCH WATCH → PERIODIC CHECK-IN → ATTENTION NEEDED IF MISSED → EMERGENCY PROTOCOL IF NECESSARY → SAFE ARRIVAL → LOCATION EXPIRES → AUDIT/QUALITY REVIEW.

## Accessibility
Support one-handed/voice-first check-in, text-only communication, larger controls, captions/transcripts for supported calls, alternate contact methods and extra time where appropriate. Accessibility preferences remain private and permissioned.

## Contract design
Every organization contract defines service hours, locations, included journeys/shifts/events, dispatcher SLA, escalation expectations, customer responsibilities, exclusions, pricing, insurance allocation, data/privacy terms, accessibility needs, reporting and renewal/termination terms.

## Pricing architecture
Do not hard-code one national price before insurance/labor/regulatory costs are known. Use a contribution-margin floor.

Formula:
`Price floor = direct service cost + insurance allocation + training/background-check allocation + technology + legal/compliance allocation + support + target contribution.`

Target each mature package to be contribution-positive and help TRYAMM move toward its broader 3.00× sustainability goal, without degrading service quality or safety.

Suggested launch structure for testing economics (illustrative, not a final market quote):
- Individual: low monthly base + limited included journey watches
- Family: higher monthly base + shared household coverage
- Employer closing shift: per-site monthly contract based on hours/volume
- Campus/community: negotiated contract
- Church/event: per-event or seasonal package
- Senior/disability: membership or per-journey plan, with sponsored option
- Sponsored pool: funder purchases a defined number of eligible journeys/memberships

## Quality metrics
- successful safe-arrival completions
- dispatcher response time
- missed-check-in resolution time
- service availability
- member satisfaction
- accessibility satisfaction
- complaint/incident rate
- training currency
- privacy/data-expiry compliance
- contract renewal
- contribution margin

Do not use number of confrontations, arrests, suspicious-person reports or police contacts as success metrics.

## Community impact
The service can create dispatcher jobs, accompaniment roles, training roles, accessibility-support jobs and local contracts while giving employers, families, churches, campuses and communities a non-confrontational option for helping people get from one place to another more safely.

## Sustainability integration
Eligible platform/service revenue flows to the Platform Sustainability Engine. Restricted sponsor/nonprofit funds stay separately tracked. The Safety business is considered financially healthy only if its real measured contribution remains positive after labor, insurance, screening, training, technology, legal/compliance and support costs.
