## Required before public marketing as an active security provider
- Confirm exact legal entity, public business name, service area, business contact address and phone.
- Verify state and local private security agency, employee, armed-guard, and firearms instructor licensing rules applicable to each advertised service. Do not assume a concealed-carry permit authorizes professional security work.
- Verify actual instructor qualifications and course approval where required.
- Confirm insurance, business policies, incident reporting, contracts, and background screening obligations.
- Approve final descriptions and pricing; do not invent credentials, certifications, reviews, clients, or completed projects.
- Replace generic inquiry email with an approved dedicated business inbox; implement consent-aware CRM and spam controls when desired.
- Verify HTTPS hosting route, mobile appearance, keyboard navigation, actual form submission, and response receipt.
- Add privacy notice and terms before collecting data through a server form; keep sensitive information out of ordinary inquiry forms.
- Test one complete lead-to-consultation workflow and confirm ownership of domain and hosting.
- Enable payments only after licensing, merchant verification, pricing, refund policy, and webhook reconciliation are established.
