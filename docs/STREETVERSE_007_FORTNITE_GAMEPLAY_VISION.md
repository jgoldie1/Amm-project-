# StreetVerse Gameplay Vision: Spy Missions + Shared World + Economy

This document locks the 3-part gameplay direction for StreetVerse.

## 1. Cinematic Spy-Mission Layer

StreetVerse missions should use a 007-style structure without copying protected characters, stories, branding, maps, or assets.

Core loop:

1. Benny / Stubbs AI gives a briefing.
2. Player selects loadout, disguise, gadget, and vehicle.
3. Player infiltrates a location using stealth, social engineering, hacking, driving, investigation, or direct action.
4. Missions support alternate routes and multiple solutions.
5. Player extracts, receives XP/currency, and can capture the best moment as a Reel.

Example vertical slice: **The Loop — Night Extraction**

- Target: recover an encrypted Business Passport key.
- Location: downtown Chicago.
- Route A: rooftop -> service elevator -> secure floor.
- Route B: social disguise -> lobby -> restricted access.
- Escape: vehicle chase or stealth extraction.
- Reward: XP, StreetVerse currency, unlocks, mission rating, Reel clip.

## 2. Living Shared-World Layer

StreetVerse should use a Fortnite-style shared-world philosophy without copying Fortnite assets, maps, UI, characters, or proprietary mechanics.

The city remains active around the mission system. Players can:

- Form crews and meet friends.
- Join LIVE and PK events.
- Race and use vehicles.
- Enter creator challenges and tournaments.
- Shop, visit businesses, and discover pop-up events.
- Find secret areas, Easter eggs, and seasonal events.
- Move into or out of missions without leaving the larger StreetVerse experience.

The goal is not to make StreetVerse only a battle royale. The city itself is the game platform.

## 3. StreetVerse Original Economy Layer

StreetVerse becomes its own category when gameplay connects directly to the TRYAMM creator and business ecosystem.

Primary loop:

**MISSION -> XP/REWARD -> REEL -> AUDIENCE -> COMMERCE/SPONSOR -> LEDGER/PAYOUT -> REPLAY**

This allows a single playable mission to create multiple platform outcomes:

- gameplay engagement;
- progression and reputation;
- creator content;
- LIVE/reel distribution;
- sponsored challenges;
- marketplace transactions;
- business discovery;
- rewards and payouts;
- replayable missions and recurring events.

## Production Target

Build one complete vertical slice before multiplying mission count:

**Passport -> Chicago StreetVerse -> Benny briefing -> player movement -> vehicle -> infiltration -> objective -> extraction -> XP/reward -> Reel capture -> ledger event.**

Once this path is stable, the same mission framework can support detective, federal-agent, cybersecurity, racing, delivery, creator, business, rescue, investigation, and other mission families.

## What this architecture does

It gives StreetVerse three simultaneous strengths:

1. **Cinematic missions** give players purpose and story.
2. **Persistent multiplayer world** gives the city replayability and social energy.
3. **Creator/business economy** gives completed gameplay a path into content, commerce, sponsorships, and earnings.

This specification is intended as the implementation blueprint for the StreetVerse gameplay vertical slice and future mission factory.
