# TryAMM Global Trade Architecture

## Purpose

Aniyah CrossBorder is the trade operating layer for TryAMM stores, businesses, creators, universities, workforce programs and marketplace sellers operating across Africa, Asia, Canada and other approved regions.

It does not treat international commerce as one generic market. Each country and trade corridor must have its own payment, tax, customs, language, logistics, restricted-product, consumer-protection and data-handling configuration.

## Safety and compliance boundaries

TryAMM must not independently act as a bank, customs broker, freight forwarder, money transmitter, insurer or licensed trade professional unless it obtains the required authority.

The system should route regulated activities through approved partners and record the partner responsible for each action.
