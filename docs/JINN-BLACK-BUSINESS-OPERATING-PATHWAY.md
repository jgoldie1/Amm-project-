# Jinn for Black Business — Operating Pathway

## Goal
Make Jinn the operating layer that helps a participating Black-owned business get paid, run commerce, retain customers, pay suppliers, pursue larger contracts and expand into global/Africa commerce where supported.

## Merchant journey
1. Business joins TryAMM and creates/claims profile.
2. Optional Black Business Directory opt-in and verification level recorded.
3. Jinn onboarding configures seller identity, payout account, country, currency, tax-responsibility status and supported payment rails.
4. Storefront/HoloMenu/restaurant menu configured.
5. Business accepts online checkout, payment links, QR/NFC, and provider-supported Tap to Pay.
6. Customer receives digital receipt by SMS/email/in-app; no paper printer required unless merchant wants one.
7. HoloPass/HoloCoupons/affiliate attribution attach to the same transaction record.
8. Revenue waterfall separates merchant payable, tax liability, TryAMM revenue, tips, affiliate commissions, refunds/reserves and processor fees.
9. Merchant dashboard shows sales, available/pending payout, tax responsibility, refunds/disputes, ad spend, loyalty, coupons and customer acquisition.
10. Jinn supplier tools support wholesale orders, invoices, purchase orders and supplier payments.
11. Procurement Hub can connect contract/RFP opportunities to invoicing/payment-readiness tools after award.
12. Funding Hub can use merchant-approved financial summaries to support applications to third parties; TryAMM does not guarantee approval.
13. Africa/Diaspora commerce uses country-gated PayRouter, currency, tax, shipping/customs and payout configuration.

## Features to implement
- Tap to Pay / in-person checkout where provider and country support it.
- Online checkout, payment links, invoices and recurring billing.
- QR/NFC HoloMenu checkout and receipt retrieval.
- Digital receipts with order, tax, coupon, tip and refund information.
- HoloPass loyalty and HoloCoupon redemption.
- Tips/gratuity allocation.
- Configurable revenue splits for creator affiliates, delivery, events and multi-party sales.
- Merchant payout schedule and payout-status tracking.
- Refund/dispute/chargeback workflows.
- Supplier/wholesale payments and PO/invoice matching.
- Bookkeeping/accounting exports.
- Cash-flow and business-health summaries.
- Procurement invoicing and contract payment tracking.
- Country-aware Africa/global payment routing.

## AI assistance
GrowthOS/Bennie may explain trends, flag late invoices, recommend campaigns, suggest reorder timing, surface procurement/funding opportunities and generate draft invoices or follow-ups. Merchant approval is required before money is spent, applications are submitted, prices are changed, contracts are accepted or suppliers are paid.

## Global guardrails
Jinn is not a bank by default and should not imply deposit insurance or regulated banking status. Payment, tax, stored-value, lending, money-transmission and payout features must be activated only through appropriate providers and country/legal configurations.
