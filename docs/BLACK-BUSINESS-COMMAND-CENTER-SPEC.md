# TryAMM Black Business Command Center

## Goal
Turn directory discovery into measurable business growth while keeping the broader TryAMM marketplace open to all eligible businesses.

## Merchant dashboard
A participating business gets one command center for:
- profile and ownership-verification status
- storefront and HoloMenu
- products, services, restaurant menus and inventory
- Shop Live / Business Live
- HoloCoupons and HoloPass loyalty
- Jinn/PayRouter sales and digital receipts
- orders, reservations, Ride/Delivery handoffs
- creator affiliate campaigns
- HoloAds campaigns
- verified reviews and reputation
- customer/business inbox
- payouts and revenue waterfall statements
- tax-responsibility status by jurisdiction
- procurement/RFP/RFQ matches
- grants/funding opportunities
- supplier and wholesale exchange
- Africa/Diaspora trade opportunities
- Business Academy progress
- GrowthOS recommendations

## Support Black Business feed
Content types: business profiles, short videos, Business Live, Shop Live, products, menus, HoloCoupons, books, events, creator collaborations and verified community stories.

Ranking inputs must include relevance, quality, distance when appropriate, availability, verification level, customer satisfaction, freshness and paid-placement disclosure. Paid promotion never changes the underlying verification claim.

## Procurement Hub
Supplier profiles should support capabilities, NAICS/category, geography, capacity, certifications, insurance, past performance and contact preferences. Opportunity records support buyer, due date, geography, requirements, eligibility and source link/reference. GrowthOS may match opportunities but must not claim eligibility or submit bids without merchant approval.

## Funding Hub
Track grants, accelerators, competitions, loans and other third-party capital opportunities. Show source, deadline, eligibility summary, required documents and status. Never guarantee funding. Regulated lending is handled by appropriately authorized third parties unless TryAMM later obtains required authority.

## City + global discovery
Dynamic city pages feed HoloLocal and the unified front scroll. Country launch gates control payments, payouts, tax configuration, privacy, consumer protection and regulated verticals. Africa/Diaspora discovery supports cross-border business matching without implying that every product can legally ship or be paid for in every country.

## Revenue opportunities
Optional subscription tiers, clearly labeled featured placement, HoloAds, affiliate campaign fees, commerce/booking fees, AI credits, ticketing/events, education and B2B tools. Every fee is configurable and shown before acceptance.

## Safety and fairness
Participation is opt-in. Verification levels are explicit: self-attested, document-reviewed, or recognized third-party certification. Sensitive documents are access-controlled. General marketplace eligibility is not conditioned on racial identity. Directory ranking and advertising must not misrepresent certification, ownership, reviews or availability.

## Activation checklist
1. Business identity/contact verified.
2. Directory opt-in and ownership claim recorded.
3. Verification level assigned accurately.
4. Storefront/HoloMenu configured.
5. Payment/payout/tax status configured for launch country.
6. Digital receipts enabled.
7. Refund/cancellation/support policies published.
8. Accessibility and translation settings checked.
9. Ads/affiliate/coupon terms accepted if used.
10. Business goes ACTIVE and becomes eligible for appropriate discovery surfaces.
