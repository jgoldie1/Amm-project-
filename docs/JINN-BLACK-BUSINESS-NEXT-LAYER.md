# Jinn Black Business — Next Merchant Operations Layer

## Purpose
Extend Jinn from payment orchestration into a merchant operations and growth layer for participating Black-owned businesses while keeping the same capabilities available to eligible merchants across the broader TryAMM Business platform.

## Jinn Business Money Center
One dashboard should show sales, taxes, tips, fees, refunds, reserves, affiliate commissions, ad spend, supplier bills, invoices, available payout, pending payout, next payout date and net proceeds. Amounts must be ledger-derived, not reconstructed from UI totals.

## Cash-flow tools
- invoice creation and payment links
- recurring invoices and memberships
- customer deposits and milestone payments
- estimates/quotes that can convert to invoices
- scheduled payment reminders
- partial payments where supported
- refunds and credits
- supplier bill tracking
- purchase-order/invoice matching
- payout forecasting
- cash-flow calendar

## Customer growth
- HoloPass loyalty earn/redeem
- HoloCoupon creation/redemption
- digital gift products only when compliant
- customer segments
- win-back campaigns
- birthday/anniversary offers with consent
- QR/NFC HoloMenu campaigns
- creator affiliate attribution
- HoloAds conversion attribution

## Business-to-business
- supplier directory and quote requests
- wholesale checkout
- purchase orders
- recurring procurement
- buyer/supplier invoices
- contract milestone billing
- procurement opportunity matching
- buyer inquiry inbox

## Procurement and funding readiness
Jinn may summarize verified transaction history, revenue trends, refund rates, invoice history and payout history to help a merchant prepare business records. It must not manufacture financial statements, guarantee grants, guarantee contracts, make lending decisions unless appropriately authorized, or submit applications without merchant approval.

## Jinn Business Passport
Create a reusable merchant profile containing verified business identity, contact information, capabilities, service area, certifications, storefront, payment readiness, tax configuration status, payout readiness, accessibility information and procurement profile. Sensitive information remains access-controlled. The Passport can reduce repeated data entry across storefront, procurement, funding and supplier workflows.

## Digital receipts
Paperless by default where legally permitted: SMS, email and in-app receipt. Receipt includes seller identity, items, discounts, tax/fee breakdown, tip, total, masked payment method, order/reference ID, refund/support links and verification URL. Printing remains optional where required or desired.

## Global controls
Every country/territory launch must configure supported payment rails, payouts, currencies, FX disclosure, tax/VAT/GST responsibility, invoicing/receipt rules, privacy, consumer protection, refunds, chargebacks, stored value and regulated vertical requirements. Unsupported capabilities remain disabled rather than guessed.

## AI Business Copilot
GrowthOS + Bennie can explain cash flow, identify overdue invoices, flag unusual refund patterns, suggest coupon campaigns, recommend creator partners, surface procurement/funding matches and prepare draft business documents. Human approval is required before spending money, changing prices, submitting applications, accepting contracts, issuing material refunds or moving funds.

## Fraud and trust
- device/account risk signals
- velocity limits
- coupon/referral abuse detection
- chargeback monitoring
- payout holds based on transparent risk rules
- merchant/customer dispute evidence trail
- audit log for money-moving decisions
- role-based access and step-up authentication for sensitive actions

## Accessibility
Voice navigation, screen-reader semantics, large targets, keyboard support, high-contrast compatibility, captioned help, simplified language mode and assisted workflows should be built into Jinn Business rather than added later.

## Activation order
1. Ledger and payment event integrity.
2. Merchant onboarding/KYB through approved providers where required.
3. Tax responsibility configuration.
4. Digital receipts.
5. Refund/dispute handling.
6. Payout reconciliation.
7. Money Center.
8. Invoices/payment links.
9. Loyalty/coupons/affiliate attribution.
10. Supplier/procurement tools.
11. Country-by-country cross-border expansion.

## Non-negotiable accounting rule
Customer funds, merchant payables, taxes, tips, creator/affiliate payables, reserves and TryAMM revenue are distinct ledger classifications. A payment collected is not automatically TryAMM revenue.
