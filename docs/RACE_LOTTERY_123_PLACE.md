# TRYAMM Race Lottery / 1-2-3 Place

Status: LAUNCHABLE SAFE MODE SPECIFICATION

## Core concept
A competitive race/championship experience with 1st, 2nd and 3rd place winners, sponsor/platform-funded bonus pools, charity voting and auditable results.

## Launch mode
- FREE SKILL QUALIFICATION.
- NO paid random entry, wagering or cash-purchased lottery tickets in general launch mode.
- Sponsor/platform/community-funded prize pools may be offered only under published official rules.
- Real-money gambling/lottery mode remains feature-gated OFF until licensing, geography, age controls and app-store approval are satisfied.

## Suggested safe-mode prize split
For a funded prize pool P:
- 1st place: 60% of winner pool
- 2nd place: 25%
- 3rd place: 15%

If the promotion includes separate legacy/ministry/community allocations, calculate and disclose those before the winner pool is computed. Never reduce an advertised prize after entry begins.

## Example sponsor pools
- Starter Race: $500 funded pool → $300 / $125 / $75
- City Race: $5,000 funded pool → $3,000 / $1,250 / $750
- Championship: $25,000 funded pool → $15,000 / $6,250 / $3,750
- Major Event: $100,000 funded pool → $60,000 / $25,000 / $15,000

These are product examples, not promises. Every live promotion must have money reserved before publication.

## Pricing / monetization
General launch entry price: $0.

TRYAMM can monetize around the competition without selling chances to win:
- event sponsorship
- brand placement
- optional cosmetic items
- spectator subscriptions/VIP viewing
- creator/event merchandise
- replay/highlight packages
- HoloGPT/AI Actions for creation or coaching features that do not alter competitive fairness
- advertising
- commissioned live productions

Paid competitive entry or chance-based paid play remains disabled unless separately approved for each jurisdiction/product.

## Qualification
Players qualify through verifiable skill performance such as time trials, races, ranked points, tournament placement or published challenges. Qualification criteria are frozen when the event opens.

## 1-2-3 Place determination
- Server-authoritative race result.
- Deterministic timing/scoring rules.
- Tie-break rules published in advance.
- Anti-cheat review before finalization for significant prizes.
- Results state: provisional → reviewed → final → paid/awarded.

## Charity vote
Where an event includes a charity/community allocation, eligible participants may vote from a pre-verified list. Votes are not purchasable and do not change a player's chance of winning. Publish allocation amount and voting method before the event.

## Giving structure
Preserve separately designated legacy/ministry/community allocations only when funded and disclosed in the official rules. Donation/charitable language must accurately identify the recipient and whether funds are tax-deductible; TRYAMM must not imply tax deductibility without an eligible receiving organization.

## Official rules fields
Each promotion must state:
- sponsor/developer
- eligibility and age
- geography
- free method of entry/qualification where applicable
- start/end times and time zone
- fixed prize pool and 1st/2nd/3rd values
- number of winners
- winner-selection/scoring method
- tie-breakers
- anti-cheat/disqualification rules
- prize-award date/process
- tax responsibility
- charity/community allocation if any
- dispute/contact method
- platform disclaimers, including Apple not being a sponsor where distributed on iOS

## Feature gates
PAID_PRIZE_COMPETITIONS = OFF
REAL_MONEY = OFF
REAL_PAYOUTS = OFF except separately approved non-gambling prize fulfillment path

## Future licensed mode
Only after legal/provider approval:
- age verification
- geofencing
- jurisdiction allowlist
- responsible-gaming controls if applicable
- required operator licenses
- app-store gambling application/approval
- no prohibited in-app billing for gambling credits
- separate ledger/risk/reconciliation

## Product goal
Make the competition exciting because of racing, championship status, community voting and sponsor-funded prize pools—not because users are encouraged to gamble.
