## 1. Global domain portfolio
Support discovery/registration/management through approved registrar/registry integrations for broad TLD classes, including:
- generic: .com .org .net .biz .info .xyz .online .site .store .shop .tech .cloud .digital .world .global
- app/technology: .app .dev .io .ai .software .systems .network
- professional/vertical: .health .care .law .legal .finance .media .tv .music .studio .design .agency .services
- geography/community where actually delegated: valid country-code TLDs, existing city/region gTLDs, and future ICANN-delegated brand/city/community strings
- internationalized domain names (IDNs) where registry/registrar support exists

Rules:
- never invent a public TLD that is not delegated by IANA/ICANN or the relevant registry;
- country/geo TLD eligibility rules vary and must be sourced per registry;
- Forever Domain Care uses funded renewal reserves and transferability, not false literal permanence.

## 5. Telehealth / OmniCare pathway
Build a regulated-service orchestration layer, not a pretend medical practice.

Core components:
- licensed-provider directory/verification;
- jurisdiction/service eligibility;
- appointment/video/audio workflow;
- consent and privacy controls;
- clinical documentation integration via approved systems;
- eligibility/benefit verification integrations where authorized;
- billing/clearinghouse adapter;
- audit/access logging;
- patient accessibility and language preferences;
- emergency escalation boundaries.

Medicaid: coverage and reimbursement rules vary by state and service. Only enrolled/qualified providers and covered services may be submitted through approved billing workflows. TRYAMM may provide software, scheduling, eligibility, billing support, or provider-network infrastructure, but must not claim reimbursement eligibility merely because a user has Medicaid.

Revenue options: provider SaaS, scheduling/operations fees, clearinghouse/billing administration where lawful, employer/plan contracts, approved care-management technology services. Patient cost-sharing/payment handled according to payer/program rules.
