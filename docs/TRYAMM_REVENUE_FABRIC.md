# TRYAMM Revenue Fabric

## Goal

One creator action should be able to produce several legitimate revenue opportunities without inventing money on the client.

A Reel, LIVE room, StreetVerse object, Business Passport, mission, CampusVerse event, CrossVerse post, OmniBox asset, or venue can expose a bundle of monetization intents.

## Revenue channels

1. Product sale
2. Service booking
3. Event ticket
4. Subscription
5. Verified LIVE gift
6. Sponsored mission
7. Creator affiliate attribution
8. Scout referral attribution
9. Asset licensing
10. Remix licensing
11. Virtual rental
12. Business campaign
13. Digital-twin sponsorship

## Differentiated combinations

### Scene-to-Sale

A user can look at a verified product/business with the Quantum/Holo Lens, open the owner-approved Business Passport, watch the creator Reel, enter the digital twin, and request checkout without leaving the world.

### Reel-to-World

A Reel can carry a world destination, mission, store, ticket, subscription, or licensed asset. The Reel becomes an entry point, not only a video.

### Sponsored Living Missions

A verified business or sponsor can fund a mission campaign. Completion is validated by the authoritative backend before any payable reward is created.

### Remix Rights Graph

A creator can license an asset/Reel for remix. Attribution to the original creator is preserved across derivative works. Revenue share is created only after a verified settlement.

### Digital Twin Sponsorship

A business digital twin can have sponsor placements or campaigns without changing the business's owner-approved facts.

### Virtual Rentals

Vehicles, outfits, venues, studio sets, props, and creator assets can be rented as time-limited entitlements rather than only sold.

### CrossVerse Attribution

Creator, merchant, scout, sponsor, Passport, store, content, and source-verse IDs remain attached as content moves through StreetVerse, CampusVerse, CrossVerse, Reels, LIVE, and OmniBox.

## Authority rules

- No client event creates a payable cash balance.
- Prices come from authoritative pricing/offer services.
- Purchases require verified provider payment.
- Mission payouts require server verification.
- Gifts require verified settlement.
- Creator/scout/sponsor attribution must be persisted server-side.
- Minority/small-business tags never change pricing or settlement authority by themselves.

## What makes this unusual

The differentiation is the combination: immersive world + QR business passport + creator video + missions + LIVE + commerce + asset licensing + local-business attribution in one shared identity/economy graph.

Do not market this as a feature that literally no other company has unless competitive research verifies that claim.
