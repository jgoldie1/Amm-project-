# TRYAMM Community Peace Corps

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT / BUSINESS SPECIFICATION

## Mission
Create a visible, trained, non-vigilante civilian peace presence that helps people get home safely, de-escalates low-risk conflict, connects people to resources, supports youth activities, documents hazards, and coordinates with appropriate emergency/public services when needed.

This is NOT police, armed security, private investigation, bounty hunting, gang enforcement, or vigilante activity.

## Core roles
- Peace Ambassador
- Safe Walk Ambassador
- Transit/School Route Ambassador
- Event Peace Team
- Youth Mentor
- Community Resource Navigator
- Business Corridor Ambassador
- Senior/Disability Assistance Ambassador
- Crisis Resource Connector

## Allowed actions
- visible presence in public/community spaces
- escort/safe-walk services by consent
- observe/report hazards or emergencies
- call appropriate emergency/public services
- verbal de-escalation training and conflict interruption within training limits
- connect people to shelters, food, transportation, youth programs, employment, reentry, mental-health/crisis resources and legal/resource navigation
- assist with lost persons/property and directions
- support school/event arrival/departure flows where authorized
- document non-sensitive operational incidents and service outcomes
- distribute verified safety information

## Prohibited actions
- impersonating police/security/licensed professionals
- detaining, searching, interrogating or pursuing suspects
- carrying weapons as part of the TRYAMM civilian Peace Corps role
- initiating physical confrontations
- profiling by race, religion, disability, age, clothing, neighborhood, immigration status, criminal history or other protected/sensitive traits
- publishing accusations, faces or crisis requests as entertainment
- acting as a substitute for emergency response

## JARVIS Peace Mode
Personal JARVIS may support:
`WALK WITH ME` → share a temporary route/session with selected contacts/authorized Peace Ambassador systems.
`I NEED A SAFE ROUTE` → route planning using supported mapping/public data.
`I NEED HELP` → Emergency Path / qualified resources.
`REPORT A HAZARD` → structured non-emergency report.

Location sharing is temporary, consented and minimized. No permanent surveillance history for ordinary users.

## Peace Ambassador App
SHIFT → ASSIGNED AREA/EVENT → CHECK-IN → SAFE-WALK/RESOURCE REQUEST → RESPONSE → ESCALATE IF NEEDED → CLOSE → AUDIT/IMPACT.

Features:
- shift availability
- training/credential status
- route/zone assignment
- SOS/escalation button
- incident/service forms
- accessibility profile
- supervisor communications
- check-in timer
- body-worn recording only if lawful, approved and separately governed; not a default requirement

## Training pathway
TRYAMM Academy can teach non-credential modules: de-escalation basics, communication, trauma-aware interaction, disability awareness, youth engagement, conflict avoidance, emergency recognition, first-aid/CPR readiness navigation, ethics/privacy and report writing.

External certifications/licensing remain external where required.

## Revenue model
The Community Peace Corps can be a profitable social enterprise through lawful service contracts rather than charging vulnerable people for emergency help.

Potential revenue:
- business-corridor ambassador contracts
- apartment/property common-area community ambassador programs where lawful
- school/college arrival/event support contracts
- festival/concert/sports-event peace teams
- transit/community safe-walk contracts
- hospital/campus visitor-navigation support
- employer late-shift safe-walk programs
- municipal/nonprofit community violence-prevention contracts/grants where eligible
- sponsor-funded youth peace programs
- training contracts for organizations
- Peace Corps software/dashboard subscriptions for partner organizations
- analytics/impact reporting using privacy-preserving aggregate data

Basic emergency/resource navigation remains free to the person seeking help.

## Example pricing framework (must be localized and validated)
- partner software: $49-$499/month depending on seats/features
- trained ambassador service: bill partner organizations hourly/per shift with wages, payroll burden, insurance, supervision, training, technology and margin included
- event package: fixed quote based on headcount/hours/risk/logistics
- training: per-seat or organization contract
- sponsored safe-walk/youth mission: restricted mission budget

Never price below true labor/insurance/compliance cost simply to win volume.

## Unit economics
Partner revenue
- ambassador compensation
- payroll taxes/benefits where applicable
- insurance
- training/background checks where lawful/appropriate
- supervisor cost
- communications/software
- transportation/equipment
= contribution margin.

Target positive contribution before scaling. Platform Sustainability Engine records Peace Corps revenue/cost separately and includes only eligible platform contribution in TRYAMM's 3.00x sustainability calculation.

## 3.00x sustainability connection
Peace Corps should strengthen, not drain, TRYAMM:
CONTRACT REVENUE → LABOR/INSURANCE/SERVICE COST → PEACE CORPS CONTRIBUTION → PLATFORM SUSTAINABILITY.

Do not count grant-restricted money, worker wages owed, taxes, or pass-through partner funds as free platform revenue.

## Community Gap Finder
Aggregate patterns can identify:
- unsafe walking/transit corridors
- repeated youth conflict locations
- areas lacking after-school programs
- senior/disability mobility gaps
- late-night worker transportation needs
- food/housing/resource gaps

Then route:
GAP → COMMUNITY PARTNERS → FUNDING → PROGRAM → PEACE AMBASSADORS → MEASURED OUTCOME → COMMUNITY SPOTLIGHT.

No predictive-policing score and no labeling an individual as likely criminal.

## Youth pathway
Teen/youth participants are routed toward paid/eligible constructive opportunities: sports, music, Creator Studio, coding, trades, Student JARVIS, mentors, entrepreneurship, service projects and Peace Missions. Youth are not used as unlicensed enforcement personnel.

## Metrics
- safe-walks completed
- resource connections
- conflicts resolved without emergency escalation (carefully defined)
- emergency escalations correctly made
- youth program referrals/completions
- partner satisfaction
- ambassador retention/training completion
- incidents/complaints
- service cost and contribution margin

Never claim the program caused a crime reduction without appropriate evaluation evidence.

## Launch gates
Before real-world service:
1. jurisdiction/legal review of role and licensing boundaries;
2. insurance confirmed;
3. worker classification/payroll model;
4. training and supervisor protocol;
5. background-check policy where lawful/needed;
6. incident/escalation SOP;
7. emergency-service boundaries;
8. privacy/location retention rules;
9. accessibility QA;
10. partner agreement and scope;
11. pilot measurement plan.

## Pilot
Start with one low-risk corridor/event/use case, daytime/evening safe walks and resource navigation, then measure demand, cost, incidents, satisfaction and economics before expanding.
