# TRYAMM Living World Economy + Real-World Bridge

Status: LOCKED ARCHITECTURE

## Core promise
TRYAMM's Living World is persistent: the world continues to progress while the player is away, subject to fair simulation rules and server-authoritative state.

YOU LEAVE → WORLD CONTINUES → YOU RETURN → YOUR WORLD IS STILL THERE.

## Life progression loop
get an apartment → furnish it → meet neighbors → get a job → start a business → buy a car → drive → deliver packages/food → go to school → attend concerts → play games → travel through portals → own land → build a house → create a company → expand into other worlds/planets.

The experience can be entertainment-only, educational, or connected to verified real-world TRYAMM services when the user explicitly chooses.

## Asset Creator Economy
Players/creators can design digital assets such as:
- furniture
- clothing
- decor
- vehicles/skins where permitted
- buildings/rooms
- props
- music/audio packs
- game items
- creator templates

### Sellable asset lifecycle
IDEA → JARVIS/AI TUTOR → DESIGN → RIGHTS/OWNERSHIP → SAFETY/IP CHECK → LISTING → HOLO MARKETPLACE → SALE → MONEY ENGINE → CREATOR EARNINGS → PAYOUT GATE.

TRYAMM may take a transparent marketplace/platform percentage on eligible sales. Exact fees remain configurable and must be shown before listing/checkout.

## Learn-to-Earn pathway
Stubbs AI can teach users how to:
- design assets
- model furniture
- create textures/materials
- prepare product images/videos
- price products
- write listings
- understand rights/licensing
- manage orders/customer service
- build a small business

Learning Passport can record completed training and projects. Training does not guarantee sales or income.

## Furniture + SpaceOS bridge
Digital furniture can remain digital OR move toward physical manufacturing.

DIGITAL FURNITURE → CREATOR PROJECT → CAD/PARAMETRIC REVIEW → SPACEOS SAFETY/ENGINEERING REVIEW → MATERIAL/COST ESTIMATE → PROTOTYPE → APPROVED MANUFACTURING PARTNER/JOB → PHYSICAL PRODUCT → HOLO MARKETPLACE → HOLO/PACKAGE DELIVERY.

No game asset is automatically safe to manufacture. Structural, material, fire, electrical, load-bearing, child-safety and other relevant reviews apply before BUILD.

## Housing loop
Virtual: apartment/house/land ownership is governed by game rules.
Real-world: TRYAMM may connect to verified housing/property workflows separately. Never represent a virtual deed/land asset as legal real-world title.

## Work + business loop
GAME JOBS/ECONOMY can teach skills and simulate work.
REAL WORK opportunities route through Opportunity Match, employer verification and applicable labor rules.

Business path:
GAME BUSINESS SIMULATION → LEARNING PASSPORT → BUSINESS LAUNCH OS → REAL BUSINESS PASSPORT → STUBBS HARMONY → BUSINESS JARVIS → MARKETPLACE → SUPPLIER EXCHANGE → HR → JIN PAY → DELIVERY → COMPANY DIGITAL TWIN.

## Vehicle + delivery loop
Players can own/use vehicles in the game. Real delivery work is separate and requires real-world eligibility/provider checks.

HOLO DELIVERY supports:
- restaurant/ghost-kitchen delivery
- grocery/convenience delivery
- marketplace package delivery
- local courier jobs
- scheduled package delivery

Flow:
ORDER → HOLO COUPON → JIN PAY → MERCHANT → HOLO DISPATCH → DRIVER/COURIER JARVIS → LIVE TRACKING → ARRIVAL → PROOF → MONEY ENGINE.

## Ghost kitchen / home food connection
Business JARVIS may help users learn and prepare for a ghost-kitchen, commissary, cottage-food or home-food business. Real selling/delivery is enabled only after jurisdiction-appropriate verification and required approvals.

## Education loop
Student JARVIS connects school help, Learning Passport, financial literacy, career pathways and opportunity matching to the Living World.

A player can learn through simulation, then opt into real pathways for school, trade programs, jobs, entrepreneurship or creator careers.

## Concerts/media/games
Soul Ascension, LIVE, music, creator worlds and games can exist inside the persistent world. Tickets, licensed media, merchandise and creator sales route through Marketplace/Money Engine when real money is enabled.

## Portals / planets
Portals and planetary travel are entertainment/game features unless explicitly tied to real public information or approved external experiences. They must not be presented as actual transportation.

## Persistent world state
Server-authoritative persistent state should track:
- player location/world
- housing/home state
- owned digital assets
- businesses/jobs
- inventory
- vehicles
- social/community relationships where appropriate
- quests/missions
- creator projects
- world events
- elapsed-time simulation

Offline progression must be bounded and anti-exploit protected. Money/valuable inventory changes require authoritative server rules and audit events.

## Marketplace economics
Revenue opportunities for TRYAMM include transparent fees from eligible:
- asset sales
- creator stores
- marketplace transactions
- premium creation/rendering
- HoloGPT Credits/AI Actions
- Business JARVIS/Company OS plans
- Holo Ads/promotions
- Holo Delivery service fees
- Stubbs Harmony websites
- manufacturing coordination/service fees where lawful and disclosed

Creator/provider liabilities remain separate from platform revenue.

## Community economy connection
The 12-Circulation Challenge and Supplier Exchange can surface participating businesses/suppliers but must remain voluntary. Aggregate impact can measure local supplier relationships, jobs/gigs, repeat purchases and participating-network commerce.

## Safety + truth boundaries
- simulation money ≠ real money
- digital land ≠ legal property title
- game job ≠ employment contract
- digital furniture model ≠ approved physical product
- AI suggestion ≠ licensed engineering advice
- delivery simulation ≠ real courier eligibility
- virtual business ≠ registered legal entity
- planetary portal ≠ real transport

## End-to-end flywheel
PLAY → LEARN → CREATE → SELL DIGITAL → EARN → BUILD BUSINESS → SELL REAL PRODUCTS/SERVICES → DELIVER → HIRE → BUY FROM SUPPLIERS → INVEST IN SKILLS/TOOLS → CREATE MORE → MENTOR THE NEXT USER.

This is the bridge between the Living World and the real TRYAMM economy without confusing virtual achievements with regulated real-world rights or services.
