## 1. Community Guardian Network

Purpose: create a healthy, non-vigilante, non-security-guard pathway for community safety, de-escalation, accompaniment, youth engagement, reporting, resource connection, and visible neighborhood stewardship.

The program must NOT represent unlicensed volunteers as police, armed security, private investigators, emergency responders, or people with authority to detain/search/punish others.

### Core services
- safe-walk / accompaniment requests
- school-route and transit-area community presence
- event/community ambassador staffing
- de-escalation-trained peace teams
- youth recreation, mentorship, jobs and Peace Missions
- neighborhood check-in circles
- business corridor ambassadors
- elder/disabled-person accompaniment where appropriate
- lost-person/resource navigation support
- non-emergency issue reporting to property owners/public services
- verified emergency escalation to 911/public responders when necessary
- Community Gap Finder integration for lighting, transport, youth programming, vacant property, food/resource and accessibility gaps

### Safety rules
- no weapons as a program requirement
- no chasing, detaining, searching or interrogating people
- no racial/religious/disability profiling
- no gang databases or predictive-crime scoring of individuals
- no public exposure of victims/help requests
- no retaliation
- body/video recording only where lawful, disclosed and necessary
- location sharing minimized and consented
- volunteers leave dangerous situations and call appropriate responders
- minors receive safeguarding controls

### Training Passport
Training can include:
- conflict de-escalation
- bystander intervention
- first aid/CPR through qualified providers
- naloxone awareness where lawful
- trauma-informed communication
- disability/accessibility awareness
- youth safeguarding
- domestic-violence referral boundaries
- emergency recognition
- report writing
- privacy/data protection
- ethics/non-discrimination
- local service/resource navigation

TRYAMM training does not substitute for licenses/certifications required by law.

### Business model
Possible revenue lanes, subject to local law and contracts:
- business improvement district / merchant corridor ambassador contracts
- event/community ambassador contracts
- school/nonprofit/community partnership contracts
- property-management community concierge services
- training contracts
- employer-sponsored youth/peace programs
- foundation/government violence-prevention grants where eligible
- sponsored Safe Route / Peace Mission programs
- optional organizational software dashboards

Do not charge vulnerable people a fee to request emergency help. Restricted grant/sponsor funds stay separated in Money Engine.

### Impact metrics
Measure aggregate outcomes such as safe-walk completions, youth placements, mentor matches, referrals, businesses served, hazards reported/resolved, training completions, community-event support and participant-reported safety. Do not claim the program caused a crime reduction without appropriate evidence.

## 3. IP chain of title

The product must maintain an IP register for:
- source code
- documentation
- brand names/logos
- music/jingles/sound recordings
- videos/advertising
- games/characters/worlds
- artwork/3D assets
- datasets where rights are relevant
- patents/inventions if pursued
- domains
- contracts/licenses

For each asset record: creator/author, creation date, owner, employer/contractor status, assignment/license evidence, third-party dependencies, registration status and restrictions.

U.S. copyright law generally vests initial copyright in the human author(s), unless a valid work-made-for-hire rule applies or rights are transferred. Contractor ownership therefore should not be assumed without appropriate written terms. Trademark clearance must check for confusingly similar marks before relying on a brand. 

## 4. Faith statement vs legal authorship

The founder may express a faith belief such as: `I regard Yahavah as the ultimate source of inspiration and myself as a human co-creator/steward.`

That statement is spiritual/mission language. Legal IP records and government filings still identify the relevant human beings and legal entities that authored, own, license, assign or register the actual works and marks. Do not list a divine being as a legal copyright author, contractor, corporate owner or governmental applicant unless applicable law explicitly provides a mechanism—which ordinary U.S. IP filing systems do not.
