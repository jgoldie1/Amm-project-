## Accessibility
The LLM reads only authorized Accessibility Passport preferences and may suggest/activate reversible UI/control presets such as captions, one-handed mode, larger targets, reduced motion, simplified instructions, voice navigation and extra tutorial time. It must never infer or disclose a medical diagnosis.

## Safety / authority boundary
LLM output is advisory until validated by deterministic/game-server rules. It cannot:
- award real-money prizes
- change authoritative scores
- bypass anti-cheat
- spend money
- publish content externally
- change moderation bans
- access sensitive account data
without the appropriate permissions, feature gates and approvals.

## Sustainability
Each game reports eligible revenue and measured infrastructure costs into Platform Sustainability Engine.

Revenue classes can include subscriptions, events/LIVE audiences, sponsorships, Holo Ads, merchandise, Creator Studio usage, HoloGPT Credits/AI Actions, premium replay/storage, institutional esports/education services and eligible marketplace/game commerce.

Cost classes include servers, multiplayer bandwidth, storage, AI inference, rendering, moderation, support, CDN, voice/transcription, analytics and third-party services.

Per-game sustainability ratio = eligible game platform revenue / measured game infrastructure cost.

Target: 3.00× at mature scale; do not treat projections as achieved.

## Portfolio management
Use game-level ratios to decide:
- scale
- optimize
- bundle
- sponsor
- subsidize strategically
- sunset/rework

A lower-ratio game may remain valuable if it drives measurable acquisition, education, community impact or revenue in other connected products. Cross-subsidy must be explicit in the Sustainability Engine.
