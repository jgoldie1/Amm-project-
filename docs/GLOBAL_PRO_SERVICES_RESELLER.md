# TRYAMM Global Professional Services + Registrar Reseller Path

Status: LOCKED ARCHITECTURE / PROVIDER-INTEGRATION REQUIRED FOR LIVE SERVICES

## 1. Domain registration without direct ICANN accreditation
Launch as a reseller/white-label storefront using an ICANN-accredited registrar or registrar-reseller platform. TRYAMM owns the customer experience, Business Passport, DNS dashboard, Stubbs Harmony site builder, renewal reminders, domain reserve and support workflow; the accredited registrar remains the authoritative registration provider.

Do not represent TRYAMM as ICANN-accredited until accreditation is actually obtained.

Pricing model:
- registrar wholesale cost + transparent TRYAMM service margin;
- optional Forever Domain Care reserve/admin fee;
- optional DNS/security/website bundle;
- domain transfer/export supported.

Direct ICANN accreditation becomes a later-stage option after revenue, technical maturity and capital justify it.

## 2. Tax-preparer pathway
Paid federal tax return preparers require a valid PTIN. TRYAMM can provide PTIN readiness/navigation, onboarding and workflow software, but PTIN issuance/renewal remains an IRS process.

Professional tax services lane requires provider credential verification where applicable and clear separation between:
- TRYAMM platform/service fee;
- licensed/qualified preparer professional fee;
- government/IRS fees;
- tax software/e-file/provider fees.

## 3. Regulated professional-services marketplace
One marketplace shell with profession-specific controls:

TELE-LAW | TELE-HEALTH | TELE-TAX/BOOKKEEPING | TELE-INSURANCE | TELE-REALTY | REMOTE NOTARY | TUTORING | INTERPRETING/SIGN LANGUAGE | HR/RECRUITING | CYBER/TECH SUPPORT | BEAUTY CONSULTS.

TRYAMM role: discovery, scheduling, identity/credential workflow, secure messaging/video, document exchange, payments/escrow-like provider flows only where legally supported by partners, reminders, JARVIS navigation, accessibility and audit trails.

The professional—not Stubbs AI—provides regulated advice/services.

## 4. Lawyer On Demand / Traffic Stop mode
User may open a rapid-access legal-help workflow after a traffic stop or other legal event:
- safety-first reminder to comply with lawful instructions and avoid escalating the encounter;
- jurisdiction/location if the user chooses to share;
- connect to an available qualified attorney/provider service;
- secure upload of citation/documents after safe opportunity;
- appointment/video/phone handoff;
- case/task reminders;
- no guarantee that an attorney can join a live traffic stop or that use of a phone is lawful/safe during the stop.

Emergency legal access does not replace 911/emergency services when immediate physical danger exists.

## 5. Telehealth
Healthcare services require licensed clinicians, jurisdiction eligibility, privacy/security controls, appropriate consent, provider agreements and compliant billing infrastructure. Stubbs AI may navigate, schedule, translate/summarize as permitted and prepare non-diagnostic intake, but does not replace licensed diagnosis or treatment.

## 6. Medicaid/insurance billing
Medicaid billing may be offered only through properly enrolled/credentialed providers and compliant billing/clearinghouse/payer partners. Requirements vary by state, provider type, service and managed-care plan.

Architecture:
ELIGIBILITY/PROGRAM CHECK → QUALIFIED PROVIDER → DOCUMENTED SERVICE → CODING/BILLING WORKFLOW → CLAIM SUBMISSION VIA APPROVED RAIL → ACKNOWLEDGMENT → DENIAL/REWORK → PAYMENT/RECONCILIATION → AUDIT.

TRYAMM cannot bill Medicaid merely because a user is eligible; the rendering/billing provider must satisfy enrollment and service requirements.

Revenue options for compliant healthcare admin may include software subscription, administrative service fees or contracted revenue-cycle services where lawful; do not take an impermissible percentage of professional fees where fee-splitting rules prohibit it.

## 7. Tele-insurance / tele-realty / notary
- Insurance sales/advice: appropriately licensed producer/carrier/agency partners; platform/referral arrangements must comply with state insurance laws.
- Real estate brokerage/referrals: appropriately licensed broker/agent relationships and jurisdiction-specific referral/commission rules.
- Remote online notarization: only in jurisdictions and transaction types where permitted, using compliant identity, recording/journal and technology requirements.

## 9. Pricing principles
TRYAMM should charge for platform value, not sell unlicensed professional advice.

Recommended platform structures to test:
- consumer convenience/service fee per completed marketplace booking;
- provider SaaS subscription;
- lead/referral fee only where legally allowed;
- booking/payment-processing add-on;
- Business JARVIS/Stubbs Harmony/Quantum Zapier upsells;
- enterprise contracts with firms/clinics/agencies.

Professional fees are set/collected for the licensed provider according to applicable law/provider agreement.

Avoid a single universal percentage across legal, healthcare, insurance, real estate, tax and notary services because fee-sharing restrictions differ by profession and state.

## 10. Sustainability contribution
Professional-services revenue feeds the Platform Sustainability Engine only after separating taxes, refunds, provider payouts, restricted funds and processor/provider obligations.

Goal remains 3.00x eligible platform revenue / measured infrastructure cost.
