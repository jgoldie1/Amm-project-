# TRYAMM Economic Operating System — No Gatekeeping

Status: LOCKED PRINCIPLE + PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

## Principle
TRYAMM should reduce unnecessary gatekeeping while preserving legitimate safety, legal, credential, fraud, age, licensing and quality requirements.

No gatekeeping means:
- no insider-only access to discovery merely because someone lacks status or connections;
- no paywall around basic opportunity discovery, accessibility tools, core learning pathways or business-start guidance;
- no requirement that someone already be wealthy, famous or established before they can be discovered;
- no opaque algorithm silently suppressing new/small participants without explanation;
- no selling access to grants, jobs or essential opportunities as if payment guarantees selection;
- no forced religious participation for ordinary education, work, business, accessibility or emergency-resource use;
- no discrimination against disabled users or people using accessibility settings;
- no artificial economic lock-in that prevents export/transfer of user-owned business content and domains.

No gatekeeping does NOT mean bypassing:
- professional licensure;
- medical/legal/tax/insurance/real-estate credential rules;
- age restrictions;
- fraud/identity controls where required;
- food/health/safety rules;
- financial compliance;
- employer/program qualification requirements;
- app-store/provider/registrar/ICANN requirements.

## Open Opportunity Standard
Every eligible opportunity should expose where possible:
SOURCE → WHO CAN APPLY → REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS → COST/FEE → DEADLINE → REQUIRED DOCUMENTS → WHY YOU MATCH → WHAT IS MISSING → HOW TO PREPARE.

Users may correct their own profile facts. Matching must separate LIKELY | POSSIBLE | VERIFY | NOT A MATCH rather than create secret access tiers.

## Open Discovery Standard
Marketplace, creators, businesses and talent can earn discovery through multiple user-selectable algorithms and explainable signals—not only who pays the most.

Discovery lanes can include:
FOR YOU | NEW | LOCAL | CREATOR | BLACK BUSINESS | DISABILITY-OWNED | COMMUNITY CIRCULATION | SUPPLIER EXCHANGE | SECOND CHANCE | STUDENT/YOUTH OPPORTUNITY.

Paid promotion must be labeled and must not erase organic discovery.

## Economic Passport
A voluntary progress layer can show a person's or business's readiness without becoming a social-credit score:
- learning milestones;
- verified skills/credentials;
- grant/business readiness;
- business formation tasks;
- seller/supplier readiness;
- financial-literacy milestones;
- accessibility preferences;
- applications/projects in progress.

Do not combine protected/sensitive characteristics into a worthiness score.

## Community Wealth Loop
Global earning and trade can coexist with stronger local/community circulation:
EARN GLOBALLY → BUY INTENTIONALLY → DEVELOP SUPPLIERS → CREATE JOBS → OWN IP/ASSETS/BUSINESSES → TRAIN NEXT PERSON → TRADE GLOBALLY FROM STRONGER LOCAL CAPACITY.

The 12-Circulation Challenge remains educational/voluntary and measures network relationships rather than falsely claiming a dollar can be forced to circulate twelve times.

## Access Fund / Subsidy Logic
Platform Sustainability above 1.00x can create capacity for reserves and reinvestment. TRYAMM's stated long-term target is 3.00x eligible platform revenue divided by measured infrastructure cost.

Subject to real accounting/obligations, surplus capacity can support:
- basic Student JARVIS and learning access;
- accessibility features;
- opportunity discovery;
- limited AI Actions/HoloGPT Credits for sponsored learners/creators/business starters;
- community missions;
- security/moderation/support;
- infrastructure reserves.

Restricted grants, creator earnings, taxes, refunds, payout liabilities or charitable funds are never treated as free platform surplus.

## Why this matters
No gatekeeping turns TRYAMM from a content platform into an economic mobility and ownership system. A user who enters with no audience, no business and few resources can still receive a visible path to learning, opportunity, creation, ownership and commerce—while regulated/licensed activities retain legitimate qualification gates.
