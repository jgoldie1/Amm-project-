8. **Measure**: approved aggregate funnel analytics only. Never infer subscriptions, revenue or clinical status from a browser click.

## Denotation, connotation and etymology
- **Denotation**: directly established lexical meaning or, in a brand context, the factual service description.
- **Connotation**: cultural, theological or emotional associations that should not be presented as literal dictionary definitions.
- **Etymology**: historically attested word development; ancient letter drawings alone do not prove origins.
- Separate sourced scripture, verified manuscript metadata, scholarly lexical glosses, commentary and AI explanation. Avoid unlicensed copying of dictionary/lexicon datasets.

## Dead Sea Scrolls (research, not bundled copyrighted text)
- Israel Antiquities Authority Leon Levy digital archive: https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/home?locale=en_US
- Library of Congress scholarly exhibition: https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/scrolls/
- Research catalog examples: Great Isaiah Scroll (1QIsa-a), Community Rule (1QS), Habakkuk Commentary (1QpHab), Great Psalms Scroll (11QPs-a), and Some Works of the Law (4QMMT).
- The scrolls are diverse biblical and non-biblical manuscripts, not a standalone canon or missing chapters of the KJV.
- Images, scans, high-res facsimiles, critical editions and modern translations must not be copied or monetized without rights assessment.
- Citation, artifact identifier, collection, century, fragment/gap provenance, translation, copyright and curator review are prerequisites for in-app transcription display.
