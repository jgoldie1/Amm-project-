# Soul Ascension

Status: RESTORED / LOCKED PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

Soul Ascension is TRYAMM's soul-music, dance, culture, testimony and community broadcast experience. It carries forward the previously established TRYAMM music/broadcast direction: music videos, livestreams, virtual concerts, artist worlds, fan walls, studios, listening rooms, awards, AR/VR/MR/holographic experiences and Set Apart Music integration.

## Naming / brand safety
Use `Soul Ascension` as the TRYAMM product name. Do not market the product as affiliated with or endorsed by third-party television/music brands without a license. Historical music/dance traditions may be discussed editorially, but TRYAMM owns and develops its own show identity.

## Experience
SOUL ASCENSION → LIVE STAGE → DANCE LINE → ARTIST WORLD → TESTIMONY → COMMUNITY SPOTLIGHT → SET APART → CREATOR MARKET → ACADEMY → AWARDS → ARCHIVE.

### Live stage
- live bands, singers, rappers, DJs, choirs, dancers and spoken word
- emerging-artist showcases
- legacy/elder interviews
- audience reactions and moderated chat
- multilingual captions/transcripts
- replay and clip creation

### Ascension Line
A signature interactive dance/performance lane with solo, family, crew, school and global-community modes. Remote participants can submit or join approved performances. Holo OS can later render spatial/AR/VR versions.

### Artist Worlds
Each artist can connect music, videos, LIVE events, merchandise, Omni Box, tickets, fan wall, Creator Project, rights, collaborators and Passport achievements.

### Set Apart stage
Dedicated faith-centered programming for Yahuah-centered music, gospel, testimony, worship, spoken word and community service. Participation in faith programming is voluntary and clearly labeled.

### Community spotlight
Highlight entrepreneurs, students, educators, mentors, returning citizens, community projects and service organizations alongside entertainment.

### Learning integration
All American Marketplace University can turn episodes into lessons: music history, dance, production, audio engineering, rights/royalties, financial literacy, entrepreneurship, broadcasting and cultural preservation.

### Awards
Eligible categories can connect to TRYAMM awards: Soul Ascension Artist, New Artist, Live Performance, Dance/Choreography, Community Impact Artist, Faith & Inspiration, immersive music experience and audience choice. Voting and Academy judging follow the broader awards governance rules.

## Money and rights
Every monetized performance or recording must connect to Creator Project + Rights Registry + Money Engine. Record contractual performer/rightsholder splits; keep sponsorship, charity/community allocations and creator earnings separate; do not assume music licenses or royalty percentages.

## Distribution loop
LIVE → replay → short clips/Reels → audio/podcast where licensed → transcript → Academy lesson → artist world → Omni Box → charts/certifications → awards/archive.

## Accessibility
Captions, transcripts, audio description where appropriate, keyboard/switch navigation, reduced-motion option, readable contrast, and adaptable participation modes are baseline requirements.

## Purpose
Soul Ascension is not only entertainment. It creates a bridge between culture, creators, businesses, education, faith/service, community stories and economic opportunity while preserving rights and attribution.
