# TRYAMM Community Guardian Network

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT + BUSINESS SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Create a safer, healthier community-safety pathway that is civilian, non-vigilante, trauma-informed, de-escalation-first, and connected to jobs, youth opportunity, emergency resources, businesses, schools, ministries, neighborhoods, and public agencies.

The system is inspired by the positive idea of visible community guardianship but is NOT an armed patrol, private police force, gang, militia, or self-appointed law-enforcement body.

## Core model
SEE → DE-ESCALATE → SUPPORT → ESCORT → CONNECT → DOCUMENT → ESCALATE TO PROFESSIONAL SERVICES WHEN NEEDED → FOLLOW UP → MEASURE OUTCOMES.

## Roles
- Community Ambassador
- Safe Passage Escort
- Youth Mentor
- Violence Interruption Partner
- Event/Transit Ambassador
- Business District Ambassador
- Reentry Navigator
- Crisis Resource Navigator
- School Arrival/Dismissal Ambassador
- Faith/Community Liaison
- Dispatcher/Coordinator
- Supervisor

Role permissions are scoped. No role receives police powers.

## Prohibited behavior
- no vigilantism
- no chasing suspects
- no interrogations
- no detention/arrest beyond ordinary lawful civilian rights
- no weapon-based patrol model
- no impersonating police/security guards
- no gang intelligence databases built from rumor
- no public shaming/doxxing
- no discriminatory profiling
- no monetizing crisis reports through advertising

## Safety services
### Safe Walk / Safe Ride
Users can request a trained ambassador escort between home, transit, school, work, parking, events, appointments, or businesses.

### Business Safe Corridor
Participating businesses can subscribe to visible ambassador coverage, opening/closing escorts, employee walk-outs, incident routing, youth engagement, and community resource navigation.

### School Safe Passage
Schools/partners can sponsor trained civilian presence before/after school, route students toward resources and mentors, and report environmental hazards without treating students as suspects.

### Event Safety Ambassadors
Concerts, Soul Ascension, sports, fairs, markets, churches, campuses, and TRYAMM events can book trained ambassadors for wayfinding, de-escalation, accessibility help, lost-person support, and emergency handoff.

### Youth Peace Team
Paid/eligible youth pathways in sports, music, media, gaming, coding, trades, entrepreneurship, community service, mediation support, and peer leadership. Youth are not asked to intervene in dangerous confrontations.

### Violence Interruption Partner Network
Where qualified organizations already operate violence-interruption programs, TRYAMM can provide scheduling, case coordination, training records, resource matching, funding alerts, and impact analytics instead of duplicating their professional work.

## Guardian JARVIS
A dedicated JARVIS mode can:
- dispatch approved ambassadors
- show safe-route waypoints
- send check-in prompts
- route emergencies to appropriate services
- surface nearby verified safe locations
- coordinate accessibility needs
- log non-sensitive incident categories
- prepare shift briefs
- show de-escalation/resource scripts
- never provide tactical confrontation instructions

## Safe Place Network
Participating verified businesses, ministries, schools, libraries, community centers, and other partners may opt into a `SAFE PLACE` directory.

Each location can publish:
- open hours
- accessibility information
- staff contact pathway
- emergency calling availability
- restroom/water availability where offered
- charging/phone access where offered
- youth-safe policy where appropriate

## Incident flow
USER/AMBASSADOR REPORT → TRIAGE CATEGORY → IMMINENT DANGER? → EMERGENCY SERVICE HANDOFF → NON-EMERGENCY? → RESOURCE/AMBASSADOR RESPONSE → FOLLOW-UP → AGGREGATED IMPACT METRICS.

Sensitive details are minimized and separated from advertising.

## Training passport
Required training can include:
- de-escalation
- conflict mediation fundamentals
- trauma-informed communication
- first aid/CPR where appropriate
- overdose-response awareness where lawful/appropriate
- disability/accessibility assistance
- youth safeguarding
- domestic-violence referral boundaries
- harassment prevention
- privacy/data handling
- emergency escalation
- local legal boundaries
- report writing
- cultural humility

Completion of TRYAMM training does not create a law-enforcement or regulated security credential unless an authorized external credentialing body grants it.

## Workforce pathway
LEARN → TRAIN → BACKGROUND/ELIGIBILITY CHECK WHERE LEGALLY REQUIRED → SHADOW → SUPERVISED SHIFT → CERTIFIED/APPROVED ROLE WHERE APPLICABLE → PAID SHIFTS → SUPERVISOR → TRAINER → PROGRAM MANAGER.

This creates constructive employment for community members, returning citizens where permitted, mentors, retirees, students, and people with lived experience while matching each person to legally appropriate roles.

## Revenue model
The Guardian Network can be profitable without charging people in crisis for help.

Revenue sources:
- business-district subscriptions
- commercial property/retail contracts
- event ambassador contracts
- campus/school contracts where permitted
- municipal/county/state contracts
- nonprofit/subrecipient contracts
- hospital/community-benefit partnerships
- transit partnerships
- insurance/risk-reduction partnerships where lawful
- housing/property-management contracts
- employer safe-walk programs
- sponsored Safe Passage corridors
- training/admin software subscriptions for partner organizations
- grants for violence prevention/workforce/youth programming

Basic Emergency Path access remains free to the person seeking help.

## Example pricing framework (illustrative; validate locally)
- Small business Safe Corridor: $99–$299/month/location
- Multi-location/business district: custom contract
- Event ambassador staffing: hourly per ambassador + coordination fee
- Employer late-shift escort program: monthly contract
- Partner-organization software/dispatch: SaaS subscription
- Training administration: per-seat or organization license
- City/state/nonprofit programs: contract/grant budget based on staffing and outcomes

Do not publish fixed pricing as final until labor, insurance, workers compensation, training, supervision, local licensing, and overhead are modeled.

## Unit economics
For each paid program calculate:
REVENUE − DIRECT LABOR − SUPERVISION − INSURANCE − TRAINING − DISPATCH/TECH − TRAVEL/EQUIPMENT − ADMIN = CONTRIBUTION.

Programs must maintain positive contribution unless intentionally subsidized by an approved grant/sponsor.

## Platform Sustainability integration
Eligible Guardian platform revenue contributes to TRYAMM Sustainability Engine after restricted grant/program money and pass-through wages/provider obligations are separated.

Target remains 3.00× platform infrastructure sustainability overall, but Guardian operations also require their own labor-margin model; high revenue with unpaid liabilities is not sustainability.

## Community Gap Finder integration
Aggregate data can identify:
- unsafe transit corridors
- recurring school safe-passage needs
- youth-opportunity shortages
- late-night worker escort demand
- accessibility/safe-route gaps
- neighborhoods lacking trusted safe locations
- resource-navigation shortages

The system recommends investment and programs; it does not label individuals as criminals or predict criminality from protected traits.

## Community Spotlight

- safe escorts completed

- Safe Place partners onboarded

## Evidence and evaluation
Track process and outcomes using privacy-preserving methods. Independent evaluation is preferred for major public claims. Publish methodology, limitations, and source definitions.

## Why this can make communities better
The model creates visible trusted adults, paid constructive roles, stronger youth pathways, faster resource connection, safer travel, greater business confidence, more community relationships, and non-police options for many low-level safety/support needs while preserving emergency handoff for serious danger.
