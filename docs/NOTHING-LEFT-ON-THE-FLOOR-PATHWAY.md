## 2. Jin Payment System / PayRouter

Treat Jin as the user-facing branded payment/business-money layer while PayRouter chooses the compliant underlying rail.

Possible rails by jurisdiction and use case:
- Stripe online payments
- Tap to Pay / Terminal where supported
- Apple/Google billing where required
- Paystack/approved African rails
- mobile money where supported
- bank transfer/ACH/local transfer
- future approved providers

Do not store raw card data in TryAMM.

Every payment path:
Customer -> approved payment rail -> verified event/webhook -> Revenue Waterfall -> Unified Value Ledger -> seller/creator/driver/artist/business payable + TryAMM revenue + taxes/reserves.

## 8. Third-Party TV Licensing Path

Do not retransmit third-party channels simply because a stream can technically be embedded or captured.

Required path:
1. Identify channel/program owner or authorized distributor.
2. Define territory, device/platform, live/VOD, ad, clip, replay, and sublicense rights needed.
3. Negotiate commercial terms/minimum guarantees/revenue share if applicable.
4. Execute written license.
5. Store rights metadata and expiration/territory rules.
6. Configure entitlement/geo restrictions.
7. Add content only after rights status is APPROVED.
8. Monitor renewal/expiration.

Create a Rights Registry with statuses:
- prospect
- negotiating
- contracted
- active
- restricted
- expired

## Core Rule

Every revenue-generating surface must answer four questions:
1. Who owns the customer relationship?
2. Who is legally entitled to the revenue?
3. What costs/fees/taxes/reserves are deducted first?
4. Which Revenue Waterfall policy controls the split and payout schedule?

Nothing should bypass the authoritative payment, ledger, rights, support, and audit systems.
