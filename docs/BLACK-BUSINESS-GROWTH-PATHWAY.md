# TryAMM Black Business Growth Pathway

## Purpose
Create a first-class, opt-in Black Business discovery and growth ecosystem inside the broader inclusive TryAMM marketplace.

## Core user journey
Business joins → verifies profile/ownership claim → storefront + HoloMenu → HoloCoupons + Business Live → Support Black Business feed + HoloLocal → customer order/booking/visit → Jinn/PayRouter → digital receipt → HoloPass reward → verified review → repeat discovery.

## Growth programs
- Support Black Business feed/tab in the unified front scroll.
- Black Business Month, Black Business Week, Juneteenth, holiday gift guides and city spotlights.
- City directories with near-me, category, open-now, delivery, HoloCoupon, live-now and accessibility filters.
- Creator affiliate campaigns with server-verified conversions and configurable commissions.
- HoloAds and sponsored placements clearly labeled as paid promotion.

## Procurement and contracts
Supplier profiles should support category/NAICS, service area, capacity, certifications, insurance status, past performance, minimum order, delivery capability and countries served.

Opportunity functions:
- RFP/RFQ alerts
- corporate/public/nonprofit buyer matching where eligible
- supplier shortlists
- team/joint-bid discovery
- buyer inquiry inbox
- contract-readiness checklist

Never imply a business is certified, eligible, awarded or preferred without evidence.

## Funding center
Provide a searchable opportunity layer for grants, lenders/funders and accelerators, with eligibility guidance, deadlines, document checklists and application tracking.

Do not promise approval or funding. Third-party terms and eligibility must be explicit.

## B2B supplier exchange
- wholesale listings
- quote requests
- purchase-order workflow
- recurring procurement
- cross-border supplier discovery
- Africa/diaspora trade discovery

## GrowthOS Business Coach
May recommend storefront improvements, HoloCoupons, ad budgets, creator matches, customer win-back, procurement readiness, funding opportunities and cross-border expansion.

Requires merchant approval before spending funds, submitting applications, accepting contracts, changing prices or making consequential commitments.

## Monetization
Use configurable policies for optional subscriptions, featured placement, HoloAds, commerce fees, booking fees, affiliate campaign fees, AI credits, B2B tools, ticketing/events and education.

Do not hard-code one universal percentage across all products.

## Fairness and trust
- Directory participation is opt-in.
- Ownership verification level must be displayed accurately.
- General TryAMM marketplace remains open to eligible businesses.
- Paid placement is labeled.
- Ranking must not misrepresent verification or certification.
- Country/age/rights/payment availability filters apply globally.
