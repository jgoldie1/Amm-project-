## 9. Pilot acceptance journey
An approved Nigeria creator or seller can:

1. Create an account.
2. Load the correct age and accessibility profile.
3. Create a creator or business profile.
4. Publish content or create a store.
5. Open a holographic showroom.
6. Run a moderated livestream.
7. Complete a sandbox or approved pilot transaction.
8. Generate a receipt and complete audit trail.
9. Process a refund or dispute.
10. Reconcile settlement.

## 10. Promotion criteria

### `sandbox` to `pilot`
Requires:
- Staging deployment
- Core journeys functional
- Payment and logistics sandbox tests
- Security review
- Nigeria legal review in progress or completed for pilot scope
- Named pilot operators and support staff

### `pilot` to `limited`
Requires:
- Successful real pilot transactions
- Settlement reconciliation
- Support and dispute tests
- Accessibility test results
- Regional legal sign-off
- Executive approval

### `limited` to `open`
Requires:
- All critical findings closed
- Required provider production approvals
- Tax and invoicing readiness
- Customs and restricted-product controls
- Monitoring and incident response
- Formal launch sign-off
