## FaithVerse source integrity and casting
- Noah's Flood: Genesis 6:9–9:17 narrative; original simulated ark, rain, supplies and animal shelter. This is a religious story reenactment, not geological proof.
- Nephilim: Genesis 6:1–4 and Numbers 13:32–33, with source-dependent ambiguity about who the Nephilim were. Huge crooked-featured fantasy giants are TRYAMM art direction, not a documented ancient body scan.
- Fallen angels / Watchers: 1 Enoch 6–15, compared with but never silently substituted for Genesis 6. 1 Enoch is canonical in the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo tradition; other traditions classify it differently. The winged, asymmetric, unsettling beings are intentionally original non-graphic fantasy.
- David and Goliath: 1 Samuel 17; distinct Hebrew and Philistine individuals, non-graphic target challenge, no real weapon training.
- All story pages include explicit original-paraphrase / not-historical-footage status and local study checkpoints.
- Family intensity excludes teen stories in the selector; this is NOT an authenticated age check. Production requires age-appropriate account/parental control integration.
- Black Hebrew and diverse nations are founder-selected cast art direction, not a substitute for historical ethnicity evidence or grounds for caricatures.

GTA VI assets or code are not included; the production target is an original immersive open-world RP quality level with distinct TRYAMM branding.

1. Each Yeshua/Noah/Nephilim/Watchers/David/Goliath character: owner-approved GLB, named joints, skin weights, multiple realistic retargeted clips, cloth/hair animation, rig QA and likeness/rights review.
2. Real 3D terrain, heightfields, flood and water simulation, wind, boat buoyancy, character collision and physics interactions with Rapier.
3. Cars and motorcycles with individual wheel colliders, suspension, steering angle, brake bias, bike balancing, tire dynamics, impact handling, curb collisions, lights, passenger seats, entrance/exit animations and validated gamepad/one-hand UI.
4. Server-authoritative multiplayer rooms, NPC dialogue service, approved moderation, age gates, consent, server persistence, signed mission rewards and load testing. Current local quest events and generic 3D prototypes do NOT establish these systems.
5. Performance profiling on actual Chromebook and iPhone: WebGL fallback, 3D skin validation, frame-time budget, RAM usage, audio captions and return journey. CI alone does not certify device acceptance.
6. A stable source-verified Bible chapter/verse navigation and publisher-cleared Enoch/Apocrypha translations. Never invent ancient scripture, historical ethnicity or supernatural sightings.
7. Block production deployment until branch CI, real-model acceptance, publisher/rights approval, security, device and owner sign-off succeed.
