## Neon Grid / digital-world upgrade

A synchronized spatial grid can carry world coordinates, object IDs, avatars, vehicles, portals, construct frames, presence and controller commands. This creates the technical foundation for a neon-grid digital-world experience without depending on one display type.

## Autonomous Cursor

The Construct Holo Grid runtime exposes a cursor control surface for point, select, drag, interact and STOP. The cursor can be driven by approved inputs such as touch, controller, gaze, voice or calibrated non-invasive intent adapters. STOP remains the highest-priority safety command.

## Digital Presence

Consent-gated digital presence can represent a person or AI agent in a shared session. Voice and face flags cannot activate unless their respective consent flags are true. This is software-based telepresence/presence architecture, not a claim that a person is physically transmitted or reconstructed.

## Construct bridge

SECS construct frames can be projected into the shared spatial grid as simulation objects. The same frame schema can later be handed to validated display, haptic or robotics adapters. Physical holograms, tactile constructs and force effects still require real hardware engineering, independent interlocks and laboratory validation.

## Holographic Internet

The Holographic Internet is implemented as a spatial protocol layer over ordinary real network transports. It provides prioritized channels for control, presence, construct, world-state, media, commerce and telemetry. It does not claim a separate physical internet, quantum communication or zero latency.

## Production gates

- Provider-backed cross-device networking still needs authenticated transport and policy validation.
- Hardware-specific holographic display/haptic adapters still need physical testing.
- Commerce cannot bypass ledger/payment authorization gates.
- Voice/face presence requires consent.
- Non-invasive biosignal adapters must provide calibrated intent/confidence signals; unrestricted mind reading is not claimed.
