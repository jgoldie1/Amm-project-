# TRYAMM Regulated Services Revenue Model

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT / COMPLIANCE ARCHITECTURE

## Core rule
TRYAMM is the technology, discovery, scheduling, communications, identity/credential-verification, workflow, billing-support and marketplace layer. Licensed professional services remain with appropriately licensed/credentialed providers. Do not use percentage-of-professional-fee models where fee-splitting, anti-kickback, insurance, real-estate, legal, healthcare or other rules may prohibit them.

## 1. Domain / DNS without ICANN accreditation
Until TRYAMM becomes ICANN-accredited, operate through an approved ICANN-accredited registrar/reseller relationship.

Recommended economics:
- registration/renewal: registrar wholesale/retail pass-through + disclosed TRYAMM Domain Care fee;
- suggested Domain Care fee: greater of $4.99/year or 20% of registrar cost, subject to registrar terms and market testing;
- DNS management included in Business Starter+ or $2.99/month standalone;
- Forever Domain Care: long-term reserve-funded renewal service; never promise a conventional DNS domain is literally free forever;
- premium TLD pricing (.ai/.io/country-code/premium names) remains pass-through + disclosed management fee;
- customer retains registrar transfer/export rights.

Direct ICANN accreditation is a future milestone, not required for launch.

## 2. Legal Now / Lawyer On Demand
Use case: traffic stop, landlord issue, arrest/booking concern, business contract, family matter, consumer problem, other urgent legal-navigation need.

Workflow:
SAFE MODE → location/jurisdiction → issue category → licensed-attorney network availability → attorney profile/rate → user chooses → secure call/video/message → attorney engagement terms.

Traffic-stop mode:
- hands-free/simple controls;
- remind user to remain physically safe and follow lawful officer instructions;
- never advise evasion, concealment or obstruction;
- do not auto-record without jurisdiction-aware consent/law checks;
- optional trusted-contact notification;
- store minimal incident notes at user direction.

Revenue model:
- TRYAMM Legal Access membership: suggested $9.99-$19.99/month;
- fixed technology/booking fee: suggested $5-$19 per completed connection where lawful;
- attorney sets and receives professional legal fees directly;
- no percentage of legal fees unless TRYAMM later operates through a structure expressly permitted by applicable professional-conduct rules.

## 3. Telehealth / Health Navigation
TRYAMM provides scheduling, secure communications/provider workflow, accessibility, reminders, intake, credential status, claims-support interfaces and patient navigation. Clinical care is delivered by appropriately licensed/enrolled providers.

Suggested provider pricing:
- solo provider SaaS: $79-$149/month;
- practice: $199-$499/month based on seats/usage;
- additional compliant video/SMS/storage usage metered;
- avoid percentage-of-clinical-revenue pricing unless reviewed for the applicable jurisdiction/payer arrangement.

### Medicaid billing support
Only enrolled/qualified providers bill Medicaid for covered services. TRYAMM can provide claims preparation, validation, submission integrations, rejection queues, reconciliation and reporting.

Suggested billing-support pricing:
- $149-$399/provider/month base;
- clean-claim processing: $1-$3/claim;
- corrected/reworked claim: $4-$10/claim;
- enrollment/credentialing workflow support: $99-$299 one-time per application package, excluding government/payer fees;
- do not take an automatic percentage of Medicaid reimbursement without specialized legal/compliance review.

Claims must preserve payer rules, provider enrollment, covered-service requirements, coding/modifier/POS rules and audit evidence.

## 4. Tele-Tax / Bookkeeping
- bookkeeping workspace: $29-$99/month;
- business books + Business JARVIS reporting: $79-$199/month;
- tax-professional connection: fixed $5-$15 technology booking fee;
- paid federal tax return preparers must use their own valid PTIN and sign returns as required;
- TRYAMM AI may organize records, explain concepts and prepare drafts/checklists but must not impersonate a credentialed tax professional.

## 5. Tele-Insurance
- licensed producer/agency owns sales, solicitation, negotiation and advice;
- TRYAMM provides education, comparison UX from approved carrier/broker data, scheduling and CRM/workflow;
- Illinois-safe baseline for an unlicensed platform: flat advertising/listing/SaaS/referral payments not conditioned on the consumer purchasing insurance and not based on premium or commission amount;
- suggested producer SaaS/listing: $49-$199/month;
- suggested fixed qualified-lead/connect fee only if allowed and structured so it is not conditioned on purchase.

## 6. Tele-Realty
- licensed broker/agent performs licensed brokerage activity;
- TRYAMM provides Holo tours, scheduling, lead routing, documents/workflow, PropertyVerse, Marketplace and advertising;
- baseline revenue: $49-$299/month agent/broker SaaS + flat advertising/lead products;
- do not take a closing-based commission/referral percentage unless TRYAMM/entity/people hold required licenses and the arrangement is lawful.

## 7. Remote Notary
- connect to commissioned notaries/electronic notaries only;
- jurisdiction and commission status verified before appointment;
- TRYAMM charges a disclosed technology/booking fee, while statutory notary fees remain subject to local law;
- Illinois electronic/remote notaries require appropriate commission and bonding requirements.

Suggested platform booking fee: $3-$10 per completed notarization plus provider subscription options.

## 17. 3.00x sustainability rule
Every service is evaluated on contribution after direct variable costs. Goal: $3 of eligible platform revenue for each $1 of measured infrastructure cost, without counting creator/user funds or restricted liabilities as TRYAMM revenue.

Do not chase 3.00x by overcharging vulnerable users. Favor B2B SaaS, professional subscriptions, enterprise contracts, premium compute/storage, advertising/sponsorship and transaction infrastructure where lawful, while keeping core emergency/navigation/education pathways affordable or sponsor-funded.
