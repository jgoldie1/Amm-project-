## OmniCare360 owner-reported checklist versus actual readiness
A user-marked checkbox is not adequate evidence. Every line below is **UNVERIFIED** until a qualified reviewer validates primary-source documentation:
- Licensed clinical operator selected, contract signed
- Clinician licenses / malpractice documentation, board verification and patient-state coverage
- Vendor/subprocessor inventory, BAAs and legal applicability determination
- Clinical appointment scheduling and provider calendar with atomic booking safeguards
- Secured patient/clinician identities, MFA, patient consent, accessibility and guardian review
- Approved telehealth video/audio system with scoped room credentials
- Protected EHR partnership and audited clinical records handling
- Written risk analysis, penetration test, incident-response exercise and backup restore
- Actual Chromebook/iPhone clinical pilot acceptance
- Independent privacy, security, clinical, legal and executive signoffs

When evidence arrives, record control owner, expiration, primary source, reviewer and date in a restricted compliance system; do not place copies of credential documents, PHI or contracts in general website files.

## Separations and release gates
- **Marketing and commerce:** opted-in leads only; AI may draft customer support and content, but charges, contracts and payouts require explicit authorization.
- **Education and trading simulation:** use fictional money; never imply returns or send brokerage orders.
- **Clinical:** no PHI may cross into AI Commons, Mycelium, ad tracking, game progress, AI law drafts or creator money.
- **Compliance AI:** assists with checklists, research and evidence reminders; qualified professionals must approve legal/clinical issues.
- **Publishing:** content permissions, licensing and youth safety before release.
- **Production:** no deployment or merger until tests, independent reviews and current operating requirements pass.
