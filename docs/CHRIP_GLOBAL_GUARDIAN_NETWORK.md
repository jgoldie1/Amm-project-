# CHRIP Global Guardian Network

Status: RECOVERY-AWARE LOCKED SPECIFICATION

## Recovery note
The user states CHRIP was created previously with multiple account types for Community Guardian service, business, public use, and a `Force Forward Me` concept. Exact historical implementation details were not recoverable from the currently available repository/context, so this specification preserves those names and builds a safe integration path without inventing privileged powers.

## Purpose
CHRIP is a community-safety and mutual-aid communication layer designed to help people get assistance, escorts, witnesses, resource navigation, business/community support and rapid nonviolent coordination. It is not a vigilante force, police impersonation system, or substitute for emergency services.

## Account types
- `PUBLIC` — ordinary community member account for alerts, requests, check-ins, witness uploads, safe-route sharing and resource navigation.
- `COMMUNITY_GUARDIAN` — trained/verified participant for approved nonviolent support tasks such as presence, escort, welfare check-in, event support, de-escalation support and documentation.
- `BUSINESS` — merchant, venue, school, church/ministry, property or organization account for staff alerts, opening/closing escorts, incident reporting, safe-zone participation and Guardian service requests.
- `FORCE_FORWARD_ME` — user-controlled personal safety workflow that can notify selected trusted participants, share a temporary route/check-in window, request an escort or support presence, and escalate to appropriate public emergency resources when necessary.
- `ORGANIZATION_COORDINATOR` — authorized manager for approved Guardian teams, schedules, training, incidents and service areas.

## Safety principles
1. Observe, assist, document and de-escalate; do not chase, punish or confront suspects.
2. No impersonating police, security officers or emergency responders.
3. Weapons are not a CHRIP service feature.
4. No racial, religious, disability, neighborhood or other protected-class profiling.
5. No public doxxing, suspect lists or crowd-sourced accusations.
6. Emergency/imminent-danger flows route toward qualified emergency services.
7. Minors receive additional safeguarding and guardian/organization controls where required.
8. Location sharing is temporary, consented and minimized.

## Core flows
### Force Forward Me
START → choose trusted contacts/Guardian network → choose destination/time window → temporary location/check-ins → route risk notices from verified/public data where available → missed check-in escalation → safe arrival → automatic location-sharing expiry.

### Walk/ride escort
REQUEST → service type → pickup/destination → eligible Guardian/provider → accept → identity/role confirmation → live check-in → completion → feedback/audit.

### Business Guardian
OPEN/CLOSE ASSISTANCE | EMPLOYEE WALK-OUT | EVENT PRESENCE | SAFE-ZONE | INCIDENT DOCUMENTATION | DELIVERY/COURIER SUPPORT | COMMUNITY RESOURCE LINK.

### Community alert
USER REPORT → safety classification → moderation/verification → localized notification if appropriate → resource/action options → expiry/archive. Alerts must distinguish verified official information, participant reports and unverified observations.

## Training / Guardian Passport
Suggested modules:
- de-escalation and conflict avoidance
- first aid/CPR pathway through approved providers where certification is required
- bystander intervention
- disability awareness and accessible assistance
- youth safeguarding
- privacy and evidence handling
- emergency escalation
- anti-bias/profiling
- trauma-informed communication
- local-law boundaries
- event/community support

Learning Passport can record TRYAMM training completion; external professional/certification status remains separately verified.

## Business model
CHRIP can be profitable without selling fear:
- free public safety/check-in tools
- optional individual/family premium plans for expanded coordination/storage/features
- business subscriptions for staff escort requests, safe-zone tools, incident workflow and analytics
- event/community contracts
- trained Guardian service marketplace where lawful, with provider compensation and transparent TRYAMM platform fee
- institutional partnerships with campuses, residential properties, venues and community organizations
- sponsorship/grant support for free service in high-need communities

Do not charge users for contacting emergency services or hide critical safety information behind a paywall.

## Global expansion
COUNTRY → REGION/STATE/PROVINCE → CITY → COMMUNITY/ZONE.
Each geography gets a policy pack: emergency numbers, private-security/licensing boundaries, volunteer rules, background-check constraints, privacy/location laws, youth rules and insurance requirements. Features stay disabled until jurisdictional requirements are mapped.

## Stubbs AI / JARVIS integration
Personal JARVIS can initiate a CHRIP check-in or Force Forward Me flow. Business JARVIS can request an approved Guardian service, open an incident case or schedule opening/closing support. Stubbs AI may recommend de-escalation/resource options but cannot identify a person as criminal based on appearance or protected characteristics.

## Community Gap Finder integration
Aggregate privacy-preserving signals can identify missing safe transportation, lighting/resource issues, recurring escort demand, youth-program shortages, business closing-time concerns and community-service gaps. Individual reports are not exposed as public dossiers.

## Quantum Discord / CHRIP communities
Dedicated community rooms can support verified local announcements, Guardian shifts, safety education, resource events and peace missions. Anti-mob rules prohibit using CHRIP rooms to organize retaliation, harassment or confrontation.

## Sustainability
Eligible CHRIP platform revenue feeds the Platform Sustainability Engine. Guardian compensation, partner pass-through money, restricted grants and refunds remain separate from TRYAMM operating revenue.

## Metrics
- requests safely completed
- average response/check-in time
- safe-arrival confirmations
- businesses/zones participating
- training completion
- de-escalation/resource referrals
- accessibility satisfaction
- incidents escalated appropriately
- complaints/abuse findings
- geographic gaps closed

Metrics must never become a social-credit or neighborhood-danger score tied to individuals.
