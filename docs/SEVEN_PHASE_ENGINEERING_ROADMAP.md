# TRYAMM Seven-Phase Engineering Roadmap

| Phase | Work | Business result | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quantum Cloud cross-device recovery and secure authentication | Protect source, customers, gameplay | Signed-in save, restore on second device, access controls, cost budget |
| 2 | MiddleVerse CRM, referrals, test-mode Stripe | Measurable sales pipeline | Two-agent RLS isolation, signed webhook, reconciliation |
| 3 | AI Profit Controller and fulfillment queue | Establish unit economics | Verified revenue, commissions, real fulfillment costs and job delivery |
| 4 | Approve vetted international agents | Scale through variable costs | Contracts, screening, payout compliance, performance |
| 5 | Holo Reels and StreetVerse LIVE production | Sell creator services | Media ownership, recorded ingest, approved clips, cost tracking |
| 6 | StreetVerse Chicago certification | Introduce paid game experiences | 3D mission, drive, reload, reward and security checks |
| 7 | Global logistics and enterprise products | Diversified revenue | Supplier and freight verification, compliant quotes and pilots |

## Financial controls
The proposed 15% agent commission is a cost/liability. The $50 per qualifying sale is an internal allocation, not an additional expense until it is used. Track actual provider fees, refunds, chargebacks, fulfillment costs and taxes separately. Financial controller calculations are advisory. No automatic bank transfers, refunds, payouts or live checkouts. Keep StreetVerse fictional money away from real-money ledgers.

## Safeguards
Maintain PR #707 recovery work separately. Merge only after passing both CI and real browser-to-cloud restoration. Protect private leads with owner-scoped RLS and require owner authorization for role changes. Sanctions and territorial review are mandatory before global-market enablement.
