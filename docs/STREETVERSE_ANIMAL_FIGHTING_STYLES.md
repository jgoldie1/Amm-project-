# StreetVerse Animal Fighting Styles

Status: EXPERIMENTAL / DRAFT PR #251

## Historical labeling

The International Green Dragon Society's own published material describes eight Chi Tao Chuan animal systems:
- Leopard
- White Crane
- Panther
- Praying Mantis
- Tiger
- Black Cobra
- Wild Boar
- Eagle

The same published material mentions Tibetan Dragon Arts separately. StreetVerse therefore treats Dragon as its own advanced legacy/reconstructed style rather than falsely listing it inside the documented eight.

## Gameplay styles

### Panther
Fast repositioning, stealth-like movement and angle changes.

### Leopard
Fast chains, pressure and combo flow.

### Wild Boar
Stability, guard strength and forward pressure.

### Black Cobra / Snake
Precision, timing and counter windows.

### Praying Mantis
Parry rhythm, disruption and control.

### White Crane
Balance, evasion and spacing.

### Eagle
Reach, positioning and elevated-mobility challenges.

### White Tiger
Power, courage and stagger.

### Dragon
Adaptive mastery. Dragon can blend unlocked bonuses from previously mastered animal styles and becomes a late-game / Time Machine style.

These are game mechanics, not instructions for injuring people.

## Why this improves StreetVerse

### 1. Every player can fight differently
Instead of one universal melee animation, players develop a recognizable identity.

### 2. RP gets deeper
A player can belong to a dojo, bond with a mentor, train students, run tournaments, own a dojo business and build neighborhood reputation.

### 3. Time Machine history matters
Historical dojo-war missions can unlock present-day training rooms, mentor dialogue, animal-style cosmetics and secret missions.

### 4. One-hand combat becomes viable
Each style has accessibility assists:
- automatic facing;
- context attacks;
- hold-to-guard/parry;
- camera recenter;
- large finisher/action prompts;
- smart style suggestions.

### 5. The nine styles become progression classes
Style XP persists. At 500 game XP a style can be marked mastered. Dragon requires multiple mastered animal styles plus the Time Machine Dragon Legacy trial.

### 6. Tournaments gain strategy
Different matchups create different game choices without requiring graphic violence.

### 7. Creator content becomes recognizable
Animal-style intros, emotes, tournament entrances, replay cameras and themed Reels create shareable visual identity.

### 8. Easter eggs become systemic
Examples:
- Cobra clue hidden in Pedway;
- Eagle challenge on elevated transit;
- Crane balance clue near Riverwalk;
- Boar service-route challenge;
- Panther night mission;
- Dragon five-layer secret dojo.
