COMMUNITY RULES

Be real. Be safe. Help creators grow.

These house rules guide everyday use. Formal Terms, Privacy, copyright, payments and regional legal policies must also be completed before production launch.

01

No fake activity

No bots, stream farms, fake gifts, purchased views, chart manipulation, impersonation or scams.

02

Respect creators

No harassment, threats, hate, stalking, doxxing, sexual exploitation or disrupting rooms.

03

Own what you upload

Upload only music, video, artwork, performances and samples you own or are authorized to use.

04

Protect minors

Teen and adult lanes remain separate. No substance content, sexualized content, grooming, private-location sharing or adult solicitation involving minors.

05

Lawful adult substance content

Lawful adult discussion or depiction of alcohol or cannabis may appear only in age-restricted rooms and only where legal. In the United States, use 21+ for alcohol and recreational cannabis unless stricter local law applies. No sales, delivery, impaired driving, dangerous challenges, promotion to minors or illegal distribution.

06

BRB and Bathroom Break privacy

Use BRB, Bathroom Break or Intermission instead of abandoning a live room. Bathroom Break must mute the microphone and stop or disable camera transmission at the source.

07

Music during breaks

Use your own music, properly licensed music, public-domain music or the approved TryAMM royalty-cleared library.

08

Moderation is fair

Hosts and moderators may warn, mute, slow chat, remove, report or ban users. Serious actions should be logged and appealable.

09

No dangerous conduct

No credible threats, self-harm encouragement, violent challenges, weapon sales, trafficking, exploitation or instructions for serious wrongdoing.

10

Accessibility and culture

Respect disability access, captions, translation, one-hand controls, faith rooms, Sabbath settings and cultural differences.

11

Transparent promotion

Ads, sponsorships, paid boosts and affiliate promotions must be labeled. Paid promotion cannot purchase an organic chart position.

12

Second chances with boundaries

Correctable mistakes may receive warnings and education. Fraud, exploitation, severe threats and repeated abuse can lead to immediate suspension.

Before going live

Creators confirm that they accept these rules, have rights to broadcast their content, will use the correct audience rating and will protect private audio and video during breaks.
