Privacy Policy

TryAMM is designed to collect only data needed to provide, secure, improve, and support its services. Data categories may include account information, device/session data, transaction metadata, creator/content metadata, support communications, analytics events, and region/country information when needed for eligibility, fraud prevention, or compliance.

Payments

Card and regulated payment processing is handled by licensed payment providers such as Stripe and approved regional partners. TryAMM should not store raw card numbers. Payment providers may independently collect identity, payment, tax, KYC/KYB, fraud, and compliance data under their own policies.

Analytics

TryAMM may use first-party analytics to understand product usage, attribution, funnels, performance, and security. Sensitive secrets, passwords, raw payment credentials, and private health data must not be written into analytics events.

Your choices

Users may request access, correction, export, or deletion of eligible personal data, subject to legal, fraud-prevention, tax, financial-record, safety, and dispute-retention requirements.

Children and teens

Age-appropriate experiences, parental/guardian controls where required, and additional safety protections must be used before youth features are enabled.

Security

TryAMM uses access controls, provider tokenization, audit logs, encryption where applicable, and monitoring. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Use the support/contact page for privacy or data requests.
