PRIVACY NOTICE

Your information should serve your experience—not become the product.

Effective August 17, 2026. This public notice describes the current TRYAMM web prototype and should be updated before major commercial release or entry into additional jurisdictions.

Information collected

When you create or use an account, TRYAMM may process your display name, email address, authentication data, creator profile, posts, room activity, preferences and technical service information. Payment providers may process transaction details directly when payment features are enabled.

How information is used

  • Provide accounts, creator tools, publishing and community features.
  • Authenticate users, moderate content and protect the service.
  • Operate, debug and improve TRYAMM.
  • Meet legal, safety and accounting obligations when applicable.

Public content

Approved network posts, public creator material and confirmed public team profiles can be visible to anyone. Do not publish passwords, government identifiers, financial account details, private health information or another person’s personal data.

Storage and sharing

The current service may use hosting, database, authentication, media and payment vendors. Information is shared only as needed to operate those services, protect users, follow the law or complete an action you requested. TRYAMM does not promise that prototype data will be retained indefinitely.

Your choices

You may sign out, avoid posting publicly and request review or removal through the moderated Network until a dedicated privacy-request channel is published. Parents or guardians should supervise minors and use the designated age lanes.

Security and changes

No system can guarantee perfect security. TRYAMM uses staged controls and will revise this notice as the platform, vendors and legal requirements change.
