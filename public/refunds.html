Refund & Cancellation Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the product or service purchased, the amount of work already completed, the applicable provider or marketplace rules, and local law.

Subscriptions

Users may cancel future renewal of eligible subscriptions through the applicable account or billing interface. Cancellation generally stops future renewal and does not automatically refund prior billing periods unless required by law or stated otherwise.

AI and generation credits

For metered AI, rendering, 3D generation, or other provider-backed jobs, failed jobs should not silently consume the full customer charge. TryAMM may retry, re-credit, partially refund, or charge only for completed billable work according to the job state and provider recovery.

Marketplace purchases

Refunds for digital assets, creator products, or services may depend on whether the item was downloaded, delivered, consumed, licensed, or materially used. Rights and fraud concerns may require review.

Payment disputes

Chargebacks and disputes are handled through the applicable regulated payment provider. Users should contact TryAMM support first when possible so the issue can be investigated.

Regional rules

Consumer rights and mandatory refund rights vary by jurisdiction and will take priority where applicable.
