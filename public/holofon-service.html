HoloFon Mobile + Bill Pay

TRYAMM's future mobile and wireless service hub, connected to Quantum Boost and NuMerica.

Development preview, not a live cellular carrier or payment portal. No SIM/eSIM activation, telephone number, cellular plan or bill-payment processing is available here. No card or account information is collected. Service will require a signed carrier/MVNO agreement, compliant provisioning and verified billing integration.

Proposed customer journey

  1. Check availability and carrier coverage using an authorized partner
  2. Choose an approved plan with clear pricing and taxes
  3. Complete identity/porting steps only in an approved secure provider workflow
  4. Provision SIM/eSIM after eligibility and verified payment
  5. Access invoices, account status, permitted payment methods and support
