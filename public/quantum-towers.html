Quantum Boost · Diagnose the real bottleneck

ISP speed issue: compare wired performance against the customer's subscribed service, check provisioning/congestion, then escalate to the ISP with authorization. A router cannot create bandwidth the provider does not deliver.

Wi-Fi issue: improve coverage, interference, access-point placement, backhaul and router configuration. For gaming, measure latency, jitter, packet loss and device frame rate separately before and after changes.

Internet speed reality: If an ISP supplies 100 Mbps, Quantum Boost cannot turn that service into guaranteed 1 Gbps Internet. A higher-capacity ISP connection or another authorized uplink is required. Quantum Boost instead targets measurable local Wi-Fi, congestion, latency and application bottlenecks.

Quantum Boost is a brand for practical optimization, caching and performance tuning—not quantum hardware, unlimited Internet speed or guaranteed zero lag.
