A new branded gateway for composing business inquiries. This preview opens your email app; it does not create mailboxes, send autonomously, or encrypt messages end-to-end.

Future upgrade: branded @yourdomain inboxes through a licensed mail provider, authenticated sending, spam controls, consent and attachment security.

Memento Web Revival — Archive Discovery

Memento (RFC 7089) is an open framework for navigating historical versions of websites. The former central aggregator was discontinued; this independently branded interface links to existing archives rather than pretending that aggregator is running.

Only public URL lookups are supported. Archives may contain missing captures, restrictions, or redirects. Snapshot dates are not guaranteed.

Custom-Domain Quantum Internet Mail

Recommended first: verify a free Zoho Mail custom-domain plan for the business domain you already own, subject to regional and account eligibility. A paid Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or Proton Mail plan is an optional later upgrade.

Before activating mail, verify domain ownership; then configure provider-issued MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records and perform inbound/outbound deliverability tests. No mail account, MX record or payment has been changed by this preview.
