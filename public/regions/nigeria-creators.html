TryAMM · Africa Commerce · Marketplace

Nigeria’s creator economy, connected to the world

TryAMM is being built for Nigerian musicians, filmmakers, streamers, educators, vendors and small businesses who want one place to create, learn, sell and reach audiences beyond their local market.

Create and publish

Produce music in Quantum Vocal Studio, publish music videos, host livestreams, release short dramas, teach courses and build a storefront from one creator account.

Local payments and global reach

Planned support includes naira pricing, Paystack and Flutterwave checkout, bank transfer, USSD, wallet records, creator settlements and Aniyah cross-border corridors. Live money movement remains subject to provider approval, KYC, AML and local compliance.

Built for mobile realities

Low-bandwidth mode avoids forced autoplay, reduces image weight, keeps checkout text-first and supports large one-handed controls.

Grow through the platform

Creators can join regional showcases, share referral links, earn transparent rewards and use Streaming Academy to improve production, monetization and business skills.
