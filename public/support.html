TryAMM Support

Use this page for account, billing, OmniPay, privacy, safety, creator, marketplace, and app-review support.

Payments and OmniPay

For failed payments, duplicate charges, refunds, payout questions, or payment-method availability, provide the transaction date, amount, country, and receipt/reference shown in your account. Do not send full card numbers or passwords.

Account and access

For sign-in, account recovery, security, or deletion requests, use the account tools available in the app when present or the account-deletion instructions below.

Privacy and safety

Privacy, data-access, abuse, harassment, impersonation, copyright, trademark, likeness, and Digital Twin concerns should be escalated through the appropriate reporting or support workflow.

App-review contact

Reviewers should use the support contact configured in the applicable app-store listing and may reference this public support page for policy and service information.
