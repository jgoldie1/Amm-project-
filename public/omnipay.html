OmniPay

One TryAMM payment experience across creators, commerce and digital services.

OmniPay is the TryAMM payment orchestration layer for eligible checkout, subscriptions, marketplace transactions, creator commerce, OmniCredits, TapPay and regional payment methods. Regulated money movement is handled through licensed payment providers and financial partners where available.

Regional approach

United States & eligible global markets

Stripe is the preferred primary rail for supported checkout, subscriptions, wallets, Connect marketplace flows and eligible in-person payment experiences.

Nigeria & Africa

Country, currency and capability-based routing may use Stripe where eligible and approved regional providers such as Flutterwave or Paystack where required.

South America & Latin America

Country-specific routing can support eligible local methods such as Pix in Brazil through compatible licensed providers, with cards, wallets and regional alternatives where available.

Safety and transparency

No raw card storage

TryAMM should use tokenized provider-hosted payment flows and avoid storing raw card numbers or sensitive authentication data.

Verified payment status

Payment completion, refunds and disputes should be confirmed through signed provider webhooks and durable transaction records.

Clear fees

Applicable platform, processor, FX, cross-border or service fees should be disclosed before payment where required and practical.

Regional compliance

Availability depends on local laws, provider support, KYC/KYB, sanctions screening, tax obligations, account capability and product eligibility.

Refunds, disputes and support

Refund eligibility depends on the purchased product or service, merchant terms, fulfillment state, provider rules and applicable law. Payment disputes and chargebacks are handled through the relevant regulated payment provider and TryAMM support workflows.

Important: OmniPay is a TryAMM orchestration and user-experience layer. TryAMM should not claim to be a bank, licensed money transmitter, card network or payment processor unless and until the applicable licenses and registrations are actually held.
