Delete Your TryAMM Account

Users may request deletion of their TryAMM account and eligible associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Sign in to TryAMM when possible and use the account/privacy settings deletion workflow once enabled.
  2. If you cannot sign in, use the support channel listed in the app-store listing or the TryAMM support page and request account deletion from the email/identity associated with the account.
  3. Complete identity verification when required to prevent unauthorized deletion.

What may be deleted

Eligible profile information, preferences, non-required content, and other personal data may be removed or anonymized according to applicable law and product rules.

What may need to be retained

Certain transaction, tax, fraud-prevention, safety, dispute, licensing, payout, audit, or legal-compliance records may need to be retained for required periods. Retention does not permit unrelated use of that data.

Connected providers

Deletion of a TryAMM account does not automatically delete accounts or legally required records held independently by payment providers, app stores, or other third-party services. Users may need to contact those providers separately.
