Privacy and preview status

This site is a prelaunch preview. This page describes only the current static website behavior; a final privacy policy must be approved before launching services that collect customer data.

Inquiry form

The current inquiry form runs in your browser. It does not send messages to a server or store submitted inquiries. Because no recipient is configured, it displays a copyable message for you to send using verified contact details.

Template preview builder

Changes to business name, headline, description and industry are processed locally in the browser. The sample HTML export is generated on your device. No account, checkout or server storage is connected.

Cookies and analytics

The authored preview pages do not intentionally set cookies, run advertising pixels or include third-party analytics. The eventual hosting provider may have its own infrastructure logs; those practices must be reviewed and disclosed before launch.

Healthcare and sensitive information

Do not submit medical information, financial account numbers, Social Security numbers, passwords or other sensitive personal data through these preview pages. OmniCare360 and the AI Call Center are not live patient-care or emergency services.

Upcoming services

Before enabling customer records, email or SMS campaigns, recordings, payments, AI tools or healthcare services, this page will need updated disclosures, verified contact information, consent choices and appropriate contractual and security controls.
