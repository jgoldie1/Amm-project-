# Raymond Jarreau — podcast and template launch kit

## Podcast pilot: "Legacy Conversations with Raymond" (working title)
Audience: families, founders and small-business owners. Educational discussions only, no personalized financial advice.

Episode 1: Five questions every family should ask about financial protection.
Episode 2: Retirement account basics: what a 401(k) is and questions to ask a qualified adviser.
Episode 3: Infinite banking: potential benefits, costs, policy loans, and alternatives.
Episode 4: Estate planning: beneficiaries, wills and trusts, with a licensed attorney guest.
Episode 5: Starting a business: budgeting, risk, operations and protecting continuity.
Episode 6: Meet the Stubbs — Los Angeles entrepreneurs and community legacy.

Per-episode workflow: topic approval → guest release → outline → record → edit → accessibility transcript and captions → compliance review → publish to verified host → create 3 social clips → link to an opt-in landing page → review analytics. Never publish without speaker consent and necessary rights.

## Sponsorship offer — proposal only
- 15-second pre-roll acknowledgement
- 30–60-second clearly disclosed sponsor message
- Newsletter / website partner listing
- Optional sponsored episode subject to editorial independence and review
- Require written sponsor terms, cancellation/refund policy and proof of delivery.
- Do not promise audience size, impressions or revenue without measured data.

## Advertising funnel
Awareness: original podcast clips, search-friendly educational pages and voluntary cross-promotion from the 20 business sites.
Interest: audience-specific landing page and optional educational checklist.
Action: consultation inquiry or template preview.
Follow-up: only after consent, with unsubscribe, access controls and retention policy.
Measurement: campaign URL/ref code, visits, consented inquiries, scheduled meetings, settled template orders and net revenue.
No third-party pixels, SMS, automated emails, or behavioral tracking enabled by this starter kit.
