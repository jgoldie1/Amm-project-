Ownership that works for you

Independent website

Your own brand and domain account; website files and approved assets delivered to you. Domain registration and third-party service costs must be disclosed separately.

Shared marketplace storefront

A branded shop within My Tribe with exportable product, order and customer records where legally permissible. You may migrate later; no forced paid subscription.

The founder keeps ownership of their original content, brand, and lawful customer relationships. Platform-owned software and licensed third-party assets remain subject to their own terms. The exit process must be documented in the participation agreement.

What the free starter program proposes

Accessible website template, business idea workshop, basic launch checklist, product/service menu, simple inquiry workflow, training sessions, and community membership. Storefront publishing, payment collection, and AI automation are subject to technical testing and written owner approval.

Sponsor-supported microgrants

Microgrants are a future proposal, not currently funded. Once donations or sponsorships actually clear into an appropriate account, publish eligibility, award limits, review criteria, conflicts rules and expense documentation before accepting grant applications. Never require a purchase to be considered.

Selection and accessibility

Proposed pilot: adults 18+ who self-identify as having a disability and consent to participate. No medical records or diagnosis proof requested. Review feasibility (30%), founder's stated goal (25%), accessibility and support plan (20%), readiness to participate (15%), and responsible cost (10%). Offer phone, email, and assisted application routes; provide captions, screen-reader-compatible forms, plain-language explanations, and flexible schedules. Reserve an appeal or feedback route. Do not automatically reject someone because they need accommodations or have limited funds.

Before paid self-employment, participants receiving SSI, SSDI or Medicaid should consider independent benefits counseling. See SSA WIPA and NDI Small Business Hub.
