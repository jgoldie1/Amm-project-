# StreetVerse × NuMerica — Founding Creator Pilot

This is an **isolated creator-intake and mod-review pilot**, not a deployed product.

## Founding creator funnel
1. Recruit modders with the flyer.
2. Collect applications through the local v20 ZIP's creator-program page (an email draft, **not** automatic storage).
3. Invite accepted applicants to submit original declarative mod manifests in PRs.
4. Run automated manifest checks plus human rights, moderation and device-performance review.
5. Only after independent approval, stage approved content in a preview environment.

## Rewards
Proposed 70% creator / 30% platform split on eligible net sales; **no payouts or earnings are active or guaranteed**. Creator contracts, tax verification, payment ledger, refunds and moderation must precede monetization.

## AI integration
MiddleVerse AI can eventually assist intake and explain schemas; HoloGPT can eventually help model scenes and missions. Both remain planned; no AI service or secrets are attached here.

## Safe contributions
Do not commit secrets or personal applicant details. No third-party executable JavaScript. No copied GTA/FiveM assets. A submitted manifest must include license provenance and creator rights.
