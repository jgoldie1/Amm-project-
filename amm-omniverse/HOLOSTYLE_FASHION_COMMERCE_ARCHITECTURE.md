# TRYAMM HoloStyle Fashion Commerce Architecture

Status: APPROVED PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE — implementation and production verification still required.

## Mission
HoloStyle Design → Create → Sell connects Benny, Construct, AI Twin visualization, fashion education, brand building, global sourcing, StreetVerse commerce, creator media and server-authoritative payouts.

## Core Flow
IMAGINE → BENNY → CONSTRUCT → DESIGN → AI TWIN TRY-ON → HOLO WRAP → BRAND → TRAIN → TECH PACK → RIGHTS VAULT → QUANTUM SOURCE → RFQ/BID → SAMPLE/QC → PRODUCTION → STREETVERSE STORE → REELS/LIVE/AI TV → CHECKOUT → SERVER VERIFICATION → LEDGER → PAYOUT.

## HoloStyle Fashion Academy
Training tracks:
- Fashion foundations: silhouette, color, materials, construction and fit.
- Digital fashion: 3D garments, AI Twin presentation, HoloStyle scenes and digital twins.
- Technical design: measurements, size ranges, tech packs, BOMs, tolerances and revision control.
- Brand Builder: naming, positioning, audience, visual identity, packaging, pricing and launch planning.
- Fashion business: costing, landed cost, inventory, preorder/drop models, margins, returns and customer service.
- Creator commerce: StreetVerse stores, LIVE selling, Reels, affiliate campaigns and product placement.
- Adaptive/accessibility design: configurable closures, fit and presentation requirements.
- Sourcing readiness: RFQs, samples, quality control, logistics and supplier scorecards.
- Rights & provenance: creator ownership records, permissions, licensed digital twins and asset clearance.

Training can support free lessons, paid courses, certifications/badges, sponsored challenges, mentorship, designer cohorts and brand-launch programs. Credentials must not claim accreditation unless separately authorized.

## Benny Personal Fashion Mentor
Benny acts as the conversational guide across Academy, Brand Builder and HoloStyle. She can translate user intent into structured design briefs, explain costing, recommend training modules, prepare a sourcing checklist and guide a seller toward a launch-ready product. Recommendations must distinguish generated suggestions from verified facts.

## Send to Design
Approved concepts can generate a designer/manufacturer handoff with front/back views, measurements, size range, materials, trims, color references, construction notes and revision history. Human technical-design validation is required before manufacturing.

## Quantum Source / Global Manufacturing
Quantum Source compares approved suppliers across regions such as the United States, Mexico, Africa, China, Japan and other Asian manufacturing hubs. Compare total economics rather than unit price alone:

unit cost + MOQ + sample/tooling + materials + QC + freight + duties/tariffs + insurance + expected defect allowance = estimated landed cost.

Supplier scorecards should include capabilities, MOQ, lead time, quality history, communication, compliance evidence and logistics. Supplier facts and quotes require current verification.

## Rights / NNN / NDA Vault
Before sensitive manufacturing files are released, the Rights Vault records ownership and permissions and applies the appropriate approved agreement workflow. International NDA/NNN and manufacturing agreements require jurisdiction-appropriate legal review. Use staged disclosure so preliminary bidders receive only information necessary to quote.

## Fashion-to-Media Flywheel
DESIGN → TRY ON → TRAIN/BUILD BRAND → SOURCE → SELL IN STREETVERSE → CREATOR WEARS PRODUCT → REEL/LIVE/AI TV → PRODUCT DISCOVERY → CHECKOUT → PAYOUT → DATA-INFORMED NEXT DROP.

This makes fashion a repeatable creator-business loop rather than a standalone store.

## Data / Safety / Quality
- minimize retention of raw identifiable activity data
- retain transaction/compliance records only as required
- do not manufacture or sell copied designs without rights
- label AI-generated concepts and speculative representations appropriately
- require human validation for production measurements and safety-critical specifications
- provide accessible non-3D fallback paths
