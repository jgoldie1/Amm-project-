Research & education

Accessibility

One-handed interaction, captions, reduced motion and persistent accessibility preferences are treated as core product requirements.

Release philosophy

RECOVER → ADAPT → WIRE → MIGRATE → TEST → REPAIR → BENCHMARK → DEPLOY. Features are not considered production-proven until the relevant gates actually pass.
