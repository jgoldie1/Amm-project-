TRYAMM PRIVACY NOTICE

Your information should serve your experience.

Effective September 25, 2026. This notice covers the TRYAMM alpha web and Android candidate.

Information we process

Depending on the features you use, TRYAMM may process account and authentication information, profile information, user-created content, room and moderation activity, device/service diagnostics, accessibility preferences, and transaction records. LIVE/PK may transmit camera and microphone media when a host explicitly joins a room. Payment providers process payment details when payment features are enabled.

Age and youth safety

The Android candidate is designed for an intended audience beginning at age 12, subject to Google Play review and regional content ratings. The age-safety gate retains a coarse age band rather than the birth date entered into that gate. The age-12 lane is viewer-only for LIVE/PK in this release: camera, microphone, hosting, paid gifts/tips and freeform personal-information exchange remain disabled until adult-managed controls are verified.

Location and permissions

Camera, microphone, notification, location or motion permissions are requested only where a feature needs them and the operating system allows you to decide. Denying a permission may limit that feature without authorizing unrelated collection.

Safety and moderation

Reports, blocks, mutes, moderation evidence and safety records may be processed to protect users and enforce TRYAMM rules. Serious safety, fraud, payment and security records may be retained where legally or operationally required.

Retention and deletion

TRYAMM aims to minimize raw identifiable activity data and delete or irreversibly de-identify it when it is no longer needed. Legal, accounting, tax, fraud/security, transaction, safety, contractual or other required records may be kept for their required period. You can request account deletion from the web or in the app.

Public content and vendors

Content you choose to publish may be visible to others. TRYAMM may use hosting, database, authentication, media, communications and payment vendors as needed to provide the service. Do not publish passwords, government identifiers, financial credentials, private health information, exact home location or another person’s private data.
