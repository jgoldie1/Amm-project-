Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a product today?
No. The site is a public discovery preview and has no active checkout. Do not send money to anyone claiming to represent a Mobility 360 order that does not appear in a verified checkout.
Why are product prices missing?
We are obtaining manufacturer and wholesaler quotes. Prices can only be shown reliably after confirming the exact product model, shipping, duties, taxes, payment costs, returns and product suitability. Low advertised factory prices are not the total delivered cost.
Are the pictures and videos real products?
Current product icons are illustrations, not verified photographs of merchandise. Real photos and on-demand captioned demonstrations will appear only after we have supplier authorization or our own properly documented sample footage. Please do not interpret concept graphics as an exact model or accessory.
Are these products medically approved?
Not as a group. Ordinary daily-living consumer aids and regulated medical devices have different requirements. Braces, electrical stimulation devices, powered gloves and exoskeletons require specific manufacturer/model checks. A business being FDA registered does not by itself mean an individual device is FDA approved. We will not publish unsupported recovery or cure claims.
Does Stubbs AI give medical advice?
No. The Mobility 360 Shopping Guide offers general catalog navigation, practical comparison questions and links to learning resources. It cannot diagnose conditions or decide which therapy or brace fits you. For symptoms or personal rehabilitation decisions, consult a qualified professional. For new stroke symptoms or a medical emergency, call 911 in the United States.
Do you sell to seniors, caregivers and people with other disabilities?
That is the plan. Our catalog is organized by everyday task and includes hand function, mobility, communication, vision, hearing, sensory preferences, care routines and more. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis to browse.
What is the shipping time, return policy and warranty?
We have not started sales, so we cannot offer a shipping-time guarantee, refund policy for real orders or product-specific warranty yet. Before we activate checkout, each seller and product will need clearly displayed delivery expectations, returns/refunds, damage handling and warranty terms. We will not charge customers until these are ready.
Can I use Medicare or insurance?
We do not currently advertise Medicare reimbursement, insurance claims processing or medical equipment supplier accreditation. Future coverage or provider partnerships would need separate eligibility, regulatory and operational review.
Will using voice shopping share my health information?
The basic on-page Shopping Guide works locally for catalog questions without sending its text to our AI backend. Optional voice recognition can be processed by your browser's speech service, which may have its own policies. Full HoloGPT uses separate services. Do not enter passwords, medical records or sensitive diagnoses in public shopping chats. Routine site delivery may create hosting logs.
How do I share a product with someone?
Use the Share product idea button beside the product detail or copy the store link. A product-specific link opens that item's preview information. Sharing does not create an order and does not send private health information.
Who can I contact about a return or an order?
There are no Mobility 360 customer orders yet. Verified support channels and business contact details must be published before any orders are accepted. For now, don't submit payment or private patient information through unrelated social media accounts claiming to process Mobility 360 orders.

Our approval checklist

For each product we want to sell, we plan to verify the following before enabling payments:

  1. Manufacturer identity, exact model and accurate photographs or licensed demonstration media.
  2. Product specifications, instructions, customer fit information and relevant safety requirements.
  3. Written fulfillment arrangements, real stock source, import duties and delivered costs.
  4. Customer service contacts, returns, refunds and warranty obligations.
  5. Applicable consumer safety and FDA requirements, especially for higher-risk equipment.
  6. A tested, server-controlled checkout with documented totals and customer receipts.

For manufacturers and disability-owned sellers

We are preparing a future supplier and entrepreneur marketplace. Media licensing, authentic product identification, one-unit shipping, quality documentation, and transparent commissions will be necessary. No seller applications or partnership approvals are open on this preview page. We will not reuse product videos or photos from social media advertising without permission.
