How we intend to beat the competition

1. Actual delivered pricing

Compare manufacturing price, freight, tariffs, defects, returns and payment costs. Publish one clear delivered price after quotes, never a fake discount.

2. Tested in real life

Sample products with consent-based feedback from disabled customers and occupational therapy partners. Show honest pros, limitations, sizing and left-/right-hand options.

3. Accessible by default

Large touch targets, high contrast, keyboard navigation, plain language, screen-reader labels, captioned videos and low-bandwidth pages.

4. Responsible fulfillment

Require traceable suppliers, country-of-origin information, recall checks, realistic shipping dates and clear returns and warranty coverage.

Important safety distinction

Reachers and everyday adaptations can often be evaluated as ordinary consumer goods. Walking supports, electrical stimulation, powered gloves, exoskeletons and certain other products may be regulated medical devices. Products in clinical review are NOT available to order, and must not be marketed with unproven recovery claims.
