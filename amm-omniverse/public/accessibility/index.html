Accessibility Statement

TRYAMM is committed to making our website, applications, media, commerce tools, and Living Worlds usable by people with disabilities. This includes people who are blind or have low vision, Deaf or hard of hearing, people with mobility, speech, cognitive, neurological, or other disabilities, and people who use assistive technology.

Our accessibility goals

We are working toward accessible experiences consistent with WCAG 2.2 Level AA principles. Our ongoing work includes keyboard access, meaningful headings and labels, screen-reader support, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, scalable text, captions and text alternatives for media, reduced-motion support, accessible forms and controls, and touch targets designed for mobile and alternative input.

Blind and low-vision access

We aim to provide semantic page structure, accessible names for interactive controls, useful alternative text for meaningful images, keyboard navigation, screen-reader announcements for important status changes, and interfaces that remain usable when text is enlarged or browser zoom is increased.

Accessibility is ongoing

TRYAMM contains evolving interactive and immersive experiences. Some areas may still have accessibility barriers while they are being developed or improved. We treat accessibility as an ongoing design, engineering, testing, and feedback responsibility.

Need help or found a barrier?

If you cannot access part of TRYAMM, encounter an accessibility barrier, or need information in another accessible format, please contact the TRYAMM team and describe the page, feature, or content involved and the accommodation or format that would help.
