The platform should adapt to the player.

Accessibility is part of the release architecture for TRYAMM, not a separate side feature. The platform is being designed around one-handed interaction, captions, sign-language support, keyboard control, reduced motion, readable interfaces and persistent accessibility profiles across worlds.

Open TRYAMM

One-handed use

Core actions should remain reachable without requiring simultaneous multi-touch or complex hand combinations.

Captions & visual communication

LIVE, missions and creator experiences are designed to support captioning and sign-language pathways where available.

Persistent preferences

Accessibility settings should travel with the user across StreetVerse, LIVE, Holo experiences and future connected worlds.

Reduced motion & clarity

Holographic design should remain usable with reduced-motion modes, strong focus states, keyboard access and readable contrast.
