TRYAMM ACCOUNT DELETION

Request deletion from the web.

You do not need to reinstall the mobile app to request deletion. Use the web Account Privacy Center, sign in to the TRYAMM account you want deleted, and submit the tracked deletion request.

Open Account Privacy Center

What happens

The request is recorded for the signed-in account. TRYAMM will delete or irreversibly de-identify data that is not required for legal, accounting, tax, fraud/security, transaction, safety, contractual or other legitimate retention obligations. Required records may be retained only for the required period.
