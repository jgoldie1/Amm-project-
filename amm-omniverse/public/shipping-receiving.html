What shipping & receiving needs

Inbound

ASN, dock appointment, trailer/container ID, seal, unload, barcode/QR scan, count, inspection, photo evidence, discrepancy, quarantine, lot/serial capture, put-away, inventory release.

Outbound

Order release, inventory allocation, pick, pack, carton/pallet ID, label, manifest, carrier handoff, tracking, delivery confirmation, returns and reverse logistics.

Warehouse hardware

Phones or scanners, barcode/QR labels, thermal printer, shipping scale, dimension capture, photo station, dock camera, optional temperature sensors for Holo Fridge/cold-chain.

Documents

PO, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading/air waybill, certificates when required, receiving report, damage/shortage record, proof of delivery and return authorization.

What is still missing before real logistics revenue

Carrier accounts

UPS/FedEx/USPS or multi-carrier API for parcel; LTL/FTL partners for freight; ocean/air forwarder relationships for international moves.

Warehouse partner

Either your own facility or a 3PL with receiving SLAs, storage rates, pick/pack rates, cycle counts, returns, insurance and API/EDI access.

Customs/import partner

Licensed customs broker for client customs business; importer-of-record workflow; bond; tariff classification/origin/value evidence; agency permits where product-specific rules apply.

Export compliance

Schedule B, ECCN/EAR99 determination, restricted-party/end-use screening, AES/EEI workflow where required, license management when applicable.
