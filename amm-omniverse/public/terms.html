Prototype and safety limitations

Features may change, pause, degrade or remain simulations. Do not rely on TRYAMM for emergencies, medical decisions, safety-critical navigation, unverified financial transfers or other high-stakes operations.

You may request deletion of your TRYAMM account from the app or from the web. Some records may be retained where legally or operationally required.
