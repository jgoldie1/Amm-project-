Know before you enter

Campus questions

Is Greenville a real accredited university?

No. This Greenville University is a fictional CampusVerse experience inside StreetVerse.

Can I book a dorm or submit tuition?

Not yet. Housing and academics are game locations; no real admissions or payment service is active.

Is the AI counselor live?

The current concierge answers from a local campus guide. A secure live AI service would require a separate backend and operational testing.

Can I use one hand to play?

The game has one-hand control features under development. Accessibility and final device testing remain required.
