TRYAMM COMMUNITY RULES

Be real. Be safe. Help people create.

Protect minors

No grooming, sexual exploitation, adult solicitation, private-location sharing, coercion or attempts to move a child into unsafe private contact. Protected youth lanes may limit hosting, paid gifts, personal-information exchange and mature content.

Respect people

No harassment, threats, hate, stalking, doxxing, impersonation, scams, dangerous challenges or disruptive abuse. Report, block or mute when necessary.

Own what you upload

Broadcast or upload only material you own or are authorized to use. Respect copyrights, trademarks, privacy and publicity rights.

Keep mature content in adult lanes

Adult nightlife, substance-related or other mature themes must remain behind appropriate age restrictions and may be unavailable in child or teen experiences.

LIVE/PK responsibility

Hosts must use the correct audience lane, protect private audio/video during breaks, follow moderation requirements and avoid exposing personal information. Viewers should not share passwords, school details, exact home locations, phone numbers or other sensitive information.

No fake activity or financial deception

No fake gifts, stream farms, purchased views, bot manipulation, payment fraud or claims that simulated rewards are withdrawable money.

Moderation and appeals

TRYAMM may warn, mute, remove, restrict or suspend content/accounts to protect users or comply with law. Serious enforcement should be logged and eligible decisions should have a review or appeal path.
