# La Taeasia BeautyVerse — free-first launch readiness

This document is a release checklist. It does not assert that a service, account, or deployment is active.

## Can prepare at no new cost
- Brand description, owner-approved photos and portfolio with customer consent.
- Five separate service categories: lashes, hair, esthetics, skincare, makeup.
- Public inquiry form draft, FAQ, cancellation policy draft, accessibility statement.
- Fashion and beauty catalog with verified product claims; no checkout until supplier and merchant approvals.
- Streaming content calendar, reusable vertical video scripts, and approved official social links.
- CrossVerse/StreetVerse Tampa promotional pathway and QR code once a real public destination exists.
- Academy interest list and non-licensure content plan, with regulator review of any paid program.
- SEO copy targeting actual shop service area in Tampa; no fake reviews or fake addresses.

## Owner-supplied launch prerequisites
- Confirm Florida licenses/registrations and shop permission for each regulated service.
- Confirm actual business/shop name, address, hours, intake terms and booking calendar.
- Approve service durations and prices; confirm cancellation and deposit rules.
- Confirm rights/consent for portraits and portfolio images.
- Verify merchant account, payout ownership, suppliers, tax and shipping settings.
- Confirm official streamer platform accounts and links.

## Engineering acceptance gates
- Development branch only until lint, TypeScript/build, tests and real-device checks pass.
- VITE_ENABLE_TAMPA_VOLCANO_BETA stays off for production until explicit release approval.
- Owner operations demo must never show private real bookings: use authenticated server APIs and row-level isolation first.
- CrossVerse must not treat clicks, in-game actions or unverified payments as settled earnings.
- Preserve backups and last-known-good deployment rollback before release.
- Avoid any paid API, subscription, advertising or new domain without approval.

## Marketing experiment
Offer one free portfolio consultation call and ask past consenting clients for genuine photos/reviews, never incentives for positive reviews. Publish three short-form beauty tutorials a week, one behind-the-scenes livestream and one approved portfolio look. Measure inquiries, confirmed appointments, repeat bookings, average order value and contribution margin. These are proposed activities, not scheduled posts.
