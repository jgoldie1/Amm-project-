## Intake requirements before activating an artist
1. Confirm the person or their authorized representative initiated or accepted contact.
2. Confirm identity and eligibility; separate minor/guardian path.
3. Collect only necessary contact details with consent, in access-controlled storage.
4. Obtain explicit scope-specific signed consent for name, face, likeness, voice, choreography, music/master, and livestream usage.
5. Offer a review/approval opportunity for character scans and voice representations.
6. Agree in writing on compensation, discounts, credits, revenue sharing, promotional benefits and cancellation/withdrawal terms.
7. Confirm production rights, music synchronization, performers and stage agreements.
8. Provide accessible participation methods, including voice/remote options.
9. No bulk unsolicited DMs, impersonation, or use of unverified public profiles.
10. Mark invited, responded, agreed, character approved and stage confirmed as separate states; never count invitations as partners.
