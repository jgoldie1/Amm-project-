# TRYAMM / All American Marketplace — Quantum Tower Launch Playbook

## Release principle
One working commercial offer, one playable StreetVerse Chicago scene, one protected Gamecast. Expand only on proof. Quantum Tower is a virtual software headquarters/world landmark, not evidence of physical quantum computing or self-aware AGI. Main/production stays unchanged until CI and acceptance pass.

## Architecture: one business, multiple floors
- **Quantum Tower Ground Floor:** StreetVerse Circle Park → Taylor Street, BJ, six-seat vehicle, NPC, mission complete.
- **LIVE Studio:** LiveKit host/viewer gamecast; TikTok only through authorized LIVE access.
- **CrossVerse Passport:** user identity, licensed avatars, recorded likeness/voice consent, controlled travel.
- **AI Operations Floor:** Stubbs AI show planning, MiddleVerse moderator queues, Lyons Tech diagnostics, JARVIS/HoloGPT panel navigation; human approvals and minimal privileges.
- **Creator Workshop:** Mod Pass/Dev Pack sandbox → review → production-certified catalog.
- **Marketplace Showroom:** TRYAMM website services, creator storefronts, approved add-ons, receipts, refunds.
- **Broadcast Tower:** TRYAMM network, short vertical Reels, original music, guest showcases, news only when factual and sourced.

## Launch products (published offerings only when delivery and checkout verified)
1. TRYAMM Preview website/reel service — $499 planned list price.
2. Experience landing page and 3 reels — $1,250 planned list price.
3. Digital Twin / StreetVerse storefront — $2,500 planned price only after integration proven.
4. Business Pass — $149/month planned recurring offer only after subscription delivery verified.
Never sell future capabilities as currently functional or promise earnings. Payment authorizations, refunds and creator commissions are backend-only.

## Marketing funnel
1. Show **actual gameplay evidence** as a 15–30 second video; never substitute an AI render without disclosure.
2. TikTok: original hook, live development, demo, behind-the-scenes; use authorized TikTok LIVE when eligible.
3. Instagram/Facebook: vertical clips, local Chicago small-business transformation examples, compliant original content.
4. YouTube: 3–8 minute detailed progression/build-log episodes and chapters after full gamecast works.
5. Twitch/Kick: gaming community streams after channel authorization.
6. Every clip should ask for one action: **request a website preview**, **join early access**, or **apply as a creator**.
7. Use a clear landing page with offer scope, demo, genuine price, contact CTA and privacy disclosures.
8. Contact 10 relevant local businesses with personalized, no-pressure examples; track replies and outcomes.
9. Review weekly: published demo count, unique landing visits, qualified leads, proposals, paid orders, delivery acceptance, actual paid margin, replay viewers and 7-day retention.

## First four-week rollout (gated, not date promises)
- Week 1: build/test six-seat mission and protected gamecast on two devices; polish a genuine product landing page.
- Week 2: post 3 original short demos; reach out to 10 eligible businesses; offer first paid preview service.
- Week 3: 1 creator showcase or pilot LIVE, only if LiveKit and platform rights pass; collect feedback.
- Week 4: publish one case study if customer authorizes it; invite a small creator beta for vetted cosmetic/asset mods.

## Three creator levels
- Internal sandbox: unpublished code, private assets, staging-only tests.
- Approved creator program: uploader, rights documentation, security/content/performance review, audit trail.
- Certified marketplace: signed/versioned manifest, backend payment verification, refund/rollback, revocation rights, creator settlement and support.
Marketplace listings and payouts stay disabled until provider setup, taxes/policies, eligibility, payment flows and review pass.

## Operator checklist
- LiveKit cloud project, server-only URL/key/secret, approved host Supabase UUID.
- Vercel deployment/account blocker resolved or alternate secure hosting verified.
- Passing package build, static contract tests, signed host/viewer/microphone/screen-share acceptance.
- TikTok account eligible with approved encoder and rights-cleared gameplay/music.
- One-hand Chromebook/mobile controls working during broadcast.
- Never confuse audience chat, gifts, game XP or monetizable tokens.
- CrossVerse consent and minor safety rules checked before public footage.
