# Stubbs Mobility 360 — store preview (2026-10-08)
Accessible product-discovery lane for the TRYAMM / All American Marketplace ecosystem.

## Current status
- Implemented: `/mobility360.html` self-contained, mobile-friendly preview of 26 product concepts across 10 departments; searchable and filterable by practical task, accessible controls, large text toggle, risk notices.
- Not implemented: payments, supplier integration, prices, physical inventory, FDA verification, customer contact intake, quotes, shipping, refund processing, paid ads, medical advice or claims. No product on this page is represented as for sale.

## Priorities
1. Validate grabbers, dressing aids, no-tie laces, nonslip kitchen aids and one-handed cooking boards with samples and landed-cost quotes.
2. Negotiate 2+ suppliers per SKU; compare direct China export, US-stock wholesaler and 3PL fulfillment; confirm non-counterfeit products and returns.
3. Verify safety, labeling, CPSC recalls, FDA classification/registration/clearance as applicable. Powered therapy devices remain gated.
4. Connect inventory and server-authoritative Stripe checkout only after product sourcing and compliance are documented.
5. Evaluate accessibility with screen reader, keyboard, single-handed phone use and WCAG 2.2 AA checks; have real disabled customers test.
6. Launch with captioned demos and consented affiliate/creator partnerships; disclose sponsorships and substantiate claims.

## Research snapshot (2026-10-08)
Third-party ASINsight reports suggest reachers and shower seats have notable Amazon demand, but their estimates are not audited sales. TikTok Shop's adaptive one-handed board listings have diverse prices with limited visible sales, so do not treat them as proven viral winners.

## Scope boundary
This static preview intentionally has no checkout. Live sale authorizations must flow through existing TRYAMM commerce services, with vendor identity, inventory, taxes, shipping, refunds and compliance validated server-side. Do not advertise Medicare acceptance without DMEPOS prerequisites.
