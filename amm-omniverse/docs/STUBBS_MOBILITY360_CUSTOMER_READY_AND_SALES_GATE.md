## What this website DOES NOT imply
Approved suppliers, paid stock, purchased samples, licensed marketing media, genuine testimonials, guaranteed outcomes, FDA authorizations, live checkout, real customer-support channel, insurance billing, return warehouse or delivery promises.

## Must be complete before ANY buy button is enabled
1. Legal merchant entity, valid payment account, bank/tax setup, importer roles and product liability coverage.
2. Supplier business identity and exact SKU/model, independent risk review, quality/labeling/traceability and appropriately sampled products.
3. Actual delivered cost including freight, duties, payment charges, refunds and marketing; documented price and stock.
4. Written rights for official product photos and videos, or original media of authorized real samples, plus subtitles and accurate claims.
5. Relevant U.S. consumer and medical-device requirements; professional review for powered therapy and safety-critical items.
6. Inventory, fulfillment contract, shipping times, tracking, accessible support channel, warranty, damaged goods and transparent returns/refunds.
7. Server-controlled approved-product cart/payment, valid webhook verification, order receipts, failed-payment/refund testing, mobile accessibility checks.
8. Founder approval and legal review for merchant privacy, legal disclosures and public policies.
9. Production browser verification and tested first real orders and support procedures.
