# Stubbs Mobility 360 — "What else can we build?" Expansion Blueprint
October 8, 2026 • Roadmap, not currently operating services or confirmed vendors

## Zero extra domain needed
Main: tryamm.online
Product discovery: /mobility360.html
Education, future video plans and community: /mobility360-learn.html
A later branded subdomain could be set up through verified DNS without necessarily buying a second domain. Reuse existing TRYAMM authentication, accessibility, commerce architecture and legitimate checkout protections when available.

## Keep the initial $100 unspent until actual quotes arrive
Use documented caps: reacher $25; button hook $12; elastic laces $10; one-hand jar opener $15; freight/import $18; reserve $20. Never promise 48-hour delivery or start paid ads without legitimate supply, fulfillment and customer-support readiness.

## All 15 founder-requested customization and expansion areas
1. **Brand name changes:** Use Stubbs Mobility 360 publicly for consistency, optionally test alternate campaign subtitles such as "Independent Living Marketplace." Check trademarks before rebranding or private labeling.
2. **Color theme:** Mint / teal / warm-white defaults, with meaningful accessible contrast, dyslexia-friendly readable type where requested, larger text controls and reduced motion.
3. **Customer priority:** First focus on adults seeking one-handed daily living, seniors and caregivers; expand by *task* to mobility, low vision, hearing, speech, neurodiversity, arthritis, Parkinson's, wheelchair living and spinal cord injury. No mandatory diagnostic questions.
4. **Budget versus premium:** Low-cost essential aids first, higher-end clinician-vetted equipment and larger items later through specialist providers. Show honest delivered-price comparisons; don't compare unlike specifications.
5. **Bundles:** One-Hand Independence Kit, Dressing Ease, Adaptive Kitchen, Senior Home Essentials, Caregiver Relief, Low-Vision Starter and Accessible Tech Starter. A bundle cannot be sold until every item and total delivered cost is verified.
6. **Shop-by-need guidance:** Browse "I want to cook, get dressed, reach objects, communicate, see labels, travel, use my phone, prepare a bathroom." Voluntary filters only, without collecting medical diagnoses.
7. **Founder story:** Use an accurate founder-approved first-person draft, reflecting life after stroke without disclosing additional private health information or inventing therapeutic results.
8. **Logo & design:** Accessible wordmark and icon; symbolic disability-friendly brand graphics. Do not suggest all disabilities are identical or reduce people to a stereotype; solicit paid community design feedback.
9. **Faith-friendly messaging:** Optional, welcoming, clearly separate from product eligibility and customer support. Accessible to people of every belief.
10. **Video-heavy pages:** One product video per approved SKU when affordable; captions, transcripts and product warnings; videos user-initiated, bandwidth-sensitive and brand-rights reviewed.
11. **LIVE shopping:** TRYAMM short reels → honest product demo → authorized listing → live Q&A → validated cart. Never pretend to have live stock, stream capability or permission to sell a regulated product.
12. **Affiliate and ambassadors:** Disability content creators can earn disclosed referral fees after fraud, tax and contract checks; no fabricated earnings guarantees; mandatory paid/gifted partnership disclosures.
13. **Disability-owned seller marketplace:** Optional disability-owned business discovery with consent for self-identification, verified seller profiles, fair payout contract and accessible vendor dashboards. Support makers and co-designers with IP respected.
14. **Caregiver education:** Free comparison checklists, captioned product setup lessons, ergonomic principles from properly qualified instructors; no individualized clinical advice and no paid caregiver certification claims without valid credentialing.
15. **Doctor/therapist/facility B2B:** B2B vendor catalog, quote requests, procurement documentation, returns, group shipping and professional equipment trial partnerships. No referral kickbacks or medical endorsements; Medicare DMEPOS enrollment/accreditation is a separate track if sought.

## New suggestions to go further
16. **Accessible enterprise storefront service:** License accessible storefront templates and help other disabled entrepreneurs launch their own online shops on TRYAMM; clear recurring pricing and reseller rights.
17. **Refurbish, repair and trade-in:** Qualified vendors only; serialized device traceability, hygiene and recalls, safety reinspection and written warranty. Avoid unsafe re-sale of regulated equipment.
18. **Community purchasing co-op:** Transparent volume quotes for nonprofits, faith/community organizations and care agencies; disclose fees and verify nonprofit eligibility before claiming grant/Medicare funding.
19. **Ask-a-human product concierge:** Trained accessibility-aware support with chat/voice/captions, respectful language and a clear non-clinical scope; no AI diagnosing suitability.
20. **Adaptive gaming / accessible creator tools:** Input switches, large controllers, one-hand layouts, captions and assistive phone mounts integrated with TRYAMM activities, provided product safety and compatibility are verified.
21. **Subscription reorder without lock-in:** Restock nonclinical consumables with transparent cadence, cancellation and consent; never bill for unavailable items.
22. **Institutional equipment lending libraries:** Pilot with qualified partners before operating rentals. Establish sanitization, maintenance, safety, insurance and warranty rules.
23. **Global regional shipping:** Expand U.S. first, then consider local fulfillment for Africa and other regions after import, labeling, VAT, privacy, safety and local partnerships are verified. Don't presume global regulatory equivalence.
24. **Product development / private label:** Take customer feedback into factory design of easier grips, inclusive sizing and lower-cost adaptive bundles. Validate prototype IP, product liability, recall capability, manufacturing and test compliance.
25. **Consumer product-testing panel:** Compensated, voluntary, accessible usability testing across disabilities. No collection of sensitive diagnosis data unless necessary and lawfully protected. Publish honest pros and cons; avoid fake or coerced testimonials.

## Revenue model proposals — none live yet
Retail gross margin after complete landed cost and refunds; optional own-brand bundles; affiliate percentages by approved contract; vendor subscriptions only for delivered software value; B2B purchasing margins; education courses taught or reviewed by qualified professionals; local verified home-service referral fees within legal boundaries; accessible software development services.

## Launch phases
0–14 days: first four genuine quotes, media licensing permission requests, practical use-case questions, independent safety review, reserve $100.
15–30: order affordable samples only after founder authorizes spending; photograph/film real items; write and verify SKU listings and shipping terms; screen reader and iPhone user testing.
31–60: activate 5–15 independently approved low-risk SKUs with server-verified fulfillment and payment; first real receipts/refunds, accessible customer support and return testing; begin no-cost organic social educational posting with disclosure.
61–90: up to 51 SKUs as each passes its own supplier gate; test bundles and follow-up support; start two or more B2B conversations; only then evaluate paid advertising and premium devices.

## What "ready" must mean
- Concept designed ≠ vendor approved.
- Photo permission ≠ copyright ownership transferred.
- Supplier's listing ≠ verified inventory, warranty or FDA compliance.
- "FDA registered" ≠ "FDA approved".
- Draft reel ≠ recorded original demonstration.
- GitHub merge ≠ a successfully deployed publicly reachable website.
- A planned store ≠ functioning checkout or a legal DME provider.
- Product sale revenue ≠ profit after all costs.
