# TRYAMM Academy + Creator + Jobs Recovery

## Purpose
Recover and protect the education-to-opportunity architecture without replacing newer main safety contracts.

## Canonical loop
LEARN → PRACTICE → BUILD → EARN

ACADEMY → SKILLS/TALENT PASSPORT → STREETVERSE PRACTICE/MISSIONS → PORTFOLIO → CREATOR/EMPLOYEE/AGENT OPPORTUNITY → VERIFIED WORK → AUTHORITATIVE EARNINGS ATTRIBUTION → CREATE MY BUSINESS → HIRE FROM STREETVERSE → MENTOR NEXT LEARNER

## Streamers Academy
Tracks include camera/lighting/audio, LIVE hosting, PK/panels, Reels/editing, podcast/radio, music/64-Track Studio, safety/moderation, copyright basics, interviewing/journalism, LIVE commerce, sponsorship/product placement, Holo Ads, audience development, AI-assisted production, accessibility, multi-platform distribution, CTV/OTT/FAST and long-form media.

## Opportunity Center
Provide pathways for creators, employees, agents, apprentices, mentors and business owners. Preserve work-from-home MiddleVerse support, Business Scouts, Holo FON agents/dealers, All American Store operators, Beauty operators, YAHAVAH sellers, LIVE/PK sales, warehouse/delivery and apprenticeships.

## Create My Business
Guide: choose business → training → Skills/Business Passport → territory → storefront/service → provider/compliance gates → catalog → hiring → advertising/LIVE-PK → fulfillment → authoritative payments/ledger → launch readiness.

## Hire From StreetVerse
Post real job → match verified skills → interview → required training → employer decision → authorized payroll/provider → retention/outcome evidence.

## Credential truth
TRYAMM course completion must never be represented as an accredited degree or third-party license unless the authorized issuer verifies it. Hazardous or regulated work must retain applicable external credential and provider requirements.

## Money truth
Clients do not settle real money. Real wages, payouts, commissions and provider settlement remain server/provider authoritative. Agency/commission splits require valid agreements.

## Youth/family
Use age-appropriate discovery, guardian controls where applicable, privacy/data minimization and no automatic agency enrollment.

## Rollout
1. Preserve contracts and regression tests.
2. Build Streamers Academy UI and Skills/Talent Passport views.
3. Build Opportunity Center and MiddleVerse job matching.
4. Build Create My Business and Hire From StreetVerse workflows.
5. Connect Creator Portal, LIVE/PK/Reels, Omni Radio, Holo Music, Holo Ads and CTV/OTT/FAST.
6. Connect Chicago 77 territory demand and business/job missions.
7. Add country-specific provider/employment/compliance configuration before global activation.
8. Certify with evidence; label LIVE only when production and provider evidence exists.
