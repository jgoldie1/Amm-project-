3. server-authoritative result or eligible revenue evidence
4. anti-fraud/Jacobie Vision
5. calculate allocation server-side
6. immutable/idempotent ledger entry
7. payout provider sandbox submission
8. webhook settlement
9. failed payout handling
10. refund/chargeback/reversal handling
11. tax/reporting state where required
12. two-account audit proof
