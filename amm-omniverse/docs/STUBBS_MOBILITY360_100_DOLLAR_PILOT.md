## Selling more SKUs than we physically sample
Yes, an approved supplier/3PL can ship inventory the seller does not own. The seller remains responsible for accurate product descriptions, good faith shipping deadlines, refund processing, warranty and customer support.
- Sample personally: the four initial hero products and every new risk-heavy item or private-label product where practical.
- To add a **basic, nonclinical product** without personally buying one of every color/size: require reliable manufacturer specifications, actual authorized photographs, third-party quality evidence, U.S. stock/ship times or confirmed supplier capabilities, landed cost, warranty/returns, and independent risk review. Record which variations were *not* sampled.
- Regulated, implantable, powered rehabilitation or high-risk safety devices are **not** eligible for generic unsampled dropship auto-approval. Require exact model regulatory assessment and appropriate partner service.
- Re-verify SKU stock and price before any charge, track supplier failure, and shut off an item if safety or shipment evidence lapses.

## Sales approval checklist for each SKU
Supplier and legal entity; business verification; manufacturer/model/variant and batch traceability; category and relevant safety/medical device status; product image rights; validated product claims; intended-user fit and warnings; current ship origin, time, tariff and all-in landed price; inventory source; realistic delivery date; return/refund procedures; warranty and product liability; server-authoritative pricing and payment gate; accessible plain-language product description; accessibility feedback; founder release sign-off.

## Launch order
Phase A: first four samples and full catalog preview only.
Phase B: enable verified low-risk products only after supplier and checkout testing; consider 15–20 such SKUs without owned inventory.
Phase C: extend to 51 as individual supplier and product approvals pass.
Phase D: B2B, community-led demonstrations, repeat-supply bundles and professional clinical device partners, in that order.

## Compliance
FTC Mail/Internet/Telephone Order Merchandise Rule: support advertised ship deadline with a reasonable basis and delay-consent/refund process. Dropship does not transfer seller responsibility. FDA device importing classification and labeling requirements vary by actual item and role.
