# TRYAMM LearnVerse + Workforce Release Architecture

**Status: LOCKED ARCHITECTURE / IMPLEMENTATION REQUIRED**

This document locks the production direction for All American University, LearnVerse, MiddleVerse Jobs, Employment Passport, Holo Connect, StreetVerse training simulations, certificates, and verified economic outcomes.

Architecture documentation is not a claim that these systems are LIVE. Founder Command Center status must remain one of: LIVE / READY / BUILDING / LOCKED / COMING SOON. A capability becomes LIVE only after implementation, testing, deployment, and production verification.

## 1. Core Economic Learning Loop

PLAY -> LEARN -> PRACTICE -> REPEAT -> EXPLAIN BACK -> LAB -> PRACTICAL TEST -> TRYAMM CERTIFICATE -> EMPLOYMENT PASSPORT -> APPRENTICESHIP / JOB -> VERIFIED WORK -> EARN -> BUILD A BUSINESS -> HIRE THE NEXT LEARNER

The purpose is to convert real platform activity into education, demonstrated competency, supervised work, and legitimate economic opportunity.

## 2. All American University LearnVerse Engine

Every supported course can use a common mastery loop:

1. Teach the concept.
2. Demonstrate it.
3. Let the learner perform it in a game/simulation.
4. Give accessible corrective feedback.
5. Repeat weak competencies with varied scenarios.
6. Ask the learner to explain the concept back by supported voice, text, or accessible input.
7. Complete a realistic lab or StreetVerse mission.
8. Complete a knowledge assessment.
9. Complete a practical assessment.
10. Issue a TRYAMM training certificate only when required competencies are demonstrated.

XP, play time, purchases, or payment for a course must never by themselves qualify a learner for a certificate.

- Holo Music / Omniverse Radio Lab: hosting, production, call-ins, requests, scheduling, advertising operations, rights-aware workflows.
- Creator Studio Lab: capture, editing, captions, translation, publishing, authorized product placement, creator support.
- Customer Support Lab: simulated calls, escalation, accessibility, de-escalation, documentation, multilingual workflows.
- StreetVerse Operations Lab: QA, missions, events, community operations, Digital Twin assistance.
- Technology Lab: QA/testing, accessibility testing, AI-assisted operations, junior technical workflows.
- Global Operations Lab: localization, regional merchant support, translation workflows, territory-aware operations.
- Entrepreneurship Lab: business planning, Business Passport, Digital Twin, marketplace launch, lawful advertising, customer acquisition.

## 4. Explain-Back Mastery

The mastery engine should store competencies rather than only course completion.

For each competency track:
- instruction evidence;
- practice attempts;
- scenario variation;
- corrective feedback;
- explain-back evidence;
- lab performance;
- assessment results;
- practical demonstration;
- reviewer/appeal state when required.

AI may provide formative feedback and recommendations. Consequential certification, employment, earnings suspension, or disciplinary decisions must have defined human-review and appeal paths where appropriate.

## 5. Employment Passport

With user consent, the Employment Passport can contain:
- verified identity state;
- skills and demonstrated competencies;
- TRYAMM certificates;
- languages;
- accessibility/accommodation preferences;
- availability;
- training history;
- practical assessment results;
- apprenticeship/work history;
- verified platform work records;
- employer-visible evidence selected by the worker;
- earnings records where legally and operationally appropriate.

Do not expose private learner information to employers without appropriate permission.

## 6. TRYAMM Certificate Integrity

TRYAMM certificates must identify:
- issuing organization;
- program/course;
- demonstrated competencies;
- completion date;
- unique verification identifier;
- verification URL or QR workflow when implemented;
- credential status/revocation state where applicable.

TRYAMM training certificates must not be represented as accredited college degrees, government credentials, or regulated professional licenses unless the required external authorization/accreditation actually exists.

## 8. Compensation Guardrails

Compensation must be configured by jurisdiction, employment classification, role, experience, schedule, and applicable law. Planning ranges are not employment promises.

For U.S. planning only, initial budget bands may be evaluated around:
- entry support/moderation/data operations: $15-$22/hour;
- merchant onboarding/specialized support: $18-$28/hour;
- production/editing/QA: $20-$35/hour;
- engineering, management, licensed/professional work: market-based role-specific compensation.

Before any role goes LIVE, validate minimum wage, overtime, employee/contractor classification, benefits, payroll/tax obligations, accessibility/accommodation obligations, and written compensation terms for the applicable jurisdiction.

Scout incentives must be tied to documented merchant/customer acquisition or attributable commerce, not recruitment of additional Scouts.

## 9. Holo Connect: One Human Doorway

Holo Connect is the planned routing layer for one primary TRYAMM/Holo contact number plus supported regional/local/SIP/other channels.

Supported intents can include:
- Holo Music / Omniverse Radio requests and call-ins;
- business advertising intake;
- Business Passport help;
- employment and training intake;
- employer hiring requests;
- Marketplace support;
- creator support;
- StreetVerse support;
- human-agent transfer.

The public phone number must be acquired and controlled through an appropriate telecom/voice provider. Never invent a production number.

Benny/HoloGPT may classify intent and route calls, but recording/consent, privacy, emergency limitations, accessibility, human escalation, and regional telecom requirements must be respected.

## 10. Business + Creator + Employment Passport Triangle

BUSINESS PASSPORT <-> CREATOR PASSPORT <-> EMPLOYMENT PASSPORT

All three connect to StreetVerse, LearnVerse, MiddleVerse, Marketplace, Holo Ads, Holo Music/Radio, PUBLISH, and the server-authoritative ledger.

This enables a learner to become a worker, a worker to become a creator or entrepreneur, and a successful business to become an employer/sponsor of future learners.

## 13. Server-Authoritative Earnings

Real-money work and commerce flows must be verified server-side before balances become payable.

Track, as applicable:
- work/transaction identifier;
- worker/creator/business/Scout identities;
- gross amount;
- processor/payroll fees;
- tax treatment;
- refunds/chargebacks/disputes;
- platform share;
- worker/creator/business/Scout share;
- restricted/pending balance;
- payout-eligible balance;

Never trust client-side values for compensation, price, commissions, rewards, ownership, or payout eligibility.

## 16. Competitive Moat

The moat is not a claim that competitors cannot copy features. The defensible system is the integrated evidence loop:

GAME/SIMULATION -> MASTERY -> VERIFIED COMPETENCY -> CREDENTIAL -> WORK -> VERIFIED ECONOMIC OUTCOME -> BUSINESS/CREATOR DEMAND -> MORE WORK -> MORE TRAINING

Protect it through reliable software, trademarks/IP where appropriate, documented rights, security, auditability, employer relationships, creator/business network effects, accessible design, high-quality curriculum, and verified outcomes.

## 17. Release Definition

A release is complete only when the targeted gate:
1. exists in production code;
2. passes automated checks;
3. passes accessibility and security review appropriate to the feature;
4. deploys successfully;
5. is manually verified on the production user path;
6. has monitoring/rollback support;
7. has correct Founder Command Center status.

**Locked principle:** planning is LOCKED; code can be BUILDING/READY; only verified production behavior is LIVE.
