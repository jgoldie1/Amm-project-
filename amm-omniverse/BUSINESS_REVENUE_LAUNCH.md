## Customer funnel
REEL / AD → BUSINESS LANDING PAGE → CHOOSE OFFER → INTAKE → PAYMENT → BUSINESS VAULT → SCOPE LOCK → ASSETS → BUILD → QA → CUSTOMER REVIEW → LAUNCHREADY TEST → ACCEPTANCE → LAUNCH → LAUNCHCARE → BUSINESS PASS / UPSELL / REFERRAL

## Fulfillment rule
No feature is sold merely because it exists in planning. Customer-facing capabilities must be marked LIVE or READY and must pass the LaunchReady test before delivery. BUILDING, LOCKED and COMING SOON capabilities are not represented as currently deliverable.

## Scope-of-work minimum
Every paid build must record deliverables, accepted asset formats, included revisions, expected turnaround, customer responsibilities, IP/usage permissions, third-party costs, hosting/service terms and change-order rules.

## Checkout gate
Before enabling a real payment button for an offer:
1. Create/verify the matching server-side price/product mapping.
2. Verify payment success and failure paths in sandbox.
3. Verify authoritative server-side payment confirmation.
4. Create the order and fulfillment record exactly once.
5. Verify refund/cancellation handling where applicable.
6. Pass production smoke test.
