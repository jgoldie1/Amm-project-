# TRYAMM Business + StreetVerse Revenue Architecture

This document locks the current commercial architecture into the production repository. A feature described here is not considered LIVE until implemented, tested, deployed, and verified in production.

## Core Business Growth Engine

TRYAMM combines the Black Business Directory and Support Black Businesses discovery lane with the broader Global Business Registry. Eligible businesses can progress through:

Black Business Directory / Global Business Registry -> Business Passport -> StreetVerse location -> Marketplace -> Creator/UGC -> Sponsored Missions -> Holo Ads -> Digital Twin -> Business SaaS -> Delivery/Services -> Sales -> Server-authoritative Ledger -> Growth Analytics.

The global network can serve all eligible businesses while preserving a dedicated, prominent Black-owned-business discovery experience. Ownership designations require a documented verification standard.

## Five Additional Entry Revenue Streams

1. Business Passport Activation: target $29-$49. Includes verified listing workflow, QR Passport, basic storefront, location pin, and introductory analytics where implemented.
2. Sponsored Mission: target $79-$199. Businesses can sponsor lawful branded discovery/challenge experiences with clear reward terms.
3. Holo Ad / Placement Campaign: target $99-$299. Virtual billboards, screens, storefront placement, and authorized creator/media placement.
4. Commerce+: target $49-$149/month. Ordering/booking, catalog, coupons, loyalty, CRM/AI assistance, and enhanced analytics.
5. Performance Marketplace: low/no setup fee with an agreed attribution commission on qualified sales/bookings.

Pricing is commercial planning and must be finalized before checkout is activated.

## Business Help Center

Business assistance can include business-plan support, storefront/site setup, product photography and creator content, AI-assisted marketing, customer-service tooling, bookkeeping integrations, funding/grant discovery, procurement-readiness guidance, supplier discovery, shipping/delivery integrations, accessibility, translation, and workforce/job listings.

Optional paid Growth Services can include: build my storefront, create my Digital Twin, produce my ad, set up my catalog, translate my campaign, and create my StreetVerse experience.

Funding tools must distinguish grants, loans, and investment and must never imply guaranteed approval.

## Scout / Agent Acquisition

Scout discovers business -> owner scans TRYAMM QR -> business claims/creates Passport -> directory/storefront activation -> optional growth services -> StreetVerse activation -> advertising/missions -> attributable activity enters ledger.

Compensation must be based on documented business/customer acquisition or attributable commerce, not recruitment itself.

## StreetVerse Vault + Virtual Economy 2.0

Player inventory can support clothing, shoes, jewelry, furniture, vehicles, vehicle parts, business equipment, creator equipment, collectibles, tickets/access, property customization, music/entertainment items, and mission-earned objects.

Economy capabilities: rarity, collections, customization, crafting/upgrading, gifting, bundles, creator-designed items, digital twins, and authorized branded merchandise.

Competitive monetization should emphasize cosmetics, expression, access, and creation rather than pay-to-win advantages.

### Fictional Game Equipment / Weapons

Fictional game inventory may support classes, skins, rarity, durability, cosmetic attachments, animations, display cases, mission unlocks, and fictional crafting/upgrades. This is game content only and is not a system for real-world weapon commerce.

Example monetization path:
Base item -> skin -> animation -> cosmetic attachment -> themed collection -> display case -> creator edition.

## Creator Marketplace

Approved creators/businesses can publish compatible virtual merchandise. Real products can have optional StreetVerse digital counterparts, subject to rights and merchant authorization.

REAL PRODUCT <-> DIGITAL TWIN <-> STREETVERSE MISSION <-> REEL/MOVIE <-> PRODUCT PLACEMENT <-> MARKETPLACE PURCHASE.

Creators may tag opted-in businesses/products in authorized content. Affiliate attribution can generate commissions on qualified sales under documented merchant terms.

## PUBLISH Pipeline

StreetVerse/Holoverse -> mission/performance -> capture -> Reel/Movie Composer -> AI edit/translation/captions -> rights/safety checks -> authorized product placement -> PUBLISH -> TRYAMM feed/creator page -> share/export -> monetization ledger.

This is the common pipeline for Chicago music missions and future film, sports, business, education, and other mission systems.

## HoloGPT / Benny Action Layer

Planned permitted actions include commands such as record mission, create trailer, translate content, add captions, show authorized products, and publish to creator profile. Commands must execute through permissioned application actions rather than unrestricted client-side financial operations.

## HoloDisplay Abstraction

Target presentation modes:
- 2D phone display
- floating 3D/spatial panel
- AR/MR presentation
- TV/CTV presentation
- compatible holographic/spatial hardware renderer

Ordinary phones should use 3D/AR simulation and must not be represented as producing true free-space holograms without compatible hardware.

## Server-Authoritative Ledger

Every real-money flow must be verified server-side. The ledger should distinguish gross payment, processor fees, applicable tax treatment, refunds/chargebacks, platform share, creator/business/agent shares, restricted reward reserves, and payout-eligible balance.

Never trust client-side values for price, ownership, commissions, rewards, or payout eligibility.
