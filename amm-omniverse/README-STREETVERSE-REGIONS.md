## Shared player contract

One user → one avatar → one career history → one verified business identity → one reputation state → many regional adapters.

Each region owns its local law/licensing/public-safety/business adapter. State-level hubs such as Tennessee and Florida are containers for city/county adapters rather than a claim that one set of city rules covers an entire state.

## Economy loop

Job → shift → performance → skill XP/reputation → reference/promotion → entrepreneurship option → verified business claim → digital storefront → staff hiring → simulated foot traffic → eligible real marketplace traffic.

## Business claim rule

A public business-registry record is not ownership proof. Claimants must authenticate and prove an authorized relationship before a real business storefront can be controlled.

## Civic/death-care rule

Death-care missions stay non-graphic, fictionalized and jurisdiction-aware. Real identifiable death/arrest records are not used as game characters. Government coroner/medical-examiner work is not a commission-bearing marketplace transaction. Verified private providers can participate only through applicable licensing/compliance gates.
