## Legal and regulatory matrix

For each area record jurisdiction, licensed reviewer, scope, conclusion, required changes, completion evidence, and review date.

Areas include privacy, children/teens, employment, marketplace, advertising, media/music/IP, university branding, healthcare support, food delivery, rideshare, real estate, cross-border commerce, payments/credits, blockchain/tokens, AI, and hardware.

## Certification matrix

For each product/platform record authority, submission identifier, product/version, territory, required tests, submission date, status, decision, restrictions, renewal/expiration, and evidence reference.

Potential rows include Apple App Store, Google Play, IARC/ESRB, Bluetooth qualification, FCC/radio, electrical safety, battery transport, laser/IR safety, retailer/vendor onboarding, console platform approval, and XR platform approval.

## Art and audio rights ledger

For each asset record asset ID, title, creator/performer, source, license/release type, permitted media, territory, term, commercial permission, modification permission, attribution, proof reference, expiration, and approval status.
