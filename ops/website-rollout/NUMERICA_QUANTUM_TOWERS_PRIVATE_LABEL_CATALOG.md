## Diagnosing speed vs Wi-Fi
1. Document customer's subscribed ISP tier and modem/router model; wired speed, Wi-Fi speed (near/far), latency, jitter, packet loss, device count and peak-hour behavior.
2. If wired service is below provisioned speeds, check modem provisioning/cables, congestion and outages; obtain customer approval to escalate to ISP or quote a lawful alternative provider. TRYAMM cannot change carrier capacity by itself.
3. If wired is healthy but Wi-Fi poor, adjust AP placement, bands/channel width/interference, firmware, QoS/SQM if router has it, Ethernet backhaul, guest isolation and Wi-Fi 6/6E/7 hardware where supported.
4. Quantum Boost means software/network optimization, measured before/after: reduce preventable Wi-Fi bottlenecks, local caching for owned content, image compression, edge/CDN delivery, sensible game stream settings and traffic prioritization. No quantum hardware, beyond-ISP bandwidth, guaranteed zero lag, bypass of carrier throttling, or promise of faster-than-light access.
5. For StreetVerse, minimize downloads, cache appropriate public assets, reduce render work, load lightweight fallback during 402 failures, and record mobile FPS and network metrics separately.
