## Quantum Sourcing workflow
1. Receive supplier quote and model-specific documentation.
2. Verify authorization/brand license and U.S. equipment approval (FCC ID and US power compliance as applicable). Hard fail when missing.
3. Calculate total landed cost (supplier unit cost + freight + duties + broker + tax + payment fees + warranty/return provision + variable support + logistics).
4. Estimate channel margin and post-sale support obligations; no public retail price without quote.
5. Confirm consumer returns, shipping tracking, fulfillment SLA, security update policy, warranty and country-of-origin disclosures.
6. Publish product only after U.S. eligibility, stock, approved reseller contract, sample evaluation and checkout-fulfillment integration are proven.
7. Obtain customer payment via verified checkout; then and only then place authorized fulfillment order, deliver tracking, support refunds and RMA.

## Safety controls
