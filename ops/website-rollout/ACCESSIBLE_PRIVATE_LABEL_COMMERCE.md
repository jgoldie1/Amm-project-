# Stubbs AI — accessible private-label commerce specification
**State:** Design / sourcing and price gate; not production commerce activation.

## Brand separation
- Newumerica: electronics, AR/VR/MR, men/women/children fashion, home/lifestyle.
- Stubbs Mobility: accessibility-first product curation across disability categories. Never imply one product suits every person.
- Shared, centrally licensed technology: Stubbs AI Quantum Sourcing, catalog media, shipping and payments integration. Separate merchants, orders, rights, receipts, support, and analytics.

## Inclusive needs, not diagnoses-as-targets
Catalog filters and human-authored descriptions: one-handed use / limited dexterity, wheelchair clearance, vision and screen-reader support, hearing and captions, cognitive clarity and easy instructions, chronic fatigue / low exertion, speech/voice alternatives, neurodivergent sensory preferences, height/reach, mobility, caregiver support, and children/adult age fit. Let shoppers specify preferences and requirements; do not infer disability from viewing behavior or use disability as a marketing profiling signal.

## Supplier onboarding gate
1. Verify manufacturer identity, beneficial owner, export capability, references, sample and warranty policy.
2. Get model-by-model documents: real photos, demo video rights, measurements, loading limits, instructions, spare parts, battery transport docs, customs classification, origin, test reports, CPSC/FDA/FCC relevance.
3. Obtain transparent EXW/FOB/DDP terms, Incoterms, production and shipping lead times, packaging tooling, order MOQ, sample costs, return address, defect allowance.
4. Get English plus the supplier's preferred language; retain both versions and require English confirmation of final operative contract clauses.
5. Inspect samples and test accessibility with opt-in reviewers; video reviews disclose compensation, sponsorship and free samples.

## Pricing and margin gate
Use quote.cjs with documented *actual* per-unit costs. Product cost + logo/printing + VIP box + freight/duties + pick/pack + payment costs + expected returns + creator commission + customer support reserve are embedded before price approval. Target is contribution margin, not guaranteed net profit; compare market prices and accessibility affordability. Allocate one-time brand tooling and certification cost across a conservative number of units, never ignore them. No item may publish with an unverified landed cost, unapproved margin or missing compliance documents.

## Customer workflow
Product selection → compatibility/accessibility checklist → truthful product video → HoloGPT answers only from reviewed catalog → accessible cart → server-verified payment → supplier fulfillment API → tracking + delivery proof → returns/warranty/recall → verified creator payout → separated business ledgers. 
Mobile first; keyboard, screen reader, captions, reduced motion, readable fonts, one-handed controls, easy returns and assisted support. Do not promise telehealth, therapy, recovery or suitability for an individual's condition.

## VIP box
Standard protective packaging included in base SKU cost; VIP unboxing upgrade has separate line items for sturdy recyclable box, inserts, large-print/QR instructions, multi-language card, video review insert, tracking, extra postage, breakage reserve. Show VIP price transparently. No medical claims, deceptive "free gift" pricing or undisclosed sample incentives.

## Launch guardrails
Start with low-risk accessories and products with independently validated documentation. For powered chairs, patient lifts, braces, helmets, electrical/battery products and children's items use product-specific specialist compliance review and insurance before sales. Never claim current authorization, clinical efficacy, service eligibility, LIVE shopping, Stripe payout, refunds, or real Quantum hardware until verified.
