# Quantum Internet Network & Quantum Towers — preserved concept and deployment boundaries
Status: architecture design only. No physical towers, independent ISP backbone, spectrum license, operational mesh, or quantum networking hardware has been verified.

## Unified stack
1. **HoloGPT / Stubbs AI / Lyons Tech AI:** AI product interfaces and governed developer API adapters.
2. **Holographic Internet / CrossVerse / StreetVerse:** accessible digital portals, games and historical archive exploration.
3. **Quantum Internet:** historical Time Dial, Memento-compatible archive discovery, communication gateway, and future private network services.
4. **Quantum Towers:** proposed local wireless nodes, edge compute, connectivity handoff and network monitoring; tower name does not imply quantum communications or quantum speed.
5. **Independent network:** first build an authorized private IP network / community mesh, then investigate ISP interconnection and regulatory compliance if viable.

## Architecture
User device → Wi-Fi or lawful licensed/unlicensed radio → Quantum Tower node → authenticated encrypted backhaul → regional edge service → approved Internet transit / peering → TRYAMM applications.
Applications do NOT work without normal connectivity unless offline caching and local networking are separately implemented.
Memento archives and external AI vendors require external Internet service.
Potential components: Wi-Fi access points, appropriate backhaul, routing, managed DNS, caching/CDN, network telemetry, UPS/power, fiber or fixed wireless uplinks, firewalls, failover and accessible support.

## Execution gates
- Survey sites, permission/lease, right of way, antennas, power, physical safety, uptime and build costs.
- Verify FCC spectrum/device obligations, municipal zoning, structural engineering and telecom requirements with specialists.
- Carrier/ISP peering, address allocation, abuse handling, cybersecurity, privacy, and terms for community users.
- No unsupported claims about quantum entanglement, FTL, limitless bandwidth or invulnerable encryption.
- Keep customer communications and mail hosted by approved providers until deliverability and security are verified.
- Test a lab with two owned nodes and one authorized backhaul; record latency, throughput, packet loss, failover and monthly cost.
- Separate demo status from hardware deployment status.
