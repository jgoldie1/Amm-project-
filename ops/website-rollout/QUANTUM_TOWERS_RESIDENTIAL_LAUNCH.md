# Quantum Towers — Residential Launch Addendum
Status: prelaunch; no customer charges, active ISP resale, or installed residential towers.
## Segments
Apartments and renters, single-family homes, seniors, remote workers, gamers, creators, multi-dwelling buildings. Accessible one-hand and plain-language inquiries.

Optional on-site install, ISP plan upgrades, mesh/AP hardware and travel are separate written quotes, and depend on qualified partner availability.
These are proposed only; no public payment link or subscription enabled until qualified reviewer, written scope/refund policy, verified payment and delivery exist.
## Residential service workflow
1. Owner/renter inquiry; no passwords, payment credentials or precise network secrets.
2. Confirm permission for equipment changes, and landlord permission where required.
3. Obtain ISP tier and assess speed using repeated wired/Wi-Fi testing; report jitter, latency, loss and upload/downlink separately.
4. Try approved zero-cost measures first; router placement, interference, firmware, Wi-Fi bands, QoS/SQM.
5. When ISP capacity is the bottleneck, explain plan limits and escalate to provider with customer consent. 100 Mbps ISP service cannot become guaranteed 1 Gbps via software.
6. Offer optional FCC-authorized mesh/AP devices from approved sources, customer-owned by default.
7. Provide measurements, written recommendations, optional referrals and customer feedback; do not claim guaranteed zero lag.
## Apartments / multi-tenant
Written owner/manager permission for shared physical infrastructure and common-area Wi-Fi. Segregate household networks, protect tenant privacy, respect ISP sharing/resale terms, verify fire/building codes for cabling.
## Business operations
Assign technician, set geographic service area, support hours, refund terms, cancellation rights if recurring, insurance, returns/RMA, taxes and verified Stripe order fulfillment. Do not promise 24/7 monitoring before operational.
## Marketing
"Quantum Boost: assess home dead zones, video call drops and gaming lag. Reuse your existing equipment first." Do not advertise free Internet, quantum hardware, carrier capacity boosts or speed guarantees.
