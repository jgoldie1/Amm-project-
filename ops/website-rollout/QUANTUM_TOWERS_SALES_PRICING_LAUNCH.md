## Early customers
Chicago retailers, cafés, barbershops, beauty businesses, churches, nonprofits and multi-tenant building managers.
Start from a remote assessment plus a qualified local installation partner. Offer interested site hosts a sponsored pilot conversation, not free services as a guarantee.

## Zero-budget pipeline
1. Public landing page: clear preview status, packages **subject to service availability**, and inquiry by email.
2. Customer intake: business address at general location granularity, service needs, existing ISP, number of users/devices, consent to review networking, accessibility preferences.
3. Qualify: avoid collecting passwords/payment-card data; verify partner availability and legal terms.
4. Send scoped written quote, exclusions, cancel/refund terms and completion timeline.
5. Secure provider permission before installing or reselling any service.
6. Collect payment only via server-confirmed checkout after a working service and delivery process exist.
7. Deliver assessment/report, then request feedback and optional network-care consent.

## Pilot partnership requirements
- Written authorization for site use, equipment access, electricity and upstream Internet connectivity.
- Determine whether proposed role is network consultant, managed Wi-Fi operator, reseller or carrier partner; each role differs legally and commercially.
- No installation or transit of third-party traffic without contract.
- Insurance, local permits and applicable communications rules evaluated before physical deployment.

## Ad compliance
Advertise "Quantum Towers partner pilots and network-assessment waitlist" while service is not yet verified.
Do not advertise "free Internet," "owned cellular towers," "quantum-speed Internet," or "nationwide coverage."

## Sales measurement
Track inquiries, qualified leads, quotes, accepted scope, delivered projects, gross margin, recurring subscriptions and churn. Track partner fees and customer-support workload.
