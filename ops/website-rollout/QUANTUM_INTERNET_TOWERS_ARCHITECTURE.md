# TRYAMM Independent Internet + Quantum Towers — Architecture Preservation
Status: proposed infrastructure architecture, NOT an operational independent Internet, deployed tower network, quantum communications hardware or licensed ISP.

## Vision
Create a TRYAMM-owned application network connecting HoloGPT, Quantum Internet, Holographic Internet, CrossVerse, StreetVerse, Quantum Internet Mail, merchants and creators. Pursue physical tower/mesh/backhaul deployments only after a lawful, funded pilot and engineering validation.

## Technology layers
1. **Application network (available to prototype now)**: signed identities, tenant isolation, HoloGPT routing, web/PWA interface, content addressing, CDN/cache, encrypted transport, operational observability.
2. **Community connectivity / Quantum Towers concept**: proposed rooftop or existing-site radios, Wi-Fi/community mesh, licensed or license-exempt spectrum as applicable, fiber/wireless backhaul, power and backup, monitoring, equipment maintenance, resilient topology. 'Quantum Towers' is a brand name, not evidence of quantum networking, quantum encryption or signal amplification beyond physics.
3. **Internet interoperability**: normal IP routing, DNS, TLS and connectivity via authorized ISPs/carriers. Do not claim replacement of global Internet, free nationwide cellular service, ownership of radio spectrum or independent access without upstream network.
4. **Historical and immersive layer**: public Memento and Wayback/Arquivo archive discovery with rights-aware Holographic Internet exhibits, explicitly distinguish reconstructed assets from historical evidence.
5. **Quantum Mail**: use a trustworthy hosted email provider first (MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, domain verification, abuse controls), avoid building independent SMTP service until commercial need justifies security and reputation costs.
6. **Cross AI**: per-provider permissions, server-side tokens, verified quotas, transparent fees, cache isolation and approved persistence.

## Pilot milestones
A. Prove stable HoloGPT + Quantum Internet site and contact/website-sales funnel using existing hosting and domain.
B. Build a private lab mesh using legally permitted hardware; benchmark actual latency, packet loss, uptime, throughput and failover, recording baseline and costs. No public tower construction.
C. Seek qualified wireless/network engineer and site survey; evaluate tower zoning, lease, structural safety, electrical, spectrum, FCC and local approvals, insurance and accessibility.
D. Only after funding + written permissions, test one authorized site using an established connectivity provider.
E. Expand through real demand and sustainable recurring revenue, not blanket 'free unlimited' promises.

## Non-negotiable release gates
- Preserve StreetVerse and existing production; avoid deploying untested changes.
- Confirm CI, browser accessibility, mobile performance, archive checks, tenant isolation, SMTP deliverability and customer checkout.
- Prevent cross-tenant data leaks, public secret exposure, unsafe unvalidated URLs, and unapproved purchases or deployments.
- Obtain explicit owner approval for infrastructure contracts, recurring bills or DNS changes.

## Value
Provides a branded unified interface, possible future network resilience and local access, and potential subscription/business connectivity services. Performance claims must use independent real measurements. Real tower deployment requires major capital and ongoing operations.
