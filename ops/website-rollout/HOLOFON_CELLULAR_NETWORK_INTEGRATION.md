# HoloFon × Quantum Towers × Quantum Boost — cellular roadmap
Status: roadmap integration only; no verified HoloFon carrier core, spectrum license, radio access network, SIM/eSIM provisioning, carrier agreement, or live mobile network.
Preserve earlier HoloFon companion/controller and TRYAMM services design. This plan extends it toward potential cellular access without claiming hardware exists.
## Layer 1 (current pilot): software and existing authorized connectivity
- HoloFon concept supports Wi-Fi and customer-owned mobile data over their existing lawful provider, not private cellular transmission.
- Quantum Boost remote diagnostics reports customer-supplied speeds/latency and permitted repair guidance; separate ISP vs Wi-Fi vs device rendering.
- Accessibility: one-handed operation, readable text, explicit consent, no credential collection.
## Layer 2: partner-carrier / MVNO feasibility
- Pursue contract with MNO or MVNE for U.S. lawful voice/text/data resale, SIM/eSIM activation, coverage, E911, porting, billing, tax, privacy, fraud controls, customer support, consumer protections and accessibility.
- Verify whether TRYAMM would be a branded reseller, MVNO, or software service; responsibilities differ.
- No claim of coverage, free data, speed uplift or activation until written terms and tested pilot.
## Layer 3: private/neutral-host access (separate economics)
- With site permission and contracted backhaul, evaluate permitted CBRS/private LTE/5G through authorized integrators and applicable spectrum coordination, radio approvals and operational requirements.
- Do not use existing towers/radios without permission; no uncontrolled signal boosters.
- Measure coverage, handoffs, throughput, congestion, QoS, jitter, loss and uptime.
## Layer 4: HoloFon hardware / OpenXR
- Existing vision: companion/controller for TRYAMM immersive services, adapter-first OpenXR and later secure hardware. Do not misrepresent this as an already built phone or cellular carrier.
- Prototype app UI on current handsets before purchasing/custom-building devices.
## Required go/no-go
- Carrier or infrastructure partner signed
- Lawful spectrum/radio and FCC equipment compliance independently verified
- Operating entity, recurring costs, E911 and customer care obligations scoped
- SIM/eSIM provisioning in sandbox then authorized controlled test
- Security/privacy review and test evidence
- Genuine measured field performance
- Production/checkout gated until acceptance
## Messaging
"Quantum Towers builds managed Wi-Fi and remote diagnostics today; HoloFon is a proposed future branded mobile experience, dependent on licensed/authorized carrier infrastructure."
