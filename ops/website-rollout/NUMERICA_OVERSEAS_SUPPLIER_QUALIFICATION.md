## Required qualification before listing hardware for checkout
1. Manufacturer confirms reseller/private-label terms in writing. No rebranding of trademarks, firmware or regulatory IDs without permission.
2. Exact model SKU, FCC identifier, US power/plug certification, firmware maintenance and vulnerability support.
3. Sample-unit quote, lead time, shipping to Chicago, customs/duties, taxes and warranty.
4. Real unit economics: landed cost + payment fees + defect reserve + handling + support + return costs; target margin justified by actual quote.
5. Test with one authorized network: wired throughput baseline, Wi-Fi throughput in multiple rooms, latency, jitter, packet loss, roaming, power and stability.
6. Confirm RMA/DOA handling, returns, warranty ownership, technical support escalation and product liability allocation.
7. Check compatible router/mesh/access point models and supplier's explicit US-market distribution authorization.
8. Require customer-owned or approved equipment purchases; do not buy bulk stock until paid orders and cash flow are established.

## Quantum Boost assessment deliverable
- Customer-approved diagnostic intake
- ISP plan vs wired measured speed (when client can measure)
- Wi-Fi near/far measurements, latency/jitter/loss, known dead zones
- Device/router firmware and configuration recommendations
- Distinguish ISP limitation from LAN/Wi-Fi or game FPS limitation
- Before/after report with measured methods and any residual limits
- No guaranteed increase in subscribed Internet bandwidth; no unauthorized carrier equipment or repeater installation.
