# TRYAMM Website OS — First Paid Client and Repeatable Sales Pilot
Status: internal sales plan, NOT an accepted order or verified payment.

## First client to approach: Sculptify LTD
Reason: Established project requirements: body sculpting, massage, Reiki/wellness services, academy, shop, booking, mobile-first storefront. Existing repository work may help reduce implementation costs. Do not assume agreement or payment; seek owner approval.

## First limited deliverable
Offer a **$499 Website Preview** (subject to customer's agreement and licensing) including:
- Single accessible branded landing page
- Contact/inquiry or booking request link verified to reach Sculptify owner (do not promise integrated paid appointments unless tested)
- One short promotional Reel subject to approved assets/content
- QR code linking to deployed page
- Client review/approval step, basic accessibility/mobile checks, written handoff and agreed revision allowance
Do not include academy enrollment, full inventory, merchant payouts, automated scheduling or StreetVerse features in the $499 package unless separately scoped and ready. Those could be phase 2 upsells.

## Suggested sales sequence
1. Secure a client decision-maker's agreement to receive a precise fixed-price proposal; disclose current TRYAMM platform updates and any hosting constraints.
2. Confirm licensed business branding and media, website domain, payment account ownership, contact destination, privacy requirements, delivery deadline and accessibility expectations.
3. Send simple scope with price, revisions, milestones, responsibilities, cancellation/refund terms and any hosting recurring fees.
4. Demonstrate a working preview. Client approves scope.
5. Take payment only through a **server-verified live checkout** with business entity, receipt, refunds and owner settlement confirmed. Do not rely on client-side success flags or collect card details in email.
6. Complete mobile and desktop QA; verify inquiry reaches owner and no checkout bypass exists. Record evidence.
7. Deliver site access, source/content portability and independent customer dashboard as contracted; request testimonial only with consent.
8. Offer optional $149/month Business Pass ONLY after documenting support terms and verifying billing/cancellation; no automatic enrollment.

## Replication across industries
Use reusable modules for landing pages, lead capture, scheduling request, accessible catalog, CRM handoff, content management and independent account access. Build each module once, with per-tenant isolation/RLS and payments separated by business. Never imply 111 templates are fully ready based on category entries.

## Revenue guardrails
Avoid invented ROI, guaranteed sales or auto-publishing. Price includes labor and infrastructure margin review before binding quotes. No migration that overwrites an existing client site before tested backup and explicit approval. Preserve backups and no-password inquiry process.
