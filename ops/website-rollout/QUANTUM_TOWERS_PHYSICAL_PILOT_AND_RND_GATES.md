# Quantum Towers — Physical Pilot and Quantum R&D Gates
Status: pre-deployment planning; no verified tower, ISP capacity upgrade, quantum hardware, or physical pilot installed.
## Truthful speed claim
An ISP delivering 100 Mbps cannot be transformed by Quantum Boost into guaranteed 1 Gbps Internet. Actual upstream capacity must be upgraded or added through an authorized alternate uplink. Wi-Fi optimization may improve local usable performance and latency when local bottlenecks are present.
## Gate 1: Measurable classical connectivity improvement
- Secure consenting customer or test location and authorized network administrator.
- Baseline ISP subscribed tier and repeated wired and Wi-Fi measurements (Mbps up/down, p50/p95 latency, jitter, packet loss, peak/off-peak); note device FPS separately.
- Try zero-cost router placement/firmware/approved QoS or SQM; only buy hardware if evidence suggests it.
- Repeat measurements with same devices, locations, methods; deliver report with raw figures and limitations.
## Gate 2: Real two-node edge pilot
- Written permission for site/roof access, installed equipment, power and agreed upstream broadband use.
- Confirm ISP agreement permits the intended sharing/resale; avoid bypassing restrictions.
- Qualified technician, risk assessment, radio compliance, access security and customer privacy.
- Two permitted Wi-Fi/edge nodes using authorized broadband; record uptime, coverage and power failure behavior.
- Document monthly all-in costs and who owns/maintains equipment.
## Gate 3: Commercial service and tower partner
- Business insurance, service terms, privacy/security practices, clear refund/support and service-level commitments.
- Carrier/tower landlord agreements; review FCC/state/local obligations for actual service model.
- Verify customer billing, support, complaints, outage escalation and fulfillment before accepting recurring payments.
## Gate 4: Quantum networking (separate experimental research)
- Academic or specialist optical-quantum communications partner, mutually agreed scope and institutional safety review.
- Define actual qubit transmission, fidelity, entanglement, detector/memory/repeater metrics; funding and protected lab.
- Public claims must distinguish classical network from demonstrably quantum links.
## Go/no-go
No network speed claims without measured tests. No U.S. wireless hardware retail without authorization. No 24/7 service claims without operations capacity. No physical tower claims before installed/verified deployment. Preserve staged storefront and don't charge for unavailable services.
