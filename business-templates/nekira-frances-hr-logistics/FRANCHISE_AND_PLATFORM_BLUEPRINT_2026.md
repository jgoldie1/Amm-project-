# Nekira Frances HR Logistics — Frances AI platform and licensing blueprint
Updated 2026-10-25. Planning document; not legal or financial advice.

## Product modules (all included in proposed scope)
1. Public branded logistics site, quote requests, consent-based CRM and accessible forms.
2. Carrier portal: authority/insurance checks, equipment, lanes, compliance documents.
3. Shipper portal: approved quote, order updates, tracking, document exchange.
4. Freight operations: licensed board integration, dispatch queues, appointments, rate confirmations and audit.
5. HR workforce hub: recruitment, job workflows, onboarding, permissions, accessibility.
6. Remote-work workstation and AI-assisted call center: schedules, tickets, scripts, human escalation; telephony/AI backend pending.
7. Frances AI (branded MiddleVerse/HoloGPT): domain-approved knowledge, retrieval, intent routing, task execution only after permission checks, audit trails; prototype scripted only.
8. Merchant storefronts, secure billing, subscriptions, refund rules and product ownership.
9. Business Scouts: consented merchants, enrollment, documented referral attribution, payable commission only after verified settled transaction.
10. Detroit StreetVerse RP: 3D city map and logistics missions, earned practice XP, businesses and optional LIVE creator streaming with moderation; live route pending.
11. Franchise/license administration: onboarding, training, branding, QA, support, territory management, royalty ledger, reporting and renewals.
12. Operations: backup/recovery, incident response, security testing, role isolation, costs, analytics and accessibility.

## Proposed franchise-like territory packages (not offers)
- Starter $4,995 entry; templates, approved training and limited software access.
- Professional $14,995 entry; configured workflows, AI receptionist and operations training after service activation.
- Premium $29,995 entry; additional territory/teams and marketing/operational support based on written scope.
- Enterprise $49,995+ negotiated; integration, exclusivity and support require separate underwriting.
Prices are hypotheses for market validation, NOT appraisals, sales comps, earnings claims or legally authorized franchise offers.

## Alternative faster go-to-market
Start by selling software implementation/subscriptions WITHOUT brand/territory or regulated franchise features after lawyer review. Franchise definition depends on legal realities rather than simply the product label.

## Costs and valuation
Published developer estimates of $50k-$220k refer to costs of creating a custom digital freight platform, not current prototype resale value and not expected income. Full multi-tenant systems with real voice, audit, payroll, payments, 3D game and franchise administration may exceed that estimate.
Operating profit = collected revenue - all verified expenses. No income guaranteed.

## Compliance gates
- FMCSA freight broker vs bona fide dispatch authority determined before offering freight transactions. Human approval of shipments and payment flows.
- FTC Franchise Rule/FDD, possible state registrations and franchise counsel BEFORE franchise solicitation, signing or payment.
- Applicant privacy, employment laws, background screening, consent, wage/hour and contractor classification.
- AI caller disclosure, call recording consent by jurisdiction, retention and human handoff.
- Regulated payables and commissions only from settled auditable transactions.
- Verify portrait rights and business name before public website/ads.
- No live streaming/content release without consent, creator rights and moderation.
- Never overwrite main StreetVerse game routes; release new modules behind feature flags and a rollback path.

## Acceptance criteria for production
Owner approval; 3 test user roles isolation; demo shipper-to-verified-carrier workflow; secure employee onboarding; 2-way telephony and tested human fallback; checkout webhook ledger reconciliation; backup restore drill; mobile and one-handed accessibility test; signed franchise/legal review. Launch one market pilot before multi-territory rollout.
