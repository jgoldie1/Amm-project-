## Technical maturity, not valuation
Static interactive templates are saleable only subject to rights and working-delivery testing. The full backend, role-based authentication, HoloGPT inference and live calls, real licensed freight data, payment/commission settlement, multiplayer game, 3D Detroit world, storefront inventory and franchise operations are not yet verified live. A third-party bespoke software construction quote is not a valuation of current code.

## Product tiers
1. Basic template purchase: unhosted, user-controlled assets.
2. Customized website service: customer approves branding and scope.
3. Hosted Business-in-the-Box subscription: requires real service operations, support, billing and data isolation.
4. White-label software licensing: code, hosting, APIs and maintenance contract.
5. Franchise: legal counsel and relevant disclosures/registrations required; do not solicit/sell as a franchise before compliance.

## Lowest-license-cost architecture
- Frontend: authored HTML/CSS/JS or existing React/Next stack.
- Database: SQLite for single-machine pilots; PostgreSQL for multi-tenant production.
- Backend: Node built-ins or Python stdlib for prototypes; no dependency licensing fees.
- AI: local inference optional on capable hardware, but models carry their own licenses/hardware costs. Voice/telephony is not cost-free at real-world scale.
- Files: customer-controlled object store or filesystem subject to backup/security.
- Payments: payment processors levy transaction fees.
- Live freight listings: authorized access and provider terms are necessary.
- Distribution: downloadable templates first, real SaaS only after tests and support.

## Non-negotiable release tests
- Own-or-authorized asset checklist passes.

- Checkout webhook and payable commissions reconciled, not simulated.
- Backup and restore tested.
